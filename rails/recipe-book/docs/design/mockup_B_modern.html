Spicy Chicken Stir-Fry
Entrées · from your collection
⏱ 25 min🍽 Serves 4 ★ 4.6weeknightone-pan
Ingredients
2 chicken breasts, sliced
4 Tbsp canola oil
1 lb butternut squash
10 oz fire-roasted peppers
2 cubes minced ginger
2 cups edamame
¼ cup peanut satay
Soy · sriracha · lime
Method
  1. Sear seasoned chicken in hot oil, 4–5 min; set aside.
  2. Caramelize squash 6–7 min; add peppers + ginger.
  3. Add edamame, satay, soy, sriracha, lime; return chicken.
  4. Finish with cilantro + peanuts; serve over rice.
