Spicy Chicken Stir-Fry
🥘
Total 25 min · Serves 4 · ★ 4.6 · from your collection

Ingredients

2 large chicken breasts, thinly sliced
4 Tbsp canola oil
1 lb butternut squash, chopped
10 oz fire-roasted peppers, thawed
2 cubes minced ginger
2 cups shelled edamame
¼ cup peanut satay sauce
2 Tbsp soy sauce · 1 Tbsp sriracha
Juice of 1 lime
Cilantro · peanuts · white rice

Method

  1. Season the chicken; sear in hot oil until browned, 4–5 min, then set aside.
  2. Caramelize the butternut squash, 6–7 min; add peppers and ginger.
  3. Add edamame, satay, soy, sriracha and lime; return chicken and toss to heat through.
  4. Finish with cilantro and peanuts. Serve over white rice.
