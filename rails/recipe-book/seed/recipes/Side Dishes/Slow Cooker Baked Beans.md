# 5-Ingredient Slow-Cooker Beans With Bacon

*Total: 5 hrs 15 mins*

## Ingredients
- 6 to 8 strips bacon (cut in 1/2- to 1-inch pieces)
- 3 (16 oz.) cans baked beans
- 1/4 cup brown sugar (packed)
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard powder
- 1/2 cup ketchup (or use chili sauce)

## Instructions
1. Gather the ingredients.
2. Arrange the bacon in a skillet and place it over medium-low heat. Fry the bacon until it is browned and crisp, stirring and turning frequently.
3. Remove the bacon to paper towels to drain.
4. Pour the beans into the slow cooker and add the bacon.
5. In a bowl, combine the brown sugar, mustard powder, and ketchup or chili sauce. Add the sauce mixture to the beans and stir to blend.
6. Cover and cook on LOW for 4 to 5 hours or on HIGH for about 2 hours.
7. Serve and enjoy!

## Shopping List
- bacon (6 to 8 strips)
- baked beans (3 cans of 16 oz.)
- brown sugar (1/4 cup)
- mustard powder (1/2 teaspoon)
- ketchup or chili sauce (1/2 cup)
