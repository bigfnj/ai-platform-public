# Cilantro Marinated Cucumber

## Ingredients
- ½ cup cilantro chopped
- ½ teaspoon garlic chopped
- ¼ teaspoon jalapeno chopped
- 1 teaspoon toasted walnuts chopped
- ¼ cup white wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon black raisins
- 1 cucumber sliced thin (1/2 if you are using an English cucumber)
- 1 carrot grated

## Instructions
1. Combine first seven ingredients in blender and puree.
2. Pour into a bowl and add cucumber and carrot.
3. Cover and refrigerate for ½ hour or more.
