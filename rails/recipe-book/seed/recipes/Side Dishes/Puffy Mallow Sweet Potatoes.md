# Puffy Mallow Sweet Potatoes

*6 servings*

## Ingredients
- 3 - 5 medium sweet potatoes, cut in large chunks
- 1 cup pineapple or orange juice
- ¼ cup butter
- 1 slightly beaten egg
- 1 tsp salt
- ¼ tsp cinnamon
- 8 marshmallows

## Instructions
1. Cook potatoes in covered pan in juice until tender, about 20 min.
2. Drain and save juice.
3. Mash potatoes and add butter, eggs, salt, cinnamon and 1/3 cup juice.
4. Beat until creamy.
5. Spoon into 8 inch round casserole.
6. Press marshmallows into top, lightly .
7. Bake, uncovered for 30 min at 325 – 350.
