# Mom’s Thanksgiving Stuffing

## Ingredients
- 1.5 C chopped onion
- 1 C chopped celery with leaves
- ¾ C butter
- ½ lb ground pork sausage meat
- ½ C chopped mushrooms
- 8 C coarse breadcrumbs
- 1 T salt
- .5 t pepper
- 1 t sage
- .5 t thyme
- .5 t rosemary
- .5 t nutmeg
- 1 C white wine or chicken stock
- 1 large egg, beaten
- .5 chopped dried apricots

## Instructions
1. Saute onion and celery in butter until tender.
2. In a separate skillet, cook sausage and mushrooms. Pour off the fat & add to onion and celery.
3. Combine this mixture with the wine or chicken stock, add the egg and apricots and mix well.
4. Stuff the poultry just before roasting.
