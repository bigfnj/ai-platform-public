# Sausage & Mushroom Stuffing

## Ingredients
- 1 loaf French baguette, stale
- 1 (32 oz) box chicken broth
- 2 celery stalks, chopped fine
- 10 baby carrots, chopped fine
- 1 small brown onion, chopped fine
- 1 yellow bell pepper, chopped fine
- olive oil
- basil
- sage
- rosemary
- salt and pepper
- ¾ cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 pound sausage (spicy), if links remove from casing
- 3-4 boxes assorted mushrooms, coarsely chopped

## Instructions
1. Pre-heat oven to 325°F.
2. Cut bread into medium size pieces, into bowl use crumbs too.
3. Saute vegetables in olive oil until soft. Pour vegetable into bowl with the bread.
4. Brown and break up sausage, add sausage and fat to bread.
5. Saute mushrooms until soft in olive oil, add to bread mixture.
6. Chop herbs medium fine and add to bread mixture.
7. Add chicken broth to bread mixture to the consistency that you like your stuffing.
8. About ½ to ¾ of box for medium wet. Whole carton for wet.
9. Add cheese to mixture . Salt and pepper to taste.
10. Mix together.
11. Place in an 11x14 baking dish and bake for about 1 hour or until golden brown.
