# Sweet Potato Brûlée

## Ingredients
- 3 large red-skinned sweet potatoes
- ¾ cup whipping cream
- 3 large egg yolks
- 1 ½ tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper
- ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ⅛ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- Chopped nuts, optional

## Instructions
1. Cook sweet potatoes until tender.
2. Drain and puree until smooth.
3. Whisk cream, egg yolks, brown sugar, salt, pepper, cinnamon , and nutmeg in a bowl to blend.
4. Whisk 2 ½ cups potato puree into cream mixture.
5. Preheat oven to 350°F.
6. Butter or spray a baking dish.
7. Spoon mixture into baking dish sprinkle with brown sugar.
8. Place dish into another pan, or casserole dish, add enough hot water to pan to come halfway up sides.
9. Cover pan with foil, bake for 45 minutes to one hour.
10. Remove, add nut and more brown sugar and heat until crisp.
