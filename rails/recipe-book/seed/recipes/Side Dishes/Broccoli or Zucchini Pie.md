# Broccoli or Zucchini Pie

## Ingredients
- 4 medium zucchini or broccoli, chopped
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup bisquick
- ½ cup oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cups grated cheddar cheese
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Instructions
1. Mix all the above together until oil is mixed.
2. Spray a round Pyrex with Pam.
3. Pour mixture and bake @ 350 degrees for about 1 hour or until browned.
