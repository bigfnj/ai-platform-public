# Butternut Squash Soufflé

## Ingredients
- 1 lb baked butternut squash (sliced in half, covered in foil)
- 1 lb cooked carrots (pre-peeled baby carrots)
- 1 stick butter (melted)
- ⅓ cup brown sugar
- 5 tablespoons flour (potato starch for Passover)
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 5 large eggs

## Instructions
1. Combine first 6 items in a food processor.
2. Blend 2 minutes.
3. Add eggs one at a time until smooth.
4. Pour into a buttered medium or large soufflé dish.
5. Sprinkle ⅓ cup brown sugar on top before baking.
6. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour and 15 minutes.

## Shopping List
- 1 lb butternut squash
- foil
- 1 lb carrots (baby)
- 1 stick butter
