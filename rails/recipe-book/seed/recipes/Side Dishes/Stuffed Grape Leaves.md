# Stuffed Grape Leaves

## Ingredients
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- ½ cup olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- Lemon juice
- Salt & pepper
- 1 cup white rice
- 1 (16 oz.) jar grape leaves

## Instructions
1. Saute onions in hot oil in a skillet over medium heat until onions are soft.
2. While the onions are cooking peel the garlic cloves and mash them to a paste.
3. Add this to the pan along with the lemon juice, salt and pepper to taste.
4. Cook for another 5 minutes.
5. Stir in the rice.
6. Remove from the heat.
7. Set aside to cool to room temperature.
8. While this is cooling, drain the grape leaves, pull them apart.
9. Put them into a bowl and cover with cold water.
10. Let them soak until you are ready to roll.
11. Heat oven to 350°F
12. Place a grape leaf on work surface, shiny side down.
13. Add 1 tablespoon of the rice mixture to the middle of the grape leaf.
14. Fold the sides over the rice and roll.
15. Repeat with remaining rice.
16. Cover the stuffed leaves with several layers of flat grape leaves and pour in 1 cup of water.
17. Cover and bake for 1 hour.
18. Let cool to room temperature and refrigerate.
