# Mashed Baked Potatoes, AKA Mashed Potato Casserole

*Difficulty: Medium - Prep time: 10 minutes - Cook time: 10 minutes - Servings: 10*

## Ingredients
- 2 cups french-fried onion rings
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 (7.6 oz) package butter & herbed mashed potato mix
- 1 cup Monterey Jack cheese
- 4 cups frozen hash browns
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 1 stick butter
- 2 cups water

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
2. Bring a pot of water to the boil and add the hash browns. Cook for 5 minutes and drain.
3. In a large bowl, mix together the cooked hash browns, mashed potato mix, butter, cream cheese, jack cheese, sour cream, garlic salt, salt and pepper.
4. Stir in the boiling water.
5. Place in a greased 2-quart casserole dish.
6. Bake for 35 to 45 minutes.
7. Sprinkle the onion rings over the casserole and bake for about 5 to 8 minutes longer until onions are golden brown.
