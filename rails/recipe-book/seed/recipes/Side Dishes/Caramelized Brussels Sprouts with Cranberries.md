# Caramelized Brussels Sprouts with Cranberries

*Serves 8-10*

## Ingredients
- 10 cups water
- Salt
- 4 pounds Brussels sprouts
- 5 or 6 pieces of bacon, minced (can also use pancetta)
- 1 red onion, diced
- 4 tablespoons butter, divided
- ¼ cup red wine vinegar
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 cup fresh cranberries
- Black pepper

## Instructions
1. Bring water to a boil.
2. Generously salt the water and add the sprouts. Cook until medium tenderness has been reached, about 8-10 minutes.
3. In a large saucepan over medium heat fry the bacon until crisp, stirring occasionally. Remove bacon onto paper towel.
4. Leave the bacon fat in the pan and add the onions and 3 tablespoons butter. Sauté onions until translucent, about 2-3 minutes.
5. Stir in the red wine vinegar and brown sugar and cook until liquid has slightly reduced, about 5 minutes.
6. Drain the sprouts and add to pan with the onions. Stir to coat and add the remaining 1 tablespoon butter.
7. Stir in the bacon and cranberries. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
