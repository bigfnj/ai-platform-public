# Pizza Stuffing

*Serves 4*

## Ingredients
- 1 (12 oz) loaf day old French bread
- 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup prepared pizza sauce
- 1 egg
- 8 ounces mozzarella cheese, shredded
- 1 (6 oz) package Jones Dairy Farm Canadian Bacon, cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 1/4 cup shredded basil

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Cut bread into 1/2-inch cubes and place in single layer on baking sheet.
3. Toast in oven until golden, about 12 minutes, stirring halfway through.
4. In large bowl, combine chicken broth, pizza sauce and egg.
5. Add toasted bread cubes, mozzarella and Canadian Bacon, reserving some cheese for topping.
6. Spray 3-quart baking dish with nonstick cooking spray and pour mixture into it.
7. Top with reserved cheese.
8. Bake until cooked through and cheese is melted, about 25-30 minutes.
9. Top with basil to serve.
