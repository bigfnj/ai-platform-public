# Sweet Corn Souffle

## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 2 cans (15 ounce) creamed corn
- ¼ cup sugar
- 1 stick margarine or butter, melted
- ½ cup flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ¾ Tbsp vanilla flavored soy milk creamer
- ¼ cup water
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Instructions
1. Pre-heat oven to 350.
2. Mix all of the above, and bake for about 1 to 1 ½ hours until firm.
