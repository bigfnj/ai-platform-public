# Butternut Squash Casserole

*Serves: 8*

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 1 onion, chopped
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 strips bacon or 2 sausages (1/2 lb)
- 1 box mushrooms sliced
- 2 butternut squashes small (or 1 large)
- 1/2 cup chicken stock
- Salt to taste

### Crumble
- 3/4 cup all purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons Italian seasoning
- 1 tablespoon Nutmeg
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme (or 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 stick cold unsalted butter, cut into pieces

## Instructions

### For the filling
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Butter a 2 quart baking dish.
2. In a large pan, heat the oil until hot. Saute the onions, garlic, and bacon/pancetta until the onions are soft. Add the mushrooms and squash and saute until the squash starts to soften and brown, roughly 10-15 minutes. Add the parsley, stock, and salt and pepper to taste. Spoon the squash mixture into the baking dish and cover with foil. Bake until just tender, 40-45 minutes.

### For the crumble
1. While the squash is baking, make the crumble. In a medium bowl, combine the flour, Italian seasoning, brown sugar, thyme, and salt. Add the butter pieces and pinch with your fingers until the mixture is the consistency of coarse meal (there may still be small lumps of butter). Chill the topping until ready to use.
2. Once the squash is done baking, uncover and scatter the crumble on top. Bake another 30-40 minutes more or until the topping is golden brown. Serve warm.
