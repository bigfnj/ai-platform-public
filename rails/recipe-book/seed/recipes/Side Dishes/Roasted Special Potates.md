# Roasted Special Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 4 baking potatoes (Russet, New)
- 4 tbsp melted butter
- Salt
- Slices of lemon (optional)
- Rosemary or thyme (optional)
- Smoked paprika (optional)
- Chopped parsley (optional)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 450°F Speed Convection.
2. Peel potatoes then cut in halves and then into 3/8” slices.
3. Blanch the potatoes in boiling salted water for 3 minutes, MAX!
4. Pat dry.
5. Arrange in single layer in buttered baking dish.
6. Dribble with melted butter.
7. Generously sprinkle with salt.
8. Bake for 15 minutes.
9. Turn and bake an additional 15 minutes.
10. With convection, rotate left/right and shelves up/down. Turning is not necessary.
11. Serve hot.
