# Oriental Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 Cup toasted almonds
- 16 oz. bag of cabbage mix (Cole slaw)
- 4 Roma tomatoes chopped
- 4 green onions chopped
- 2 pkgs. Top Ramen Oriental flavor soup, noodles broken up, reserve Pkgs. of seasoning for dressing

## Dressing
- ½ cup sesame oil
- ½ cup rice or red wine vinegar
- ¼  cup sugar
- Pepper to taste
- Seasoning packages from soup

## Instructions
1. Mix well and pour over salad, let salad sit until noodles have absorbed the dressing and are soft.
