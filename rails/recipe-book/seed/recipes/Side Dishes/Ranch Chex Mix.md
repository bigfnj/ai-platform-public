# Ranch Chex Mix

## Ingredients
- 8 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 4 tbsp ranch mix
- chex cereal
- cheese-its crackers
- pretzels
- Doritos chips
- crunchies snack mix

## Instructions
1. Pre-heat oven to 250°F.
2. Combine ingredients on stove and mix until butter is melted.
3. Toss with chex, cheese-its, pretzels, Doritos, and crunchies.
4. Spread onto 2 cookie sheets and bake for an hour.
5. Mix every 15 minutes.
6. Let cool.

## Shopping List
