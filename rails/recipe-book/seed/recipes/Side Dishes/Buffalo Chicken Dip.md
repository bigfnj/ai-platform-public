# Buffalo Chicken Dip

## Ingredients
- Rotisserie Chicken, shredded
- 2 cups Buffalo Sauce
- 2 cups Ranch Dressing
- 1 cup soft cream cheese
- Mexican Cheese

## Instructions
1. Mix all together and bake at 350 until hot.

## Shopping List
- rotisserie chicken
- buffalo sauce
- ranch dressing
- cream cheese
- mexican cheese
