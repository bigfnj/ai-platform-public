# Mango Salsa

## Ingredients
- 2 ripe mangoes, peeled and diced (yes isni[pe)
- 1 small red onion, finely chopped
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and minced
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- juice of 1 lime
- salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Combine mangoes, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, and lime juice in a bowl.
2. Mix well and season with salt.
