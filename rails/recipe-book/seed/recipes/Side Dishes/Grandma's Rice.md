# Grandma's Rice

## Ingredients
- 2 (4 ounce) cans mushroom stems and pieces, undrained
- 1 (10 ounce) can beef consommé
- ½ cup water
- 1 cup white rice
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 stick butter, cut into 4 pieces
- 2 tsp. garlic powder
- Parmesan cheese (optional)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Stir the mushrooms with its liquid, beef consommé, water, rice, onion and garlic powder, together in glass baking dish.
3. Arrange the butter on top of the rice.
4. Cover and bake for 1 hour.
5. (This is optional, sprinkle with cheese and continue to bake until cheese is melted).
