# Sweet Potatoes AU Rhum

*Serves 6.*

## Ingredients
- 2 pounds sweet potatoes, washed
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 3 eggs, slightly beaten
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup orange juice
- 3 oranges, halved, fruit removed from shells
- 1/3 cup dark Jamaican rum

## Instructions
1. Boil potatoes in skins till done.
2. Peel and mash the sweet potatoes.
3. Vigorously mash in melted butter and eggs.
4. Add cinnamon, sugar, nutmeg, vanilla, orange juice, and rum.
5. Bake in casserole in 350-degree F oven until half done, about 20 minutes.
6. Fill hollowed-out orange shells with mixture and finish baking.
7. Sprinkle top of each orange half with brown sugar and let brown lightly, till sugar’s caramelized.
