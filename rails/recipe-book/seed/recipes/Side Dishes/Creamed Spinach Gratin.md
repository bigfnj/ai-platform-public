# Creamed Spinach Gratin

*Serves 6*

## Ingredients
- 3 pounds spinach, stemmed
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 ½ tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 cup milk
- ¼ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp freshly ground pepper
- ¼ tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- ½ cup fresh bread crumbs
- ¼ cup freshly grated parmesan cheese (optional)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees.
2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil over high heat. Add the spinach and cook until it is limp but still bright green, 2 to 5 minutes, depending upon the tenderness of the leaves.
3. Drain in a colander and rinse with cold running water to stop the cooking and preserve the color.
4. Using your hands, squeeze out the excess liquid, then chop the spinach finely or coarsely, depending on your preference. Set aside.
5. In a medium saucepan over med-high heat, melt 1 ½ tbsp butter. When it foams, remove pan from heat and whisk in the flour to make roux, or paste.
6. Returning the pan to med-high heat and pour in the milk a little at a time, whisking to prevent lumps from forming.
7. When all the milk has been incorporated, whisk in the salt, pepper and nutmeg.
8. Reduce the heat to low and simmer until the taste of flour has dissipated and the sauce has thickened, about 15 min.
9. If the sauce seems too thin, increase heat to med-high and cook until it is medium-thick, 2 to 3 min longer. Remove from heat.
10. Heavily butter a medium gratin or baking dish, then put in the chopped spinach. Pour the béchamel sauce over it and, using a fork, lift the spinach a little so the sauce runs through it.
11. In a small saucepan, over med heat, melt the remaining 1 ½ tbsp butter. Scatter the bread crumbs over the spinach and pour the butter over the crumbs.
12. Scatter the cheese over the top if desired. Bake until the top is golden and the sauce is bubbling around the edges, about 10 minutes.
13. Serve immediately.

## Shopping List
- 3 pounds spinach
- unsalted butter
- all-purpose flour
- milk
- salt
- pepper
- nutmeg
- bread
- parmesan cheese (optional)
