# Easy Cheesy Stovetop Scalloped Potatoes

*Serves: 4 servings*

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 small onion, chopped (roughly ½ cup)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 ½ cups milk
- 4 cups potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions
1. In a large saucepan, melt the butter. Add the onion and garlic and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in the flour, salt, and garlic powder until a roux forms. Whisk in the milk.
2. Bring to a boil and add the potatoes. Cook for 10-20 minutes or until the potatoes are done, stirring often (cooking time depends on thickness and the bottom of the pan may burn if not stirred often). Add the cheese and stir until melted.
