# Crock-Pot Chicken Enchiladas

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts, cooked and shredded
- 16 ounce can red kidney beans, drained
- 15 ounce can black beans, drained
- 15 ounce can corn, drained
- 1 - 14 ounce can enchilada sauce
- 1 - 10 ounce can cream of chicken soup
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- ½ teaspoon cumin
- 1-1/2 teaspoons chili powder
- 2 cups shredded Mexican blend cheese
- 6 large flour tortillas

## Instructions
1. Mix together the enchilada sauce, cream of chicken soup, chili powder, cumin, and black pepper.
2. In a crock-pot, layer ½ of the chicken in the bottom of the crock-pot.
3. Next, layer 2 tortillas on top of the shredded chicken.
4. Add ½ of the black beans, kidney beans, and corn.
5. Add ½ of the enchilada sauce and seasoning mixture
6. Repeat these layers again (chicken, tortillas, beans, enchilada sauce).
7. Top with the remaining two tortillas.
8. Add two cups of shredded Mexican blend cheese on the very top.
9. Cook on high for 3-1/2 hours. Serve with guacamole and sour cream.
