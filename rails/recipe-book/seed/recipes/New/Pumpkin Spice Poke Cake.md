# Pumpkin Spice Poke Cake

## Ingredients
- 1 box spice cake mix
- 1 (15 oz) can pumpkin puree
- 4 large eggs
- 2 boxes instant vanilla pudding mix
- 3 cups milk
- whipped topping, for spreading on top of the cake
- pumpkin candies, optional

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350° F.
2. Spray a 9x13-in. baking pan with cooking spray and set aside.
3. Place the cake mix, pumpkin and eggs in a large mixing bowl. Mix on low speed with an electric mixer for 1 minute. Stop the mixer and scrape down the sides of the bowl with a spatula. Mix on medium speed for another minute or two or until the mixture looks well combined. Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Level out with the spatula.
4. Bake for 30 minutes or until toothpick inserted near the center tests clean. Remove from the oven and allow to cool while you prepare the pudding mixture.
5. Using the handle part of a wooden spoon, poke holes in the cooled cake. There is no set number of holes, but you want plenty of nooks and crannies for your pudding to fill. In a large mixing bowl, combine the pudding mixes and milk. Whisk until the pudding mix completely dissolves. Pour the pudding over the cake - filling the holes as best you can. Level out the pudding and gently push pudding into the holes to fill. Cover your cake pan with plastic wrap and refrigerate for about 2 hours or until the cake has cooled completely and the pudding is set up.
6. Remove the cake from the refrigerator and spread the whipped topping evenly over the top of the cake. If desired, top with pumpkin candies to decorate.
7. Store cake covered, in the refrigerator.
