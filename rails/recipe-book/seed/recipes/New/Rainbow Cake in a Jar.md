# Rainbow Cake in a Jar

*Serves 6*

## Ingredients
- 1 white cake, prepared but not baked
- food coloring (gel variety preferred)
- prepared frosting
- sprinkles (optional)

## Instructions
1. Split prepared cake batter into 5 separate parts, each in a small bowl or cup.
2. Put a small amount of food coloring into each part, stirring to achieve a bold color.
3. Carefully drop a spoonful of each color batter into each of 6 half pint canning jars.
4. Place jars in a 9x13 pan, with ½" water in the bottom. Bake at 350°F for 35-40 minutes.
5. Once cakes have cooled, frost the tops and decorate with sprinkles.
