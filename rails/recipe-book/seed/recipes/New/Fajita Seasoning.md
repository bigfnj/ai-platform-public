# Fajita Seasoning

## Ingredients
- 1 Tbsp chili powder
- 1½ tsp cumin
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp paprika
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp crushed red pepper (optional)

## Instructions
1. Whisk together all of the spices and store in an airtight container.
