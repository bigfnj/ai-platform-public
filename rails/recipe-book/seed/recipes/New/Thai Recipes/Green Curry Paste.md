# Green Curry Paste

*10 servings, Prep Time: 30 Minutes, Total Time: 30 Minutes*

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon sliced cilantro roots
- 1 tablespoon coriander seeds
- 1/2 tablespoon cumin seeds
- 1 1/2 tablespoons galangal
- 1/4 cup garlic cloves
- 1 kaffir lime (zest only)
- 3-4 tablespoons sliced lemongrass
- 1/2 teaspoon peppercorns
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1/2 cup sliced shallots
- 1 teaspoon shrimp paste
- 1/4 cup chili leaves, optional
- 10-15 green Thai chili peppers

## Instructions
1. Toast coriander seeds, peppercorns and cumin seeds in a pan until light brown.
2. Let the spices cool so they will grind easily.
3. Slice shallots, lemongrass, galangal and cilantro roots into small pieces.
4. Grind the spices into powder.
5. Add lemongrass and galangal into the mortar. Grind them into rough fibers.
6. Add salt, garlic, kaffir lime zest, cilantro roots and shrimp paste next.
7. Add fresh green chili peppers and leaves next.
8. Pound until the mixture turns into a fine paste so that you can’t recognize individual ingredients.
