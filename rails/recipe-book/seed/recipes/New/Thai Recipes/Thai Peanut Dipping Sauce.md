# Thai Peanut Dipping Sauce

*5 Servings, Prep Time: 5 Minutes, Total Time: 5 Minutes*

## Ingredients
- 5-7 sprigs sliced cilantro
- 1 teaspoon ground fresh chili paste (can use sirachi or siracha chili paste)
- 2 tablespoons toasted and crushed peanuts
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 tablespoons vinegar

## Instructions
1. In a microwavable bowl, combine sugar, ground fresh chili paste, salt and vinegar.
2. Heat up for a minute or until sugar is dissolved.
3. Stir the sauce with a spoon to blend all the ingredients in.
4. Add the crushed, toasted peanuts and top with the sliced cilantro.
