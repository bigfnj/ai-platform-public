# Sweet and Sour Sauce

*5 Servings, Prep Time: 2 Minutes, Total Time: 2 Minutes*

## Ingredients
- 1 teaspoon ground fresh chili paste
- 1 pinch salt
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 2 tablespoons water

## Instructions
1. Put all ingredients together in a small bowl and microwave it until the sugar is dissolved.
2. Stir to prevent the sugar from crystalizing on the bottom.

## Shopping List
- ground fresh chili paste, 1 teaspoon
- salt, 1 pinch
- sugar, 3 tablespoons
- vinegar, 1 tablespoon
