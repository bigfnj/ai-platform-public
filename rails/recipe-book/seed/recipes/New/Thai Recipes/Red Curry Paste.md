# Red Curry Paste

*10 servings, Prep Time: 30 Minutes, Total Time: 30 Minutes*

## Ingredients
- 6-20 dried whole chilies
- 1 - 2 tablespoons galangal
- 1/2 cup garlic
- 5 tablespoons lemongrass
- 1/2 tablespoon peppercorns
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup shallots
- 1 tablespoon shrimp paste
- 3-4 inches turmeric
- 1 teaspoon kaffir lime zest

## Instructions
1. Break dried whole chilies into pieces and soak in plenty of warm water.
2. Slice lemongrass into thin slices.
3. Peel garlic and shallots and slice them thinly.
4. Peel the zest of kaffir lime and slice into thin pieces.
5. Slice galangal into match stick pieces.
6. Chop turmeric into smaller pieces.
7. In a stone mortar, grind peppercorns into fine powder.
8. Pound lightly to crack and break the peppercorns open so that the round balls don't bounce out of the mortar.
9. When most the peppercorns are split, you can pound harder.
10. When the peppercorns are powdery, squeeze out the water from the chilies and add to mortar.
11. Add salt and pound until the chili peppers turn into a paste.
12. Add galangal and lemongrass. Pound until everything is mixed and ground into a paste.
13. Add kaffir lime zest and garlic and pound.
14. Add turmeric and pound it into a paste.
15. Add shrimp paste and pound to mix.
16. Add shallots and pound everything together.
17. It's done when you can no longer see chunks. The paste should be smooth with uniform bright red in color.
