# Chicken Basil - Pad GaPow

*2 Servings, Prep Time: 20 Minutes, Total Time: 20 Minutes*

## Ingredients
- 3 tablespoons fish sauce
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- ½ lb ground chicken
- 1-2 packages holy basil
- 4-7 minced Thai chili peppers
- 2 tablespoons cooking oil

## Instructions
1. Mince garlic and chili pepper together.
2. Clean and pick holy basil leaves from their stems.
3. Fry the garlic and chili pepper in oil over high heat.
4. When garlic starts to turn brown, drop the ground chicken in.
5. Stir constantly until all the juice is gone.
6. Add fish sauce.
7. Then add holy basil.
8. Quickly turn it over a few times to mix the leaves with the meat and then remove from the heat and put the gai pad ga pow in the serving plate or dishes.
