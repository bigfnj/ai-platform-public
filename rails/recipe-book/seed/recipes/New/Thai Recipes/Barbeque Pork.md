# Moo Dang (Barbecue Pork)

*10 servings, Prep Time: 5 minutes, Total Time: 1 hour*

## Ingredients
- 1 teaspoon Chinese five spice powder
- 1 lb pork
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons sugar

## Instructions
1. Select pork that is a big chunk and not a pork chop.
2. Cut the pork with the grain into long pieces, 2 inches x 2 inches cross-sectional and any length you like.
3. Marinade with the rest of the ingredients for at least half an hour or overnight.
4. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and bake for an hour on a tray, cast iron pan or piece of aluminum foil.
5. When done, the pork should be reddish, firm and dry but not burned.

## Shopping List
- Chinese five spice powder
- pork
- salt
- soy sauce
- sugar
