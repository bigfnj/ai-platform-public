# Spinach And Ricotta Gnocchi

*Serves: 4 servings*

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup frozen spinach, thawed and excess liquid squeezed out (roughly 8 ounces)
- 3/4 cup ricotta
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 teaspoon lemon zest
- 3/4 cup all purpose flour
- 1 egg
- Olive oil, for serving

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, mix together the spinach, ricotta, Parmesan cheese, lemon zest, flour, and egg to form a soft dough.
2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Meanwhile, on a lightly floured surface, divide the dough and roll into several thin ropes, roughly 8 inches long. Cut 1-inch pieces from each rope. Place on a tray or plate as you cut.
3. Add the pieces to the boiling water. Cook until they float to the top, roughly 5 minutes. Drain. Serve with a few drizzles of olive oil.

## Shopping List
