# Homemade Ketchup

*Serves: 1 cup*

## Ingredients
- 6 ounces tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- ½ cup water
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ⅛ teaspoon allspice

## Instructions
1. In a medium saucepan, whisk together all of the ingredients.
2. Bring to a boil.
3. Turn the heat down to simmer, cover, and cook for 30-60 minutes*.
4. Cool completely then chill before using.
