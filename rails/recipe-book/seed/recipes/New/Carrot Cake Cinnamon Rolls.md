# Carrot Cake Cinnamon Rolls

*Serves: 12 rolls*

## Ingredients
### Dough
- 3 1/2 cups + 1-2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 1/2 teaspoons yeast (one 1/4 ounce package)
- 1 cup milk (heated to 120°F)
- 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick or 2 ounces) butter, melted
- 2 eggs, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon butter, softened (to spread in pan)

### Filling
- 2 cups shredded carrots
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1/3 cup raisins
- 1/3 cup chopped walnuts
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 tablespoon butter, softened

### Frosting
- 4 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions
### For the dough
1. In a large mixing bowl (paddle attachment if using a stand mixer), combine the flour, sugar, salt, and yeast.
Add the milk and melted butter. Mix on low speed until the dough forms a soft mass. Beat in the eggs, one at a time. Continue beating on low until the dough starts to pull away from the sides of the bowl, 5-6 minutes.

2. If using a stand mixer, change the attachment to the dough hook. Sprinkle the dough with 1 tablespoon of flour. Beat on low speed until the dough is smooth (not sticky) and springs back when you press it with your finger. If needed, add the extra tablespoon of flour. If not using a stand mixer, place the dough on a lightly floured surface and knead by hand for 4-6 minutes.

3. Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover with a tea towel, and let rise in a warm place (about 85°F) for 45-60 minutes or until it has almost doubled.

### For the filling
1. In a medium saucepan, combine the carrots, brown sugar, cinnamon, raisins, walnuts, and water. Bring to a boil, stirring, then remove from the heat. Let cool 15 minutes.

### To assemble
1. Spread 1 tablespoon butter in a 13x9 pan.
2. On a floured surface or pastry mat, roll the dough into a 12x16 rectangle. Spread remaining 1 tablespoon butter onto dough, being careful not to rip it. Spread the carrot filling on top. Starting with a short end, roll up the dough and form a tight 12-inch cylinder. Cut into 12 slices. Place each slice, spiral side up, into the buttered pan. Cover a tea towel and let rise in a warm place until almost doubled, 45-60 minutes.
3. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Bake for 18-20 minutes or until the rolls have risen and are brown. Cool.

### For the frosting
1. In a large mixing bowl, beat together the cream cheese and powdered sugar until smooth. Beat in the milk and vanilla. Spread on top of rolls.
