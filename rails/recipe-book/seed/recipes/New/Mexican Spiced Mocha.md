# Mexican Spiced Mocha

*Serves: 1 cup*

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon Dutch-processed cocoa powder
- ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ⅛ - ¼ teaspoon chili powder (depends on spice level)
- 6 - 8 ounces (¾ -1 cup) hot coffee
- ¼ cup milk
- Whipped cream, for serving

## Instructions
1. In the bottom of a coffee mug, stir together the powdered sugar, cocoa powder, cinnamon, and chili powder.
2. Pour in hot coffee and stir until dissolved.
3. Stir in milk.
4. Top with whipped cream and sprinkle with cinnamon, if desired.
