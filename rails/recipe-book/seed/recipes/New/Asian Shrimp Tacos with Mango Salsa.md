# Asian Shrimp Tacos with Mango Salsa

*Serves: 4 servings*

## Ingredients
### Shrimp
- 12-16 ounces raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 1/2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons oil
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon curry powder
- 2 scallions, chopped

### Mango Salsa
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon fish sauce (or substitute soy sauce)
- 1 mango, peeled and cubed (roughly 1 cup)
- 1/2 cup red bell pepper, chopped
- 1-3 teaspoons fresh cilantro leaves

### Tortillas
- 4 flour tortillas

## Instructions
### For the shrimp
1. In a large bowl, combine shrimp, lime juice, soy sauce, oil, brown sugar, garlic powder, ginger, and curry powder. Let marinade for at least 30 minutes or overnight (the longer it sits, the more flavorful it'll be).

### For the mango salsa
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the lime juice, sugar, and fish sauce. Add the mango, bell pepper, and cilantro.

### To assemble
1. Remove the shrimp with a slotted spoon; discard leftover marinade.
2. Saute the shrimp and scallions in a skillet until the shrimp is cooked, roughly a few minutes (do not overcook). Remove from the heat. Top one tortilla with some of the shrimp then top with some of the salsa. Repeat with remaining food.
