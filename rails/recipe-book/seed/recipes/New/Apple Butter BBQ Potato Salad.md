# Apple Butter BBQ Potato Salad

*Serves: 4 servings*

## Ingredients
### BBQ Sauce
- 1/2 cup apple butter
- 1/4 cup ketchup (homemade or store-bought)
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 - 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder

### Potato Salad
- 1 pound red potatoes, cubed (roughly 8 small potatoes)
- 1/2 cup crumbled bacon (roughly 5-6 slices)
- 1/4 cup chopped green onion (green part only)
- 1/2 cup corn kernels
- salt

## Instructions
1. In a large pot of salted water, add the potatoes. Bring to a boil. Once boiling, cook for 10-15 minutes or until potatoes are soft (but not mushy). Drain and cool slightly.
2. Meanwhile, make the sauce: In a large saucepan, whisk together the apple butter, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, 1/2 teaspoon chili powder (add the extra 1/2 teaspoon if desired), paprika, onion powder, and garlic powder. Bring to a boil. Turn the heat down to simmering, cover with a lid, and cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Toss the still-warm potatoes with the sauce. Let sit for 20 minutes.
4. Stir the bacon, green onion, and corn into the potato salad. Chill in the refrigerator at least an hour or until cold before serving.
