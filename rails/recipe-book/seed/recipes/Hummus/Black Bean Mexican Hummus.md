# Black Bean Hummus

*Total time: 10 seconds*

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1 tbsp tahini
- 1 clove garlic, quartered
- 15 oz can black beans, drained, retaining 1 tbsp of liquid
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
- ¼ tsp smoked paprika

## Instructions
1. Add ingredients to Twister jar in order listed and place Twister lid on jar. Hold Twister lid, and with other hand select "Speed 1" and blend for 5-10 seconds, or until desired texture is reached. Turn Twister lid counter-clockwise during blending.
2. To make in FourSide or WildSide+ jar, double the ingredients and stop blending to scrape the sides of the jar back down to the blade.
