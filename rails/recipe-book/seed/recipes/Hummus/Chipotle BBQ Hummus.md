# Chipotle BBQ Hummus

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp barbecue sauce
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 can garbanzo beans (less than ¼ cup of liquid reserved)
- 2 chipotle chiles in adobo
- 2 tsp adobo sauce

## Instructions
1. Blend well in blender.
