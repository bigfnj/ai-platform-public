# White Bean Hummus

*Total time: 25 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp. olive oil
- 2 tbsp. lemon juice
- 2 tbsp. tahini
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 (15 oz.) can northern white beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 tsp. ground cumin
- 2 drops Tabasco sauce

## Instructions
1. Combine olive oil, lemon juice, tahini, garlic, salt, cumin, and Tabasco in a food processor or blender.
2. Add the northern white beans to the food processor or blender and blend until smooth.
