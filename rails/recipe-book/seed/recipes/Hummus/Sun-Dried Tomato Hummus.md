# Sun-Dried Tomato Hummus

*Total time: 23 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- ¼ tsp paprika
- 4 slices sun-dried tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 15 oz can chickpeas, with ¼ cup liquid reserved

## Instructions
1. Add ingredients in order listed to Twister jar, and place Twister lid on jar.
2. Select “Sauce,” and turn lid counterclockwise during blending.
