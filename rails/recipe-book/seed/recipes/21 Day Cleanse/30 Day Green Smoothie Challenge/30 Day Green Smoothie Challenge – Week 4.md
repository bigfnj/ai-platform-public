# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Week 4

## Watermelon Fresca
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup water
- 1.5 cups watermelon, seeded and cubed
- 1.5 cups strawberries, hulled and quartered
- 1-inch fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first; add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Blackberry Peach Cobbler
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 2 cups coconut milk, unsweetened
- 2 cups blackberries
- 1 peach, pitted and chopped (or substitute mango or nectarines)
- 2 tablespoons flaxseed, whole or ground
- ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first; add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Melon Berry
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 3 cups watermelon, seeded and cubed
- 1 cup frozen mixed berries
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first; add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Blackberries and Cream
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 2 cups coconut milk, unsweetened
- 2 cups blackberries
- 1 banana, peeled and chopped
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first; add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Berry Citrus-Ade
- 2 cups fresh kale
- 1 cup water
- 1 orange, peeled and segmented
- 2.5 cups frozen mixed berries
- ½ lemon, peeled and chopped
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first; add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Shopping List
- 1 large watermelon, seeded
- 16 ounces strawberries
- 30 ounces blackberries
- 2 peaches (or substitute mango or nectarines)
- 30 ounces frozen mixed berries
- 2 bananas
- 1 orange
- 1 lemon
- Coconut milk, unsweetened (32 ounces)
- 2 bags fresh spinach (10-16 ounces each)
- 1 small bag fresh kale (6-10 ounces)
- Fresh ginger, 1-2 inches
- Small bag flaxseed, whole or ground (2 ounces)
- Ground cinnamon
