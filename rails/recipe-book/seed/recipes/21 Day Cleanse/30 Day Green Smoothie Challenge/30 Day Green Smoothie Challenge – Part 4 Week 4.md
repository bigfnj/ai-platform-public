# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Part 4 Week 4

## Strawberry Mint Green Smoothie Pops
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 cup almond milk, unsweetened
- 1 cup strawberries
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Papaya Sunrise
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups coconut water, unsweetened
- 1 orange, peeled
- 1 cup papaya, seeded
- 1 cup strawberries
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Melon-a Sunrise
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 cup almond milk, unsweetened
- 2 cups honeydew melon
- 1 banana
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Kiwi Strawberry Punch
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups coconut water, unsweetened
- 2 cups strawberries
- 2 kiwifruit
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Honey-Kissed Nectarines
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 cup coconut water, unsweetened
- 3 nectarines
- 1 1/2 cups honeydew melon
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Mango Green-a Colada
- 2 cups kale
- 2 cups coconut milk, unsweetened
- 2 cups mangoes (frozen)
- 1 cup pineapple
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

- orange

- vanilla extract
