# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Part 2 Week 1

## Beginner's Luck
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup mango, peeled and diced
- 1 cup pineapple, peeled and diced
- 2 bananas, peeled and sliced
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Quick Pick Me Up
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 grapefruit, peeled
- 3 cups pineapple, peeled and diced
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Strawberry Daiquiri
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 grapefruit, peeled
- 1 1/2 cups strawberries, hulled and quartered
- 4 medjool dates, pitted
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Shopping List
- 1 whole pineapple (or 24 oz frozen)
- 1 mango
- 2 bananas
- 2 grapefruits
- 12 oz strawberries
- 4 medjool dates, pitted
- 1 bag fresh spinach (10-16 oz)
