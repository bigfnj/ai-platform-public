# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Part 2 Week 5

## Caring Carrot
- 2 cups kale, stems removed
- 1 cup water
- 2 oranges, peeled
- 1 large carrot, chopped and steamed if desired
- 1 cup mango
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Pom Power
- 2 cups kale, stems removed
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup blueberries
- 1 cup strawberries
- 1 cup pomegranate seeds (or substitute with any type of berries)

## Banana Berry Blast
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups almond milk, unsweetened
- 1 cup mixed berries
- 2 bananas

## Peaceful Pomegranate
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups water
- 1½ cups pomegranate seeds (or substitute with any type of berries)
- 1½ cups mango

## Berries 'n Cream
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups water
- 1 avocado
- 2 cups mixed berries

## Shopping List
- 8-10 oz blueberries
- 8-10 oz strawberries
- 4 pomegranates (or 24-32 oz of seeds)
- 3 mangoes (or 24-30 oz frozen)
- 24-32 oz mixed berries
- 2 avocados
- 3 bananas
- 3 oranges
- 2 large carrots
- 1 bag fresh spinach (10-16 oz)
- 1 bag fresh kale (10-16 oz)
- 1 carton almond milk, unsweetened (32 oz)
