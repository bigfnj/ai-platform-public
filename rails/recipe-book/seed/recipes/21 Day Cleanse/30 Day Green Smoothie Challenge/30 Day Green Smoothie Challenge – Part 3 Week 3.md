# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Part 3 Week 3

## Cilantro Mango Detox
- 1 1/2 cups spinach
- 1/2 cup cilantro
- 2 cups water
- 1 1/2 cups mango, fresh or frozen
- 1 cup pineapple, fresh or frozen
- 1/2 avocado
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Blueberry Kale Punch
- 2 cups kale
- 1 cup almond milk unsweetened
- 2 oranges, peeled
- 1 cup blueberries
- 2 bananas
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Pineapple Berry
- 1 1/2 cups spinach
- 1 cup water
- 1 orange, peeled
- 1/2 cucumber
- 1 cup pineapple, fresh or frozen
- 1 cup strawberries
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Strawberry Blast
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups almond milk unsweetened
- 2 cups strawberries
- 1 banana
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Morning Fuel
- 2 cups kale
- 1 cup almond milk unsweetened
- 2 oranges, peeled
- 1 cup pineapple, fresh or frozen
- 1 banana
- 1/4 avocado
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Shopping List
- 1 avocado
- 5 bananas
- 8 oz blueberries
- 4 oz cilantro
- 1 cucumber
- 3 mangoes (or 16oz frozen)
- 7 oranges
- 1 pineapple (or 24-32 oz frozen)
- 24 oz strawberries
- 1 bag fresh spinach (10-16 oz each)
- 1 bag fresh kale (10-16 oz each)
- 1 carton almond milk unsweetened (32 oz)
