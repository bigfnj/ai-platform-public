# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Part 4 Week 1

## Cocoa-Cherry Berry Cooler
- 2 cups kale
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 2 cups cherries, pitted
- 1 cup blueberries
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Citrus Breeze
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 2 oranges, peeled
- 1/2 cup mango
- 1/2 cup pineapple
- 1 banana
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Pineapple Upside Down Cake
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 1 cup pineapple
- 1 cup cherries, pitted
- 1 banana
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Shopping List
- 32 oz unsweetened coconut milk (or any variety of nut milk)
- 8 oz blueberries
- 24 oz cherries, pitted
- 1 mango, fresh (or 6 oz frozen)
- 2 oranges
- 1 pineapple, fresh (or 12 oz frozen)
- 1 small bag fresh kale (8-10 oz)
- 1 small bag fresh spinach (8-10 oz)
- 3 bananas
