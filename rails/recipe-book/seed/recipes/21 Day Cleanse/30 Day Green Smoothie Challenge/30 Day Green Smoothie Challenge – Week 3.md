# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Week 3

## Grape Lime Rickey
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup water
- 3 cups grapes, red or purple
- 1 lime, peeled
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Peaches N' Cream
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 2 cups almond milk, unsweetened
- 4 peaches, pitted*
- 1 banana
- ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Strawberry Patch
- 2 cups fresh chard, red or swiss
- 2 cups almond milk, unsweetened
- 20 ounces strawberries
- 1 banana
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Carrot Mango Peach
- 2 cups fresh chard, red or swiss
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup mango
- 1 large carrot, chopped
- 1 peach, pitted*
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Razz Peach Melba
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 2 cups almond milk, unsweetened
- 4 peaches, pitted*
- 10 ounces raspberries
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Shopping List
- 1 bag fresh spinach (10-16 ounces)
- 1 bag fresh chard, red or swiss (10-16 ounces)
- 3 cups grapes, red or purple
- 9 peaches*
- 1 lime
- 20 ounces strawberries
- 4 bananas
- 2 mangoes, fresh or frozen
- 1 large carrot
- 10 ounces raspberries
- Almond milk, unsweetened (64 ounces)
- Vanilla extract
- Ground cinnamon
- Water
