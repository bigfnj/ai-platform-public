# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Part 4 Week 3

## Strawberry Grape Lime Rickey
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 cup water
- 22 oz grapes, red or purple
- 1 lime, peeled
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Carrot Mango Peach
- 2 cups chard
- 2 cups water
- 1 mango (8 oz)
- 1 large carrot, chopped
- 1 peach, pitted*
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Razz Peach Melba
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups almond milk
- 2 peaches, pitted*
- 10 oz raspberries
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Peaches N' Cream
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups almond milk
- 3 bananas
- 2 peaches, pitted*
- ¼ tsp cinnamon

*Can substitute peaches with mango or nectarines.
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Shopping List
- 1 bag fresh chard (10-16 oz)
- 4 bags fresh spinach (20-32 oz)
- 8 cups unsweetened almond milk
- 3 bananas
- 1 large carrot
- ground cinnamon
- 22 oz grapes, red or purple
- 1 lime
- 1 mango (8 oz)
- 6 peaches
- 10 oz raspberries
- vanilla extract
- 5 cups water
