# Rawesome Recipes for July 4 - 10

## Rockin' Raspberry Smoothie

### Ingredients
- 2 cups kale
- 2 cups coconut water
- 2 cups raspberries
- 1 cup pineapple
- 1 green apple
- 1 banana

## Sweet Freedom Smoothie

### Ingredients
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 1 cup strawberries
- 1 cup blueberries
- 1 banana

## Summer Orchard Smoothie

### Ingredients
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups water
- 1 peach, pitted*
- 1 large plum, pitted
- 2 apricots, pitted

*Can substitute peaches with mango or nectarines.

## Straw-Bana Peach Smoothie

### Ingredients
- 2 cups kale
- 1 cup water
- 1 orange, peeled
- 1 peach, pitted*
- 1 cup strawberries
- 1 banana

*Can substitute peaches with mango or nectarines.

## Pineapple Apricot Ginger Bliss Smoothie

### Ingredients
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup pineapple
- 6 apricots, pitted
- 1-inch fresh ginger

## Instructions
1. Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first.
   For recipes that use whole oranges, melons, cucumbers or grapes, we recommend blending those ingredients in this first step.
2. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Shopping List
- 32 oz coconut milk, unsweetened (or any variety of nut milk)
- 32 oz coconut water, unsweetened
- 1-2 inches fresh ginger
- 10 apricots
- 3 bananas
- 8 oz blueberries
- 1 green apple
- 2 oranges
- 3 peaches (or mangoes or nectarines)
- 10 oz pineapple
- 1 large plum
- 20 oz raspberries
- 20 oz strawberries
- 1 bag fresh kale (10-16 oz)
- 1 bag fresh spinach (10-16 oz)
