# 30 Day Green Smoothie Challenge – Part 3 Week 5

## Berry Chazberry
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups water
- 2 cups cherries (or berries)
- 1 cup raspberries
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Berry Protein Bash
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups almond milk
- 1 cup strawberries
- 1 cup blueberries
- 1 banana
- 1/2 cup almonds (soaked in water overnight)
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Almond Butter Cup
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups almond milk
- 2 bananas
- 3 tablespoons cacao powder
- 2 tablespoons almond butter
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Sweet Dream
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups almond milk
- 2 cups mango
- 1 banana
- 2 tablespoons almond butter
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Cherry Tart
- 2 cups spinach
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup pineapple
- 1 cup cherries (or berries)
- 1 banana
- *Method:* Blend leafy greens and liquid base together first. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.

## Shopping List
- 6 bananas
- blueberries (8 oz)
- cherries (28 oz)
- 3 mangoes (or 16 oz frozen)
- 1 pineapple (or 8 oz frozen)
- raspberries (10 oz)
- strawberries (10 oz)
- 2 bags fresh spinach (10-16 oz)
- 1-2 cartons almond milk unsweetened (48 oz)
- almond butter
- almonds (4 oz)
- cacao powder (or unsweetened cocoa)
- cayenne pepper
- cinnamon, ground
