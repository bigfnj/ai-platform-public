# Red Lentil Soup

## Ingredients
- 2 teaspoons coconut oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled and chopped
- ¾ teaspoon ginger, peeled and finely chopped
- 1¼ teaspoons ground cumin
- ½ teaspoon turmeric
- ¾ cup tomatoes, diced
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- ¾ cup dry red lentils, rinsed well and drained
- 1 cup fresh spinach, roughly chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice

## Instructions
1. Heat the coconut oil in a large 4-5 quart saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and celery. Cook 5-10 minutes, or until the onion is translucent. Add the garlic and ginger, and cook for another minute, stirring constantly.
2. Add the cumin and turmeric. Stir for 30 seconds, or until fragrant. Add the tomatoes and stir again.
3. Add the vegetable broth and lentils. Lower heat to a simmer and partially cover the pan. Cook for 25-30 minutes, or until the lentils are soft.
4. Stir in the spinach and lemon juice. Add salt, pepper and red chili flakes.
