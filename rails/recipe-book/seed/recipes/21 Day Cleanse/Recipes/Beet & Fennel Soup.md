# Beet & Fennel Soup

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 2 tablespoons garlic, peeled and chopped
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 teaspoons ginger, peeled, chopped and divided
- ½ cup fennel bulb, chopped
- ¾ cup beet, peeled and grated
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
- ¼ avocado (optional)
- Black pepper
- Sea salt

## Instructions
1. Heat coconut oil in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and 1 teaspoon of ginger. Cook until onion is translucent.
2. Add the fennel to the pan and continue cooking for a few more minutes.
3. Add the beet, vegetable broth, remaining ginger, and garlic. Simmer on low heat for 20-30 minutes.
4. To serve, ladle soup into bowls and top with avocado slices and fresh lemon juice.
5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
