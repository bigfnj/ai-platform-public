# Avocado & Sunflower Seed Snack

## Ingredients
- ¼ avocado, diced
- 2 tablespoons sunflower seeds
- 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
- Black pepper
- Sea salt

## Instructions
1. Place all ingredients in a bowl and toss to combine.
2. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
