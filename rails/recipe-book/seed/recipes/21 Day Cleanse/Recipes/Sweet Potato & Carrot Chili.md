# Sweet Potato & Carrot Chili

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, peeled and chopped
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- pinch chili powder
- ½ cup sweet potato, raw and cubed
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 1 can (15 oz.) red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (15 oz.) tomatoes
- 1 zucchini, chopped
- ½ cup vegetable broth
- 1 cup cooked quinoa

## Instructions
1. Heat coconut oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and garlic and sauté until onion is translucent.
2. Add the cumin, coriander and chili powder. Cook for one minute until fragrant.
3. Add the sweet potato and cook for two more minutes, stirring often.
4. Add the carrots, kidney beans, tomatoes, and vegetable broth. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes. Add the zucchini and continue cooking for 5-10 minutes, until all the vegetables are tender.
5. Serve over quinoa.

## Shopping List

- onion
- garlic
- ground cumin
