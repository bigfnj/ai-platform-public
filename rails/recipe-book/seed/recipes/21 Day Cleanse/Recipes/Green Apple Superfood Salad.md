# Green Apple Superfood Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 green apple, unpeeled, cored and diced
- 1 celery stalk, chopped
- ¼ cup walnuts, chopped
- ¼ cup fresh mint, chopped
- 2 tablespoons fresh orange juice

## Instructions
1. Combine apple, celery, walnuts, and mint in a bowl.
2. Add the orange juice and toss to combine.

## Shopping List
- 1 green apple
- 1 celery stalk
- ¼ cup walnuts
- ¼ cup fresh mint leaves
- 2 tablespoons fresh orange juice
