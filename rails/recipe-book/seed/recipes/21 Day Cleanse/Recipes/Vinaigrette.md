# Vinaigrette

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- ½ tablespoon raw honey
- pinch of sea salt
- 1 tablespoon fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions
1. Whisk together the lime juice and honey until smooth.
2. Slowly whisk in the olive oil.
3. Stir in the cilantro and season with a pinch of salt.
