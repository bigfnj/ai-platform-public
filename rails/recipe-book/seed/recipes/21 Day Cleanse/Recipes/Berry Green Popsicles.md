# Berry Green Popsicles

## Ingredients
- 1 cup coconut milk, unsweetened
- 1 banana
- 1 cup frozen mixed berries
- ½ cup fresh spinach

## Instructions
1. Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
2. Transfer mixture into 6 popsicle molds, paper cups, or small tumblers.
3. Place in the freezer.
4. When half frozen, stick in a popsicle stick and leave in the freezer until completely frozen.
