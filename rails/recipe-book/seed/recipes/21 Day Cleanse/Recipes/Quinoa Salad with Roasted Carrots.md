# Quinoa Salad with Roasted Carrots

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil, melted
- 2 cups baby carrots, halved
- 2 tablespoons sliced almonds
- 2 tablespoons sunflower seeds
- 2 tablespoons pepitas
- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- ½ cup fresh mint, chopped
- ½ cup fresh cilantro (coriander), chopped
- ½ cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tablespoon onion, minced
- 1 ½ tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon tahini*
- ½ garlic clove, peeled and chopped
- 1 ½ teaspoons fresh lemon juice

## Instructions
1. Prepare dressing by whisking all of the ingredients in a small bowl. Season to taste and set aside.
2. Preheat oven to 350° F (180° C). Toss the carrots with the coconut oil and place on the baking sheet. Roast for 25-30 minutes, or until just tender and caramelized.
3. On a separate baking sheet, toast the almonds and seeds in the oven for 5 minutes, or until lightly toasted. Remove from oven and set aside.
4. Combine quinoa, herbs, onion, and dressing. Top with carrots, nuts and seeds.
