# Cauliflower Couscous

## Ingredients
- ½ head of cauliflower, roughly chopped
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, peeled and chopped
- ½ teaspoon curry powder
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric
- ⅛ teaspoon cinnamon
- ½ cup carrots, finely chopped
- 1 cup fresh kale, stems removed and chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- ½ teaspoon sea salt
- ½ teaspoon red chili flakes

## Instructions
1. Place the chopped cauliflower into the bowl of a food processor fitted with the chopping blade. Pulse until the cauliflower is finely chopped and has a rice-like consistency. Set aside.
2. In a large skillet or frying pan, heat the coconut oil over medium heat. Add the onion and sauté for a few minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 2 minutes.
3. Add the curry powder, turmeric, and cinnamon to the pan. Stir and cook for a minute, until fragrant. Add the carrots and chopped cauliflower to the pan and stir to combine. Cook the cauliflower for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Once tender, add the chopped kale and stir well.
4. Remove from heat and add the lemon juice, salt, and red chili flakes. Stir to combine. Add extra seasonings to taste.
