# Chimichurri Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1 cup fresh cilantro (coriander), tightly packed, washed well and patted dry, leaves and tender stems only
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled and chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon tamari
- black pepper
- red chili flakes

## Instructions
1. Finely chop cilantro and place in a medium bowl.
2. Add the olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, dried oregano, and tamari. Stir well.
3. Add fresh ground black pepper and red chili flakes to taste.
4. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week.
