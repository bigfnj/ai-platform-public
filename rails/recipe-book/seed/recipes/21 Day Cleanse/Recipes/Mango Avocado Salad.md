# Mango Avocado Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 small ripe mango*
- ½ avocado
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro (coriander), chopped
- 2 tablespoons sunflower seeds
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 cups fresh spinach

## Instructions
1. Cut mango and avocado into ½ inch cubes and place in a bowl.
2. Add the chopped cilantro, sunflower seeds, lime juice and olive oil.
3. Stir to coat and add spinach.
4. Toss well and serve.
