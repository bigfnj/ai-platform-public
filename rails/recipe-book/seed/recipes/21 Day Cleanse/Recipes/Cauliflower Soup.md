# Cauliflower Soup

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 1 leek, sliced (white part)
- 1 medium potato, unpeeled and cubed
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled and chopped
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- ¾ cup cauliflower, chopped
- Pinch of sea salt

## Instructions
1. Heat the coconut oil in a saucepan. Add the leek and a pinch of salt. Cook until leek starts to soften. Add the potato and cover with lid. Cook for 5 minutes, or until the potato starts to soften.
2. Add the garlic and broth and bring to a simmer.
3. Add the cauliflower and cook for a few minutes, until cauliflower is tender.
4. Remove from heat and puree using an immersion or standard blender. Taste and add more sea salt, if necessary. If soup is too thick, add a little more vegetable broth.
5. Ladle into bowl. Top with Kale Croutons (recipe on next page), nuts or seeds.
