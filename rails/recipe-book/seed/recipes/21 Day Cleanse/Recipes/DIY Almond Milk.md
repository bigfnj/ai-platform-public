# DIY Almond Milk

## Ingredients
- 1 cup almonds
- ½ teaspoon fresh lemon juice
- 3 cups filtered water
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract (or ½ vanilla bean, scraped)
- A pinch of sea salt
- 1 nut milk bag (or cheesecloth)

## Instructions
1. Place the almonds in a bowl, generously cover with water and lemon juice. Let soak at least 8 hours or overnight. The almonds will absorb a lot of water, so be sure to cover them with enough.
2. Pour off the soaking liquid and rinse well until water runs fairly clear. (This helps prevent bitterness.)
3. Transfer the soaked almonds to a blender and add 3 cups filtered water – the less water the creamier the milk. Add the vanilla and salt. Blend on high for at least 30 seconds or longer until completely pureed.
4. Place a nut milk bag, or several layers of cheesecloth over a large bowl. Pour the mixture over the top to strain out the solids.
5. Gently squeeze the solids to extract as much of the almond milk as possible from the pulp. Transfer the milk to a glass jar or bottle and refrigerate. Milk will keep for up to 4 days.
