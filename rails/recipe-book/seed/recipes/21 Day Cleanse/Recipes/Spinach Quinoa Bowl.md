# Spinach Quinoa Bowl

## Ingredients
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1/4 cup cooked quinoa
- 1/4 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
- 5 cherry tomatoes, cut in half
- 2 tablespoons cilantro (coriander), chopped
- 1/2 avocado, diced
- 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice

## Instructions
1. Place spinach on plate.
2. Warm quinoa and beans and place on top of spinach.
3. Mix tomatoes, cilantro, avocado together and place on top of quinoa and black beans.
4. Squeeze lime juice over the top of everything and serve.

## Shopping List
- 1/4 cup black beans (canned)
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1/4 cup uncooked quinoa
- 5 cherry tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons cilantro (fresh)
- 1 avocado
- 1 lime
