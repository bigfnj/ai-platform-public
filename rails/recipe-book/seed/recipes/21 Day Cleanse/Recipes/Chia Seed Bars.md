# Chia Seed Bars

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds
- 6 tablespoons water
- 1 cup almond meal
- ½ cup coconut flakes
- 1 cup gluten-free rolled oats
- ½ cup dried fruit*
- 1 cup mashed banana (super ripe)**
- ⅓ cup pepitas
- ⅓ cup sunflower seeds
- ⅓ cup sliced almonds
- ½ teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- Pinch of ground cinnamon
- Pinch of sea salt

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Grease or line a baking pan with parchment (baking) paper. Use 9” x 13” pan for crunchy bars, or 8” x 8” pan for more muffin-like consistency.
2. Soak chia seeds in 6 tablespoons of water for 5 minutes.
3. Combine the chia seeds and remaining ingredients in a bowl.
4. Press the mixture into the prepared baking sheet.
5. Bake for 25-35 minutes, or until golden on top. Remove from oven and let cool.
6. Slice into 8 bars. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.
