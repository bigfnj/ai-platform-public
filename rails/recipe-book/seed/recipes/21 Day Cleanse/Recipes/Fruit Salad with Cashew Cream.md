# Fruit Salad with Cashew Cream

## Ingredients
- ½ green apple, cored and chopped
- ½ orange, peeled
- ½ cup strawberries, chopped
- 2 tablespoons coconut flakes, unsweetened
- cashews (quantity not specified)

## Instructions
1. Place cashews in a bowl and cover with water. Allow to soak for at least 2 hours. Drain and rinse well.
