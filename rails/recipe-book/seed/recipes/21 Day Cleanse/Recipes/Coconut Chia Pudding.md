# Coconut Chia Pudding

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds
- ½ cup coconut milk, unsweetened
- 1 tablespoon coconut flakes, unsweetened
- ⅛ teaspoon cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon pure vanilla extract (or ½ vanilla bean, scraped)
- 1 teaspoon maple syrup
- ¼ cup blueberries*
- 2 tablespoons pepitas
- 2 tablespoons sliced almonds

## Instructions
1. Stir together chia seeds, coconut milk, coconut flakes, cinnamon, vanilla, and maple syrup in a small bowl or jar.
2. Cover and set aside for at least 10 minutes, or overnight.
3. Before eating, top the pudding with a layer of the berries and sprinkle the nuts on top.
