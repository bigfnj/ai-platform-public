# Orange Cinnamon Cooler

## Ingredients
- 2 cups filtered water
- 1 orange, sliced
- 2 cinnamon sticks

## Instructions
1. Add ingredients to a pitcher, mason jar, or water bottle.
2. Fill water to top of glass.
3. Drink immediately or cover and refrigerate for up to 3 days.
