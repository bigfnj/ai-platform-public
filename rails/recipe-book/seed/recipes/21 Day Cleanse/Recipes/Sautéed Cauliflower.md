# Sautéed Cauliflower

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 1 whole cauliflower, cut into florets or bite-size pieces
- 10 cherry tomatoes

## Instructions
1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the coconut oil and let it melt. Add the cauliflower florets. Cook, stirring often, letting cauliflower brown evenly on each side.
2. When cauliflower is barely tender, add the tomatoes and let cook for 5 more minutes until tomatoes start to brown, but have not popped open.
3. Remove from heat and serve immediately with some of the Cilantro Chimichurri drizzled over the top.

## Shopping List
- coconut oil - 1 tablespoon
- cauliflower - 1 whole
- cherry tomatoes - 10
