# Green Smoothie

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup coconut water, unsweetened
- 1 ripe pear, cored
- ½ cup mango (frozen optional)
- ¼ avocado
- 2 tablespoons hemp hearts

## Instructions
1. Blend spinach and coconut water together until smooth.
2. Add remaining ingredients and blend again.
