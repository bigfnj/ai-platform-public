# Zucchini Noodles with Tomato Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1 small zucchini, raw
- olive oil
- pepitas
- sunflower seeds
- basil pesto (optional)
- fresh basil leaves (optional)

## Instructions
1. Using a spiralizer or vegetable peeler, cut zucchini into spaghetti. Sprinkle with a little olive oil, and set aside.
2. Place raw zucchini noodles on plate and top with the tomato sauce.
3. Sprinkle pepitas and sunflower seeds on top.
