# Fruit Cream

## Ingredients
- ½ cup cashews
- ½ cup strawberries
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- ½ cup unsweetened coconut milk
- 1 teaspoon maple syrup

## Instructions
1. Place soaked cashews, ½ cup strawberries, chia seeds and coconut milk in a blender and blend until smooth. Taste and add maple syrup if needed, to sweeten.
