# Hummus

## Ingredients
- 1 cup cooked chickpeas (garbanzo beans), drained and rinsed well
- ½ cup beet, raw and grated
- 1 garlic clove, peeled
- 2 tablespoons tahini
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro (coriander), chopped
- Black pepper and sea salt, to taste

## Instructions
1. Place chickpeas, beet, garlic, tahini, and lemon juice in the bowl of a food processor or blender jar. Pulse or blend.
2. Drizzle the oil through the feed hole with the motor running. If needed, add a little water to loosen up the mixture.
3. Transfer to a serving bowl and sprinkle with cilantro, salt, and pepper. Serve with fresh veggies.
