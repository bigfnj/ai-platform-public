# Carrot Cilantro Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 carrot (yields about ½ cup)
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro (coriander), chopped
- 2 tablespoons sunflower seeds
- 2 tablespoons fresh orange juice
- zest of one orange
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- black pepper
- sea salt

## Instructions
1. Prepare dressing first. Whisk together orange juice and zest, lemon juice, and olive oil. Set aside.
2. Finely chop, julienne, or use a vegetable peeler to make ribbons of the carrot.
3. Combine the carrot strips, cilantro, and sunflower seeds on a plate.
4. Drizzle dressing over the salad. Season with salt & pepper to taste.
