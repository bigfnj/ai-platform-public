# Cashew Cream

## Ingredients
- ½ cup cashews
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice + 1 cup water to soak
- 2 teaspoons fresh lime juice
- ¼ cup water
- A pinch of sea salt

## Instructions
1. Place cashews and lemon juice in a bowl and cover with water and let soak for 1 - 4 hours. The longer they soak, the creamier they’ll be. Drain and rinse well.
2. Place the soaked cashews, lime juice, ¼ cup water, and pinch of salt in a food processor. Puree until smooth. Stop and scrape down sides as needed. Taste and add more lime juice or salt, if desired.

## Shopping List
- ½ cup cashews
- 1 lemon
- 1 lime
- Sea salt
