# Basil Pesto

## Ingredients
- 1 cup basil, washed well and patted dry
- ⅔ cup walnuts
- 2 garlic cloves, peeled
- 2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice
- ¼ cup olive oil
- ¼ cup olives, pitted
- Black pepper
- Sea salt

## Instructions
1. Place the basil, walnuts, garlic, lemon juice, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, and olives in a mortar.
2. Mash with a pestle until desired consistency is achieved, or process in a food processor.
3. Drizzle remaining olive oil on top. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
4. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week.
