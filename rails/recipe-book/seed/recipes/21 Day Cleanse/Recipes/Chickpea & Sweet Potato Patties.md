# Chickpea & Sweet Potato Patties

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- 3 tablespoons water
- ¾ cup sweet potato, cooked and cubed
- ¾ cup chickpeas, drained and rinsed well
- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- 1 tablespoon onion, minced
- ¼ cup finely sliced kale, stems removed
- ¼ cup sunflower seeds
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro (coriander), chopped
- 2 tablespoons parsley, chopped
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 1 tablespoon tamari
- 1 avocado, diced

## Instructions
1. Place 1 tablespoon of chia seeds in a small bowl or cup with 3 tablespoons of water and leave to sit for 10 minutes until the liquid is absorbed.
2. Place the sweet potato and chickpeas in a large bowl and mash well with a fork or potato masher.
3. Add all other ingredients (except for coconut oil if frying in a pan) and using your hands, mix until well combined. Allow the mixture to sit for a few minutes. Take ¼ cup scoops of the mixture and shape into 6 little patties. Transfer to the fridge and leave to sit for half an hour (or overnight).
4. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment (baking) paper. Place patties on the lined baking sheet and bake for 45 minutes, flipping halfway through baking time for even browning. Alternatively, broil on high for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway through so they are evenly browned. Patties can also be fried in a little coconut oil for 5 minutes on each side, or until a lovely golden color. When frying, you may need to work in batches depending on the size of pan used.
5. To serve, top with the diced avocado.
