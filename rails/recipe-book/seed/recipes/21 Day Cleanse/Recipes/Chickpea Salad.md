# Chickpea Salad

## Ingredients
- ½ cup fresh spinach
- ½ cup (4 oz) cooked chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- ¼ cup sunflower seeds
- ¼ cup pepitas
- ¼ cup chopped raw carrots, diced
- ¼ cup cucumbers, diced
- 2 tablespoons red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tablespoon tahini
- 1 tablespoon fresh orange juice
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
- ½ teaspoon tamari
- ¼ teaspoon minced garlic

## Instructions
1. Place spinach on a plate.
2. Top with chickpeas, seeds, and veggies.
3. Whisk together dressing ingredients and drizzle over salad.
