# Roasted Butternut Squash with Zucchini Pasta

## Ingredients
- 2 cups butternut squash, peeled and cubed (or pumpkin)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 2 zucchini ("pasta")
- black pepper
- sea salt
- 2 tablespoons basil pesto

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350° F (180° C). Line baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Toss the butternut squash cubes with 2 tablespoons olive oil and sea salt. Spread in an even layer over the lined baking sheet. Bake for 30 minutes, or until cooked through. During the last 10 minutes of baking time, add the cherry tomatoes to the baking sheet.
3. To prepare pasta, use a vegetable peeler or spiralizer to slice the zucchini into spaghetti noodles. Sprinkle with a little olive oil and set aside.
4. Place half of the noodles on a plate with half of the vegetables and top with 2 tablespoons of basil pesto. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Shopping List
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 butternut squash (or pumpkin)
- sea salt
- black pepper
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 2 zucchini
- 2 tablespoons basil pesto
