# Strawberry Mint Salad

## Ingredients
- 2 cups spring mix salad greens or baby spinach
- 1 cup strawberries, sliced and stems removed
- 1 nectarine, sliced*
- 6 mint leaves
- 2 tablespoons sunflower seeds
- 2 tablespoons walnuts

## Instructions
1. Place the salad greens on a plate. Top with sliced fruit and mint leaves.
2. Whisk all dressing ingredients together in a small bowl and drizzle over the salad.
3. Sprinkle the nuts and seeds over the top.
