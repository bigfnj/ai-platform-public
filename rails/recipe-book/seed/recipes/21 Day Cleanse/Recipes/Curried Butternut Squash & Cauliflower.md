# Curried Butternut Squash & Cauliflower

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil, melted
- 2 cups butternut squash or pumpkin, peeled and cut into 1” chunks
- 2 tablespoons red curry paste*
- 1 ½ cups coconut milk, unsweetened
- 2 cups cauliflower, chopped
- ¼ cup coconut flakes, unsweetened
- ¼ cup pepitas

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Toss cubed squash with 1 tablespoon of coconut oil and place in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Bake for 45 minutes, or until tender.
2. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of coconut oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the curry paste and cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
3. Add the coconut milk and cauliflower and cook for 10 minutes (or until cauliflower is tender). Add baked squash and stir.
4. Place the coconut flakes and pepitas on a baking sheet and bake for 5 minutes, or until toasted.
