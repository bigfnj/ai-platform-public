# Quinoa Salad

## Ingredients
- ½ cup cooked quinoa
- ¼ red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tablespoon onion, minced
- ½ cup fresh spinach
- ¼ avocado, diced
- ½ navel orange, peeled and sliced
- pinch cayenne pepper, optional
- lime juice
- olive oil
- honey
- sea salt
- cilantro

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, whisk lime juice, olive oil, honey, and sea salt. Stir in the cilantro. Add salt and honey to taste. Set aside until ready to use.
2. In a medium bowl, mix quinoa with red pepper and onions. Add a little vinaigrette and stir well. Let stand for 3 minutes (allows the quinoa to absorb the vinaigrette).
3. Place spinach on plate and top with the quinoa mixture. Top with diced avocado and orange slices.
4. Drizzle with vinaigrette and garnish with extra cilantro and a pinch of cayenne pepper, if desired.

## Shopping List
- ½ cup uncooked quinoa
- ¼ red bell pepper
- 1 onion
- ½ cup fresh spinach
- 1 avocado
- 1 navel orange
- cayenne pepper
- limes
- olive oil
- honey
- sea salt
- cilantro
