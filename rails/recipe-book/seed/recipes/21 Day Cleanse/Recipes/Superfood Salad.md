# Superfood Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- ¼ cup raw beet, peeled and grated
- 1 celery stalk with leaves, chopped
- 2 tablespoons fennel bulb, finely sliced
- ¼ cup bean sprouts
- ¼ cup sliced almonds
- ½ avocado, diced

## Instructions
1. Place spinach on a plate and top with the beet, celery, fennel, sprouts, and almonds.
2. Whisk all of the dressing ingredients together in a small bowl.
3. Drizzle on top of salad and top with avocado.
