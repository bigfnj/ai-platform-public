# Jadah’s Mango Detox Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 small ripe mango*
- ½ avocado
- ¼ cup cilantro, chopped
- sunflower seeds
- lime juice
- olive oil
- spinach

## Instructions
1. Cut mango and avocado into ⅛ inch cubes and place in a bowl.
2. Add the chopped cilantro, sunflower seeds, lime juice and olive oil.
3. Stir to coat and add spinach.

## Shopping List
- 1 small ripe mango*
- ½ avocado
- ¼ cup cilantro
- sunflower seeds
- 1 lime
- olive oil
- 1 bag of spinach
