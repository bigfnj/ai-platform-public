# Blueberry Spinach Smoothie Bowl

## Ingredients
- ¾ cup unsweetened almond milk
- ¾ cup frozen blueberries
- ½ cup fresh spinach
- ½ banana, frozen
- ¼ avocado
- 3 tablespoons hemp hearts
- ½ tablespoon almond butter

## Instructions
1. Place all of the ingredients in blender.
2. Blend for a good 30 seconds. If too thick, add a little more almond milk.
3. Transfer to a bowl and top with ¼ cup of your desired topping.

## Shopping List
- ¾ cup unsweetened almond milk
- ¾ cup frozen blueberries
- ½ cup fresh spinach
- 1 banana
- ¼ avocado
- 3 tablespoons hemp hearts
- ½ tablespoon almond butter
