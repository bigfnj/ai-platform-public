# Avocado Salad

## Ingredients
- ½ avocado
- ½ clove garlic, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon fresh lime juice
- 1 tablespoon onion, diced
- 4 cherry tomatoes, diced
- 3 teaspoons fresh cilantro (coriander), finely chopped
- Black pepper
- Sea salt

## Instructions
1. Mash avocado in a bowl with a fork.
2. Add garlic, lime juice, onion, tomato, and cilantro and mix together.
3. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
