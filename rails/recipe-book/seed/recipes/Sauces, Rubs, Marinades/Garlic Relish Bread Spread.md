# Garlic Relish Bread Spread

## Ingredients
- 20 garlic cloves, chopped
- ¼ cup chopped fresh Italian parsley
- ½ cup olive oil
- coarse salt and freshly ground pepper (1-2 teaspoons of pepper, 2-3 teaspoons of salt... to taste)
- 1 teaspoon dried red pepper flakes

## Instructions
1. Combine garlic cloves, parsley, olive oil, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes in a bowl.
2. Mix well.
