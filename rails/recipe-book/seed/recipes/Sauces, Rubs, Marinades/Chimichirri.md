# Chimichurri

## Ingredients
- 1 lb Parsley, unchopped (about 1.5 qt chopped)
- 8 oz Garlic, chopped
- 1 oz Fresh thyme, minced (about 6 tbsp)
- 1 oz Dry oregano (about 6 tbsp)
- 2 tsp Tabasco sauce
- 4 oz Soy sauce
- 12 cups Blended oil

## Instructions
1. Chop parsley until fine.
2. Add all remaining ingredients.
3. Blend well.
4. Season to taste.
