# Jerk Marinade

## Ingredients
- ¾ pound scotch bonnets, stem removed and roughly chopped
- ½ pound thyme leaves
- 6 ounces ground allspice
- ¼ cup fresh minced ginger
- 1 large onion, rough diced
- 4 bunches scallions, white and green parts only
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper
- 1 cup soy sauce
- ½ cup olive oil

## Instructions
1. Place all ingredients in food processor except soy sauce and olive oil.
2. After ingredients are finely pureed, place in mixing bowl and add soy sauce and oil.
3. Mix thoroughly & add 2 bottles of BBQ Sauce
4. Re-fill jars (2 bottles of bbq makes 3 bottles of jerk bbq)
5. Marinade BBQ Sauce overnight before using.
