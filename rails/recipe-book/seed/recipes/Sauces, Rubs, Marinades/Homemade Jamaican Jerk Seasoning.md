# Homemade Jamaican Jerk Seasoning

*Prep Time: 2 mins, Total Time: 2 mins, Makes: about 1/2 cup (6 servings)*

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons cayenne pepper
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 teaspoons ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1 teaspoon dried parsley
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon hot pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon ground clove
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin

## Instructions
1. Add all of the ingredients to a small mixing bowl.
2. Mix it all together evenly.
3. Store in an airtight container.
4. Use as needed.
**Tips**
5. As a wet rub or marinade, mix 2-3 tablespoons of the seasoning with 1 tablespoon oil per pound of meat.
6. Good on chicken, shrimp and other seafood, and vegetables like grilled corn.
7. For more heat, add a superhot pepper powder such as ghost pepper or scotch bonnet.
