# Ham Glaze

## Ingredients
- Marmalade
- Bourbon
- Maple Syrup
- Cinnamon
- Cloves
- 2 TBS melted Butter
- Orange Juice

## Instructions
1. Put ham on rack.
2. Place Orange Juice and Bourbon to cover bottom 1”.
3. Glaze last 15 minutes and while resting.
