# Sunday Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1 can Roma tomatoes (Ralphs)
- 1 small can tomato paste (Contadina)
- 1 small can tomato sauce (del Monte)
- Fresh Italian parsley & basil
- Dried oregano & basil
- Garlic, chopped
- Herb de Provence
- Garlic powder
- Salt & pepper
- 4 large carrots, sliced
- Water
- Spicy Italian sausage links (Ralphs)
- Country style ribs
- Ground meat for meatballs

## Instructions
1. Brown sausage and ribs.
2. Prepare meatballs.
3. In a heavy saucepan squish tomatoes, add tomato paste & sauce, parsley, basil, garlic, salt, pepper, and garlic powder, carrots, and water.
4. Cook for 2-2½ hours uncovered.
5. Add water if needed and season to taste.
6. After the sauce has been cooking for ½ hour, add all the meats.
7. Continue to cook until the sauce is ready, stirring frequently.
