# Lamb Mustard and Herb Marinade

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp dry mustard
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp water
- 3 medium garlic cloves, peeled and crushed
- 2 small dried red chili peppers, broken in half
- ½ cup plus 6 tbsp olive oil
- ¼ cup loosely packed fresh rosemary leaves
- ⅓ cup lemon juice
- 2 tsp coarsely ground black pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar

## Instructions
1. Mix together the mustards, sugar, water and crushed garlic. Set aside, covered, for 30 minutes to let the flavors develop.
2. Cook the chili peppers in 3 tbsp olive oil until they are dark, but not burned, 45 – 60 sec. Remove to a dish to cool. Reserve the oil.
3. Finely chop the rosemary with the metal blade of a food processor, about 10 sec. Mix together the rosemary, mustard mixture, chili peppers, the cooking oil, the remaining olive oil., lemon juice, the pepper, the salt, the oregano and the vinegar.
4. Place a one gallon plastic food storage bag inside another and put lamb inside. Place the bag in a large bowl. Pour in the marinade and close bag with a wire twist. Turn to coat the lamb well. Refrigerate 24 hours or longer, turning the bag occasionally.
5. Preheat oven to 450°F. Put lamb in oven and reduce to 400 degrees, cook 20 minutes. Let rest 10 minutes, then serve.
