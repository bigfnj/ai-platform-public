# Garden Salsa

*Prep time 10 mins Cook time 20 mins Total time 30 mins*

## Ingredients
- 2 cups tomatoes, skinned and diced
- 1/2 cup jalapenos, chopped
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup + 2 Tbsp vinegar
- 1 (6 oz) can tomato paste
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce

## Instructions
1. Combine all of the ingredients into a large sauce pan.
2. Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer, cover, and cook for 20 minutes.
3. Stir occasionally.
