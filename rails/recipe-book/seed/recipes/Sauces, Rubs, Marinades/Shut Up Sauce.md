# Shut Up Sauce

## Ingredients
- 10 habaneros
- 6 serranos
- 15 Thai chilies
- 6 jalapeños
- ½ bunch cilantro
- 1 small onion
- 1 tbsp garlic
- ½ tbsp salt
- ½ cup lemon juice
- 2 tbsp ponzu sauce

## Instructions
1. Blend all ingredients for 2 minutes.
