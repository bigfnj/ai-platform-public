# Brown Sugar & Spice - Salmon Rub

## Ingredients
- cooking spray
- 2 tablespoons packed light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 6 (6-ounce) salmon fillets, skin and any pin bones removed
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

## Instructions
1. Coat your grill or a grill pan with cooking spray and preheat over medium heat.
2. While the grill is heating, combine the brown sugar, chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper.
3. Brush each salmon fillet with 1/2 teaspoon of the oil, then rub each fillet with about 1/2 tablespoon of the spice mixture.
4. Grill the salmon, flesh side down, until charred, 4 to 5 minutes.
5. Flip the salmon and cook another 5 to 6 minutes for medium doneness.
6. For well done fish, cook an additional 1 to 2 minutes.
7. Remove to a platter and serve immediately.

## Shopping List
- cooking spray
- light brown sugar (2 tablespoons)
- chili powder (1 tablespoon)
- ground cumin (1 teaspoon)
- salt (1/8 teaspoon)
- black pepper (1/8 teaspoon)
- 6 salmon fillets (6 ounces each)
- olive oil (1 tablespoon)
