# Affogato Yonanas

## Ingredients
- 2 frozen ripe bananas
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 shot of espresso or coffee
- chocolate sauce (optional)

## Instructions
1. Insert ½ frozen banana.
2. Add ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon into Yonanas chute.
3. Insert remaining frozen bananas.
4. Stir in bowl to combine.
5. Scoop into glass or bowl.
6. OPTIONAL: Drizzle your favorite chocolate sauce on top of Yonanas.
7. Pour a shot of espresso or coffee over top.
