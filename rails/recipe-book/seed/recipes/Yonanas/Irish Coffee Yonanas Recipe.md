# Irish Coffee Yonanas

*Serves: 2*

## Ingredients
- 2 frozen over-ripe bananas
- 1 oz. coffee-infused dark chocolate bar
- 1 shot of coffee liqueur

## Instructions
1. Slightly thaw your frozen fruit prior to putting through your Yonanas maker.
2. Insert 1 frozen banana.
3. Add 1/2 oz. coffee-infused dark chocolate bar.
4. Alternate remaining bananas and chocolate.
5. Scoop coffee chocolate chip Yonanas into a glass and pour shot of coffee liqueur over Yonanas.

## Shopping List
- 2 bananas
- 1 oz. dark chocolate bar
- 1 shot of coffee liqueur
