# Eggnog Yonanas

## Ingredients
- 2 frozen over-ripe bananas
- 1/4 tsp. nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp. ground clove
- 1/4 tsp. ground cinnamon
- 3 coconut milk cubes (frozen)
- 2 T. bourbon
- 1/2 tsp. vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. Coconut Milk Cubes: Freeze coconut milk in ice cube trays. Do not shake can of coconut milk. Skim the top of the can to fill ice cube trays leaving remaining coconut water.
2. Bourbon Soaked Bananas: In a small bowl combine 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract & 2 tablespoons bourbon, pierce bananas with fork. Soak bananas with bourbon and vanilla extract for 1 hour. Remove bananas and freeze overnight.
3. Sprinkle nutmeg and clove over frozen bourbon bananas and let sit for 10 minutes
4. Insert 1 frozen banana into Yonanas machine.
5. Add frozen coconut milk cube.
6. Alternate remaining bananas and coconut cubes.
7. Stir in bowl to combine.
8. Sprinkle with cinnamon.

## Shopping List
- 2 bananas
- nutmeg
- ground cloves
- ground cinnamon
- canned coconut milk
- bourbon
- vanilla extract
