# Tropical Sorbet

## Ingredients
- ½ cup frozen chopped mango
- ½ cup frozen chopped pineapple. Let fruit thaw 7-10 minutes.

## Instructions
1. Insert ½ cup slightly thawed pineapple.
2. Insert ½ cup slightly thawed mango.
3. Repeat.
4. Enjoy yonanas vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free recipes made with 100% fruit!
