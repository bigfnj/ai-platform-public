# Mango Avocado Sorbet

## Ingredients
- ½ cup frozen mango
- ½ cup frozen avocado slices

## Instructions
1. Insert ¼ cup frozen mango.
2. Add ¼ cup frozen avocado slices.
3. Alternate remaining ingredients.
4. Stir in bowl to combine.

## Shopping List
- ½ cup frozen mango
- ½ cup frozen avocado
