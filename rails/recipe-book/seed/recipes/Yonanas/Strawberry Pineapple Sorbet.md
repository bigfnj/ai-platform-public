# Strawberry Pineapple Sorbet

## Ingredients
- ½ cup frozen strawberries
- ½ cup frozen pineapple

## Instructions
1. Let fruit thaw for 7 to 10 minutes.
2. Insert ½ cup slightly thawed frozen strawberries into Yonanas machine.
3. Insert ½ cup slightly thawed frozen pineapple into Yonanas machine.
4. Repeat.

## Shopping List
- ½ cup frozen strawberries
- ½ cup frozen pineapple
