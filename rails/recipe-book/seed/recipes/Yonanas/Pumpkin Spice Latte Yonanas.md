# Pumpkin Spice Latte Yonanas

## Ingredients
- 2 frozen over-ripe bananas
- 4 spiced pumpkin cubes*
- 2 oz coffee infused chocolate

*Optional Toppings:*
- Whip Cream (natural)
- chocolate sauce
- cinnamon to sprinkle on top

## Instructions
1. Insert 1 frozen banana.
2. Add 2 oz spiced pumpkin cubes (2 frozen cubes).
3. Insert 1 oz coffee infused chocolate.
4. Repeat with remaining ingredients.
5. Top with optional toppings and ENJOY!
