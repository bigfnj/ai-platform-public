# Tiramisu Yonanas

## Ingredients
- 1 ladyfinger (vegan options available to make online)
- 1 teaspoon dark chocolate shavings
- 1 shot coffee liqueur (or 2 tablespoons cold espresso for a non-alcoholic version)

## Instructions
1. Make Cinnamon Cocoa Yonanas recipe.
2. Add two scoops of yonanas to a serving dish.
3. Next add a ladyfinger in the bowl and pour coffee liqueur or espresso over yonanas.
4. Sprinkle with additional cinnamon powder and top with dark chocolate shavings.
