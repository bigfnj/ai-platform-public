# Orange Liqueur Yonanas

*Serves: 2*

## Ingredients
- 2 frozen over‐ripe bananas
- 1 cup frozen seedless orange segments
- 1 shot of orange liqueur

## Instructions
1. Slightly thaw your frozen fruit prior to putting through your yonanas maker.
2. Insert one frozen banana
3. Add 1/2 cup of frozen seedless orange segments
4. Alternate the remaining ingredients
5. Scoop Orange Yonanas into a glass and pour a shot of your favorite orange liqueur over Yonanas

## Shopping List
- 2 bananas
- 1 cup oranges
- 1 shot of orange liqueur
