# Mango Raspberry Sorbet

## Ingredients
- ½ cup frozen mango, thawed for 7-10 minutes
- ½ cup frozen raspberries, thawed for 7-10 minutes

## Instructions
1. Insert ½ cup slightly thawed mango.
2. Insert ½ cup slightly thawed raspberries.
3. Alternate remaining ingredients.
4. Stir in bowl to combine.
5. Enjoy.

## Shopping List
- frozen mango
- frozen raspberries
