# Old Fashion Yonanas

*Serves: 2*

## Ingredients
- 2 frozen over-ripe bananas
- 3/4 cup frozen pitted cherries
- 1/4 cup frozen orange segments
- 1/2 teaspoon orange zest
- 1 oz. dark chocolate
- 1/2 cup bourbon or cherry flavored bourbon

## Instructions
1. Make Bourbon Soaked Bananas in advance: Pierce peeled, over-ripe bananas with a fork and soak in a freezer bag with 1/4 cup of bourbon for about 1 hour. Remove bananas and freeze overnight.
2. Slightly thaw your frozen fruit prior to putting through your Yonanas maker.
3. Insert 1 frozen bourbon soaked banana
4. Add 1/2 cup of frozen pitted cherries
5. Add 1/4 cup of frozen orange segments
6. Add 1/2 teaspoon orange zest
7. Add 1 oz. dark chocolate
8. Alternate remaining banana and cherries

## Shopping List
- bananas
- cherries
- oranges
- bourbon
- dark chocolate
