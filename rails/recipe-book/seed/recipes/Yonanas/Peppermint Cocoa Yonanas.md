# Peppermint Cocoa Yonanas

## Ingredients
- 2 frozen over-ripe bananas
- 4 Tbs. cocoa powder
- crushed candy canes (peppermint flavor)
- dark chocolate chips (optional)

## Instructions
1. Sprinkle cocoa powder over frozen bananas.
2. Insert bananas into Yonanas machine.
3. Stir in bowl to combine.
4. Sprinkle crushed candy canes and dark chocolate chips over top.

## Shopping List
- 2 bananas
- 4 Tbs. cocoa powder
- peppermint candy canes
- dark chocolate chips
