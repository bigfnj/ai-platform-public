# All-Purpose Chintan

## Ingredients
- 4 kg chicken backs
- 6 L water
- 1 onion, peeled and halved
- 10 cloves garlic, peeled

## Instructions
1. Add neck bones and chicken backs to freshwater in a stockpot, bring to a simmer, and skim any scum that rises, for around 5-10 minutes.
2. Reduce heat back down to below simmer (around 88 °C/190 °F), hold for 5 hours
3. Add your onion and garlic. Cook for an additional hour.
4. Strain the soup and hold it until needed.
