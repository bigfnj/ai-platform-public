# Menma

## Ingredients
- 500 g lacto fermented bamboo shoots
- 100 g mirin
- 15 g sugar
- 20 g soy sauce
- sesame oil, as needed

## Instructions
1. The night before, submerge the bamboo in a bowl of cold water and allow them to soak in the fridge for 12-24 hours.
2. Drain the bamboo thoroughly, set aside.
3. In a saute pan, add your bamboo, mirin, sugar, and soy sauce.
4. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the liquid is slightly absorbed, 10-15 minutes.
5. Add the sesame oil to coat.
6. Transfer the menma to a container and allow them to soak for 24 hours. The menma will keep over time.
