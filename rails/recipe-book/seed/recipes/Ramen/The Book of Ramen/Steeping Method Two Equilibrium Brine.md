# Steeping Method Two: Equilibrium Brine

## Ingredients
- Water (varies)
- Soy sauce (varies)
- Mirin (varies)

## Instructions
1. In the container you will also soak the eggs in, weigh your peeled eggs. Add water to cover completely, and record the total weight of the eggs and water.
2. Add in 10% of this weight in soy sauce, and 8% in mirin.
3. Soak in the fridge for at least 24 hours, and reserve in the brine until needed.
