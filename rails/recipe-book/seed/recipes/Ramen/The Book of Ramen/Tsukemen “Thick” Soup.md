# Tsukemen “Thick” Soup

## Ingredients
- 4 L water
- 1.5 kg pork bones (femurs and necks)
- 1.5 kg chicken backs
- 0.5 kg chicken feet
- 1 kg belly, skin, ribs or 0.5 kg ribs and 1 kg pork belly roast with skin
- 1 yellow onion (peeled and quartered)
- 1 piece ginger (5 cm, peeled and sliced into 0.5 cm coins)
- 10 garlic cloves
- 20 g kombu
- 80 g niboshi
- 50 g thick-cut katsuobushi
- 50 g thick-cut sababushi
- 30 g gyofun (fish powder)

## Instructions
1. Add water and pork bones to a pressure cooker, at least an 8-quart size cooker, but preferably 10-quart.
2. Bring to a boil, and skim the scum until little to none rises, around 20 minutes.
3. Cover, bring to high pressure, cook for 1 hour.
