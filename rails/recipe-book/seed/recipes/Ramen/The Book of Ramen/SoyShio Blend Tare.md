# Soy/Shio Blend Tare

## Ingredients
- 7 g kombu
- 75 g mirin
- 50 g sake
- 25 g white wine
- 75 g white soy sauce
- 25 g usukuchi soy sauce
- 10 g salt
- 5 g MSG (optional)
- 25 g niboshi
- Sesame oil for toasting
- 150 g water
- 10 g katsuobushi

## Instructions
1. Soak the kombu in the mirin, sake, and white wine, for at least 6 hours and up to 24, in the fridge.
2. When ready to make, add the kombu and steeping liquid to a saucepot. Cook on medium heat until the cooking liquid registers at 160 F degrees. Hold here for 5 minutes.
3. Remove the kombu, boil slightly to remove some residual alcohol, around 5 minutes.
4. Add the soy sauces and salts. Reserve this liquid on the side.
5. In the now-empty pot, add the niboshi and sesame oil to a pot. Cook until fragrant on medium-high heat, around 40 seconds.
6. Add the water, bring to 82 °C/180 °F.
7. Add the katsuobushi and steep for 10 minutes.
8. Strain this liquid, adding it to the one in step 4.
9. Combine the two strained liquids.
