# Jiro Noodle

## Ingredients
- 94g King Arthur bread flour
- 6g whole wheat flour
- 36g water
- 1g powdered kansui (60% potassium 40% sodium)
- 1g salt

## Instructions
1. Add kansui powder and salt to the water, dissolve completely.
2. If using both sodium carbonate and potassium carbonate, mix them together in a 60:40 ratio before adding to the water.
