# Tantanmen Tare

## Ingredients
- 35 g soy sauce
- 3 g rice wine vinegar
- 25 g ground sesame (either tahini or nerigoma)

## Instructions
1. Combine the above ingredients, whisking until fully incorporated, at the bottom of a serving bowl. You can, alternatively, up the amount by scaling the amounts as needed.
