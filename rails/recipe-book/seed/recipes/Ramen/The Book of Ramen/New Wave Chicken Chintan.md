# New Wave Chicken Chintan

## Ingredients
- 4 kg/8.8 lb chicken backs (or two small chickens, broken down, breasts removed)
- 4 L water

## Instructions
1. Add the chicken to a stockpot, cover with water.
2. Bring the soup up to a boil briefly, skim any scum. Hold here for 5-10 minutes, or until scum subsides.
3. Reduce heat back down to below simmer (around 88 °C/190 °F), hold for 6 hours.
4. Strain the soup and hold it until needed.
