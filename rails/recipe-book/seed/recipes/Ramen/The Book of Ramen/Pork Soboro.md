# Pork Soboro

## Ingredients
- 15 mL vegetable oil (approx 1 tbsp)
- Half of one white onion, diced finely
- 0.5 kg ground pork (or around 1 lb)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 0.5-cm/0.25-in coins of ginger, minced
- 30 mL soy sauce (approx. 2 tbsp)
- 45 mL sweet bean paste (approx. 3 tbsp)
- 15 mL mirin (approx. 1 tbsp)
- 15 mL sake (approx. 1 tbsp)
- salt to taste

## Instructions
1. In a large saute pan, swirl the vegetable oil over medium heat until it begins to shimmer.
2. Add the onion and cook until translucent.
3. Add the ground pork and cook until browned.
4. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
5. Add the soy sauce, sweet bean paste, mirin, sake, and salt. Stir well to combine.
6. Cook until the liquid has evaporated and the mixture is dry.
7. Remove from heat and let it cool slightly before serving.
