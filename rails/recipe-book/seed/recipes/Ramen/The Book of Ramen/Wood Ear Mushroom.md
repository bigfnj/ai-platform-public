# Wood Ear Mushroom

## Ingredients
- Dried wood ear mushrooms
- Water

## Instructions
1. Cover the dried wood ear mushrooms with boiling water.
2. Allow to steep for 15 minutes, or until rehydrated.
3. Strain and reserve as needed. You can optionally cut into thin strips if desired.

## Shopping List
- Dried wood ear mushrooms
