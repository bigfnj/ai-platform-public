# Kitakata Shoyu Tare

## Ingredients
- 200 g dashi
- 300 g soy sauce
- 20 g small niboshi (such as iriko)
- 40 g sugar
- 40 g salt

## Instructions
1. Combine the above in a small saucepan, and bring to a boil to dissolve the salt and sugar.
2. Once fully dissolved, allow tare to cool, then strain.
3. If not used immediately, store in the refrigerator for up to 6 months.
