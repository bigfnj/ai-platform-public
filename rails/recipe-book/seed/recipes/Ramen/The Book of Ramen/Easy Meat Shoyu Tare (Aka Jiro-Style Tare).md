# Easy Meat Shoyu Tare (Aka Jiro-Style Tare)

## Ingredients
- Chashu, cooked in broth until tender
- 450 g soy sauce
- 100 g mirin
- 15 g msg
- 15 g brown sugar

## Instructions
1. Combine the soy sauce, mirin, sugar, and MSG in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then remove from the heat.
2. Place the chashu into the liquid, reserve in the fridge for 4-12 hours.
3. Remove the chashu, use it for eating or other purposes. Store the tare in a sealed container for up to 2 months.

## Shopping List
