# Balsamic Vinegrete

## Ingredients
- ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
- ¾ cup olive oil
- ¼ cup red wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons good mustard
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Combine all the above.
2. Shake well.
