# Chinese Chicken Salad

## Ingredients
- 12 cups shredded iceberg lettuce
- 3 cups chicken breast
- 2 cups thinly shredded red cabbage
- 1 cup fresh bean sprouts
- ¾ cup finely chopped green onions
- water chestnuts
- ½ cup slivered almonds, toasted
- ¼ cup toasted sesame seeds
- 2 cups crisp deep-fried rice noodles
- sliced mandarin oranges
- crisp fried won ton strips

## Plum Sauce Dressing
- 1 cup plum sauce
- 1 tablespoon hoisin sauce
- 1 teaspoon Lite soy sauce
- ¼ cup lemon juice
- 3 tablespoons sesame oil
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- cider vinegar

## Instructions
1. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the plum sauce dressing ingredients until well blended.
2. Toss all ingredients and serve.
