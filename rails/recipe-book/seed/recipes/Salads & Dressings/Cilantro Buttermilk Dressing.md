# Cilantro Buttermilk Dressing

## Ingredients
- ½ cup buttermilk
- 1 Tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 Tbsp Hidden Valley Ranch Mix
- 1 ½  Tbsp grated  Romano cheese
- ⅓ cup vegetable oil
- ½ cup cilantro, chopped

## Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients in a blender until smooth.
2. If dressing is too runny, add more Romano cheese.
3. Chill.
