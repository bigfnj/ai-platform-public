# Kale Salad

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 3 garlic cloves minced
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Pinch hot pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons bread crumbs or panko

## Instructions
1. Mix the above, add kale.
2. Toss and let sit at room temperature for 30 minutes.
3. Then add the cheese and bread crumbs when serve.
