# Parmesan Ranch Dressing

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- juice of 1/2 lemon
- 4 tablespoons rice wine vinegar
- 4 teaspoons salt seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 4 teaspoons minced fresh dill
- black pepper
- 2 teaspoons fresh minced parsley
- 2 teaspoons fresh minced chives
- 3/4 cup Parmesan cheese

## Instructions
1. Blend all the ingredients except the fresh herbs and Parmesan cheese until smooth.
2. Finally add the fresh herbs and cheese and stir well. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
