# Sesame-Sambal Turkey Lettuce Cups with Mushrooms, Sweet Peppers & Cashews

## Ingredients
- 10 oz ground turkey
- 4 oz sweet peppers
- 4 oz cremini mushrooms
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 head butter lettuce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp sambal oelek
- 1-inch piece ginger
- 3 tbsp roasted cashews
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 2 tsp honey

## Instructions
1. Wash and dry all produce.
2. Peel and mince the garlic.
3. Peel and grate the ginger.
4. Separate the lettuce leaves.
5. Cut out the cores of the peppers; thinly slice the peppers.
6. Thinly slice the mushrooms.
7. In a bowl, combine the soy sauce, sesame oil, sambal oelek, vinegar, honey, half the minced garlic, and the grated ginger; season with salt and pepper to taste. Stir well.
8. Heat a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the turkey to the hot pan in a single layer. Cook without stirring until browned, 2 to 3 minutes. Break up the turkey into pieces and cook, stirring occasionally, until cooked through, 4 to 5 minutes. Transfer to a bowl.
9. In the same pan, add 1 tablespoon of oil. Add the sliced peppers and mushrooms to the hot pan in a single layer. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3 to 4 minutes. Stir in the remaining minced garlic and cook until fragrant, 30 seconds. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
10. Return the turkey to the pan with the peppers and mushrooms. Add the sauce (to taste) and stir to combine. Cook until heated through, 1 to 2 minutes.
11. Divide the turkey mixture between the lettuce leaves. Top with the cashews.
