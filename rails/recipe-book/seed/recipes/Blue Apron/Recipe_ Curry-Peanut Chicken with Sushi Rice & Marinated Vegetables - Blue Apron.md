# Curry-Peanut Chicken with Sushi Rice & Marinated Vegetables

## Ingredients
- 10 oz chopped chicken breast
- 2 Persian cucumbers
- 2 scallions
- 6 oz carrots
- 1 tbsp smooth peanut butter spread
- 1 tbsp yellow curry paste
- 2 tbsps mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp mirin (salted cooking wine)
- ¼ cup cornstarch
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 3 tbsps roasted peanuts
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
- ½ cup sushi rice

## Instructions
1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
2. Cut off and discard the ends of the scallions; thinly slice, separating the white and green parts.
3. Peel the carrots; cut into matchsticks.
4. Thinly slice the cucumbers on an angle.
5. In a bowl, combine the sliced scallion whites, sliced carrots, sliced cucumbers, 1 tablespoon of sugar (you’ll have 2 tablespoons remaining), and vinegar; toss to combine. Set aside to marinate, at least 5 minutes.
6. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels; season with salt and pepper on both sides. Working in batches, add the cornstarch to a bowl. Add the chicken to the bowl; toss to coat with the cornstarch, shaking off any excess.
7. In a large pan, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium-high heat until hot. Add half of the coated chicken and cook, without stirring, 2 to 3 minutes, or until lightly browned. Flip and cook 2 to 4 minutes more, or until cooked through. Transfer to a plate; season with salt and pepper. Repeat with the remaining olive oil and coated chicken.
8. In a bowl, combine the peanut butter spread, curry paste, mayonnaise, mirin, and ¼ cup of water; stir until smooth. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
9. Thinly slice the scallion greens. Roughly chop the peanuts.
10. Divide the cooked chicken between 2 plates. Top with the marinated vegetables. Drizzle over the sauce. Garnish with the sliced scallion greens and chopped peanuts.
