# Crispy Pan-Fried Ramen with Soft-Boiled Eggs

*Est. 750 calories per serving*

## Ingredients
- 2 pasture-raised eggs
- ½ lb fresh ramen noodles (previously frozen)
- ½ lb red cabbage
- 4 oz sweet peppers
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- ⅓ cup Asian-style sautéed aromatics
- 2 tbsp black bean sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar

## Instructions
1. Bring a small pot of water to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, carefully lower the eggs into the water and cook for exactly 6 minutes.
2. While the eggs cook, thinly slice the cabbage. Halve the sweet peppers lengthwise, then remove the stems and seeds; thinly slice crosswise.
3. Heat 1 teaspoon of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the sliced cabbage and sliced sweet peppers to the hot pan and cook, stirring frequently, until slightly softened, about 2 minutes. Remove from pan and set aside.
4. In a bowl, combine the aromatics, black bean sauce, soy sauce, vinegar, and remaining sesame oil; season with salt and pepper to taste. Stir to combine.
5. Once the eggs have finished cooking, drain them in a colander and immediately transfer to an ice bath to cool for 1 minute. Peel the eggs under cold running water, then carefully slice each egg in half lengthwise.
6. In the same pan used earlier, add enough oil to coat the bottom of the pan over medium-high heat. Add the ramen noodles to the hot pan and cook, stirring frequently, until lightly browned and crispy, about 3 minutes.
7. Stir the cooked cabbage mixture into the pan with the ramen noodles; season with salt and pepper to taste. Divide between plates and top each serving with the egg halves. Drizzle the sauce over the top.
