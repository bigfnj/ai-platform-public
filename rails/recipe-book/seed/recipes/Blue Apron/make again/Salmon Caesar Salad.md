# Salmon Caesar Salad

## Ingredients
- 6 cups romaine lettuce, chopped
- 2 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 anchovy fillets
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Place salmon fillets on a baking sheet, drizzle with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and bake for about 12-15 minutes or until cooked through.
3. While the salmon is baking, prepare the Caesar dressing. In a blender, combine eggs, Parmesan cheese, anchovies, garlic, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, and remaining olive oil. Blend until smooth.
4. In a large bowl, toss the chopped romaine lettuce with the Caesar dressing.
5. Once the salmon is done, flake it into bite-sized pieces and add it to the salad.
6. Serve immediately.
