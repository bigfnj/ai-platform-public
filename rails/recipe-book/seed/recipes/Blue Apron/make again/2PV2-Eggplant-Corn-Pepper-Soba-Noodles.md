# Sweet Pepper, Zucchini & Corn Stir-Fry with Soba Noodles & Ginger Cashews

*Makes: 2 servings | Calories: about 690 per serving | Prep Time: 15 minutes | Cook Time: 15–25 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 6 ounces soba noodles
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 summer sweet peppers
- 2 scallions
- 1 ear of corn
- 1 zucchini
- 3 tablespoons cashews
- 2 tablespoons ponzu sauce
- 2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 1-inch piece ginger
- ¼ cup sweet chili sauce

## Instructions
1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. Heat a large pot of salted water to boiling on high.
2. Peel and mince the ginger. Cut off and discard the root ends of the scallions; thinly slice the scallions on an angle, separating the white bottoms and green tops. Peel and mince the garlic. Cut off and discard the stem end of the zucchini; small dice the zucchini.
3. Remove and discard the husks and silks of the corn. Cut the corn kernels off the cob; discard the cob. Cut off and discard the stem ends of the peppers; remove and discard any seeds, then cut the peppers into ¼-inch-thick rings.
4. Add the noodles to the pot of boiling water and cook 3 to 4 minutes, or until tender. Drain thoroughly; rinse under cold water for 30 seconds to 1 minute to stop the cooking process.
5. While the noodles cook, in a medium pan (nonstick, if you have one), heat 1 teaspoon of olive oil on medium-high until hot. Add the cashews and ginger; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, 1 to 2 minutes, or until lightly browned and fragrant.
6. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate; immediately season with salt and pepper to taste. Wipe out the pan.
7. In the same pan, heat the sesame oil on medium-high until hot. Add the white bottoms of the scallions and garlic; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, 30 seconds to 1 minute, or until softened and fragrant.
8. Add the zucchini, corn and peppers to the pan of aromatics; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, 5 to 7 minutes, or until slightly softened.
9. While the vegetables cook, rinse the cooked noodles under warm water to loosen them; drain thoroughly. To the pan of vegetables, add the rinsed noodles, ponzu sauce and sweet chili sauce. Cook, stirring constantly, 30 seconds to 1 minute, or until thoroughly combined and heated through.
10. Remove from heat and season with salt and pepper to taste. Divide the finished stir-fry between 2 dishes. Garnish with the ginger cashews and green tops of the scallions.
