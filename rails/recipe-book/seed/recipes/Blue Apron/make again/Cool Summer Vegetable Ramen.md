# Cool Summer Vegetable Ramen

*Makes 2 servings*

## Ingredients
- 12 ounces fresh ramen noodles
- 6 ounces summer beans
- 2 scallions
- 1 ear of corn
- 1 persian cucumber
- 1 ounce amaranth leaves
- ¼ pound multicolored cherry tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons ponzu sauce
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon summer ramen spice blend (togarashi, black sesame seeds, white sesame seeds & dried orange peel)
- salt
- pepper

## Instructions
1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. Heat a large pot of salted water to boiling on high.
2. Cut off and discard the stem ends of the beans; halve on an angle. Cut off and discard the root ends of the scallions; thinly slice the scallions, separating the white bottoms and green tops. Remove and discard the husks and silks of the corn. Cut the corn kernels off the cob; discard the cob. Pick the amaranth leaves off the stems; discard the stems. Halve the tomatoes. Cut off and discard the ends of the cucumber. Halve the cucumber lengthwise; using a spoon, scoop out and discard the seeds. Cut the cucumber lengthwise into thin strips; toss with ¼ of the vinegar.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together the ponzu sauce, sesame oil and remaining vinegar until combined.
4. Add the beans to the pot of boiling water. Cook 3 to 4 minutes, or until tender. Using a slotted spoon or strainer, remove the cooked beans from the pot, leaving the water boiling on the stove. Rinse the cooked beans under cold water for 30 seconds to 1 minute to stop the cooking process.
5. In a small bowl, toss the blanched beans with a splash of the sauce and a pinch of the spice blend; season with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Using your hands, carefully separate the strands of the noodles. Add the noodles to the pot of boiling water used to cook the beans. Cook, stirring occasionally, for exactly 90 seconds, until tender. Drain thoroughly and rinse under cold water to stop the cooking process. Rinse and dry the pot.
7. In the pot used to cook the noodles, heat 2 teaspoons of oil on medium-high until hot. Add the corn; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, 1 to 2 minutes, or until lightly browned. Add the white bottoms of the scallions; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, 1 to 2 minutes, or until softened and fragrant.
8. Divide the cooked noodles and remaining sauce between 2 dishes; toss to coat and season with salt and pepper to taste. Top with the cooked corn, dressed beans, cucumber, tomatoes and amaranth leaves. Garnish with the green tops of the scallions and remaining spice blend.
