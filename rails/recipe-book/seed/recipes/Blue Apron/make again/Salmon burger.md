# Salmon Burgers & Aioli

*Makes 2 Servings*

## Ingredients
- 2 skinless salmon fillets, finely chopped
- 2 potato buns, halved
- 2 cloves garlic, peeled and minced
- 1 ear of corn, husks and silks removed, kernels cut off
- 1 lemon, quartered and deseeded
- 1 ounce arugula, plus extra for topping
- 1 bunch oregano leaves, picked and finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 shallot, peeled and minced
- ¼ cup panko breadcrumbs
- salt
- pepper
- olive oil

## Instructions
1. Wash and dry the fresh produce. Remove and discard the husks and silks of the corn. Cut the kernels off the cob; discard the cob.
2. Peel and mince the shallot. Pick the oregano leaves off the stems; discard the stems and finely chop the leaves. Peel and mince the garlic; using the side of your knife, smash until it resembles a paste (or use a zester). Quarter and deseed the lemon. Halve the buns.
3. To a large bowl, add the chopped salmon, breadcrumbs, oregano, half the garlic paste, the remaining shallot and a drizzle of olive oil; season with salt and pepper. Gently mix to combine. Form into two ½-inch-thick burgers.
4. In a medium pan (nonstick, if you have one), melt half the butter on medium-high until hot. Add the corn and half the shallot; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, 2 to 4 minutes, or until the shallot is softened and the corn is lightly browned. Transfer to a large bowl. Wipe out the pan.
5. In the pan used to cook the corn and shallot, heat 2 teaspoons of olive oil on medium-high until hot. Add the burgers; cook 3 to 4 minutes per side, or until browned and cooked through. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate. Wipe out the pan.
6. While the burgers continue to cook, in a small bowl, combine the mayonnaise, remaining garlic paste, the juice of 1 lemon wedge and a drizzle of olive oil. Stir to combine and season with salt and pepper to taste.
7. To the bowl of cooked corn and shallot, add all but a pinch of the arugula, the juice of remaining lemon wedges and a drizzle of olive oil; toss to coat and season with salt and pepper to taste.
8. In the pan used to cook the burgers, melt the remaining butter on medium until hot. Add the buns, cut sides down, and toast 30 seconds to 1 minute, or until lightly browned. Transfer to a clean, dry work surface.
9. Spread a layer of the aioli onto the cut sides of the toasted buns. Place a cooked burger onto the bun bottoms; season with salt and pepper. Top each with the remaining arugula and the toasted bun tops.
