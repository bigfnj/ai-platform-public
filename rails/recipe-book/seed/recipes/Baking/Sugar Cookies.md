# Easy Recipe for Sugar Cookies

*Makes 24-36 cookies*

## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter (salted)
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking powder

## Instructions
1. Cream the butter and sugar together for a full 2 minutes until extremely light and fluffy.
2. Scrape sides of bowl, then add in your egg and vanilla.
3. Add in your flour and baking powder. The dough will look dry; keep mixing until it comes together. If it doesn't come together, you can add water to the dough 1 tsp at a time. The dough should be the same consistency as play-doh. If it is too dry, add some water. If it is too wet, add some flour.
4. Roll out the dough to desired thickness (about ¼ inch for small cookies or ½ inch for large cookies).
5. Cut out shapes with cookie cutters and place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
6. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 8-10 minutes, until the tops are no longer glossy and they have puffed up slightly. Do not overbake!
7. Allow the cookies to cool on the pan for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire cooling rack.
