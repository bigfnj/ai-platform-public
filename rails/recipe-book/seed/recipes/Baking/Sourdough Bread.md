# Sourdough Bread

## Ingredients
- 120 g starter
- 350 g water
- 10 g salt
- 500 g bread flour

## Instructions
1. Combine flour and water. Wait 1 hour.
2. Combine starter and salt. Mix well then knead with dough hook for 10 min.
3. Transfer to an oiled bowl, let sit 30 min. Then bend and snap every hour about 4 times. Then let sit for 3-4 hours to rise.
4. Turn dough out onto floured surface. Do 1 last bend and snap.
5. Transfer to bowl with bread paper. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
6. With Dutch oven in oven, preheat to 500°F (260°C). Place bread in Dutch oven and cook with lid on for 30 minutes. Take lid off and cook for another 5 minutes.

## Shopping List
- 120 g starter
- 350 g water
- 10 g salt
- 500 g bread flour
- cooking oil
