# Persimmon Bread

## Ingredients
- 1 cup raisins
- ½ cup brandy
- 2 cups firmly packed dark brown sugar
- ½ cup white sugar
- 2 cups persimmon pulp
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 4 eggs
- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking soda
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- ½ tsp ginger
- 1 cup chopped walnuts

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Plump raisins in brandy and set aside.
3. Mix together the sugars with persimmon pulp and oil.
4. Add eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5. Sift together the dry ingredients and add to egg mixture, blending well.
6. Add raisins and walnuts.
7. Pour into 4 buttered and floured 7” loaf pans.
8. Bake for 1 hour or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
9. Cool on a rack.
