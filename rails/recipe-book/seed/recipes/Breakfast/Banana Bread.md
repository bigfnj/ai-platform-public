# Banana Bread

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup melted butter
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. sour cream
- 1 ¼ cups flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- white chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Mash the bananas and mix in brown sugar and melted butter.
2. Add eggs, sour cream, flour, baking powder, and baking soda.
3. Mix well and add white chocolate chips.
4. Bake at 350°F for about 40 minutes.
