# Cranberry Granola

## Ingredients
- 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1 cup sliced almonds
- 1 cup cranberries, chopped
- ¼ cup vegetable oil
- ¼ cup plus 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Toss oats, almonds ,and cranberries in a bowl.
2. Pour the oil and honey over the mixture.
3. Add the cinnamon and stir until all the oats and nuts are coated.
4. Pour onto a baking sheet.
5. Bake, stirring occasionally until mixture turns golden brown (about 25 min).
