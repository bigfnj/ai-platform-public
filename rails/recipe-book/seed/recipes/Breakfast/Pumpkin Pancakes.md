# Pumpkin Pancakes

## Ingredients
- 1 ¼ cups buttermilk
- ¾ cup canned pure pumpkin
- 4 large eggs, separated, room temperature
- ¼ cup sugar
- ¾ teaspoon vanilla extract
- ¼ cup unsalted butter, melted (½ stick)
- 1 ⅓ cups cake flour
- 1 ¾ teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- ½ teaspoon salt
- vegetable oil
- ½ cup finely chopped walnuts
- maple syrup

## Instructions
1. Whisk buttermilk, pumpkin, egg yolks, sugar, and vanilla extract in a medium bowl to blend; whisk in melted butter.
2. Whisk flour, pumpkin pie spice, baking soda, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl to blend.
3. Add dry ingredients to buttermilk mixture and whisk to combine.
4. Using electric mixer beat egg whites in medium bowl until soft peaks form.
5. Fold whites into batter.
6. Lightly oil or butter skillet.
7. Cook pancakes and serve with syrup.
8. Sprinkle with nuts.
