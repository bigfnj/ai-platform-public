# Eggs a la Burch

## Ingredients
- 10 - 12 eggs
- 1 lb hot bulk sausage (Jimmy Dean or Farmer John)
- 6 slices white bread (with crusts removed)
- 2 cups sharp cheddar cheese, grated
- 2 ½ cups milk
- 8 oz. chopped green chilies
- Salt
- Pepper

## Instructions
1. At least 2 hours before (best is overnight) grease 9 x 12 glass oven-proof pan.
2. Line the bottom with diced white bread.
3. Brown sausage and drain well before sprinkling over bread.
4. Cover with grated cheese.
5. Beat eggs in bowl, adding milk and chopped green chilies.
6. Season to taste.
7. Pour mixture over layers of bread, sausage and cheese.
8. Pat down so everything is covered with the egg mixture.
9. Cover pan with saran wrap and refrigerate.
10. Pre-heat oven to 350 degrees or little less.
11. Remove saran wrap and place in oven.
12. Bake for approximately 45 minutes.
13. It’s done when it isn’t jiggly in the middle.
14. Remove from oven and serve.
