# Pancakes

## Ingredients
- 1 cup flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- pinch salt
- 2 tbsp white sugar
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- pumpkin puree or fruit of choice

## Instructions
1. Combine dry ingredients and set aside.
2. Mix egg, milk, vanilla and melted butter. Make sure butter isn’t too hot.
3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and mix until combined. Set aside for 5-10 min.
