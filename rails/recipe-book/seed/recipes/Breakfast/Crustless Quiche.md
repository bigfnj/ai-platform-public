# Crustless Quiche

## Ingredients
- 10 oz. frozen spinach
- sliced zucchini
- ½ cup onions, sautéed
- 5 large eggs or 7 egg whites
- 1 clove garlic, minced and sautéed
- 1 cup grated cheese with 1 Tbsp. flour
- 1 ¼ cup milk
- salt
- ⅛ tsp. nutmeg

## Instructions
1. Beat together the eggs or egg whites.
2. Combine all ingredients in a baking dish.
3. Bake at 400°F for about 40 minutes or until done.
