# Date Nut Bread

## Ingredients
- 2 cups coarsely chopped dates (10 oz. pitted)
- ⅓ cup orange liqueur (Cointreau or Triple Sec)
- 4 Tbsp. unsalted butter, at room temp.
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- 1 extra large egg
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 1 Tbsp. grated orange zest
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp. ground nutmeg
- ¼ tsp. ground cloves
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¾ cup orange juice
- ¾ cup coarsely chopped pecans (3 ounces)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Spray a loaf pan with Pam.
3. Sift together the flour, baking powder ,baking soda, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, and salt.
4. Combine the dates and orange liqueur in a small bowl and set aside for 30 min. stir occasionally.
5. In a bowl of an electric mixer, beat the butter and brown sugar together for 1 min.
6. Mix in on low the egg, vanilla, and orange zest.
7. Add the flour mixture alternately with the orange juice to a creamed mixture. Beating only until combined.
8. By hand, stir in the dates with their liquid and the pecans.
9. Pour the batter into the pan, smooth the top.
10. Bake for 50-60 minutes, or until toothpick comes out clean.
11. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack and cool completely.
