# Apple Puffed Pancakes

## Ingredients
- 6 eggs
- 1 ½ cups milk
- 1 cup flour
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
- ¼ lb. butter or margarine
- Apples
- 2 -3 tablespoons brown sugar

## Instructions
1. Preheat Oven to 425°F.
2. Mix eggs, milk, flour, sugar, vanilla, salt, and cinnamon until blended, keep a little lumpy.
3. Melt butter.
4. Put apples in dish.
5. Pour batter over apples and sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon.
6. Bake until puffed and no liquid in center.
