# Cinnamon Oatmeal Banana Pancakes

## Ingredients
- 1 cup old-fashioned oats
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 ripe medium bananas, cut into chunks
- 3/4 cup milk (almond milk, skim milk, etc)
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon (or more if you prefer)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions
1. In a blender, add chunks of banana and milk. Puree for 30 seconds or until thoroughly blended and smooth.
2. Next add oats, baking powder, and salt into the blender; blend again until batter is well-combined; about 1 minute.
3. Set aside to thicken up.
4. After a few minutes check the consistency of the pancake batter; it should be somewhat thick.
5. If it appears too thick, add a tablespoon or two of milk and blend again.
6. Lightly coat a large nonstick skillet or griddle with vegan cooking spray or olive oil and heat over medium.
7. Drop batter by 1/4 cupfuls onto skillet and gently spread out a bit with a spoon.
8. Cook until bubbles appear on top, about 1-2 minutes.
9. Gently flip cakes and cook until golden brown on underside, 1-2 minutes.
10. Wipe skillet clean and repeat with more oil or cooking spray.
11. Top with peanut butter, maple syrup, fruit, or chocolate chips.
