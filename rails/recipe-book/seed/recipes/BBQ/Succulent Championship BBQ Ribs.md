# Succulent Championship BBQ Ribs

## Ingredients
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup Dutch Gold Pure Honey from Orange Blossoms
- 1 tablespoon Parkay squeeze margarine
- ¼ cup Dole pineapple juice
- Texas Rib Rangers Spicy Seasoning (to taste)
- Texas Rib Rangers Rosemary and Herb Seasoning (to taste)
- Aluminum Foil
- 1 (14.5 ounce) bottle Texas Rib Rangers Spicy BBQ Sauce (if desired)

## Instructions
1. Heat cooker to 225°F.
2. If you pull the skin off the back of ribs, season both sides. If not, season only the top of the meat.
3. To remove the membrane from the ribs: Place the ribs bone-side up. Using one square of paper towel, start with the larger end of the ribs, grabbing only the skin/membrane at the edge of one of the bones and pull it off.
