# Jeff’s Original BBQ Sauce

*Makes about a pint and a half of sauce.*

## Ingredients
- 2 cups ketchup (Heinz preferred)
- ¼ cup molasses (reduce to 1 tablespoon if using strong-flavored molasses like Brer Rabbit®)
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire Sauce
- Juice from ½ fresh lemon (approximately 2 tablespoons)
- ½ teaspoon Tabasco® Brand Hot Sauce
- ½ cup dark brown sugar (loose, unpacked)
- ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 2 cloves minced fresh garlic
- 1 tablespoon coarse black pepper

## Instructions
1. In a small saucepan add the ketchup and molasses.
2. Using the same measuring cup, add Worcestershire Sauce, lemon juice, and Tabasco® Brand Hot Sauce, mixing it all around to make sure all ingredients end up in the saucepan and are not left in the bottom of the cup.
3. Add dark brown sugar and cayenne pepper to taste.
4. Mince 2 cloves of fresh garlic into very small pieces but not so small that it becomes juice.
5. Add the coarse black pepper per the recipe and allow to simmer over low heat for 10 to 15 minutes to combine the flavors.
