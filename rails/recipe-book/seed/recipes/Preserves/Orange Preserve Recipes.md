# Orange Preserve Recipes

## Orange Jelly
- 5 cups freshly squeezed orange juice
- 5 cups sugar
- 2 packets liquid pectin
- *Method:* Boil and reduce the mixture, then add pectin to reach 220 degrees for three minutes before canning.

## Old Fashioned Marmalade
- 10 medium navel oranges
- 6 cups sugar
- 1/4 cup fresh squeezed lemon juice
- 4 cups water
- *Method:* Soak orange slices in sugar and water overnight, then boil and add lemon juice before canning.

## Sunshine Citrus Dessert Sauce
- 9 cups prepared orange slices (can use clementines, mandarin oranges, honey tangerines)
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/3 cup freshly squeezed orange juice
- 1/4 cup liquid honey
- 3 quarter-sized pieces peeled ginger root
- 1 cinnamon stick
- *Method:* Boil sugar, honey, orange juice, and spices with citrus segments for five minutes before canning.

## Orange Syrup
- 2 cups fresh squeezed orange juice
- 4 tablespoons honey
- Half bag Sure Jell
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- *Method:* Cook the mixture until thickened and bubbly, then stir for an additional five minutes before canning.

## Candied Orange Peels
- 6 navel oranges
- 4 1/2 cups sugar
- Superfine sugar for rolling
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 cup semisweet or dark chocolate (optional)
- *Method:* Blanch and simmer orange peels in sugar syrup until translucent, then roll in sugar and dry.

## Navel Orange Liqueur
- 4-5 navel oranges
- 2 cups raw sugar
- Water
- 2 cups vodka
- *Method:* Infuse vodka with orange peel and syrup for at least one month before straining and bottling.
