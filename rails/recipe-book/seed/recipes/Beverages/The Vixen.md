# The Vixen

## Ingredients
- Drop Cherry
- 3 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Peppermint Schnapps
- ¼ oz cherry juice

## Instructions
1. Drop Cherry at bottom of glass.
2. Shake and strain vodka, peppermint schnapps into martini glass.
3. Slowly pour cherry juice.

## Shopping List
- Drop Cherries
- Vodka (3 oz)
- Peppermint Schnapps (1 oz)
- Cherry Juice (¼ oz)
