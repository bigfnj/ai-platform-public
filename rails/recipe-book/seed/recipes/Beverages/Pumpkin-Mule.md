# Pumpkin-Mule

## Ingredients
- 1/2 lime, muddled
- 1.5 oz dark rum
- 1.5 oz pumpkin liquor
- 1 dash angostura bitters
- ice cubes
- ginger beer
- nutmeg for garnish
- cinnamon for garnish

## Instructions
1. Muddle 1/2 lime into bottom of glass.
2. Add dark rum, pumpkin liquor, and angostura bitters.
3. Fill with ice.
4. Top with ginger beer.
5. Roll into tin & back into mug.
6. Garnish with nutmeg & cinnamon.
