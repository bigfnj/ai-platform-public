# Peaches and Cream

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Crown Royal Whiskey
- 1/2 oz whiskey
- 1 1/2 oz peach schnapps
- 3 oz cream soda
- 1 peach wedge
- 1 maraschino cherry
- 1 mint sprig

## Instructions
1. Fill shaker 1/2 full with ice.
2. Pour in Crown Royal Whiskey and whiskey.
3. Add Peach Schnapps.
4. Shake vigorously for 30 seconds.
5. Put fresh ice in glass.
6. Strain shaken ingredients into glass.
7. Fill to top with cream soda, garnish with peach wedge, maraschino cherry, and mint sprig, and serve.
