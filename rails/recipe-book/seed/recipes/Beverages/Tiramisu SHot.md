# Tiramisu Shot

## Ingredients
- 1 oz whipped cream vodka
- 1/2 oz kahlúa
- 1/4 oz baileys
- whipped cream
- graham cracker crumbs

## Instructions
1. Shake the vodka and kahlúa.
2. Float 1/4 oz baileys on top.
3. Add whipped cream.
4. Sprinkle with graham cracker crumbs.
