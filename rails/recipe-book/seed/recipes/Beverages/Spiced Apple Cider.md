# Spiced Apple Cider

## Ingredients
- 2 oz apple cider
- 2 oz spiced rum
- 1 oz butter schnapps
- caramel for rimming
- cinnamon to taste
- nutmeg to taste

## Instructions
1. Rim glass with caramel, dash of cinnamon and nutmeg.
2. Fill glass with ice.
3. Add apple cider, spiced rum, and butter schnapps.
