# Bloody Mary Pitcher
*Makes one 750ml bottle*

## Ingredients
- 1/2 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz Worcestershire sauce
- 1½ tsp finely-ground black pepper
- 1½ tsp celery salt
- 1 tsp Tabasco sauce

## Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients together and bottle.
2. To make a Caesar or Bloody Mary, combine 1 oz premix, 2 oz vodka, and 4 oz tomato juice or Clamato in a pint glass, mix well, garnish and serve.

## Shopping List
- 1/2 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz Worcestershire sauce

- 1½ tsp celery salt
