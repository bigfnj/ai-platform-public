# J’s Nog

## Ingredients
- 2 oz nog
- ½ oz rum
- ½ oz kahlúa
- ¼ oz whiskey
- ¼ oz vanilla extract
- cinnamon
- nutmeg
- whipped cream

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake well and strain into a glass.
3. Top with whipped cream, sprinkle with cinnamon and nutmeg.
