# El Diablo

## Ingredients
- 2 oz Siembra Azul Blanco
- 1/2 oz Mathilde Cassis
- 1/2 oz Lime Juice
- 1/4 oz Key Lime Juice
- 1/2 oz Agave Nectar
- 1 oz Ginger Beer

## Instructions
1. Shake ginger beer, and strain into skinny collins over crushed ice.
2. Garnish with a lime wedge & blackberry.
