# Long Island Ice Tea

## Original Recipe
- 1 oz White Rum
- 1 oz Tanqueray Gin
- 1 oz Triple Sec
- 1 oz Vodka
- Splash Coke
- Splash Sweet Sour Mix
- *Method:* Mix the alcohol together, then add the coke and sweet sour.

## Half-Sized Recipe
- ½ oz Tequila
- ½ oz White Rum
- ½ oz Triple Sec
- ½ oz Tanqueray Gin
- ½ oz Vodka
- 1 oz Simple Syrup
- 1 oz Lemon Juice
- Splash Coke
