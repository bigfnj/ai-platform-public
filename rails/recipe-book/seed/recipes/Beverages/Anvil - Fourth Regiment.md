# Anvil - Fourth Regiment

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz rye whiskey
- 1.5 oz dolin rouge vermouth
- 2 dashes celery bitters
- 1 dash peychaud's bitters
- 1 dash orange bitters

## Instructions
1. Stir.
2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
3. Serve with no garnish.
