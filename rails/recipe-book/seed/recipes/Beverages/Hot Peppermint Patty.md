# Hot Peppermint Patty

*Hot cocktail*

## Ingredients
- 1.25 oz Dark Creme de Cacao
- 1.25 oz Peppermint Schnapps
- 1.25 oz Creme de Menthe
- Hot chocolate
- Whipped cream
- Chocolate and mint, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Add the creme de cacao, peppermint schnapps, and creme de menthe to a warm mug.
2. Top with hot chocolate.
3. Finish with whipped cream; garnish with chocolate and mint.

---
*Source: The Best 5 Hot Drinks to Warm Your Toes (Stonehurst Manor / Michael Rego)*
