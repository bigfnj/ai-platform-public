# Royal Peach Tea

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Crown Royal Peach Whiskey
- 6 oz Iced Tea
- 1 Lemon Slice
- crushed ice

## Instructions
1. Fill tall glass with crushed ice.
2. Add Crown Royal Peach Whiskey and Iced Tea.
3. Stir gently.
4. Garnish with Lemon Slice.
