# Lemon Drop Martini

## Ingredients
- 2 oz Tito's Vodka
- 2 oz Deep Eddy Lemon Vodka
- 1 oz Agave Syrup
- sugar for rimming
- lemon slice for garnish
- ice cubes
- orange bitters

## Instructions
1. Rim martini glass with sugar.
2. Garnish glass with a lemon circle.
3. Shake hard with ice.
4. Pour into glass.
5. Add 3 dashes of orange bitters.
