# Limoncello recipe

## Ingredients
- One bottle (750 ml) Everclear
- One bottle (750 ml) vodka
- Zest from 20 organic lemons
- Four cups sugar
- Four cups water

## Instructions
1. Wash the lemons in hot water.
2. Zest the lemons.
3. Put the zest in a large glass container with the vodka and everclear.
4. Swirl the lemon peel and alcohol mixture together daily in the jar for 2 weeks to 4 months.
5. Strain the mixture to remove zest.
6. Mix the sugar and water. Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally. Let boil for at least seven minutes.
7. Let syrup cool to room temperature, then combine with lemon-alcohol mixture.
8. Bottle the limoncello and let it "marry" together for a week before consuming.
