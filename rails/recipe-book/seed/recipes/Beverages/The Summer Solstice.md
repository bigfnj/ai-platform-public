# The Summer Solstice

## Ingredients
- ½ lime muddled
- 2 oz Hendricks Solstice Gin
- 1 cherry
- 2 bar spoons cherry juice
- 3 cubes of ice
- White Claw hard seltzer to top off
- Lime Juice for rim

## Instructions
1. Muddle lime.
2. Add gin, cherry and cherry juice.
3. Top with White Claw.
4. Rim glass with lime juice.

## Shopping List
- ½ lime
- Hendricks Solstice Gin (2 oz)
- 1 cherry
- Cherry Juice (2 bar spoons)
- Ice cubes (3)
- White Claw hard seltzer
