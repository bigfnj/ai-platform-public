# Sunrise Margarita Recipe

*5 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 2 teaspoons Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 1 lime wheel
- Ice
- 1 1/2 ounces silver or blanco tequila
- 1 1/2 ounces 100% pineapple juice
- 1/2 ounce freshly squeezed lime juice (1/2 medium lime)
- 1/2 ounce Simple Syrup
- 1/2 ounce Campari
- 1 pineapple wedge, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions
1. Rim a glass with salt using the lime wheel.
2. Fill the glass with ice.
3. Add tequila, pineapple juice, lime juice, simple syrup and Campari to a cocktail shaker filled with ice.
4. Shake well and strain into the prepared glass.
5. Garnish with a pineapple wedge if desired.
