# Sangria

## Ingredients
- ½ cup Brandy
- 1 cup Pomegranate Juice
- ½ cup simple syrup
- 1 cup orange juice
- 1 bottle red wine (rioja)
- 1 cup club soda
- Sliced fruit (orange, apple, lime)

## Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients, serve over ice
