# Espresso Martini

*Serves 3*

## Ingredients
- 6 oz of Vodka
- 2 oz of Coffee Liqueur
- 3 oz of Espresso or Cold-Brew
- 1 oz Vanilla Maple Syrup

## Instructions
1. Ensure Coffee or Espresso is room temperature
2. Fill Shaker with Ice
3. Add all ingredients
4. Shake
5. Pour into Martini Glass
6. Add 2-3 Chocolate covered coffee beans
7. Serve
