# Sunday Sunshine

## Ingredients
- ½ lime
- 4 oz pineapple/orange/banana juice
- 1 oz triple sec
- 2 oz vodka
- black cherry spritz, to top
- cherry, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a glass filled with ice.
3. Top with Black Cherry Spritz.
4. Garnish with a cherry.
