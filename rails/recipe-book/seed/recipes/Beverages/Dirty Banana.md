# Dirty Banana

*Serves: 1*

## Ingredients
- 1 oz rum-based coffee liqueur (Creme de Cacao)
- 1 oz rum cream
- 2 oz milk
- ½ oz simple sugar syrup (equal amounts of water and sugar, dissolved)
- ½ banana
- 5-8 ice cubes

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in blender and blend.
