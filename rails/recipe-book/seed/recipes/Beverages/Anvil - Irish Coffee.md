# Anvil - Irish Coffee

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Redbreast 12 Year
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2.5 oz coffee
- 1.5 oz heavy cream
- 8 dashes Benedictine/Turbinado blend
- grated nutmeg/pumpkin spice

## Instructions
1. Heat glass.
2. Shake cream.
3. Build in glass & top with cream.
4. Garnish.
