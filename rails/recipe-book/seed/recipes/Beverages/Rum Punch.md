# Rum Punch

## Ingredients
- 2 oz dark rum
- 1/2 fresh lime
- 1/2 oz Maraschino Liquor
- 1/2 oz Agave
- Dash Angostura bitters
- Dash ground nutmeg
- Ice cubes and lime slices

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake well and strain into a glass filled with ice.
3. Garnish with a lime slice.
