# Thai Iced Tea

## Ingredients
- 2 cups Thai tea leaves (for 6 glasses)
- 5 cups water (for 6 glasses)
- 1 1/2 cups sugar (for 6 glasses)
- 4 1/2 cups evaporated milk (NOT sweetened condensed) (for 6 glasses)

OR

- 4 cups Thai tea leaves (for 12 glasses)
- 10 cups water (for 12 glasses)
- 3 cups sugar (for 12 glasses)
- 9 cups evaporated milk (NOT sweetened condensed) (for 12 glasses)

OR

- 1/3 cup Thai tea leaves (for 1 glass)
- 1 1/6 cups water (for 1 glass)
- 1/4 cup sugar (for 1 glass)
- 3/4 cup evaporated milk (NOT sweetened condensed) (for 1 glass)

## Instructions
1. Bring your water to a boil in a pot with enough room for the tea mix.
2. Remove it from the heat, let the boiling subside, and immediately add the Thai tea leaves.
3. If you’re using a tea sock, measure out the leaves into the sock beforehand and dunk it into the pot. Swish it around and mix it with a spoon while it’s in the water.
4. Set your timer and let it steep for about 4 minutes.
5. When your timer goes off, if you’re using a tea sock, remove the sock from the pot and squeeze the sock to get all of the liquid out. Pour the tea concentrate into your pitcher.
6. If you dumped the tea into the pot, pour out the tea mixture and strain through your muslin or cheesecloth-lined sieve into your heat resistant pitcher. Squeeze out the lump of leaves left in the cloth.
7. Add your sugar (or alternate sweetener) to the pitcher and whisk/stir briskly to dissolve.
8. Put the pitcher in your refrigerator for a few hours to let it chill.
9. When you’re ready to serve your tea, fill a glass with some ice and pour the Thai tea concentrate over it. Leave about 1/4 – 1/3 space in the glass for the milk.
10. Pour in your choice of milk slowly to preserve the two distinct layers. Serve with a straw.
