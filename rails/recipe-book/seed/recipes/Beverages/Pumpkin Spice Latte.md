# Pumpkin Spice Latte

## Classic
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 1/2 cups superfine sugar
- 4 cinnamon sticks
- 1/2 tsp ground cloves
- 1/2 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp ground nutmeg
- 2 tablespoons pumpkin puree
- milk
- espresso

## Iced
- ice cubes
- milk
- espresso
