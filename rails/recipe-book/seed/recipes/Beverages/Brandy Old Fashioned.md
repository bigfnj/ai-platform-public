# Brandy Old Fashioned

## Ingredients
- 1 sugar cube OR 1 tsp 2:1 simple syrup
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- 1 orange wedge
- 1 cherry (preferably Amarena or Maraska)
- 2 oz brandy OR Cognac

## Instructions
1. In a chilled old fashioned glass, muddle the sugar, bitters, orange wedge and cherry into a thick paste, careful not to work the orange peel.
2. Add brandy or Cognac, stir, and fill glass with crushed ice and serve.
