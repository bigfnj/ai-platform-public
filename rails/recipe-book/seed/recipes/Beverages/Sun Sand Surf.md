# Sun Sand Surf

## Ingredients
- 3.0 oz raspberry vodka
- 1.0 oz sweet and sour mix
- 0.75 oz triple sec
- 1.0 oz blue curacao
- 0.25 oz caramel vodka or butter schnapps

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake well and strain into a chilled martini glass.
3. Garnish with a cherry or orange slice if desired.
