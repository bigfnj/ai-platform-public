# Imbibe Summer Daiquiris

## Strawberry Daiquiri
- 1½ oz. white rum
- ¾ oz. simple syrup (1:1)
- ½ oz. manzanilla sherry
- ½ oz. fresh lime juice
- 2 fresh strawberries
- Pinch each of salt and pepper
- *Method:* Muddle strawberries, shake with ice, double strain into a chilled glass.

## Cardamom Daiquiri
- 2 oz. white rum
- ½ oz. fresh lime juice
- ½ oz. green Chartreuse
- ½ oz. vanilla syrup
- ¼ oz. rhum agricole blanc
- 3 dashes cardamom tincture
- *Method:* Shake with ice and strain into a glass filled with crushed ice.

## Daiquiri de Plata
- 2 oz. aged rum
- ¾ oz. fresh lime juice
- ½ oz. passion fruit liqueur
- ¼ oz. rich simple syrup (2:1)
- 4 dashes yellow Chartreuse
- *Method:* Shake with ice and double strain into a chilled coupe.

## Daiquiri Menta
- 2 oz. white rum
- ¾ oz. fresh lime juice
- 2 tsp. granulated sugar, or to taste
- 1 tsp. white créme de menthe
- 4-5 leaves of fresh mint
- 10 oz. crushed ice
- *Method:* Blend all ingredients except rum, gradually add rum and pour into a chilled glass.

## Roho Daiquiri
- 2 oz. aged rum
- 1 oz. fresh lime juice
- ¾ oz. brown sugar syrup (1:1)
- ½ oz. medium-bodied red wine
- *Method:* Shake first three ingredients with ice, strain into a glass with a large ice cube and carefully pour the wine atop to create layers.

## Carrot Daiquiri
- 1¾ oz. white rum
- 1 oz. carrot juice
- ¾ oz. falernum
- ½ oz. fresh lime juice
- ¼ oz. rich simple syrup (2:1), or to taste
- One cup crushed ice
- *Method:* Blend all ingredients with crushed ice until creamy and pour into a glass.

## Three Dots Daiquiri
- 2 oz. rhum agricole blanc (100 proof recommended)
- 1 oz. fresh lime juice
- ¾ oz. starfruit syrup
- ½ oz. pineapple juice
- *Method:* Shake with ice and double strain into a chilled glass.

## Mulata Daiquiri
- 1½ oz. aged rum
- ¾ oz. fresh lime juice
- ½ oz. crème de cacao
- ½ oz. rich demerara syrup (2:1)
- *Method:* Shake with ice and strain into a chilled glass.
