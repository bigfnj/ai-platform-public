# Amaretto Sour
*Makes 1 awesome drink.*

## Ingredients
- 1½ oz amaretto (Lazzaroni or DiSaronno)
- ¾ oz cask-proof bourbon (Booker’s, from the Jim Beam distillery)
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 1 tsp. 2:1 simple syrup
- ½ oz egg white, beaten

## Instructions
1. Dry shake ingredients to combine.
2. Shake well with cracked ice.
3. Strain over fresh ice in an old fashioned glass.
4. Garnish with lemon peel and brandied cherries, if desired.
5. Serve.
