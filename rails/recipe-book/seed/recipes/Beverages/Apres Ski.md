# Apres Ski

*Hot cocktail*

## Ingredients
- 2 oz French Brandy
- 2 oz Jim Beam Red Stag Honey Tea Bourbon
- Warm apple cider
- Cinnamon stick, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine the brandy and bourbon in a warm mug.
2. Top with warm apple cider.
3. Garnish with a cinnamon stick.

---
*Source: The Best 5 Hot Drinks to Warm Your Toes (Stonehurst Manor / Michael Rego)*
