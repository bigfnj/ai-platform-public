# Pumpkin Spice Bourbon

## Ingredients
- 2 oz pumpkin puree
- 2 oz maple syrup
- 3 oz bourbon
- 1 oz grand marnier
- 2 dashes bitters

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake well and strain into a glass filled with ice.
