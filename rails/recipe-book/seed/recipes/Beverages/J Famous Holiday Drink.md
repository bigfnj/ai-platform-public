# J Famous Holiday Drink

## Ingredients
- 4 oz Apple Juice
- 3 oz Vanilla Vodka
- 2 oz Carmel Apple Vodka
- 1 oz Butter Schnapps
- 2 oz Fireball
- Garnish with Nutmeg and Cinnamon

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker.
2. Shake well and strain into a glass filled with ice.
3. Garnish with nutmeg and cinnamon.

## Shopping List
- Apple Juice, 4 oz
- Vanilla Vodka, 3 oz
- Carmel Apple Vodka, 2 oz
