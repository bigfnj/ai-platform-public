# Miami Vice Drink Smoothie

*Total time: 2 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 1/2 medium banana
- 4 tbsp agave (or honey or maple syrup)
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 cups frozen pineapple chunks
- 4 fl. oz water
- 1 medium orange, peeled & halved
- 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 2 cups frozen strawberries
- 8 fl. oz light coconut milk (canned)

## Instructions
1. To make the Pina Colada portion of the smoothie, add coconut milk, banana, agave, salt and pineapple to jar in order listed and secure lid. Blend on "Smoothie" cycle or on a Medium speed for 50 seconds.
2. Pour into glasses until half-way full.
3. To make the Strawberry Daiquiri portion of the smoothie, add water, orange, lime juice, agave and strawberries to jar in order listed and secure lid. Blend on "Smoothie" cycle or on a Medium speed for 50 seconds.
4. Gently pour on top of Pina Colada to fill glass and enjoy!
