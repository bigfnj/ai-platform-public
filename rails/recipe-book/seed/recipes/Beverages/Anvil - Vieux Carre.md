# Vieux Carre

## Ingredients
- 0.75 oz Cognac
- 0.75 oz Rye
- 0.75 oz Cocchi Torino
- 2 barspoons Benedictine
- 2 dashes Angostura Bitters
- 2 dashes Peychaud's Bitters
- Lemon zest, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Stir and strain into cocktail glass.
2. Garnish with lemon zest.
