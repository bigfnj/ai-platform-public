# Savannah Banana

*Hot cocktail*

## Ingredients
- 1.25 oz Myers Dark Rum
- 1.25 oz Malibu Banana Rum
- 1.25 oz Banana Liqueur
- Coffee
- Whipped cream
- Banana, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Add the rums and banana liqueur to a warm mug.
2. Top with hot coffee.
3. Finish with whipped cream and a banana garnish.

---
*Source: The Best 5 Hot Drinks to Warm Your Toes (Stonehurst Manor / Michael Rego)*
