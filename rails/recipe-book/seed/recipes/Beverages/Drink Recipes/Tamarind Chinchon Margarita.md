# Tamarind Chinchon Margarita

*Makes 1 drink*

## Ingredients
- 2 to 3 tablespoons tajin spiced salt*
- 1 wedge orange
- 2 ounces tequila reposado
- 1/2 ounce Chinchon Spanish anis sweet liqueur
- 2 ounces tamarind puree (see recipe below)
- 1 ounce freshly squeezed orange juice
- Ice for the cocktail shaker

## Instructions
1. Cover a small plate with tajin to a depth of 1/8 inch. Run an orange wedge around the rim of a martini glass and dip glass in tajin to coat rim. Set aside.
2. Combine tequila, Chinchon, tamarind puree and orange juice in a cocktail shaker filled with ice. Shake vigorously until thoroughly chilled and strain into martini glass. Serve immediately.
