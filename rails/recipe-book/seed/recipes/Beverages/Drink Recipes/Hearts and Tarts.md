# Hearts and Tarts

*Serves 1*

## Ingredients
- 1 jalapeno slice
- 1½ ounces El Silencio mezcal
- ¾ ounce passionfruit syrup
- ¾ ounce orange curaçao
- ¾ ounce fresh lime juice

## Instructions
1. In a mixing glass, with a muddler ,press a jalapeno slice.
2. Add rest of ingredients, add ice and shake. Strain over fresh ice into a rocks glass. Garnish with a lime wedge.

## Shopping List
- 1½ ounces El Silencio mezcal
- ¾ ounce passionfruit syrup
- ¾ ounce orange curaçao
- 1 jalapeno
- 1 lime
