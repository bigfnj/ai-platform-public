# Yellow Bird

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Malibu coconut rum
- 2 oz white rum
- .5 oz pineapple juice
- .5 oz orange juice
- 1 dash sprite
- creme de banana, to top
- cherry, for garnish
- lime, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a glass filled with ice.
3. Top with creme de banana.
4. Garnish with a cherry and lime.
