# The Blitzen 52

*Hot cocktail*

## Ingredients
- 1.25 oz Kahlua
- 1.25 oz Bailey's Irish Cream
- 1.25 oz Grand Marnier
- Coffee
- Whipped cream

## Instructions
1. Add the Kahlua, Bailey's Irish Cream, and Grand Marnier to a warm mug.
2. Top with hot coffee.
3. Finish with whipped cream.

---
*Source: The Best 5 Hot Drinks to Warm Your Toes (Stonehurst Manor / Michael Rego)*
