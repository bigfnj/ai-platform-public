# Poison Apple

## Ingredients
- 4 oz Crown Royal Apple Whiskey
- 2 oz Cranberry Juice (unsweetened)
- 1 oz Sour Apple Pucker
- 1 oz Fireball Whiskey
- 1 oz Grenadine
- 2 dashes Angostura Bitters

## Instructions
1. Shake/Stir, garnish red apple round and serve.
