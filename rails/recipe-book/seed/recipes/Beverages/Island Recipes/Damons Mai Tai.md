# Damon’s Mai Tai

*Serves two.*

## Ingredients
- 2 ounces light rum
- 2 ounces dark rum
- 2 ounces unsweetened pineapple juice
- 2 ounces orange juice
- 1 ounce fresh lime juice
- 1 ounce orgeat syrup
- ¼ ounce orange curaçao

## Instructions
1. Shake in cocktail shaker full of crushed ice.
2. Pour into two double old-fashioned glasses.
3. Garnish each glass with lime wedge and pineapple chunk speared to cocktail cherry.
