# Barrel o-Rum

## Ingredients
- 2 ounces white Puerto Rican rum
- 2 ounces dark Jamaican rum
- 2 ounces fresh lime juice
- 2 ounces orange juice
- 2 ounces grapefruit juice
- 2 ounces Trader Vic passion fruit syrup
- 1 teaspoon honey
- splash soda water
- 6 dashes Angostura bitters

## Instructions
1. Heat honey until liquid, then combine with all other ingredients in a cocktail shaker.
2. Shake with plenty of ice cubes and pour into ceramic rum barrel or large snifter.
