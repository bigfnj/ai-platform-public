# Havana Banana

## Ingredients
- 3/4 ounce light rum
- 3/4 ounce crème de banana
- 4 ounces bottled pineapple-coconut juice (any brand)
- ice cubes
- cocktail cherry
- orange slice

## Instructions
1. Shake well with ice cubes and pour into tall glass.
2. Garnish with cocktail cherry speared to orange slice.
