# Rangoon Gimlet

*Serves four.*

## Ingredients
- 1 ounce fresh lime juice
- 2 ounces rock candy syrup
- 3 ounces vodka
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- 2 cups crushed ice
- lime slice, for garnish
- green cocktail cherry, for garnish

## Instructions
1. Blend with 2 cups crushed ice on high speed for at least 20 seconds.
2. Pour into cocktail glasses.
3. Garnish each glass with lime slice and green cocktail cherry.

## Shopping List
- fresh limes
- sugar (for rock candy syrup)
- vodka
- Angostura bitters
- ice
