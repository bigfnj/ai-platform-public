# Trader Vic Daiquiri

*Serves 6 to 8.*

## Ingredients
- 3 ounces fresh lime juice
- 2¼ ounces grapefruit juice
- 3 teaspoons rock candy syrup
- 1½ ounces maraschino liqueur
- 6 ounces white Puerto Rican rum

## Instructions
1. Pour all ingredients into blender (5 cup capacity).
2. Fill blender to top with ice cubes.
3. Blend until frappéd.
4. Pour into cocktail glasses or saucer champagne glasses.

## Shopping List
- fresh limes
- grapefruits
- rock candy syrup
- maraschino liqueur
- white Puerto Rican rum
