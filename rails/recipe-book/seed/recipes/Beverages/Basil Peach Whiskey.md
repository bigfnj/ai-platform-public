# Basil Peach Whiskey

## Ingredients
- 2 peaches, pitted and quartered
- 3 sprigs fresh basil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3 oz whiskey
- ice
- soda water
- peach slices for garnish
- small basil sprig for garnish

## Instructions
1. In shaker, add peach quarters, basil sprig and sugar.
2. Muddle lightly to break down the peach. Don't crush the basil too much.
3. Add whiskey and fill with ice.
4. Shake until frosty on outside.
5. Fill low ball glass with crushed ice and pour half contents of shaker into each.
6. Top with splash soda water and garnish with slice of peach and small sprig basil.

## Shopping List

- fresh basil
- sugar
