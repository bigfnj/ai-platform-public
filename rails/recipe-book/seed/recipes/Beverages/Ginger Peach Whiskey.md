# Ginger Peach Whiskey

## Ingredients
- 1 oz whiskey
- 2 oz peach nectar
- 4 oz ginger ale

## Instructions
1. Fill glass with ice.
2. Add whiskey and peach nectar.
3. Pour ginger ale into the glass to fill.
