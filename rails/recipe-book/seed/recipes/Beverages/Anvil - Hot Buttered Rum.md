# Hot Buttered Rum

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz FDC Gold rum
- .75 oz Lustau East India Solera Sherry
- .75 oz Turbinado Syrup (or .5 oz Caramel Liquor)
- Half Broken Cinnamon Stick
- .25 oz Plantation Overproof Rum
- 2 Walnuts
- 1 Pad Butter

## Instructions
1. Ignite rum with walnuts and butter in toddy glass to heat the glass.
2. Meanwhile build remaining ingredients in OXO Jigger, Pour into glass to extinguish flames.
3. Break cinnamon stick in half, stir drink while adding hot water and drop stick in.
