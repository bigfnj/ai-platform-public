# Champagne Party Punch

## Ingredients
- 2 750 ml bottles of champagne, chilled
- 1 750 ml bottle of Moscato, chilled
- 4 cups sparkling water, chilled
- 1 ½ cups Cognac
- Kumquats (optional)

## Instructions
1. In Punch Bowl stir together Champagne, Moscato, Sparkling Water and Cognac.
