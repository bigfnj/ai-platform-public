# Bourbon Coffee Old Fashioned

## Ingredients
- 2 oz Bourbon
- 1/4 oz St. George NOLA Coffee Liquor
- 1/4 oz Brown Sugar Syrup
- dash of chocolate bitters

## Instructions
1. To make syrup: Small saucepan, combine equal parts water and brown sugar. Warm on low heat and gently stir until sugar is dissolved.
2. When syrup has cooled: Combine bourbon, coffee liquor, syrup, and chocolate bitters in a mixing glass.
3. Add ice and stir to chill, then strain into rocks glass over ice.

## Shopping List
- 2 oz Bourbon
- 1/4 oz St. George NOLA Coffee Liquor
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
