# Botanist

*Makes two.*

## Ingredients
- 3 oz St. George Botanivore Gin
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1 oz St-Germain elderflower liqueur
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- 2-4 slices cucumber, depending on size (sliced in half)
- 2-4 mint leaves (torn)

## Instructions
1. Shake over ice.
2. Strain over ice cubes.
3. Garnish with lemon and mint.
