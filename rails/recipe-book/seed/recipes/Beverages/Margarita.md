# Margarita

## Ingredients
- 1 lime, muddled
- 2 oz tequila (Anejo or silver)
- 1/2 oz triple sec (30%)
- 1/2 oz agave nectar

## Instructions
1. Muddle the lime.
2. Add tequila, triple sec and agave nectar.
