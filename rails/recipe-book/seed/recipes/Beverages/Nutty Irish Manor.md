# Nutty Irish Manor

*Hot cocktail*

## Ingredients
- 2 oz Frangelico
- 2 oz Bailey's Irish Cream
- Coffee
- Whipped cream

## Instructions
1. Add the Frangelico and Bailey's Irish Cream to a warm mug.
2. Top with hot coffee.
3. Finish with whipped cream.

---
*Source: The Best 5 Hot Drinks to Warm Your Toes (Stonehurst Manor / Michael Rego)*
