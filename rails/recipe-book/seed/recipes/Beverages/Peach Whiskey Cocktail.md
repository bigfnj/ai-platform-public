# Peach Whiskey Cocktail

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Peach Whiskey
- 1 tsp monk fruit sweetener
- 1/8 tsp peach bitters
- 1/4 cup peach slices
- 1 12 oz can no-calorie peach-flavored seltzer - chilled

## Instructions
1. In cocktail glass, mix peach whiskey, monk fruit, and bitters with a spoon.
2. Place frozen peaches in glass, enough to loosely fill glass.
3. Slowly pour sparkling water over peaches until glass is full. Stir and serve.
