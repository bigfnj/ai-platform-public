# Pumpkin Syrup

## Ingredients
- 3 cups water
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- 6 tablespoons pumpkin puree
- 1 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon ground cloves
- ½ teaspoon ground ginger

## Instructions
1. Blend all in blender.
