# Hot Cranberry Punch

## Ingredients
- 6 cups water
- 4 cups fresh or frozen cranberries
- 1/3 to ½ cup sugar
- 2 cinnamon sticks (2 inches each)
- 10 whole cloves
- 2 black tea bags
- 1 lemon (½ tsp. zest and 1 TBS juice)
- ½ tsp. orange zest
- ½ cup rum

## Instructions
1. In a large saucepan, bring water to boil.
2. Add cranberries. Boil gently for 3-4 minutes or until skins burst.
3. Pour mixture through fine mesh sieve, pressing lightly on cranberries; discard solids.
4. Return strained liquid to saucepan.
5. Stir in sugar, cinnamon sticks, and cloves.
6. Bring to boiling, stirring to dissolve sugar.
7. Reduce heat. Simmer, uncovered, for 5 minutes.
8. Remove from heat.
9. Add tea bags, lemon zest and juice and orange zest.
10. Let stand for 3 minutes; discard tea bags.
11. Stir in rum.

## Shopping List
- 6 cups water
- 4 cups fresh or frozen cranberries
- ½ cup sugar (plus more to taste)
- 2 cinnamon sticks (2 inches each)
- 10 whole cloves
- 2 black tea bags
- 1 lemon

- ½ cup rum
