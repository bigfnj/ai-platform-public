# Twin Peaks

*Makes two.*

## Ingredients
- 3.5 oz St. George Terroir Gin
- 0.5 oz green Chartreuse
- 1.5 oz simple syrup
- 1.5 oz lime juice
- ice cubes
- fresh sage for garnish

## Instructions
1. Shake over ice, strain over single large ice cube.
2. Garnish with fresh sage.
