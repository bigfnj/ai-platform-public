# The Clover Club Cocktail

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz gin
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice
- 3/4 oz raspberry syrup (see recipe below)
- 1/2 oz fresh egg white

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker and dry shake for at least 10 seconds.
2. Add ice, shake to chill and dilute.
3. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
4. Traditionally the Clover Club has no garnish, but if you're feeling fancy, a delicate lemon twist is an aromatically stupendous addition.

## Shopping List
- 1.5 oz gin
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice
- 3/4 oz raspberry syrup (see recipe below)
- 1/2 oz fresh egg white
- 2 cups fresh or frozen raspberries
- 8 oz water
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- superfine sugar or simple syrup
- raspberry preserves
- lemon twist
