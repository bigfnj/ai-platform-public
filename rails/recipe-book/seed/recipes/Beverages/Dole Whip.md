# Dole Whip

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Malibu Coconut Rum
- 1.5 oz Vanilla Vodka
- 1.5 oz Pineapple Juice

## Instructions
1. Add all of the ingredients to a shaker.
2. Shake with ice for 8-10 seconds.
3. Strain into an ice-filled rocks glass.
