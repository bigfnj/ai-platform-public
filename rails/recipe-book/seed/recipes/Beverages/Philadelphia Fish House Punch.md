# Philadelphia Fish House Punch

*Makes approximately 5½ quarts.*

## Ingredients
- 1 pound superfine sugar
- 12 lemons, peeled
- 16 ounces Appleton V/X rum
- 8 ounces Smith and Cross rum
- 12 ounces Hennessy VS cognac
- 3 ounces Briotette crème de pêche
- 9 ounces Laird’s applejack
- 16 ounces lemon juice, finely strained
- 6 pints cold water

## Instructions
1. Place sugar and lemon peels into a vacuum-seal bag and seal according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
2. Let rest at room temperature for four hours, or overnight, until the sugar is soaked in lemon oil.
3. When punch is ready to assemble, pour contents into an eight-quart container and add:
   - 16 ounces Appleton V/X rum
   - 8 ounces Smith and Cross rum
   - 12 ounces Hennessy VS cognac
   - 3 ounces Briotette crème de pêche
   - 9 ounces Laird’s applejack
   - 16 ounces lemon juice, finely strained
   - 6 pints cold water
