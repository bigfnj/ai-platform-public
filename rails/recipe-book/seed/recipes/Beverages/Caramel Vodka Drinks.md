# Caramel Vodka Drinks

## Kissed Caramel Appletini
- 2.0 oz Smirnoff Kissed Caramel vodka
- 1.5 oz apple juice
- .25 oz lemon juice
- .25 oz simple syrup
- 1 tsp sugar (for simple syrup)
- 1 tbsp water (for simple syrup)
- *Method:* Shake all ingredients and strain into martini glass, garnish with thin apple slices and a caramel rim.

## Creamy Caramel Martini
- 1.5 oz Smirnoff Kissed Caramel vodka
- .5 oz butterscotch liquor
- *Method:* Shake all ingredients with ice, pour into a martini glass, drizzle with caramel before pouring.

## Caramel Candy Apple
- 1.0 oz Smirnoff Kissed Caramel vodka
- .75 oz apple juice
- .25 oz cranberry juice
- *Method:* Shake all ingredients and strain into shot glass.
