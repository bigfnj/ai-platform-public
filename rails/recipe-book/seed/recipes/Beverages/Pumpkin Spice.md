# J’s Pumpkin Spice

## Ingredients
- 1 oz pumpkin spice syrup
- 1 oz vanilla vodka
- 1 oz espresso vodka
- 1 oz half n half
- nutmeg
- cinnamon
- whipped cream

## Instructions
1. Combine the pumpkin spice syrup, vanilla vodka, and espresso vodka in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake well and strain into a glass filled with ice.
3. Top with half n half.
4. Sprinkle nutmeg and cinnamon on top.
5. Garnish with whipped cream.
