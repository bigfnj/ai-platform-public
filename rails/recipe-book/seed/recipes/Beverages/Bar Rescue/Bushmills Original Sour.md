# Bushmills Original Sour

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Bushmills Original Irish Whiskey
- 2 Lime Wedges
- 2 Lemon Wedges
- Ginger Ale (to top off)
- Lime wedge garnish

## Instructions
1. Muddle lime and lemon wedges in a tall glass.
2. Add whiskey and top off with ginger ale.
3. Garnish with lime wedge.
