# Captain Mango Chutney Daiquiri

## Ingredients
- 1 1/4 oz Captain Morgan Original Spiced Rum
- 1/4 oz Peach Schnapps
- 1/4 oz Grand Marnier
- Splash Lime Juice
- Sugar, simple syrup or agave (to taste)
- Pineapple and strawberry garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine rum, schnapps, Grand Marnier, and lime juice in a margarita glass.
2. Add sugar, simple syrup, or agave to taste.
3. Stir well.
4. Garnish with pineapple and strawberry.
