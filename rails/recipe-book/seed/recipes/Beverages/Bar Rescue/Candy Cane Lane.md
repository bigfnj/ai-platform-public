# Candy Cane Lane

## Ingredients
- 2½ oz Van Gogh Blue Vodka
- 1 oz White Crème de Menthe
- ½ oz Peppermint Schnapps
- Cream (splash)
- Grenadine (splash)
- Peppermint candy (for garnish)

## Instructions
1. Add a dash of grenadine into the bottom of a chilled martini glass and set aside.
2. Combine vodka, crème de menthe, schnapps, cream, and grenadine in a cocktail shaker with ice.
3. Shake well and strain into the glass with grenadine to create a "swirl" effect.
4. Garnish with peppermint candy.
