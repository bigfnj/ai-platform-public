# Vanilla Caramel Mochaccino

## Ingredients
- 1 can illy issimo mochaccino
- ½ oz Caramel Sauce
- 1 oz Vanilla Infused Vodka

## Instructions
1. Fill cocktail shaker half with ice.
2. Add vodka, caramel sauce and half can of illy issimo mochaccino.
3. Shake vigorously and pour into martini glass straining the ice.
