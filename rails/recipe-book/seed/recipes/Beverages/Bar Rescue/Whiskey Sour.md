# Whiskey Sour

## Ingredients
- 2 oz Whiskey
- Sweet & Sour Mix (to fill)
- Cherry (for garnish)
- Lemon wheel (for garnish)

## Instructions
1. Combine whiskey and sweet & sour mix in a highball glass.
2. Drop cherry into the glass.
3. Garnish with lemon wheel.
