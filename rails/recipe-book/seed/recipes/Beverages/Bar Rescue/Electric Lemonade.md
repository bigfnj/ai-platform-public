# Electric Lemonade

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Light Rum
- 1/2 oz Blue Curacao
- 7up (to fill)
- Sweet & Sour Mix (to fill)

## Instructions
1. Combine rum, blue curacao, 7up, and sweet & sour mix in a highball glass.
