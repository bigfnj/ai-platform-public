# Capt. Morgan's Planters Punch

## Ingredients
- 1.25 oz Captain Morgan
- Planter's Mix (to fill)
- Cherry/orange flag garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine rum and Planter's mix in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into an ice-filled glass.
3. Garnish with cherry/orange flag.
