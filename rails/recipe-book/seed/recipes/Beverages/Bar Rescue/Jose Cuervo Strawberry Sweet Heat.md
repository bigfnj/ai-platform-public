# Jose Cuervo Strawberry Sweet Heat

## Ingredients
- 3/4 oz Jose Cuervo Especial Silver
- 3/4 oz Jose Cuervo Especial Gold
- 1/4 oz Triple Sec
- Strawberry Puree
- Splash Sour Mix
- Splash Lime Juice
- Dash Tabasco Hot Sauce
- Strawberry garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine both types of tequila, triple sec, strawberry puree, sour mix, lime juice, and hot sauce in a margarita glass.
2. Stir well.
3. Garnish with a strawberry.
