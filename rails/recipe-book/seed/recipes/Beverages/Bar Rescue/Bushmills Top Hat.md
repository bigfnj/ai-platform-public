# Bushmills Top Hat

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Black Bush Irish Whiskey
- ½ oz Stirrings Coffee Liqueur
- Coke (to top off)
- Guinness Head (to top off)

## Instructions
1. Combine whiskey and coffee liqueur in a tall glass with ice.
2. Top off with coke and Guinness head.
