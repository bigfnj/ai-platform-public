# Black Spiced and Peachy

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Captain Morgan Black Spiced Rum
- 1/2 oz Peach Schnapps
- Splash Cranberry Juice
- Splash Pineapple Juice
- Splash Orange Juice
- Grenadine floater
- Sugar for rim
- Strawberry garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine rum and schnapps in a cocktail glass.
2. Add splashes of cranberry, pineapple, and orange juices.
3. Float grenadine on top.
4. Rim the glass with sugar.
5. Garnish with a strawberry.
