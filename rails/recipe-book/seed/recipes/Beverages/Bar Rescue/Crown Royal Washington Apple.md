# Crown Royal Washington Apple

## Ingredients
- 1.25 oz Crown Royal
- .5oz Apple Schnapps
- 3 oz Cranberry Juice
- Apple slice garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine whiskey, schnapps, and cranberry juice in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into an ice-filled glass.
3. Garnish with apple slice.
