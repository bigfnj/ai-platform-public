# Cadillac Margarita

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz Top Shelf Tequila
- Sweet & Sour Mix (to fill)
- 1/2 oz Grand Marnier Float

## Instructions
1. Combine tequila and sweet & sour mix in a tall glass.
2. Float Grand Marnier on top.
