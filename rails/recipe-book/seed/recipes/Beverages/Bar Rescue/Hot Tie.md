# Hot Tie

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Don Julio Tequila infused with Jalepeno
- 3 oz Fresh Watermelon Juice
- 3/4 oz Fresh Lime Juice
- 1 oz Simple Syrup
- Lime wedge garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine tequila, watermelon juice, lime juice, and simple syrup in a highball glass.
2. Stir well.
3. Garnish with lime wedge.
