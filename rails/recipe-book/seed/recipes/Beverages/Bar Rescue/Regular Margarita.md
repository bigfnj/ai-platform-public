# Regular Margarita

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz Tequila
- 1/2 oz Triple Sec
- Sweet & Sour Mix (to fill)
- Splash Orange Juice
- Salt for rim

## Instructions
1. Combine tequila, triple sec, sweet & sour mix, and orange juice in a tall glass.
2. Rim the glass with salt.
