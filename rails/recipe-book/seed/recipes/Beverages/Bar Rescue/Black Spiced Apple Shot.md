# Black Spiced Apple Shot

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Captain Morgan Black Spiced Rum
- 1/2 oz Apple Liqueur
- Splash Sour Mix
- Splash Pineapple Juice
- Sugar for rim

## Instructions
1. Combine rum, apple liqueur, sour mix, and pineapple juice in a shot glass.
2. Rim the glass with sugar.
