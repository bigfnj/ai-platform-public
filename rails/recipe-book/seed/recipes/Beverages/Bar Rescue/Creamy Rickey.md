# Creamy Rickey

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Smirnoff Whipped Cream Vodka
- 1 oz Fresh Lime Juice
- Soda (to top off)
- Lime garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine vodka and lime juice in an old fashioned glass.
2. Top off with soda.
3. Garnish with lime.
