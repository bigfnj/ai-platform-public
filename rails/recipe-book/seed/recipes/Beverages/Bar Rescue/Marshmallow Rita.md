# Marshmallow Rita

## Ingredients
- 1.25 oz Smirnoff Fluffed Marshmallow Vodka
- .25 Cuervo Gold Tequila
- .75 oz Lime Juice
- .5 oz Triple Sec

## Instructions
1. Combine vodka, tequila, lime juice, and triple sec in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a rocks glass or martini glass.
