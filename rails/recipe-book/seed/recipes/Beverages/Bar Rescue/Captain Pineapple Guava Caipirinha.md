# Captain Pineapple Guava Caipirinha

## Ingredients
- 1 1/4 oz Captain Morgan Original Spiced Rum
- Splash Guava Puree
- Splash Pineapple Juice
- Soda water (to top off)
- Lime wedges
- Sugar
- Pineapple and lime wedge garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine rum, guava puree, pineapple juice, and sugar in a rock glass.
2. Muddle the lime wedges and add them to the glass.
3. Top off with soda water.
4. Garnish with pineapple and lime wedge.
