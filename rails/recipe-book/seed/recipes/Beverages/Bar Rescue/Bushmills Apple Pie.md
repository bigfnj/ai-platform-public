# Bushmills Apple Pie

## Ingredients
- 1 ½ oz Bushmills Irish Honey Whiskey
- Apple Juice
- Sugar Syrup
- Ginger Ale (to top off)
- Apple slice garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine whiskey, apple juice, and sugar syrup in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a tall glass.
3. Top off with ginger ale.
4. Garnish with apple slice.
