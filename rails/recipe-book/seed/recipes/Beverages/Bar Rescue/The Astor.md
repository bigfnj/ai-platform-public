# The Astor

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Ketel Oranje
- 1/2 oz Aperol
- 3/4 oz Grapefruit Juice
- 3/4 oz Lemon Juice
- 1 oz Simple Syrup
- Orange twist garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine vodka, aperol, grapefruit juice, lemon juice, and simple syrup in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a coupe glass.
3. Garnish with orange twist.
