# Bushmills Coffee Martini

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Bushmills Original Irish Whiskey
- ½ oz Baileys Irish Cream
- Dash Sugar Syrup
- 1 shot Espresso
- Coffee beans (for garnish)

## Instructions
1. Combine whiskey, Baileys, sugar syrup, and espresso in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a martini glass.
3. Garnish with three coffee beans.
