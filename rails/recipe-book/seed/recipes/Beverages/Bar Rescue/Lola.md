# Lola

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Ciroc
- 3/4 oz Pom Juice
- 1/4 oz St.Germain
- 1 oz Champagne
- Lemon twist garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine vodka, pom juice, and St-Germain in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a chilled glass.
3. Top off with champagne.
4. Garnish with lemon twist.
