# Bushmills Orange Flair

## Ingredients
- ½ oz Black Bush Irish Whiskey
- ½ oz Grand Marnier
- ½ oz Stirrings Coffee Liqueur
- 1 teaspoon Orange Marmalade
- Orange peel garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine whiskey, Grand Marnier, coffee liqueur, and marmalade in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a martini glass.
3. Garnish with orange peel.
