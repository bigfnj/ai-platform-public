# Berries and Cream

## Ingredients
- 1.25 oz Smirnoff Whipped Cream Vodka
- .25 oz Smirnoff Strawberry Vodka
- .75 oz Lemon Juice
- .75 oz Simple Syrup
- Strawberry garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine vodkas, lemon juice, and simple syrup in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a cocktail glass.
3. Garnish with strawberry.
