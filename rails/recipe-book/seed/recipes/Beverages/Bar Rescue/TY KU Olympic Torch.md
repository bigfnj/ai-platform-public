# TY KU Olympic Torch

## Ingredients
- 2 oz TY KU Coconut Nigori Sake
- 1 oz Kahlua
- 1 ½ oz Cream
- 1 oz Bacardi 151 Rum

## Instructions
1. Shake and strain into a martini glass.
2. Over bar spoon, pour cream to float on top (approximately 1 ½ oz).
3. Over cream float Bacardi 151 (approximately 1 oz).
4. Light Bacardi 151 with a match.
5. As flame burns sprinkle some cinnamon onto the flame.
6. Clap hands over flame to put it out.
7. Give stir and serve.

## Shopping List
