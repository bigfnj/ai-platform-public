# The Mile High

## Ingredients
- 2 oz Bulleit Rye
- 1 oz Carpano Antica Vermouth
- 5 dashes Fee Brothers Old Fashion Bitters
- Luxardo Gourmet Maraschino Cherries (for garnish)

## Instructions
1. Combine rye, vermouth, and bitters in a coupe glass.
2. Stir well.
3. Garnish with three cherries on a cocktail pick.
