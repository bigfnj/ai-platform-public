# Captain Morgan Black and Honey

## Ingredients
- 1 1/4 oz Captain Morgan Black Spiced Rum
- 3/4 oz Fresh Lime Juice
- 1/2 oz Honey
- Lemon wedge garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine rum, lime juice, and honey in a rock glass.
2. Stir well to dissolve the honey.
3. Garnish with a lemon wedge.
