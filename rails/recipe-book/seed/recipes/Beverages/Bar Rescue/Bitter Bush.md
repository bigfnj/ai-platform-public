# Bitter Bush

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Bushmills Original Irish Whiskey
- 2 Lime Wedges
- Dash Bitters
- Guinness Beer (to top off)

## Instructions
1. Combine whiskey, lime wedges, and bitters in a tall glass.
2. Top off with Guinness beer.
