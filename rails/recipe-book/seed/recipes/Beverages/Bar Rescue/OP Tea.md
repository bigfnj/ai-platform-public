# OP Tea

## Ingredients
- 1/2 oz Tequila
- 1/2 oz Gin
- 1/2 oz Light Rum
- 1/2 oz Vodka
- Orange Juice (to fill)
- Sprite or 7up (splash)

## Instructions
1. Combine all liquors and orange juice in a highball glass.
2. Add splash of sprite or 7up.
3. Garnish with orange wheel.
