# Gingerbread Martini

## Ingredients
- 1½ parts RumChata
- ¾ part Captain Morgan Spiced Rum
- ¾ parts Ginger Liqueur
- 1/3 tablespoon Ginger Spice

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in shaker with ice.
2. Shake, strain and enjoy.
