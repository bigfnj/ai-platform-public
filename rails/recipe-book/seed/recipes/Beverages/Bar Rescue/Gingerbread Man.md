# Gingerbread Man

## Ingredients
- 1½ oz Stolichnaya Vanilla Vodka
- 1 oz Cinnamon Stick Infused Vodka
- 1 oz Heavy Cream
- ½ oz Monin Gingerbread Syrup
- Fresh Nutmeg (for garnish)

## Instructions
1. Combine all ingredients in an ice-filled mixing glass.
2. Shake and strain into a martini glass.
3. Finish with one scrape of fresh nutmeg for garnish.
