# Gin Fizz

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz Gin
- 1/2 oz Lemon Juice
- Sugar (to taste)
- Soda (to fill)

## Instructions
1. Combine gin, lemon juice, and sugar in a highball glass.
2. Fill with soda.
