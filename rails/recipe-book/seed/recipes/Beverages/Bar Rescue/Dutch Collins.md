# Dutch Collins

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Ketel One Citreon
- 3/4 oz Fresh Lemon Juice
- 1 oz Simple Syrup
- Club Soda (to top off)
- Cherry and orange flag garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine vodka, lemon juice, and simple syrup in a highball glass.
2. Top off with club soda.
3. Garnish with cherry and orange flag.
