# The Dutchess

## Ingredients
- 1½ oz Genever
- 1 oz Lemon Juice
- ½ oz Simple Syrup
- 1 bar spoon Grand Marnier
- Champagne
- Orange twist (for garnish)

## Instructions
1. Shake everything except the champagne.
2. Fine strain into a chilled flute glass and top it up with champagne.
3. Garnish with an orange twist.
