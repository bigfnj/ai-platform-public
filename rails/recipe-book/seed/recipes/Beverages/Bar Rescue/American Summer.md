# American Summer

## Ingredients
- 1 oz Bushmills Irish Honey Whiskey
- 2 Lemon Wedges
- Chilled Lemonade (to top off)
- Lemon wedge garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine whiskey and lemon wedges in a tall glass.
2. Top off with chilled lemonade.
3. Garnish with lemon wedge.
