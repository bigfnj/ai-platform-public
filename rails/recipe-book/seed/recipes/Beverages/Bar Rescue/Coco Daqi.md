# Coco Daqi

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Ciroc coconut
- ¾ oz Fresh Lime Juice
- 1 oz Simple Syrup

## Instructions
1. Combine vodka, lime juice, and simple syrup in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake and strain into a chilled glass.
