# Dark and Spicy

## Ingredients
- 1.5 oz Captain Morgan Black Spiced Rum
- .5 oz Lime Juice
- Ginger Ale (to fill)
- Lime wheel garnish

## Instructions
1. Combine rum and lime juice in a highball glass.
2. Fill with ginger ale.
3. Garnish with lime wheel.
