# imbibe holiday balmy bright

## Dead to Port
- 1 oz. bourbon
- 1 oz. overproof Jamaican rum (Smith & Cross)
- ¾ oz. spiced cranberry syrup
- ½ oz. demerara syrup (2:1)
- ½ oz. fresh lime juice
- *Method:* Shake with ice, strain into a rocks glass filled with a single large ice cube and garnish with a marigold.

## Winter Cup
- 2 oz. Pimm’s No. 1
- 1 oz. apple juice
- ¾ oz. Don’s Mix (2:1 grapefruit juice and cinnamon syrup)
- ½ oz. fresh lemon juice
- ½ oz. ginger syrup
- 1 dash Angostura bitters
- chilled soda water
- *Method:* Shake with ice, double strain into a glass filled with fresh ice, top with chilled soda, garnish festively.

## Snowbird
- 1½ oz. mezcal
- 1½ oz. fresh pineapple juice
- 1 oz. coconut cream
- ½ oz. fresh orange juice
- 1 barspoon Cointreau
- *Method:* Blend with ice until smooth, pour into a glass and top with freshly grated nutmeg.

## You Don’t Gnome Me
- 6 oz. blanco tequila
- 4 oz. apple brandy (Laird’s bonded)
- 4 oz. fresh lime juice
- 4 oz. fresh pineapple juice
- 4 oz. guava purée
- 4 oz. orgeat
- 1 oz. cinnamon syrup (1:1)
- *Method:* Combine and shake with crushed ice, pour unstrained into an oversize mug.

## Merry Spritzmas
- ¾ oz. Aperol
- ¾ oz. fresh lemon juice
- ½ oz. tawny port
- ½ oz. Cognac (Ansac VS)
- ½ oz. falernum
- 2 rounded barspoons of fig preserves
- 1 dash cardamom bitters
- 2½ oz. chilled Prosecco
- *Method:* Stir fig preserves and lemon juice, add remaining ingredients and shake with ice, double strain into a glass, stir in Prosecco and add fresh ice cubes.

## Royal Fizz
- 1½ oz. bourbon
- ¾ oz. fresh lemon juice
- ½ oz. amontillado sherry
- ½ oz. pineapple liqueur (Giffard)
- ½ oz. demerara syrup (2:1)
- 2 dashes of Tiki Bitters (or sub ½ tsp. allspice liqueur)
- 1 whole egg, pasteurized if you prefer
- 2 oz. chilled soda water, to top
- *Method:* Shake vigorously with ice, double-strain into a Collins glass filled with a single long ice column, slowly top with chilled soda water.

## Tropical Lion’s Tail
- ¾ oz. bourbon
- ¾ oz. fresh lime juice
- ½ oz. Jamaican rum (Appleton Estate Reserve)
- ½ oz. pineapple syrup
- ¼ oz. overproof Jamaican rum (Smith & Cross)
- ¼ oz. allspice liqueur
- 1 dash Angostura bitters
- *Method:* Shake with ice, strain into a glass filled with a single large ice cube and garnish.
