# Pineapple with Rum Mint Sauce

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves, tightly packed
- 1 teaspoon grated lemon rind
- 1/2 cup white rum (West Indies or Puerto Rican)
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- One large fresh pineapple

## Instructions
1. Combine sugar, water, mint, and lemon rind; bring the sauce to a boil.
2. Simmer for 3 minutes.
3. Strain the sauce; add rum and lemon juice; chill.
4. Cut pineapple into chunks (discarding the hard core) and drizzle sauce over it.
