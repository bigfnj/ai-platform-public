# 14 Carat Carrot Cake

## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 ½ tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 ½ cups oil
- 4 eggs
- 2 cups grated raw carrots
- 1 (8 oz) can crushed pineapple
- ½ cup chopped nuts (walnuts)
- ½ cup butter or margarine
- 1 (8 oz) pkg cream cheese
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 lb powdered sugar

## Instructions
1. Sift together flour, baking powder, soda, salt, and cinnamon.
2. Add sugar, oil, and eggs and mix well.
3. Stir in carrots, drained pineapple, and nuts.
4. Turn into greased pan and bake at 350°F for 35 to 40 minutes until done.
5. Cool a few minutes and turn onto wire rack to cool.

6. Combine butter, cream cheese, and vanilla and beat until well blended.
7. Add sugar and beat until well blended. If too thick add small amount of milk.
