# Éclair Cake

## Ingredients
- 1 (1-pound) box graham crackers
- 2 (3 ¼ -ounce) boxes instant French vanilla pudding mix
- 3 ½ cups milk
- 1 (8 ounce) container frozen whipped topping, thawed
- 1 ½ cups confectioners’ sugar
- ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 3 tablespoons milk
- 3 tablespoons softened butter
- 2 teaspoons light corn syrup
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. Spray the bottom of a 13 X 9 inch pan.
2. Line bottom of the pan with crackers.
3. In a bowl, with a mixer, mix pudding with milk; beat for about 2 minutes.
4. Fold in whipped topping.
5. Pour half the pudding mixture over the cracker.
6. Place another layer of crackers on top of the pudding and pour over the remaining half of pudding mixture.
7. Cover with another layer of cracker.
8. For frosting, blend together sugar and cocoa.
9. Add the butter and milk; mixing well.
10. Add corn syrup and vanilla.
11. Stir until creamy.
12. Cover cake with frosting and refrigerate for 24 hours.
