# Baked Pears with Rum and Cream

## Ingredients
- 4 firm ripe pears, peeled, halved and cored
- ¼ cup unsalted butter
- ¼ cup packed brown sugar
- ¼ cup dark rum
- ½ cup whipping cream

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350.
2. Arrange pears, cut side down, in 10 inch baking dish.
3. In small saucepan, melt butter and stir in brown sugar and rum.
4. Pour over pears, moving pears around to coat.
5. Bake 40 min, basting frequently.
6. Remove from oven and pour cream over pears.
7. Continue baking 15-20 min.
8. Place in individual serving bowls and spoon hot sauce over pears.

## Shopping List
- 4 firm ripe pears
- unsalted butter
- brown sugar
- dark rum
- whipping cream
