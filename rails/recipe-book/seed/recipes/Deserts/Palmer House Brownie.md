# Palmer House Brownie

## Ingredients
- 14 oz semi-sweet chocolate
- 1 lb butter
- 12 oz granulated sugar
- 4 oz flour
- 8 whole eggs
- 12 oz crushed walnuts
- vanilla extract

## Instructions
1. Melt chocolate with butter in a double boiler.
2. Mix dry ingredients into mixing bowl, except walnuts.
3. Mix chocolate with dry ingredients for 4 to 5 minutes.
4. Add egg.
5. Pour into 9” x 12” baking sheet, sprinkle walnuts on top, press walnuts down slightly into mixture with your hand, and bake in a preheated oven at 300°F for 30 to 40 minutes. You will know when done -- the edges will start to become a little crispy and the brownies will raise about ¼ inch.
6. Note: Even when the brownie is properly baked it will test “gooey” with a toothpick in the middle due to the richness of the mixture.
7. After removing from the oven allow to cool about 30 minutes before spreading a thin layer of the glaze on top with a pastry brush.

## Glaze
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup apricot preserves
- 1 teaspoon unflavored gelatin

## Instructions (Glaze)
1. Mix together water, preserves and unflavored gelatin in a saucepan, mix thoroughly and bring to a boil for two minutes.
2. Use hot.
