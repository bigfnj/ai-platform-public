# Cranachan

*Serves 4*

## Ingredients
- Quick-Cooking Oats – ¼ Cup
- Whipping Cream – 1 Cup
- Honey – 2 Tblsp
- Whisky – 2 Tblsp
- Raspberries – 1½ Cups

## Instructions
1. Stirring occasionally, toast oats in a pan over medium heat until they just become aromatic and lightly golden, about 8-10 minutes.
2. Remove from heat and cool.
3. Whip cream to soft peaks.
4. Add honey and whisky, beat until combined.
5. Stir 2 tablespoons of toasted oats into cream.
6. Use a fork to smash half of the raspberries into whipped cream.
7. Break berries up well.
8. Reserve a few berries for garnish, then fold remainder, gently to keep them whole, into cream.
9. Gently spoon mixture into parfait glasses or bowls.
10. Garnish with reserved raspberries and a sprinkle of toasted oats.
