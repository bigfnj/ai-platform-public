# S’more Brownie Bites

## Ingredients
- 1 stick unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 ¼ cups mini chocolate chips
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- ½ cup quinoa flour
- ¾ cup mini marshmallows
- ¼ cup graham cracker crumbs or 4 large crackers, finely crushed

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Place muffin cups in tins.
3. Heat the butter in a small saucepan over low heat.
4. Remove the pan from heat and add ¾ cup of the mini chocolate chips.
5. Stir until the chocolate is melted and mixture is smooth.
6. Transfer the mixture to a medium bowl.
7. Stir in the sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
8. Gradually beat in the flour until the mixture is thick and smooth.
9. Stir in the remaining ½ cup mini chocolate chips.
10. Using a small cookie scoop, fill each paper liner with about 2 heaping tablespoons of batter.
11. Push 3 or so mini marshmallows halfway down into the center of the batter.
12. Sprinkle with the graham cracker crumbs.
13. Bake until the marshmallows are puffed and lightly golden brown, 10-12 minutes.
