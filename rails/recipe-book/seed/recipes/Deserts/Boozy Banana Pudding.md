# Boozy Banana Pudding

*Yields:6 servings*

## Ingredients
- 2 (3.4-oz.) packages vanilla pudding mix
- 2 1/4 cups milk
- 3/4 cup rum
- 1 (8-oz.) container whipped topping
- 2 bananas, sliced
- 1 cup crushed Nilla wafers
- Whole Nilla wafers, for garnish

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, whisk together pudding mix, milk, and rum until thickened. Refrigerate 10 minutes.
2. To assemble puddings, in 6 jars, layer pudding, whipped topping, banana slices, and crushed Nilla wafers, then repeat one more time in each jar. Top final layer of whipped topping with crushed Nillas, a banana slice, and a Nilla wafer.
3. Refrigerate until ready to serve.

## Shopping List
