# Fruit Pizza

## Ingredients
- 1 package sugar cookie dough
- ½ cup butter, softened
- 1 ½ packages (8 oz each) cream cheese, softened
- 4 cups powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- assorted fresh fruit for topping

## Instructions
1. Roll out sugar cookie dough into a circle or square. Whatever you like and bake in oven.
2. Let cool.
3. Mix frosting ingredients in mixer.
4. Lay on cookie and use whatever fruit want.
