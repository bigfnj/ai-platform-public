# Cherries Hawaiiana

*Serves 6.*

## Ingredients
- 1 pound can of Bing cherries
- 1 teaspoon Curacao liqueur
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- Myers' dark Jamaican rum
- Coconut ice cream

## Instructions
1. Drain cherries, saving the syrup.
2. Combine ¼ cup of syrup with Curacao and ½ cup rum.
3. Marinate cherries in this mixture for 3 hours.
4. Make a paste out of cornstarch and a little syrup; add to fruit mixture.
5. Bring to a simmer for 1 minute in blazer pan of chafing dish, or in metal saucepan.
6. Fill a ladle with rum, lower ladle in heated fruit mixture to warm rum, then ignite rum from safe distance with long-stemmed match.
7. Pour flaming rum over fruit mixture, setting mixture aflame.
8. Let flame burn for a while before extinguishing; spoon cherries over individual servings of ice cream.
