# Lemon Lime Cake

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. lime jello
- ¾ C boiling water
- ½ C cold water
- 1 pkg lemon cake mix
- 1 envelope Dream Whip
- 1 pkg lemon instant pudding
- 1 ½ cups cold milk

## Instructions
1. Dissolve jello in ¾ cup boiling water.
2. Stir, when dissolved add ½ cup cold water; set aside at room temperature.
3. Make cake mix per directions on the box.
4. Leave the cake in the pan. Cool cake for 20 minutes.
5. Poke deep holes through the top of the warm cake (still in the pan) with a meat fork or toothpick; space holes about 1" apart.
6. Spoon the jello mixture into the holes and then refrigerate the cake.
7. In a chilled, deep bowl (put bowl and mixing beaters in freezer to chill)
8. Blend Dream Whip, instant pudding and 1 ½ cups cold milk until stiff (3-8 minutes).
9. Immediately frost cake.
10. Cake must be stored in refrigerator.
11. Serve chilled.
