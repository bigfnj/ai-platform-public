# Tiramisu

## Ingredients
- Cocoa powder
- 12 ladyfingers
- ½ cup strong, cooled coffee
- 2 tblsp rum or any other liquor
- 1 lb. mascarpone cheese
- Whipping cream
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2 tbls powdered sugar
- ⅓ cup sugar

## Instructions
1. Beat whipping cream until it starts to form whipped cream.
2. Add vanilla and powdered sugar. Adjust sugar to taste. Set aside.
3. In a separate bowl beat mascarpone cheese and sugar.
4. Add a little bit of whipped cream to the mascarpone mixture until smooth.
5. Dip ladyfingers in coffee and rum mixture and lay them in a baking dish.
6. Place part of the mascarpone mixture on top and sprinkle with cocoa powder.
7. Repeat with dipped ladyfingers, mascarpone mixture, and cocoa powder.
8. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving.
9. Be sure not to let ladyfingers soak in coffee and liquor or they will get too soggy.
