# Chocolate Chip Coffee Cake

## Ingredients
- 1 cube butter, soft
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 eggs at room temperature
- 1 small sour cream at room temperature
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ⅓ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 cup chopped walnuts
- 1 package chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. Cream together butter and sugar.
2. Add the eggs.
3. Sift together baking powder, flour, baking soda, and salt.
4. Mix together sour cream with 1 tsp. vanilla.
5. Alternating flour and sour cream add gradually to butter, and mix well.
6. Mix sugar and cinnamon together.
7. Spoon part of the batter in pan, sprinkle mixture, chocolate and nuts, spoon rest of cake batter, and add the rest of the sugar, nuts and chocolate chips.
8. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour.
