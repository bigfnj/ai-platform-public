# Mai Tai Pie

## Ingredients
- 1 envelope unflavored gelatin
- 1 teaspoon grated lemon rind
- 3 eggs, separated
- 2/3 cup granulated sugar, divided
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 ounce light rum
- 1/2 ounce dark Jamaican rum
- 1 baked 9-inch pie crust, cooled
- Mai Tai mix (1 ounce fresh lime juice, 1/2 ounce orange curaçao, 1/4 ounce orgeat and rock candy syrup)
- Beachbum’s Rum Whipped Cream

## Instructions
1. In a double-boiler, stir together 1/5 cup sugar, gelatin, lemon rind, egg yolks, and light rum until thickened.
2. Beat egg whites till stiff.
3. Gradually stir in remaining sugar mixture into the egg whites.
4. Pour filling across pie crust.
5. Top with Beachbum’s Rum Whipped Cream.
