# Honeyed Bananas

## Ingredients
- 6 bananas
- lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1/4 cup honey

## Instructions
1. Peel 6 bananas; dip in lemon juice.
2. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in skillet; stir in 1/4 cup honey.
3. Add bananas, cook over low heat, turning gently, until hot and glazed.
4. Don’t overcook -- takes only a few minutes.

## Shopping List
- 6 bananas
- 1 lemon
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1/4 cup honey
