# Pumpkin Pecan Cheesecake

*10 servings*

## Ingredients
- ¾ cup firmly packed golden brown sugar (for topping)
- 6 tbsps butter, chilled (for topping)
- 1 ½ cups chopped pecans (about 5 ½ ounces) (for topping)
- 1 ½ cups graham cracker crumbs
- 1 cup sugar
- 6 tbsps butter, melted
- 3 8-ounce packages cream cheese, room temperature
- ¾ cup firmly packed golden brown sugar
- 5 large eggs
- 1 pound canned solid pack pumpkin
- ½ cup whipping cream
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ¼ teaspoon ground cloves

## Instructions
1. For topping: Place sugar in small bowl. Add butter and cut in until mixture resembles coarse meal. Stir pecans into mixture. Set aside.
2. For cheesecake: Blend crumbs, ¼ cup sugar and butter in medium bowl. Press crumb mixture in bottom and up sides of 9 x 2 ½ inch springform pan. Chill.
3. Preheat oven to 325°F. Using electric mixer, beat cream cheese in large bowl until smooth. Mix in remaining ¾ cup sugar and brown sugar. Add eggs one at a time and beat until fluffy. Blend in pumpkin, cream, cinnamon, nutmeg and cloves.
4. Pour into crust. Bake until center no longer moves when pan is shaken – about 1 ½ hours.
5. Sprinkle topping over cheesecake. Bake 15 minutes and then transfer to rack to cool. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
