# Banana Calypso Flambe

*Serves 2.*

## Ingredients
- 1 ounce unsalted butter
- 2 bananas, halved crosswise, then lengthwise
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 3 ounces rum
- 2 scoops vanilla ice cream

## Instructions
1. Pre-heat rum in double-boiler.
2. Heat skillet, add butter, and place the bananas in the pan.
3. Sauté slightly.
4. Add brown sugar, lime juice, and ginger.
5. Stir well until sauce turns into a thick molasses.
6. Add the rum and, from a safe distance, flambé with long-stem match (see page 19).
7. Dish up the ice cream on two plates, placing the bananas on top of the ice cream.
8. Serve at once.
