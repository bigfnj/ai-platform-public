# Kona Coffee Sabayon

*Serves 6.*

## Ingredients
- 6 egg yolks
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon vanilla sugar
- Grated rind of ¼ orange
- 1 cup double strength Kona coffee
- ¼ cup Kona coffee liqueur

## Instructions
1. Beat yolks, sugars and rind until very light.
2. Cook this mixture in a double-boiler, whipping it constantly with a wire whisk until foamy.
3. Gradually add coffee and liqueur and continue whipping constantly and vigorously until the mixture becomes frothy and stiff (this could take as long as 6 minutes).
4. Serve immediately in pre-warmed champagne glasses, with a light cookie (such as ladyfingers).
