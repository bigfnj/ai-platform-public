# Peppermint Bark

## Ingredients
- 12 oz white or chocolate chips
- ½ cup crushed candy canes
- 2 tbsp crushed candy canes
- wax paper

## Instructions
1. Melt the chocolate in a microwave for 1 minute at 50% power.
2. Stir and melt for an additional 15 seconds, stirring after each session until melted.
3. Mix in ½ cup of the crushed candy canes.
4. Lay on lined cookie sheet.
5. Hit cookie sheet on counter until chocolate is spread out.
6. Pour remaining 2 tbsp of crushed candy canes on top.
7. Refrigerate until hardened.

## Shopping List
- 12 oz white or chocolate chips
- ½ cup + 2 tbsp crushed candy canes
- wax paper
