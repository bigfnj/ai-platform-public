# Peanut Butter Kiss Cookies

*Total Time: Prep: 20 min. Bake: 10 min./batch + cooling*

## Ingredients
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 30 milk chocolate kisses

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°.
2. Cream peanut butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in egg and vanilla.
4. Roll into 1-1/4-in. balls.
5. Place 2 inches apart on ungreased baking sheets.
6. Bake until tops are slightly cracked, 10-12 minutes.
7. Immediately press 1 chocolate kiss into center of each cookie.
8. Cool for 5 minutes before removing from pans to wire racks.

## Shopping List
- peanut butter (1 cup)
- sugar (1 cup)
- large eggs (1)
- vanilla extract (1 teaspoon)
- milk chocolate kisses (30)
