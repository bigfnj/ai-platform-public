# Personal Brownie

*Serves one.*

## Ingredients
- 1.5 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp water
- 6 oz brownie mix

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Mix all ingredients in bowl.
3. Pour into personal skillet.
4. Bake 16-18 minutes.
5. Remove and let cool 5 minutes.
6. Add 1 scoop of ice cream and serve.
