# Creme Anglaise

*Prep Time: 5 mins, Cook Time: 15 mins*

## Ingredients
- 1 vanilla bean split lengthwise
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 6 egg yolks
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar

## Instructions
1. Split vanilla bean in half lengthwise. Carefully scrape seeds.
2. In a saucepan over medium heat, bring 2 cups heavy cream to a bare boil with vanilla bean and scraped seeds.
3. Remove cream from heat. While cream is cooling, beat the egg yolks and 1/2 cup sugar in a mixer until thick ribbons form.
4. To temper the egg yolks and keep them from curdling, gradually add 1 cup warm cream to egg mixture, whisking constantly.
5. Add tempered egg yolk mixture back into saucepan with remaining cream and stir constantly over medium heat until thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
6. Remove from heat and strain through a fine strainer. Remove the vanilla pod.
7. Cool custard slightly and then cover with plastic wrap to prevent a skin from forming. Refrigerate until ready to use.

## Shopping List
- 1 vanilla bean
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 6 eggs
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
