# No Bake Butterfinger Pie

*Serves 8*

## Ingredients
- 16 oz packages cream cheese softened
- 3/4 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 8 oz container Cool Whip
- 3 chopped Butterfingers or a little over a cup, divided
- 1 Oreo crumb crust
- 1/2 cup melted chocolate chips

## Instructions
1. In a bowl with a hand mixer, cream together cream cheese, peanut butter, powdered sugar and vanilla.
2. Fold in Cool Whip and 3/4 cup Butterfinger, reserving 1/4 cup for the top. Place in pie crust.
3. Top with remaining candy, drizzle with melted chocolate and chill in freezer for 2 hours.
