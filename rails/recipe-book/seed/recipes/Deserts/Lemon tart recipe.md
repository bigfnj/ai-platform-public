# Lemon tart recipe

## Classic lemon tart
- all-purpose flour, for work surface
- 1/2 Pate Sucree (see below)
- finely grated zest of 2 lemons
- 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice, strained of seeds and pulp (about 2 lemons total)
- 1 cup granulated sugar, plus 2 to 3 tablespoons for caramelizing top
- 6 large egg yolks
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, cold, cut into small pieces

## Pate Sucree
- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- pinch of salt
- 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 cup unsalted butter, cold, cut into small pieces
- 2 egg yolks, lightly beaten
- 1/4 cup water

## Shopping List
- all-purpose flour
- salt (pinch)
- 1 1/3 cups granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 cups unsalted butter, cold
- 8 large egg yolks
- 2 lemons
- 1/4 cup water
