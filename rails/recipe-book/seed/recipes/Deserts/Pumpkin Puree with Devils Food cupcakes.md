# Pumpkin Puree with Devils Food Cupcakes

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. devils food cake mix
- 1 can (15 ½ ounce) pumpkin puree

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 350°F.
2. Combine cake mix with puree, scoop into cupcake tin and bake for 15-20 minutes until done.
