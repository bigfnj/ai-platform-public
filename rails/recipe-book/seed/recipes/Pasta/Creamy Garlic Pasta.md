# Creamy Garlic Pasta

## Ingredients
- Box of pasta
- 2 tbsp butter
- 4 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp flour
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tsp parsley flakes
- salt and pepper
- ⅓ cup parmesan cheese

## Instructions
1. Melt butter and add garlic.
2. Cook over medium heat for 1 minute.
3. Add flour and cook 1 minute, stirring constantly.
4. Stir in broth and milk and cook, stirring frequently, until sauce boils and thickens.
5. Add parsley, salt and pepper and cheese.
6. Stir until cheese is melted.
7. Toss with Pasta and serve.
