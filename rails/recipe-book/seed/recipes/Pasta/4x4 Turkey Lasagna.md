# 4x4 Turkey Lasagna

## Ingredients
- 1 jar marinara sauce
- 1 bag steam fresh broccoli and cauliflower
- ¾ yellow onion
- 1 white or yellow squash
- 1 zucchini
- 3 cloves garlic
- 20 oz ground turkey
- red pepper flakes
- olive oil
- salt
- pepper
- 15 oz part skim ricotta cheese
- 1 cup low fat grated parmesan cheese
- 4 oz part skim/low fat crumbled goat cheese
- 1 large egg, slightly beaten
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- 6 to 8 whole grain lasagna noodles
- 1 lb part skim mozzarella cheese, thinly sliced

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees.
2. Steam broccoli and cauliflower according to directions. Once veggies have cooled a bit, blend with water and half the jar of sauce. Place mixture and the rest of the jar of sauce into a saucepot and stir until warm. Set aside.
3. Boil water in a large pot and set 6 to 8 lasagna noodles to cook according to box directions. Add salt to keep noodles from sticking. Once the noodles are done, drain water and set noodles to rest on wax paper or aluminum foil.
4. Finely chop yellow onion, squash, zucchini and garlic. Sauté with olive oil until onions become transparent. Add ground turkey, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Sprinkle red pepper flakes and salt and pepper to taste. When turkey browns, add sauce and adjust flame to medium heat. Let simmer, uncovered, for 10 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, mix all Cheese Mix ingredients.
6. Layer the lasagna in the following order: ⅓ of the Sauce, ½ of the Noodles, ½ of the Mozzarella Cheese, ½ Cheese Mix, ⅓ Sauce, ½ Noodles, ½ Mozzarella Cheese, ½ Cheese Mix, ⅓ Sauce and top with ¼ cup of Parmesan Cheese.
7. Bake for 30 minutes or until bubbling.
