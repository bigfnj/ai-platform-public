# Macaroni and Cheese

## Ingredients
- 1 stick butter
- 6 slices white bread, crusts removed, torn into small pieces
- 5 ½ cups milk
- ½ cup flour
- 2 teaspoons salt
- ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- ¼ teaspoon freshly ground pepper
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper, to taste
- 4 ½ cups grated sharp white cheddar cheese (about 18 ounces)
- 2 cups grated Gruyere cheese (about 8 ounces) or 1 ¼ cups Romano (about 5 ounces)
- 1 pound elbow macaroni
- caramelized onions
- sundried tomatoes
- panko bread crumbs
- roasted peppers

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 375°F.
2. Place bread in a medium bowl. In a small saucepan over medium heat, melt 2 tablespoons butter. Pour butter into the bowl with bread, and toss. Set breadcrumbs aside.
3. In a medium saucepan, heat milk over medium heat. Melt remaining butter. When butter bubbles, add the flour. Cook whisking 1 minute.
4. While whisking, slowly pour in hot milk. Continue cooking, whisking constantly, until the mixture bubbles and becomes thick.
5. Remove pan from heat. Stir in salt, nutmeg, black pepper, cayenne pepper and both cheeses.
6. Cook macaroni until done.
7. Mix cheese mixture and macaroni together, pour into casserole dish.
8. Top with more cheese and bake until browned on top about 30 minutes.
