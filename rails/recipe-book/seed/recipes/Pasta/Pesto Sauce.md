# Pesto Sauce

## Ingredients
- Salt and Pepper for taste
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus more as needed
- 2 tbsp parmesan cheese
- bunch of basil
- pine nuts toasted

## Instructions
1. Add all ingredients to blender.
2. Add oil until mixture is to right consistency.

## Shopping List
- Salt
- Pepper
- 2 tablespoons + olive oil, plus more as needed
- 2 tbsp parmesan cheese
- bunch of basil
- pine nuts
