# Ravioli with Arugula

## Ingredients
- 1 pound cheese ravioli
- 6 oz thinly sliced pancetta, chopped
- 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes, drained
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- 3 cups arugula
- ½ cup thinly sliced fresh basil
- 2 tablespoons butter, at room temperature

## Instructions
1. Cook ravioli in boiling water for about 7 to 9 minutes, drain and set aside.
2. In a large skillet over medium heat add pancetta and cook stirring frequently, until crispy. Remove to paper towels to drain.
3. Add to skillet the tomatoes, olive oil, salt and pepper and cook for 2 minutes until tender.
4. Add the arugula and basil and cook until wilted, about 30 seconds.
5. Stir in butter and melt.
6. Add the ravioli and cooked pancetta and toss until coated.
