# Chicken Noodle Casserole

## Ingredients
- 1 package yolkless noodles
- 2 cans canned chicken
- 1 ½ cans cream of mushroom soup
- A little milk
- 2 tsp. sour cream
- A dash of onion powder
- Crackers for top
- ¼ cup butter
- 1 can mushrooms

## Instructions
1. Cook noodles.
2. Mix above together and bake for 25 min.
3. Crumble crackers and mix with melted butter, pour over casserole and run under broiler until crisp, about 5 minutes.
