# Cajun Mac & Cheese with Andouille Sausage & Shrimp

## Ingredients
- 1 lb dried Mezze Penne, Elbow Macaroni or Cavatappi noodles
- 13.5 oz Johnsonville Andouille Split Rope Sausages, sliced
- ½ lb peeled & deveined shrimp, chopped
- 1 sweet onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 2 Red Fresno or Jalapeno peppers, cored and chopped
- 8 oz extra sharp white cheddar cheese, shredded
- 8 oz smoked Gouda cheese, shredded
- 8 oz Muenster cheese, shredded
- 8 tbsp unsalted butter, plus additional to butter casserole dish
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 5 cups whole milk
- 2 bay leaves, preferably fresh
- ¼ tsp freshly ground nutmeg
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- Cajun seasoning (recipe follows)
- 1-2 tsp Kosher salt to taste, plus additional 2 tbsp for pasta water
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- ¼ cup sliced scallions

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F and butter a large casserole dish.
2. Season your pasta water generously with kosher salt (a couple tablespoons will do it - the water should taste like the sea) and add a couple bay leaves. Bring the water to a boil.
3. Cook pasta a couple minutes shy of al dente, per box directions, in this case I boiled mine for 6 minutes. The pasta will continue to cook in the oven so you want it a little underdone here. After your pasta cooks, drain and rinse, then set aside.
4. Season your shrimp with a couple teaspoons of the Cajun spice blend and a small pinch of salt.
5. In your skillet, melt 2 tbsp butter over medium high heat and add the onions, bell peppers and celery. Cook a few minutes until softened; season with a pinch of salt.
6. Add the sliced Andouille Sausage to your skillet and continue cooking until sausage is warmed through.
7. As sausage and vegetables start to brown, season with several teaspoons of the Cajun spice blend and toss to incorporate.
8. Finally, add the chopped hot peppers and shrimp. Cook just until the shrimp begins to turn opaque; it will continue to cook with the mac & cheese. Remove from heat and set aside.
9. Wipe out your pasta pot and return to stovetop over medium heat. Melt 6 tablespoons of butter.
10. As soon as butter starts to bubble, add the half cup of flour and quickly begin to whisk the butter and flour together to create a roux.
11. When the roux is golden and bubbly, pour in a cup or so of the steamed milk while continuing to whisk.
12. Whisk vigorously to incorporate the milk into the roux, creating what will look like a thick paste at first. Adding a cup or so of milk at a time helps to prevent lumps in the sauce.
13. Continue whisking and adding milk until it's all incorporated into a thin white sauce. Allow the sauce to simmer for a few minutes to thicken.
14. Season the white sauce with a tablespoon of Cajun spice blend, a teaspoon of salt, the freshly grated nutmeg, and good splash of Worcestershire sauce. Whisk to incorporate and simmer for a minute or so. Taste and adjust seasonings as desired.
15. When the sauce has thickened, it will easily coat the back of a wooden spoon. Promptly remove the pot from heat.
16. Set aside about 1 ½ cups of cheese (a mix of all three is fine). Cup by cup, stir in the remaining shredded cheese into your sauce to melt.
17. After all of the cheese has been melted smoothly into the sauce, add the cooked pasta and stir to coat well.
18. Start with ⅓ of the cheesy pasta, top that with half of the Andouille sausage & shrimp mix, evenly distributed, and top the sausage with half of the reserved shredded cheese.
19. Now, repeat with another ⅓ of pasta, the remaining sausage & shrimp mix, and the final ⅓ of pasta. Press it down into your casserole dish if you're coming close to the top.
20. Finally, top with the remaining ½ of the shredded cheese blend and lightly season with a few pinches of Cajun spice.
21. Transfer to the preheated oven and bake at 350°F until cheese is bubbly, about 30 min.
22. Just before serving, garnish the mac & cheese with sliced scallions and chopped parsley!
