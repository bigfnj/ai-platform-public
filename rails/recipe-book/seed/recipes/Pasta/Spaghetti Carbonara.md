# Spaghetti Carbonara

## Ingredients
- ½ lb vermicelli
- ½ pound fresh mushrooms
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 cup Canadian bacon
- 1 cup diced ham
- 6 tbls parmesan cheese, grated
- 2 eggs, well-beaten
- Additional cheese

## Instructions
1. Cook spaghetti, drain well and keep hot.
2. Saute mushrooms in oil and butter until start to turn golden.
3. Add Canadian bacon and ham. Heat but do not brown.
4. Remove from heat and stir in cheese and eggs.
5. Combine quickly with hot spaghetti and serve immediately with extra Parmesan cheese.
