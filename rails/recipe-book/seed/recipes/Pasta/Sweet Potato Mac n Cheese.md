# Sweet Potato Mac n Cheese

## Ingredients
- 1 bag sweet potato
- 2.5 cups milk
- 2 tsp mustard
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- garlic powder
- 2 cups cheese
- 3 tbsp panko crumbs
- 2 tbsp parmesan cheese
- 1 tbsp butter, melted

## Instructions
1. Pre-heat oven to 375°F.
2. Bag Sweet Potato Puree.
3. Boil bag of pasta.
4. Mix milk and sweet potato puree for about 5 minutes.
5. Remove from heat, mix in mustard, cayenne pepper, garlic powder and cheese. Stir until cheese melted.
6. Mix pasta in.
7. Mix butter, panko bread crumbs and parmesan cheese. Pour on top of pasta.
8. Add Cajun Chicken.
9. Bake until top is golden brown.
