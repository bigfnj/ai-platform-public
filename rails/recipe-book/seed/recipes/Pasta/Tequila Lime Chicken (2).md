# Tequila Lime Chicken (Large Order)

*Serves 6*

## Ingredients
- 3 packs spinach fettuccini
- 2 bunches chopped cilantro
- 3 tbsp crushed garlic
- 3 tbsp seeded/minced jalapeño peppers
- 4½ tbsp unsalted butter
- ¾ cup chicken stock
- 3 tbsp gold tequila
- 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 4½ tbsp soy sauce
- 6 chicken breast, diced into ½" pieces
- 1 each red, yellow, orange peppers
- 2 cups heavy cream

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta & drain well.
2. Sauté ⅔ of the cilantro, garlic & jalapeños in 3 tbsp. butter for 4-5 minutes.
3. Add chicken stock, tequila & lime juice, bring to a boil, reduce until paste like consistency.
4. Pour soy sauce over chicken & soak for 5 minutes.
5. Cook bell peppers with remaining butter until wilted, add chicken & soy sauce, toss, then add tequila lime paste.
6. Add heavy cream & bring to boil until chicken is cooked (approx. 3 minutes).
7. Toss with cooked/drained fettuccini noodles.
8. Sprinkle with remaining cilantro & serve with a wedge of fresh lime.
