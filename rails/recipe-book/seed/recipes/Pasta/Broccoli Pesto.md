# Broccoli Pesto (For Pizza and Pasta)

_1 cup_

## Ingredients
- 1 cup cooked broccoli*
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 teaspoons fresh chopped basil
- ¼ teaspoon sea salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

## Instructions
1. Add broccoli, cheese, basil, salt, and olive oil to a food processor or blender. Process until smooth.
