# Peanut Butter Pasta

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp chili oil
- 2 tbsp chopped scallions
- toasted sesame seeds

## Instructions
1. Combine the first five ingredients in a saucepan, stir until blended.
2. Add sesame seeds and scallions.

## Shopping List
- peanut butter
- soy sauce
- rice vinegar
- sesame oil
- chili oil
- 1 bunch scallions
- sesame seeds
