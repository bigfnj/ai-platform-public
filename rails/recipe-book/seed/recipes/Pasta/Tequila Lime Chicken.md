# Tequila Lime Chicken

*Serves 4.*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb spinach fettuccini
- ½ cup chopped cilantro
- 2 tbsp crushed garlic
- 2 tbsp seeded/minced jalapeño peppers
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- ½ cup chicken stock
- 2 tbsp gold tequila
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1¼ lb chicken breast, diced into ½" pieces
- ½ each red, yellow, orange peppers
- 1½ cups heavy cream

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta & drain well.
2. Sauté ⅓ cup cilantro, garlic & jalapeños in 2 tbsp butter for 4-5 minutes.
3. Add chicken stock, tequila & lime juice, bring to a boil, reduce until paste-like consistency.
4. Pour soy sauce over chicken & soak for ½ minute.
5. Cook bell peppers with remaining butter until wilted, add chicken & soy sauce, toss, then add tequila lime paste.
6. Add heavy cream & bring to a boil until chicken is cooked (approx. 3 minutes).
7. Toss with cooked/drained fettuccini noodles.
8. Sprinkle with remaining cilantro & serve with a wedge of fresh lime.
