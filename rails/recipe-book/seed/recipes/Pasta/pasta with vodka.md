# Pasta With Vodka

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ cup vodka
- 1 (24 oz) jar tomato sauce
- 8 oz butter
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese
- 8 oz sour cream
- 1 lb chicken or sausage
- 2 jalapenos
- Red Rooster sauce, to taste
- onion powder, to taste
- garlic powder, to taste

## Instructions
1. Cook onion with olive oil and then add garlic.
2. Stir in vodka and cook for 1 minute.
3. Stir in sauces and bring to boil - stirring occasionally.
4. Serve over pasta.
