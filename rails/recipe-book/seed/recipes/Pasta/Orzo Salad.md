# Orzo Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 box orzo
- 1 box cherry or grape tomatoes, halved
- 1 pack feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 bunch basil, chopped
- 1 cup pine nuts, toasted (brushed with olive oil first), then cooled
- salt to taste
- pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Cook orzo as directed on package and let cool.
2. Stir some olive oil into the cooled orzo to keep the grains from sticking together.
3. Add tomatoes, feta cheese, basil, a bit more olive oil, salt & pepper to taste.
4. Cover and put in fridge.
5. Stir in remaining ingredients immediately before serving.
