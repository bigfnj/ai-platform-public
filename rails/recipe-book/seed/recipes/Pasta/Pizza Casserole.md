# Low-Carb Pepperoni Pizza Casserole

*Prep Time: 10 minutes Cook Time: 1 hour Servings: 8*

## Ingredients
- 1 large spaghetti squash
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon pepper
- 1 (24-ounce) jar marinara sauce
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 ounce pepperoni slices

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Slice the spaghetti squash in half. Using a spoon, scoop out the seeds. Brush the squash halves with the olive oil.
3. Roast the squash, cut side up, on a baking sheet, for 30 to 40 minutes.
4. Remove the cooked squash from oven. Using a fork, scrape the inside of the squash until spaghetti strands form.
5. Place all spaghetti squash noodles into a lightly greased 9x13-inch baking dish.
6. Sprinkle salt and pepper on top of the noodles, then pour the marinara sauce on top.
7. Pour the beaten eggs on top and use a fork to stir until well combined.
8. Sprinkle the cheese on top, then top with pepperoni slices.
9. Bake for 30 minutes.
10. Cut into squares and serve.
