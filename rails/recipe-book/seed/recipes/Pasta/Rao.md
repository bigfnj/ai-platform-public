# Rao’s Meatballs

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 lbs ground sausage
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup Romano cheese
- 1 ½ tbsp chopped Italian parsley
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 cups bread crumbs
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Combine the meat with eggs, cheese, parsley, garlic, salt and pepper.
2. Blend the ingredients together.
3. Blend bread crumbs into mixture, slowly add water until moist.
4. Shape the balls.
5. Heat olive oil in skillet. Fry meatballs until crisp.
6. Lower meatballs into sauce and serve over pasta.
