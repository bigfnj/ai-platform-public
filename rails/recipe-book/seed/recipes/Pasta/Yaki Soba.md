# Yaki Soba

## Ingredients
- 4 or 5 Chicken breasts, cut into pieces
- Broccoli
- Cabbage
- Carrots
- Oil
- Teriyaki sauce
- Seasoning pack
- Soba noodles

## Instructions
1. Place oil in pan and sauté veggies.
2. Add a little bit of water after veggies are sautéed.
3. Add chicken.
4. Cook noodles in pot with water until soft.
5. Add noodles to chicken and veggie mix and sauté for a little but longer.
6. Add seasoning pack and add teriyaki sauce (enough to coat).

## Shopping List
- Broccoli
- Cabbage
- Carrots
- Chicken breasts, 4 or 5
- Cooking oil
- Soba noodles
- Seasoning packet
- Teriyaki sauce
