# Amazing Ribs

## Ingredients
- honey
- mesquite maple rub
- 3 tbsp butter
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- apple cider vinegar
- BBQ sauce
- ribs

## Instructions
1. Use honey and mesquite maple rub and rub all over ribs.
2. Place ribs in smoker at 225°F for 3 hours.
3. Melt butter with brown sugar and apple cider.
4. Put ribs on foil and pour mixture on ribs. Fold ribs so bones are sticking out ½ inch and smoke another 2 hours.
5. Open up ribs and baste in BBQ sauce, smoke 1 more hour.
