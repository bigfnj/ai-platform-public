# HAM

## Ingredients
- 1 cup chicken stock
- ham (size unspecified)
- foil

## Instructions
1. Place a 12 inch cast iron skillet on the grate of your smoker.
2. Pour over 1 cup chicken stock, cover with foil.
3. Smoke for 1.5 hours @ 225°F.
4. Remove foil and smoke for 2 hours @ 300°F or until internal temp reads 145°F.
5. Take off Grill, keep juice set aside in cup.
6. Pour on ham before serving.
7. Glaze ham in cast iron.
