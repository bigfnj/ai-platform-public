# Thai Green Curry with Chicken

*Servings: 4*

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons peanut oil
- ¼ cup Thai green curry paste
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 (1/4 inch thick) slice ginger, finely grated
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, thinly sliced
- 2 (13.5 ounce) cans coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 2 tablespoons palm sugar
- 6 kaffir lime leaves
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into thin strips
- 1 orange bell pepper, cut into thin strips
- 1 (15 ounce) can baby corn pieces, drained
- 1 (8 ounce) can bamboo shoots, drained
- ¼ cup thinly sliced Thai basil

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a wok until it starts to shimmer.
2. Add curry paste, garlic, and ginger; stir together and fry until fragrant, 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Add chicken breast slices, stirring until coated.
4. Cook until just slightly pink in the center, 3 to 5 minutes.
5. While chicken is cooking, place baby corn and bamboo shoots in a small saucepan, cover with water, and place over high heat.
6. Bring to a boil. Drain then cover with water again.
7. Pour coconut milk into the wok. Bring to a slow simmer, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes.
8. Drain corn and bamboo shoots and add in.
9. Stir in fish sauce, palm sugar, and kaffir lime leaves.
10. Bring back up to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes.
11. Stir in bell pepper strips and cook until crisp-tender, about 3 minutes.
12. Remove curry from heat and stir in Thai basil.
