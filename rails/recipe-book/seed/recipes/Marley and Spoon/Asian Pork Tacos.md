# Asian BBQ Pork Tenderloin Tacos

*This east-meets-west mash-up is proof that fusion can be a great thing!*

## Ingredients
- ¼ oz fresh mint
- ¼ oz fresh cilantro
- 12 (6-inch) flour tortillas
- 4 oz mayonnaise
- 1 packet Sriracha
- 2 oz celery
- 2 apples
- 20 oz pork tenderloin
- 2 packets teriyaki sauce
- apple cider vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- neutral oil
- sugar

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 450°F with a rack in the upper third.
2. Add 1 packet of teriyaki sauce to a medium bowl, then add pork and a pinch of salt; toss to coat pork. Transfer to a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet.
3. Roast pork on upper oven rack until pork is firm to the touch and reaches 145°F internally, about 15 minutes (watch closely as ovens vary). Remove from oven and brush with remaining teriyaki sauce. Set aside to rest for 5 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, quarter apples, discarding core, then cut crosswise into thin matchsticks. Trim ends from celery, then thinly slice on an angle. In a medium bowl, whisk to combine 3 tablespoons oil, 1½ tablespoons vinegar, and a generous pinch of sugar. Add apples and celery to dressing, and toss to coat. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
5. In a small bowl, stir to combine Sriracha and all of the mayo. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
6. Toast tortillas over an open flame until lightly charred in spots, 5–10 seconds per side. (Alternatively, bake on upper oven rack in batches until lightly charred, watching closely) Wrap in foil to keep warm.
7. Coarsely chop cilantro leaves and tender stems together. Pick and coarsely chop mint leaves, discarding stems. Stir half each of the cilantro and mint into apple-celery slaw. Thinly slice pork. Top tortillas with Sriracha-mayo, pork, some of the apple-celery slaw, and remaining herbs. Serve remaining apple-celery slaw alongside.
