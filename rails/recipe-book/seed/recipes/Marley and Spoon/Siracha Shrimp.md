# Sriracha Shrimp Poke Bowl

*20-30min • 2 Servings*

## Ingredients
- 5 oz quick-cooking brown rice
- 4 oz red radishes
- 1 cucumber
- 1 oz rice vinegar
- 2 oz mayonnaise
- 2 pkts Sriracha
- ¼ oz furikake
- 10 oz shrimp (use immediately or freeze until ready to use)
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- sugar
- neutral oil

## Instructions
1. Fill a medium saucepan with salted water and bring to a boil.
2. Add rice and cook (like pasta) over high heat, stirring occasionally, until tender, 20–25 minutes.
3. Drain rice well, return to pot, and cover to keep warm.
4. Trim ends from radishes, then halve (or quarter if large) and thinly slice.
5. Trim ends from cucumber, halve lengthwise, then thinly slice into half-moons (peel, if desired).
6. In a medium bowl, whisk to combine rice vinegar, 1 tablespoon water, 2 teaspoons sugar and 1 teaspoon salt.
7. Add radishes and cucumbers and toss to coat. Set aside to marinate.
8. Rinse shrimp, then pat very dry. (First thaw under cool water running, if necessary.)
9. Cut each one into thirds and season all over with salt and pepper.
10. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium skillet over medium-high.
11. Add shrimp and cook, stirring, until opaque and cooked through, 2–3 minutes.
12. Remove skillet from heat and stir in half of the sauce.
13. In a small bowl, stir to combine mayonnaise, all of the Sriracha, and 2 teaspoons furikake (save rest for step 6).
14. Using a slotted spoon, drain pickled veggies and add pickling liquid to rice and stir to combine.
15. Season to taste with salt.
16. Spoon rice into bowls then top with shrimp, pickled veggies, and drizzle with remaining sauce and sprinkle with remaining furikake.
