# Peach and Tomato Salad with Ginger-Tamari Dressing & Fried Peanuts

*Serves 4*

## Ingredients
- ½ oz fresh cilantro
- 2 cucumbers
- 2 containers grape tomatoes
- 1 oz scallions
- 5 peaches
- 2 (½ oz) tamari in fish-shaped pods
- 3 oz salted peanuts
- 1 oz fresh ginger
- 1 large clove garlic
- 2 limes

## Instructions
1. Finely grate 1 teaspoon lime zest; set aside lime until step 3. Finely chop 1 teaspoon garlic. Peel and finely chop 2 teaspoons ginger.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large heavy skillet (preferably cast-iron) over medium-high. Add peanuts and cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly toasted, 1–2 minutes. Transfer to a medium bowl, then stir in ½ teaspoon each of garlic and lime zest. Season with a pinch of salt. Set aside until step 6. Wipe out skillet and reserve for step 4.
3. Squeeze 2 tablespoons lime juice into a medium bowl. Add all of the tamari, chopped ginger, remaining garlic and 4 teaspoons sugar; whisk until sugar dissolves. Whisk in ⅓ cup oil. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
4. Halve peaches, discarding pits, then cut into ½-inch wedges. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in reserved skillet over medium-high. Add peaches, and season with salt and pepper. Cook, flipping once, until peaches are lightly browned all over, 3–4 minutes. Transfer peaches to a serving platter.
5. Trim scallions, then thinly slice. Halve tomatoes. Trim ends from cucumbers (peel, if desired) and halve lengthwise. Scoop out seeds and slice crosswise into ½-inch half-moons. Scatter tomatoes, cucumbers, and scallions over peaches.
6. Coarsely chop seasoned peanuts. Coarsely chop cilantro leaves and tender stems together. Sprinkle half each of the peanuts and cilantro over veggies and peaches. Just before serving, spoon dressing over top. Garnish with remaining peanuts and cilantro.

## Shopping List
- ½ oz fresh cilantro
- 2 cucumbers
- 2 containers grape tomatoes
- 1 oz scallions
- 5 peaches
- 2 (½ oz) tamari in fish-shaped pods
- 3 oz salted peanuts
- 1 oz fresh ginger
- 1 large clove garlic
- 2 limes
- kosher salt
- ground pepper
- neutral oil
- sugar
