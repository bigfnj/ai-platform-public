# Sesame Chicken Salad with Fried Almonds & Sugar Snap Peas

*Serves 4*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb shredded cabbage blend
- 1½ lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 oz sliced almonds
- 2 oz toasted sesame oil
- 2 oz tamari soy sauce
- 12 flour tortillas (use 4)
- ½ lb snap peas
- 1 bunch scallions
- 1 oz fresh ginger
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- neutral oil
- sugar
- white wine vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)

## Instructions
1. Finely chop ginger. Trim scallions, then thinly slice. Trim stem ends from snap peas, then halve crosswise. Cut 4 of the tortillas into ¼-inch strips.
2. In a large bowl, whisk to combine tamari, sesame oil, all of the chopped ginger, ⅓ cup neutral oil, 2 tablespoons vinegar, and 4 teaspoons sugar, until sugar dissolves.
3. Line a plate with a paper towel. Heat ¼ inch oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add tortilla strips to skillet (careful, oil may splatter) and cook, stirring, until golden-brown and crisp, 3–5 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer to paper towel-lined plate, then sprinkle with salt; reserve oil in skillet for next step.
4. Add almonds to skillet with reserved oil, and cook over medium-high heat, stirring, until golden-brown, about 1 minute. Use a slotted spoon to transfer to plate with fried tortilla strips. Rinse and dry skillet.
5. Add snap peas, ½ cup water, and a pinch of salt to same skillet over medium-high heat. Bring to a simmer, then cover and cook until snap peas are bright green and crisp-tender, about 1 minute. Drain snap peas and rinse under cold water. Dry skillet.
6. Pat chicken dry, then pound to ½-inch thickness, if desired; season all over with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in same skillet over medium-high. Add chicken and cook until golden-brown and cooked through, 3–4 minutes per side. Transfer to a cutting board to cool slightly, then thinly slice if desired.
7. To large bowl with dressing, add shredded cabbage blend, snap peas, scallions, fried tortillas and almonds, tossing to combine. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Serve chicken over salad.
