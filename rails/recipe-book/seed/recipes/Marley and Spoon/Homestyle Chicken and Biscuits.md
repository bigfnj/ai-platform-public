# Homestyle Chicken & Cheddar Biscuits

*40 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 2.5 oz peas
- 3 oz mascarpone cheese
- 1 packet chicken broth concentrate
- 12 oz chicken breasts
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 4 oz carrots
- ½ lb broccoli
- 5 oz self-rising flour
- 1 piece cheddar cheese
- ¼ oz fresh thyme leaves
- kosher salt and ground pepper
- butter
- olive oil
- ¼ cup milk
- apple cider vinegar (or white wine vinegar)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F with a rack in the center.
2. Pick 1 tablespoon thyme leaves from stems. Finely chop cheddar. In a medium bowl, combine ¾ cup flour, ⅓ of the thyme leaves, and a pinch of pepper. Cut 2 tablespoons cold butter into small pieces; rub butter into dry ingredients with your fingers until butter is in pea-sized pieces. Add cheddar, and toss to combine.
3. Lightly oil a rimmed baking sheet. Trim stem ends from broccoli, then cut crowns into 1-inch spears. Toss broccoli on prepared baking sheet with 1 tablespoon oil and season with salt and pepper; push to one side of the baking sheet.
4. In a liquid measuring cup, combine milk and vinegar; add to bowl with dry ingredients; stir until just combined. On a lightly floured board, pat dough into a 3- x 6-inch rectangle, then cut into 2 (3-inch) squares. Transfer to empty half of baking sheet.
5. Bake on center oven rack until biscuits are golden, and broccoli is crisp tender, 18–20 minutes (watch closely).
6. While biscuits and broccoli bake, scrub carrot, then trim end and cut into ½-inch pieces. Finely chop onion. Pat chicken dry. Using a meat mallet or heavy skillet, pound chicken to an even ½-inch thickness. Season all over with salt and pepper.
7. Heat oil in a medium skillet over medium-high. Add chicken and cook until well browned on one side, 3–4 minutes. Flip and cook until nearly cooked through, 1–2 minutes more. Transfer chicken to a plate. Add carrots, onions, and water to skillet; season with salt and pepper. Cover and cook over medium heat until carrots are tender, 5–7 minutes.
8. Add broth concentrate, mascarpone, remaining thyme leaves, and water to same skillet. Add chicken and any resting juices and half of the peas; cook, partially covered, over medium-high until chicken is cooked through and sauce is slightly thickened, about 5 minutes.
9. Serve chicken and gravy with biscuits and broccoli alongside.
