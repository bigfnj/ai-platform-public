# Oven Baked Potato Samosa Pockets

*Serves 2*

## Ingredients
- 3 Yukon gold potatoes
- ¼ oz fresh cilantro, plus more for garnish
- 2 plum tomatoes
- 1 shallot
- 1 oz fresh ginger
- 1 oz apricot preserves
- ¼ oz curry powder
- ¼ oz cumin seeds
- 5 oz peas
- 6 (8-inch) flour tortillas
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- neutral oil
- apple cider vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
- 4 Tbsp butter

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425ºF with a rack in the upper third.
2. Scrub potatoes and cut into 1-inch pieces. Place potatoes in a large saucepan. Add a generous pinch of salt and enough water to cover by 1-inch. Cover and bring to a boil, then uncover and cook until tender when pierced with a fork, about 8 minutes. Drain potatoes and set aside.
3. While potatoes cook, finely chop cilantro leaves and tender stems. Coarsely chop tomatoes. Finely chop shallot. Finely grate all of the ginger.
4. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium skillet over medium-high. Add shallots and grated ginger. Cook, stirring, until shallots are softened, about 3 minutes.
5. Add tomatoes, apricot preserves, ¼ cup water, 1 teaspoon vinegar, ½ teaspoon salt, and season with pepper. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium and cook, stirring occasionally, until tomatoes are softened and chutney is thickened, about 5 minutes. Use a fork to mash chutney until mostly smooth.
6. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in reserved saucepan over medium. Add all of the curry powder and 1 teaspoon cumin seeds; cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add potatoes, peas, 4 tablespoons butter, and half of the cilantro; stir until butter is melted, 2 minutes. Coarsely mash using a potato masher or fork. Season with ¾ teaspoon salt and a few grinds of pepper.
7. Place tortillas on a work surface; mound filling (about ½ cup) in the center of each, then spread into 5-inch circles. Fold tortilla sides over filling, then tightly roll up like a burrito.
8. Oil a rimmed baking sheet. Place samosas, seam side down, on prepared baking sheet and brush tops and sides with oil. Bake on upper oven rack until golden and crisp, about 20 minutes.
9. Stir remaining cilantro into chutney and season to taste with salt and pepper. Serve samosas with chutney alongside for dipping.
