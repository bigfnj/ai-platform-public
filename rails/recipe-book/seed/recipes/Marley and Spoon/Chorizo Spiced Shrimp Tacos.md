# Chorizo-Spiced Shrimp Tacos

*ca. 20min*

## Ingredients
- 1 teaspoon garlic, finely chopped
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- 1 mango, peeled, pitted, and cut into ½-inch pieces
- 1 jalapeño chile, halved, seeded, and finely chopped
- ¼ oz fresh cilantro, leaves separated from stems and chopped, plus more for garnish
- 10 oz shrimp, rinsed and patted dry
- ¼ oz chorizo chili spice blend
- 6 (6-inch) flour tortillas
- 2 (2 oz) guacamole
- olive oil

## Instructions
1. Finely chop garlic. Grate lime zest. Separately, squeeze lime juice into a medium bowl.
2. Cut skin from mango; carefully cut fruit from pit, then cut fruit into ½-inch pieces. Halve jalapeño, remove and discard seeds, and finely chop. Separate cilantro leaves from stems; finely chop stems. Wrap leaves in a damp paper towel; set aside until step 6.
3. Rinse shrimp, then pat dry. Transfer shrimp to a medium bowl; add all of the chorizo chili spice, lime zest, garlic, 1 tablespoon oil, and a pinch each of salt and pepper; stir to combine. Set shrimp aside to marinate until step 5.
4. To bowl with lime juice, stir in chopped jalapeños, mango pieces, and cilantro stems. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Set mango salsa aside until ready to serve.
5. Wrap tortillas in a damp paper towel; microwave in 30-second increments until warmed through.
6. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add shrimp and marinade in an even layer. Cook until browned and shrimp are cooked through, flipping halfway through, 2–3 minutes.
7. Spread guacamole on warm tortillas in an even layer. Top with shrimp and any pan drippings, then with mango salsa. Garnish with cilantro leaves.

## Shopping List
- garlic
- 1 lime
- 1 mango
- 1 jalapeño chile
- ¼ oz fresh cilantro
- 10 oz shrimp
- ¼ oz chorizo chili spice blend
- 6 (6-inch) flour tortillas
- 2 (2 oz) guacamole
- olive oil
- kosher salt
- ground pepper
