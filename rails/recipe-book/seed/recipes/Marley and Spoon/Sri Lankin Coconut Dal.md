# Sri Lankan Coconut Dal

## Ingredients
- 1 cup red lentils (masoor dal)
- 2 cups water
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 2 green chilies, slit lengthwise
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 4 cloves
- 4 cardamom pods
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp salt (or to taste)
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil or ghee
- Fresh cilantro, chopped for garnishing

## Instructions
1. Rinse the lentils under cold water and soak them in enough water to cover them for about 30 minutes.
2. Drain the lentils and set aside.
3. In a large pot, heat the vegetable oil or ghee over medium heat.
4. Add the mustard seeds, cumin seeds, cinnamon stick, cloves, cardamom pods, and bay leaf. Sauté until they sizzle and release their aroma.
5. Add the chopped onion, minced garlic, grated ginger, and green chilies. Sauté until the onions turn golden brown.
6. Stir in the turmeric powder and red chili powder. Cook for a minute until fragrant.
7. Add the drained lentils to the pot and stir well to combine with the spices.
8. Pour in 2 cups of water, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer until the lentils are tender (about 20-25 minutes).
9. Once the lentils are cooked, add the coconut milk and salt. Stir well and cook for another 5 minutes.
10. Garnish with chopped cilantro and serve hot with rice or flatbread.
