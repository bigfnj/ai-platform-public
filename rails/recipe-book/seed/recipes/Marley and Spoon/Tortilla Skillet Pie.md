# Tortilla Skillet Pie

*Serves 2*

## Ingredients
- 1 poblano pepper
- 1 oz scallions
- 15 oz can pinto beans, drained and coarsely mashed
- 2 limes
- ⅛ oz ground cumin
- 5 oz corn
- 4 oz shredded cheddar-jack blend
- 6 (6-inch) flour tortillas
- 1 romaine heart
- 1 oz sour cream

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
   - Cut poblano in half; remove stems and seeds and cut into ½-inch pieces.
   - Trim ends from scallions then thinly slice.
   - Reserving liquid, drain beans and coarsely mash, leaving some whole.
   - Finely zest and juice 1½ limes into a small bowl; cut remaining half into wedges.

2. In a medium ovenproof skillet, heat 1 tablespoon oil over high.
   - Add poblano, cumin, and half of the scallions; season with ¼ teaspoon salt.
   - Cook until peppers just begin to brown, about 3 minutes.
   - Add beans, reserved bean liquid, corn, and half each of the lime zest and juice.
   - Cook until bubbling and slightly reduced, 3-4 minutes.

3. Off the heat, stir in half of the cheese.
   - Season to taste with salt and pepper.

4. Brush tortillas with oil and sprinkle with salt.
   - Stack tortillas and cut in half.
   - Stack halves and cut crosswise into ½-inch wide strips.
   - Arrange on a baking sheet in a single layer and bake until golden and crisp, 7-10 minutes, stirring halfway through.

5. Cut romaine heart in half lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise.
   - In a medium bowl, combine romaine, lime zest and juice, and remaining scallions.
   - Season to taste with salt and pepper.

6. Scatter tortilla strips over bean mixture and top with remaining cheese.
   - Bake until golden and bubbling, 5-7 minutes.
   - Scoop tortilla pie onto plates and top with shredded romaine salad and a drizzle of sour cream on top.
   - Serve lime wedges on the side.

## Shopping List
- 1 poblano pepper
- 1 bunch scallions
- 1 can (15 oz) pinto beans
- 2 limes
- ground cumin
- 1 bag frozen corn
- 4 oz shredded cheddar-jack blend cheese
- 6 (6-inch) flour tortillas
- 1 romaine heart
- 1 container sour cream
- neutral oil
- kosher salt
- ground pepper
