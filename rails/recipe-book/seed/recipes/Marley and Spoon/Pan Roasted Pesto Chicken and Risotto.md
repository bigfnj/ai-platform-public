# Pan-Roasted Pesto Chicken & Risotto with Tomatoes & Sugar Snap Pea Risotto

## Ingredients
- 12 oz boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 4 oz basil pesto
- 1 shallot
- 5 oz arborio rice
- 2 oz snap peas
- 1 lemon
- ¾ oz piece Parmesan cheese
- 1 plum tomato
- 2 pkts cream cheese
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- olive oil
- balsamic vinegar (or red wine vinegar)
- butter

## Instructions
1. Pat chicken dry and pound to an even ½-inch thickness, if necessary. Transfer chicken to a medium bowl and toss with 1 tablespoon pesto and a pinch each of salt and pepper. Set aside to marinate at room temperature until step 5.
2. Finely chop shallot. In a liquid measuring cup, combine 3½ cups water and ¾ teaspoon salt; set aside for step 3.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil (or butter) in a medium nonstick skillet over medium-high. Add shallots and cook, stirring, until softened but not browned, 1–2 minutes. Add rice and cook, stirring, until lightly toasted, about 2 minutes more.
4. Meanwhile, trim snap peas, then thinly slice lengthwise. Zest lemon and squeeze 1 tablespoon juice into a small bowl. Cut any remaining lemon into wedges. Finely grate Parmesan. Finely chop tomato. In a second small bowl, stir to combine tomatoes and 1 teaspoon each of vinegar and oil. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
5. Once risotto has been cooking for 15 minutes, heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium nonstick skillet over medium-high. Add chicken and cook until golden brown and cooked through, 3-4 minutes per side.
6. To risotto, stir in snap peas, cream cheese, Parm, and 2 tablespoons butter. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until peas are crisp-tender, 2–3 minutes. Stir in lemon juice and zest. Thin remaining pesto by adding 1 teaspoon water at a time.
7. Serve risotto topped with chicken, remaining pesto, and chopped tomatoes, with any lemon wedges for squeezing over top.
