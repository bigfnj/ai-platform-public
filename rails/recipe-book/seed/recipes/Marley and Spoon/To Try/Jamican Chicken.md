# Jamaican Shrimp with Pineapple and Coconut Rice

## Ingredients
- 4 oz fresh pineapple, cut into 1-inch pieces if necessary
- 5 oz jasmine rice
- coconut milk powder
- jerk seasoning
- 10 oz pkg shrimp
- 1 lime
- 2 teaspoons serrano pepper, thinly sliced (save rest)
- ¼ oz fresh thyme leaves, finely chopped (save rest)
- 1 oz scallions, trimmed and thinly sliced, keeping dark green parts separate
- sugar
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- neutral oil

## Instructions
1. Prep ingredients.
   Trim scallions then thinly slice, keeping dark green parts separate. Finely chop 1 teaspoon thyme leaves (save rest). Thinly slice 2 teaspoons Serrano pepper (save rest). Finely grate zest from half of the lime into a medium bowl, and squeeze 1 tablespoon lime juice into a small bowl; cut any remaining lime into wedges.
2. Marinate shrimp.
   Rinse and pat shrimp dry. To bowl with lime zest, add jerk seasoning, thyme, and a pinch sugar; stir to combine. Add shrimp to bowl, tossing to coat. Let marinate while you continue the recipe.
3. Cook rice.
   In a small saucepan, combine coconut milk powder, rice, ¾ teaspoon salt, and 1¼ cups water. Bring to a boil over high. Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook until water is absorbed, about 17 minutes.
4. Sauté pineapple.
   To bowl with lime juice, add sliced pepper, scallion white and light greens, 2 teaspoons oil and a pinch salt. Heat 2 teaspoons oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high. Add pineapple and cook, stirring, until lightly browned in spots, 2–3 minutes. Transfer to bowl with lime juice; toss to combine. Let skillet cool for 2 minutes.
5. Cook shrimp.
   Heat 1 tablespoon oil in same skillet over medium-high. Add shrimp and cook, stirring occasionally, until shrimp are cooked through, 2–3 minutes. Remove from heat and stir 3 tablespoons water into skillet; season to taste with salt.
6. Serve.
   Fluff rice with a fork, then spoon into bowls. Top with shrimp and pineapple, spooning any collected juice from pineapple and pan sauce over. Sprinkle remaining scallion over. Serve lime wedges alongside to squeeze over.
