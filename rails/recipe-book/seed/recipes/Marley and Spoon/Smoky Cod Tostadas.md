# Smoky Cod Tostadas

*40 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 (10 oz) cod fillets
- 12 (6-inch) corn tortillas
- 4 oz red radishes
- 2 romaine hearts
- 2 containers Greek yogurt
- ½ oz fresh cilantro
- 2 plum tomatoes
- garlic
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- olive oil
- red wine vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
- sugar

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 450°F with racks in the center and lower third.
2. Finely chop 1 teaspoon garlic. Core tomatoes, then finely chop. Pick ¼ cup cilantro leaves from stems and set aside for serving; finely chop remaining leaves and stems together.
3. In a small bowl, stir together all of the yogurt and ½ teaspoon of the chopped garlic; season to taste with salt and pepper.
4. In a medium bowl, combine tomatoes, chopped cilantro, remaining chopped garlic, 4 teaspoons oil, and 2 teaspoons vinegar; season to taste with salt and pepper.
5. Thinly slice romaine crosswise, discarding stem ends. Halve and thinly slice radishes. In a large bowl, combine 2 tablespoons of the garlic yogurt, ¼ cup oil, 4 teaspoons vinegar, a pinch each of sugar and salt, a few grinds of pepper.
6. Add romaine and radishes to the bowl but do not toss.
7. Divide tortillas between two rimmed baking sheets. Drizzle tortillas generously with oil on both sides, then sprinkle with salt. Bake on center and lower racks until crisp and browned in spots, flipping tortillas and rotating baking sheets from top to bottom halfway through, 8–12 minutes (watch closely as ovens vary).
8. Pat cod dry, then cut into 1-inch pieces.
9. In a medium bowl, combine 2 tablespoons oil and 2 teaspoons of the smoked paprika; season with salt, and pepper.
10. Transfer cod to bowl, and stir to coat.
11. Heat 3 tablespoons oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high. Working in batches, add cod, and cook until lightly browned all over and cooked through, 2–3 minutes.
12. Using a slotted spoon or spatula, transfer cod to a paper towel-lined plate; sprinkle with salt.
13. Toss lettuce and radishes with dressing.
14. Spoon yogurt onto tortillas and top with cod, some of the slaw and salsa, and whole cilantro leaves. Serve remaining salsa and slaw on the side.
