# Dirty Rice with Garlic Aioli

*Serves 2*

## Ingredients
- 2 teaspoons garlic, finely chopped
- 2 oz celery, trimmed and cut into ¼-inch pieces
- 1 bell pepper, halved, seeded, and cut into ¼-inch pieces
- 1 medium yellow onion, cut into ¼-inch pieces
- 10 oz grass-fed ground beef
- ¼ oz Cajun seasoning
- 5 oz jasmine rice
- ¼ oz fresh parsley leaves and stems, coarsely chopped
- 2 oz mayonnaise
- ½ teaspoon red wine vinegar (or white wine vinegar)
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- kosher salt
- ground pepper
- sugar

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F with a rack in the center.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium ovenproof pot over medium-high heat. Add beef and cook, breaking up into smaller pieces, until browned, about 5 minutes.
3. Add onions, celery, and bell peppers to pot with beef. Season with a generous pinch each of salt and pepper. Cook, stirring, until veggies are softened and just starting to brown, and beef is cooked through, 5–7 minutes.
4. To pot, stir in half of the garlic, all of the Cajun seasoning, and ¼ teaspoon each of salt and sugar; cook until fragrant. Add rice and stir until combined. Add 1¼ cups water and bring to a simmer. Remove from heat, cover, and transfer to center oven rack. Bake until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed, 18–22 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, in a small bowl, whisk to combine mayonnaise, remaining garlic, and ½ teaspoon vinegar. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
6. Remove rice from oven and allow to rest, covered, about 5 minutes more. Fluff dirty rice with a fork and season to taste with salt and pepper. Garnish with parsley and serve with aioli on the side.
