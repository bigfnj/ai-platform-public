# Veggie Taco Pizza

*Sometimes we don't want to make a choice, we just want both. Enter taco pizza.*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb pizza dough
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 2 plum tomatoes
- 1 lime
- ¼ oz taco seasoning
- 15 oz can pinto beans
- 4 oz shredded cheddar-jack blend
- 2 oz sour cream
- 1 romaine heart
- 2 oz pickled jalapeños

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 450°F with a rack in the lower third. Lightly oil a rimmed baking sheet.
2. Unwrap dough, transfer to a lightly oiled bowl and turn to coat; cover with a clean kitchen towel and allow to come to room temperature.
3. Finely chop onion; reserve 2 tablespoons of the onions for step 6. Core and chop tomatoes. Squeeze 2 teaspoons lime juice into a medium bowl. Whisk in 2 tablespoons oil and season to taste with salt and pepper. Add tomatoes and stir to combine. Cut any remaining lime into wedges.
4. On a floured surface, roll or stretch dough to a rectangle, about 10- x 13-inch. Dust off excess flour, then carefully transfer to prepared baking sheet.
5. Spread beans over dough leaving a 1-inch border. Sprinkle with ⅔ of the cheese (save rest for own use). Bake on lower oven rack until bottom of crust is browned and cheese is bubbling, 12–18 minutes.
6. In a small bowl, thin all of the sour cream by adding 1 teaspoon water as needed. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
7. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a medium skillet over medium-high. Add remaining onions and cook, stirring, until soft, 3–4 minutes. Add all of the taco seasoning; cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add beans and their liquid and cook, stirring, until liquid is reduced by half, 3–4 minutes. Remove from heat; mash until mostly smooth. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
8. Thinly slice romaine crosswise, discarding stem end. Transfer lettuce and reserved onions to bowl with tomatoes; season to taste with salt and pepper and toss to combine.
9. Cut pizza into wedges. Scatter ⅓ of the salad on top, drizzle with sour cream, and garnish with pickled jalapeños. Serve remaining salad and any lime wedges alongside.

## Shopping List
- 1 lb pizza dough
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 2 plum tomatoes
- 1 lime
- ¼ oz taco seasoning
- 15 oz can pinto beans
- 4 oz shredded cheddar-jack blend
- 2 oz sour cream

- kosher salt
- ground pepper
- all-purpose flour
