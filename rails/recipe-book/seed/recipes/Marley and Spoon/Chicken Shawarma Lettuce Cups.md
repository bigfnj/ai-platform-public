# Chicken Shawarma Lettuce Cups

*30 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 2 heads bibb lettuce
- 2 oz tahini
- 4 oz roasted red peppers
- 2 cucumbers
- 1 lemon
- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 containers Greek yogurt
- garam masala (use 4 tsp)
- garlic (use 2 large cloves)
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- olive oil

## Instructions
1. Finely chop 2 teaspoons garlic. In a medium bowl, combine 4 teaspoons garam masala, ¼ cup of the yogurt, 1 teaspoon of the chopped garlic, 3 tablespoons each of water and oil, 2 teaspoons salt, and a few grinds of pepper. Pat chicken dry, then pound to an even ½-inch thickness, if desired. Transfer chicken to marinade, turning to coat. Set aside until step 3.
2. Preheat broiler with top rack 6 inches from heat source. Finely grate ½ teaspoon lemon zest into a medium bowl, then squeeze 3 tablespoons lemon juice into a separate medium bowl. Trim ends from cucumber, then peel and halve lengthwise. Scoop out seeds, then finely chop 3 cups (snack on any remaining cucumbers). Finely chop roasted red peppers.
3. Remove chicken from marinade and transfer to a rimmed baking sheet lined with foil; discard any remaining marinade. Broil on top oven rack until chicken is browned in spots and cooked to an internal temperature of 165°F, 8–10 minutes (watch closely as broilers vary). Transfer to a cutting board to cool slightly.
4. While chicken broils, add cucumbers, roasted red peppers, remaining chopped garlic, and 2 teaspoons oil to bowl with lemon zest, tossing to combine. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
5. Add tahini and remaining yogurt to bowl with lemon juice. Stir in ¼ cup water and 3 tablespoons oil (if too thick, add 1 tablespoon water at a time, as needed). Season to taste with salt and pepper.
6. Trim stem ends from lettuce and separate leaves. Slice chicken into strips. Assemble lettuce wraps at the table, filling lettuce leaves with chicken, cucumber relish, and a drizzle of the tahini sauce.
