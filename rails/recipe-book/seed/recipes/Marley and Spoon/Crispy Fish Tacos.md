# Take-Out Style: Crispy Fish Tacos with Pineapple Salsa & Lime Crema

*30-40min 2 servings*

## Ingredients
- 4 oz fresh pineapple
- 1 lime
- 1 bell pepper
- ¼ oz fresh cilantro
- 1 shallot
- garlic
- 2 oz mayonnaise
- ¼ oz taco seasoning
- 10 oz cod fillets
- 6 (6-inch) flour tortillas
- neutral oil
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- ¾ c all-purpose flour

## Instructions
1. Cut pineapple into ¼-inch pieces. Zest lime, then squeeze 2 teaspoons lime juice into a small bowl, keeping them separate. Cut any remaining lime into wedges.
2. Halve pepper, remove stem and seeds, then finely chop. Coarsely chop cilantro leaves and stems. Finely chop shallot and 1 teaspoon garlic, keeping them separate.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk to combine all of the taco seasoning, ½ cup flour, and a large pinch of salt. Whisk in⅔ cup water until smooth (should be the consistency of pancake batter).
4. In a medium bowl, stir to combine pineapple, peppers, shallots, half of the cilantro, 2 teaspoons oil, and 1 teaspoon of the lime juice. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
5. To bowl with remaining lime juice, add chopped garlic, lime zest, and mayonnaise. Stir in 1 teaspoon water at a time, as needed, to thin sauce. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
6. Heat a large heavy skillet (preferably cast-iron) over medium-high. Working in batches, add tortillas and toast on one side until warm, about 30 seconds. Transfer to a large sheet of foil, stacking and wrapping to keep warm as you toast remaining tortillas.
7. Heat ¼ inch oil in same skillet over medium-high. Once oil is hot (should sizzle vigorously when a pinch of flour is added), pat fish very dry; cut each filet into 6 strips (12 total). Season all over with salt and pepper. Place ¼ cup flour on a plate.
8. Coat half of the fish strips in flour; dust off excess. Dip fish strips into batter, then carefully add to oil. Fry fish until golden brown and cooked through, flipping halfway through, 3–4 minutes. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and season with salt. Repeat with remaining fish.
9. Fill tortillas with fish and top with salsa, lime crema, and remaining cilantro. Serve with any lime wedges on the side for squeezing over top, if desired.
