# Lentil and Quinoa Curry

*Serves 2*

## Ingredients
- 2 teaspoons garlic, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, trimmed, scrubbed, thinly sliced
- 1 bell pepper, halved, seeded, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 0.75 oz fresh cilantro, coarsely chopped
- 0.75 oz curry powder
- 2 (7.15 oz) coconut milk powder
- 3 oz French green lentils
- 3 oz white quinoa
- 0.5 oz tamari soy sauce
- 3 oz baby kale, coarsely chopped

## Instructions
1. Finely chop 2 teaspoons garlic. Trim ends from carrot, then scrub, and thinly slice. Halve pepper, remove stem and seeds, then cut into 1-inch pieces. Coarsely chop cilantro leaves and stems.
2. Heat 1½ tablespoons oil in a medium pot over medium-high. Add peppers and carrots and cook until lightly browned, 3-4 minutes. Add garlic, all of the curry powder, and 1 tablespoon oil. Cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. Add coconut milk powder, 3 cups water, and ¼ teaspoon salt into the pot. Stir in lentils. Cover and bring mixture to a boil. Reduce heat to medium, partially cover, and cook until lentils are barely tender, about 20 minutes.
4. Stir in quinoa and tamari. Reduce to a simmer over low heat; cover, and cook until quinoa is cooked through and tender and lentils are just tender, about 15 minutes.
5. Coarsely chop baby kale. Stir into pot. Cook, uncovered, until kale is wilted, about 3 minutes. Add ½ cup water if the curry seems too thick.
6. Remove from heat and stir in half of the chopped cilantro. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Serve curry garnished with remaining cilantro.
