# Chicken Kale Salad with Ranch Dressing

## Ingredients
- 2 cups kale, chopped
- 1 cup cooked chicken, diced
- 1/4 cup ranch dressing
- 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 cup cucumber, diced
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, combine the kale, chicken, ranch dressing, cherry tomatoes, and cucumber.
2. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
3. Toss to combine and serve immediately.

## Shopping List
- 2 cups chopped kale
- 1 cup cooked chicken (or raw chicken if you plan to cook it)
- 1/4 cup ranch dressing
- 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes
- 1/4 cup cucumber
