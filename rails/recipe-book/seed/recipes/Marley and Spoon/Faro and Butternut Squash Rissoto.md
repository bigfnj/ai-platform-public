# Faro and Butternut Squash Risotto

*Serves 2*

## Ingredients
- 1 shallot
- 1 teaspoon garlic
- 4 oz quick-cooking farro
- ½ lb butternut squash
- ¼ oz fresh rosemary
- 1 bunch curly kale (use half)
- 2 pieces Parmesan cheese (¾ oz each)
- 1 lemon

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 450°F with a rack in the upper third.
2. Finely chop shallot and 1 teaspoon garlic. In a liquid measuring cup, stir to combine 3½ cups hot tap water and ¾ teaspoon salt.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil (or butter) in a medium skillet over medium-high heat. Add shallots and cook, stirring, until softened, 2–3 minutes.
4. Add farro and garlic to skillet. Cook, stirring, until farro is toasted, 2 minutes.
5. Stir in ½ cup of the seasoned water; cook over medium-high heat, stirring, until water is nearly absorbed, 1–2 minutes.
6. Continue adding ½ cup of the remaining water at a time, stirring, until all of the water is nearly absorbed, 25 minutes total. Farro will be tender and suspended in a thick sauce.
7. Meanwhile, cut butternut squash into ½-inch pieces. Pick 1 tablespoon rosemary leaves from stems; discard stems and finely chop leaves.
8. On a rimmed baking sheet, toss squash and chopped rosemary with 1 tablespoon oil and a generous pinch each of salt and pepper. Roast on upper oven rack until squash is golden-brown and tender, about 15 minutes, stirring halfway through.
9. Strip half of the kale leaves from stems; discard stems. Chop about 4 cups kale into bite-sized pieces (save rest for own use).
10. In a medium bowl, massage kale with 1 tablespoon each of oil and water; season with salt and pepper. Add kale to baking sheet with cooked squash.
11. Return to upper oven rack and roast until kale is tender and lightly browned, about 5 minutes.
12. While veggies roast, finely grate all the Parmesan and 1 teaspoon lemon zest, keeping them separate. Separately squeeze 2 teaspoons lemon juice into a small bowl. Cut any remaining lemon into wedges.
13. Stir half of the Parmesan and 2 tablespoons butter into farro. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until cheese is melted, 1–2 minutes.
14. Stir in lemon juice and ½ teaspoon of the zest. Top risotto with kale, butternut squash, and remaining Parmesan and lemon zest.
15. Serve lemon wedges for squeezing.

## Shopping List
- 1 shallot
- 1 head garlic
- 4 oz quick-cooking farro
- ½ lb butternut squash
- ¼ oz fresh rosemary
- 1 bunch curly kale
- 2 pieces Parmesan cheese (¾ oz each)
- 1 lemon

- ground pepper
- olive oil
