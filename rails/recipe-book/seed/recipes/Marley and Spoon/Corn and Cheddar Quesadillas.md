# Corn and Cheddar Quesadillas

*2 servings*

## Ingredients
- 1 plum tomato
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 jalapeño chile
- 1 large garlic clove
- 8 oz plant-based ground
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 5 oz corn
- 6 (6-inch) flour tortillas
- 2 oz shredded cheddar-jack blend
- 2 oz guacamole

## Instructions
1. Preheat broiler with a rack in the top position.
2. Halve tomato lengthwise.
3. Squeeze all of the lime juice into a small bowl (about 1½ tablespoons).
4. Coarsely chop tomatoes, peel and finely chop garlic clove, thinly slice jalapeño (or less, depending on heat preference; save any remaining for own use). To bowl with lime juice, combine tomatoes, garlic, jalapeños, and 2 tablespoons oil; toss to combine.
5. Season to taste with salt.
6. Transfer jalapeño, tomatoes cut side up, and 1 large unpeeled garlic clove to a rimmed baking sheet. Lightly drizzle tomatoes with oil.
7. Broil on top oven rack until vegetables are charred in spots, 5-10 minutes (watch closely as broilers vary). Remove from oven and let cool until step 4.
8. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium nonstick skillet over medium-high. Crumble plant-based ground in large pieces into skillet. Add all of the taco seasoning and 3 tablespoons water.
9. Cook, stirring occasionally, until water is mostly evaporated and plant-based ground is browned, about 4 minutes. Remove from heat and stir in corn and 6 tablespoons water.
10. Brush one side of each tortilla generously with oil. Arrange tortillas on a same baking sheet, oiled side down. Divide plant-based ground and corn mixture among tortillas, spooning filling onto 1 half of each tortilla, then top with cheddar-jack cheese. Fold in half to close.
11. Broil quesadillas on top oven rack until cheese is melted and quesadillas are golden brown, shifting baking sheet and carefully flipping quesadillas halfway through for even browning, 1-2 minutes per side (watch closely).
12. Let stand for 5 minutes, then cut into wedges, if desired.
13. Serve quesadillas with guacamole and salsa alongside.

## Shopping List
- 8 oz plant-based ground
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 6 (6-inch) flour tortillas
- 2 oz shredded cheddar-jack blend
- 5 oz corn
- 1 plum tomato
- 1 lime
- 1 jalapeño chile
- 1 large garlic clove
- olive oil
- kosher salt
- ground pepper
