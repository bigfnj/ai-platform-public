# Skillet Moussaka

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lb Beyond Meat ground beef substitute
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- ¼ cup tomato paste
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp allspice
- 6 medium potatoes, thinly sliced
- 1 lb eggplant, thinly sliced
- 1 cup crumbled feta cheese (optional)
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

## Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and garlic, sautéing until softened.
3. Add the Beyond Meat ground beef substitute, breaking it up with a spoon. Cook until browned.
4. Stir in the oregano, salt, black pepper, crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, sugar, cinnamon, and allspice. Simmer for 10 minutes.
5. Layer the potatoes and eggplant in the skillet, overlapping slightly. Pour the meat sauce over the top.
6. Cover and cook over low heat for about 20-25 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
7. Sprinkle with feta cheese (if using) and parsley before serving.
