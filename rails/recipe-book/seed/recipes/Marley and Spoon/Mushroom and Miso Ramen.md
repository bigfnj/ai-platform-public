# Mushroom & Spinach Miso Ramen with Soft-Boiled Eggs & Furikake

*20-30min • 2 Servings*

## Ingredients
- 8 oz baby bella mushrooms
- 1 oz fresh ginger (use half)
- garlic
- 2 pkts Sriracha
- 6 oz chukka soba noodles
- 1 pkt vegetable broth concentrate
- 1.8 oz miso sauce
- 5 oz baby spinach
- ¼ oz furikake
- 2 large eggs
- neutral oil
- kosher salt

## Instructions
1. Fill a medium saucepan with water. Bring to a boil, carefully lower in 2 large eggs (the water should cover the eggs by about ½ inch), and cook for 6 minutes.
2. Using a slotted spoon, remove from pot, and place in a bowl of cold water to cool. Reserve water and saucepan for step 3.
3. Trim stem ends from mushrooms, then thinly slice caps. Peel and finely chop half of the ginger and 2 teaspoons garlic. In a small bowl, stir to combine 1 of the Sriracha packets (reserve 2nd packet for step 5) and 1 teaspoon oil.
4. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium pot over medium-high. Add mushrooms and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and any liquid is evaporated, 6–7 minutes.
5. Add chopped garlic and ginger and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 1 minute more.
6. Return reserved saucepan of water to a boil over high heat. Add noodles, and cook, stirring occasionally to prevent clumping, until just tender, 3–5 minutes. Drain noodles, then rinse under warm running water.
7. Add vegetable broth concentrate, 4 cups water, and 1 teaspoon salt to pot; bring to a boil. Cover and simmer 5 minutes to allow flavors to meld. Remove from heat, then whisk in remaining Sriracha packet and miso.
8. Peel and halve eggs lengthwise. Add spinach to soup and stir until wilted. Season to taste with salt. Spoon noodles into bowls and ladle soup and vegetables over top. Garnish with egg halves, and top with as much furikake and chili oil as desired.

## Shopping List
- 8 oz baby bella mushrooms
- 1 oz fresh ginger
- garlic (head)
- Sriracha (2 packets)
- 6 oz chukka soba noodles
- vegetable broth concentrate (1 packet)
- 1.8 oz miso sauce
- 5 oz baby spinach
- ¼ oz furikake
- 2 large eggs
- neutral oil
- kosher salt
