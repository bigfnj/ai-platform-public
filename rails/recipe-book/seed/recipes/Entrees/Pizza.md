# Pizza

## Ingredients
- 1⅓ cup warm water
- 2¼ tsp instant yeast
- 1 tb sugar
- 2 tb olive oil
- ¾ tsp salt
- 3½ cups flour
- 1.5 tb garlic
- dash nutmeg

## Instructions
1. Whisk the water, yeast, and sugar together. Cover and rest for 5 minutes.
2. Add olive oil, salt, flour, garlic, and nutmeg. Beat on low setting in mixture for a few minutes. Take out and knead until you pierce it and it bounces back.
3. Grease the bowl with olive oil. Rub the dough so all sides are covered. Cover with towel and let rise for 90 min.
4. Pre-heat oven to 475°F. Cut dough in half…Roll out and make the pizza. Heat pizza stone for 7 min. Bake once all fillings are in until cheese is bubbly and dough is done.
