# Turkey Balls

## Ingredients
- 1 lb Italian Seasoned Turkey
- 1 egg
- 1 cup Bread Crumbs
- 2 Jalapenos
- 1 tbsp. Cumin
- 2 tbsp. Worcestershire Sauce
- 1 tbsp. Red pepper and garlic blend
- 1 tbsp. Paprika
- 1 tbsp. Garlic Powder
- 2 tbsp Italian Seasoning

## Instructions
1. Blend all ingredients together.
2. Brown with little olive oil and finish baking in oven
