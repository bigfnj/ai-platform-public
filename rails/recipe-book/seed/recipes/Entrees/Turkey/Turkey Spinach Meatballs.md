# Turkey Spinach Meatballs

*Serves: 2*

*Prep Time: 10 min, Cooking Time: 20 min*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 egg
- 1 cup spinach, chopped
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp cumin

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
2. Mix ingredients in a bowl, use hands to shape into approximately 12 meatballs
3. Place meatballs on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper
4. Cook in oven for 20 minutes.
