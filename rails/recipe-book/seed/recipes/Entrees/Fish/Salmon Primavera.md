# Salmon Primavera

## Ingredients
- 3 pounds wild Pacific or sockeye salmon fillet
- Sea salt
- Pepper
- Dijon mustard
- 1 small zucchini, unpeeled, thinly sliced
- 1 yellow squash, unpeeled, thinly sliced
- 2 Roma tomatoes, thinly sliced
- ¼ cup Italian flavored breadcrumbs
- 3 Tbsp. chopped fresh dill
- 3 Tbsp. olive oil

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Season salmon with salt and pepper. Brush an even coating of the mustard all over the salmon.
3. Place the salmon on a parchment lined baking sheet. Starting at the left end of the fillet, lay a column of overlapping slices of the zucchini. On the next row, layer the squash, and then the tomatoes. Do this until the whole fish is covered.
4. In a small bowl, mix the breadcrumbs, dill, and olive oil. Spread over the top of the vegetables.
5. Bake for 30-35 minutes until fish in done.
6. Serve warm or at room temperature.
