# Salmon in a Couscous Crust

## Ingredients
- ½ bunch fresh parsley, chopped
- ¼ cup golden raisins, coarsely chopped
- 3 Tbsp. green olives, coarsely chopped
- 3 Tbsp. pine nuts, toasted and coarsely chopped
- ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- salt
- pepper
- 1 pkg. couscous
- salmon fillets, skinned
- 2 cups water

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. In a bowl stir together the parsley, raisins, olives, pine nuts, olive oil and lemon juice.
3. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Stir in couscous to moisten completely and season again as needed.
5. Season salmon with salt, place in an oven proof dish.
6. Drizzle with a little olive oil.
7. Spoon the couscous over the salmon making sure all the fish is covered.
8. Pour the water over the couscous and drizzle a little olive oil  over.
9. Bake for about 25 minutes.
