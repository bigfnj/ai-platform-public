# Salmon with Brown Sugar

## Ingredients
- 3 Tbsp brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp honey
- 2 Tbsp butter
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- ¼ cup Dijon mustard
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- grated ginger
- vegetable oil
- salt
- pepper
- 8 salmon fillets

## Instructions
1. Melt butter, sugar and honey in pan over medium-high heat.
2. Remove from heat and whisk in mustard, soy sauce, olive oil, and ginger.
3. Let cool.
4. Brush salmon with vegetable oil, and season with salt & pepper.
5. Place salmon skin side down, coat with sugar mixture.
6. Grill until salmon is done.
