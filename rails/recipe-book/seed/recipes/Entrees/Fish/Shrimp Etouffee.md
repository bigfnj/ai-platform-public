# Shrimp Etouffee

## Ingredients
- ½ cup oil
- ½ cup flour
- 1 cup chopped onions (purple)
- ½ cup chopped red pepper
- ½ cup chopped celery
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ tsp. white pepper
- ½ tsp. cayenne pepper
- 2 tsp. Cajun seasoning
- ½ cup chopped green onions
- ¼ cup parsley
- 3-4 tsp. hot sauce
- 8 ounces chicken broth
- 1 can diced tomatoes, un-drained
- 2 pounds shrimp, peeled and deveined
- ½ stick butter

## Instructions
1. To make the roux: Mix oil and flour in a large saucepan over low heat.
2. Whisk flour into the oil.
3. Continue cooking over low heat and whisk continuously until the mixture turns a caramel color, about 15 minutes.
4. Add the onions, peppers, celery, garlic and cook over low heat for about 5 minutes.
5. Add the seasonings and hot sauce, chicken broth and tomatoes stir to blend.
6. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low and simmer for about 10-15 minutes.
7. Add the shrimp and cook until shrimp are cooked.
8. Remove from heat and add the butter.
9. Serve with Jambalaya rice.
