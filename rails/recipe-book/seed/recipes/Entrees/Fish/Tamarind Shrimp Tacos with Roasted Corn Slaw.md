# Tamarind Shrimp Tacos with Roasted Corn Slaw

*Prep: 30 minutes Marinate: 30 minutes Bake: 10 minutes Oven: 350°F*

## Ingredients
- 1 pound fresh or frozen medium shrimp
- 2 tablespoons canola oil or vegetable oil
- 2 tablespoons tamarind concentrate
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar
- 1 to 2 teaspoons adobo sauce from canned chipotle chile peppers in adobo sauce
- Salt
- 2 tablespoons hot water
- 12 6-inch corn tortillas
- Nonstick cooking spray
- 1 cup frozen whole kernel corn
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise or salad dressing
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- ¾ teaspoon salt
- 2 cups finely shredded cabbage
- ¼ cup finely chopped red onion (1 medium)
- ¼ cup fresh parsley

## Instructions
1. Thaw shrimp, if frozen. Peel and devein shrimp. Rinse shrimp; pat dry with paper towels. Set aside.
2. In a bowl combine oil, tamarind concentrate, garlic, brown sugar, adobo sauce, and a dash of salt. Add the hot water, whisking until smooth. Chop the shrimp; add to tamarind mixture, tossing to coat. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.
3. Preheat oven to 350°F. Stack tortillas and wrap tightly in foil, Bake about 10 minutes or until heated through.
4. For roasted corn slaw, coat a large skillet with cooking spray; heat over medium-high heat. Add frozen corn all at once to hot skillet and spread in a single layer. Cook, without stirring, for 3 minutes; stir. Continue to cook, without stirring, for 3 minutes more until corn is light golden brown. Transfer to a medium bowl; cool completely. Set skillet aside.
5. When corn is cool, stir in mayonnaise, lime juice, and the ¾ teaspoon salt. Add cabbage, onion, and parsley; stir to coat. Set corn slaw aside.
6. Return skillet to medium-high heat. Add shrimp mixture; cook and stir for 3 to 4 minutes or until shrimp are opaque.
7. To assemble tacos, use a slotted spoon to spoon about ¼ cup of the shrimp mixture onto warm tortillas, reserving cooking juices in skillet. Top tacos with roasted corn slaw. Drizzle with reserved cooking juices. Fold taco over filling.
