# Spinach Stuffed Baked Salmon

## Ingredients
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 2 oz fresh spinach leaves
- 1 tsp grated lemon zest
- chopped roasted peppers
- fresh basil
- salmon fillets
- Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp bread crumbs
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Heat oil, cook spinach and lemon zest until spinach is wilted. Transfer to bowl. Stir in the roasted peppers and basil.
3. Line baking sheet with foil and spray with cooking spray.
4. Cut a slit in the side of the fish to make a pocket for the stuffing. Stuff spinach mixture into each fillet. Transfer to the baking sheet. Spread a little mustard on each fillet.
5. In a bowl mix together bread crumbs, oregano, garlic powder and pepper. Sprinkle over the fish. Lightly spray the tops with cooking spray.
6. Bake for 12-15 minutes, until done.
