# Teriyaki Grilled Salmon with Corn-Peach Salsa

*Makes 4 servings.*

## Ingredients
- 3 tsp teriyaki sauce
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 tsp lime juice
- 1/4 onion, finely diced
- 1/4 cup cilantro, minced
- 4 salmon fillets, skinless
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 ears corn
- 1 fresh peach, chopped

## Instructions
1. Coat grate with nonstick spray; preheat grill.
2. Combine first 3 ingredients in baking dish, stir well and marinate salmon for 5 to 10 minutes.
3. Mix remaining ingredients together for salsa; set aside for 1 hour.
4. Remove salmon from marinade; discard marinade.
5. Grill salmon uncovered over medium-high heat for 4 to 5 minutes on each side, or until flaky and fish has reached a safe internal temperature of 145°F.
6. Remove fillets from grill garnish and top with salsa mixture.
7. Garnish with lime wedges, if desired.
