# Grilled Salmon, Greens, and Balsamic Vinaigrette

## Ingredients
- ⅔ cup olive oil
- ½ cup balsamic vinegar
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- salt
- freshly ground black pepper
- 4 – 6 cups mixed greens including Bibb, leaf, arugula, and endive, torn into bite size pieces
- 4 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
- ¾ cup pecan halves, toasted
- 1½ cup ripe papaya or mango, peeled, seeded and cut into bite size pieces
- 3 small Italian plum tomatoes, quartered
- 1 avocado, peeled, seeded and thinly sliced
- 1 12 – 16 ounces salmon fillet

## Instructions
1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat.
2. In a bowl whisk together olive oil, balsamic vinegar, Dijon mustard, thyme, salt and pepper.
3. Place the salmon on the grill skin side down and cook for about 4 minutes.
4. Turn over and brush with some of the vinaigrette and continue to cook for another 2-4 minutes or until cooked through.
5. In a large bowl combine greens, feta cheese, pecans, papaya or mango, tomatoes and avocado.
6. Drizzle with remaining vinaigrette and toss gently to coat.
