# Salmon – Martine’s recipe

## Ingredients
- Tony Chaceres Original Creole Seasoning, green round box
- Cajun's Choice Creole seasoning
- Sundried Tomato Pesto (Chris Ranch is brand she has)
- Soft butter (she uses some sort of healthy butter)
- Dash of garlic salt

## Instructions
1. Before barbequing, rub into it Tony Chaceres Original Creole Seasoning and Cajun's Choice Creole seasoning.
2. When about three minutes from cooked, mix together Sundried Tomato Pesto, Soft butter, and a dash of garlic salt.
3. Slather on salmon and it will melt in.
