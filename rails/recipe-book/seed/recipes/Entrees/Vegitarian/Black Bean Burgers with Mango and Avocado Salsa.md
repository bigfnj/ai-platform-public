# Black Bean Burgers with Mango and Avocado Salsa

## Ingredients
- 2 (15 oz) cans black beans, rinsed and drained
- ¾ cup finely chopped fresh cilantro, divided
- ¾ cup Monterey cheese
- ¼ cup panko crumbs
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ½ jalapeno, finely chopped
- 2 large egg whites, lightly beaten

## Salsa Ingredients
- 1 ¼ cups peeled and chopped mango
- 3 tbsp chopped shallots
- 1 ½ tbsp lime juice
- 1 avocado, peeled and chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced

## Instructions
1. Place black beans in a bowl and mash with fork.
2. Add half the cilantro and rest of burger ingredients. Mix well.
3. Cook about 3-4 minutes each side.
4. For the salsa – mix salsa ingredients in bowl.
5. Place burgers on bun and top with salsa, lettuce and other toppings of choice.
