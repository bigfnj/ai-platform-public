# Vegetable Parmesan

## Ingredients
- Olive oil for drizzling
- Kosher salt
- Ground black pepper
- 1 med. Eggplant, cut into ¼ inch thick slices
- 2 med. Fennel bulbs, trimmed and sliced into ¼ inch thick slices
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into thirds
- 1 yellow bell pepper, cut into thirds
- 1 orange bell pepper, cut into thirds
- 2 zucchini, trimmed and cut lengthwise into ¼ inch thick slices
- 1 (28-ounce) jar marinara sauce
- 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 cup grated Parmesan
- 1 cup plain bread crumbs

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375°. Grease a 13x9 inch bake pan.
2. Drizzle the eggplant, fennel and peppers slices with olive oil. Season with salt and pepper. Grill the vegetables at 375° for about 15-20 minutes until soft.
3. Spoon ¾ cup of the marinara sauce over the bottom of the baking dish. Arrange the eggplant on bottom of dish. Sprinkle with 1 cup mozzarella and 1/3 cup of Parmesan cheese. Arrange the peppers as the next layer with both cheeses. Arrange the fennel on top and cover with remaining sauce and cheese.
4. Sprinkle the bread crumbs over the cheese and drizzle liberally with oil. Bake until top is golden and forms a crust. About 30- 40 minutes.
5. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.
