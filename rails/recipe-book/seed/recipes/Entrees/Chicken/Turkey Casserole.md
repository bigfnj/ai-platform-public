# Turkey-Sausage Casserole

## Ingredients
- 1 pound sausage links
- 1 stick butter
- ¾ pound mushrooms
- 4 tablespoons flour
- 1½ cups chicken broth
- ½ cup half-and-half or milk
- 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 teaspoon prepared mustard
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Freshly ground pepper
- 3 to 4 cups cooked turkey
- 1 cup packaged herb stuffing

## Instructions
1. Heat sausage in a pan for about 10 minutes.
2. In a large pan, melt the butter.
3. Sauté the mushrooms, stirring occasionally, until most of the liquid has evaporated.
4. Sprinkle in the flour and cook over low heat, stirring constantly, for 1 minute.
5. Stir in the chicken broth and milk.
6. Cook over moderate heat, stirring constantly, until mixture comes to a boil.
7. Cook for 1 more minute.
8. Remove from heat and stir in cheese, mustard, salt, pepper, turkey, and sausage.
9. Pour into a greased casserole dish.
10. Prepare the stuffing.
11. Spread over the casserole and bake uncovered at 350°F for 20 minutes, or until top is browned and sauce is bubbling.
