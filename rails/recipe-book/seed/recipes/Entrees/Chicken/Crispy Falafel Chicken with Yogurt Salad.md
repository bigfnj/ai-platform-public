# Crispy Falafel Chicken with Yogurt Salad

## Ingredients
- ½ cup prepared falafel mix
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- 4 boneless chicken breasts
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Milk

- ½ cup plain yogurt
- 3 cucumbers, diced
- ¼ cup fresh mint leaves, roughly torn
- ¼ cup fresh or dried dill
- ¼ red onion, diced
- 1 small clove garlic, minced
- 1 ½ tsp. kosher salt
- 1 lemon

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Place falafel mix in a pie plate or shallow dish.
3. Heat oil in a large, heavy skillet.
4. Dip chicken in milk.
5. Season chicken with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Coat chicken with the falafel mix, shaking off any excess.
7. Place chicken in pan.
8. Cook until browned, turning once, about 2-3 minutes per side.
9. Transfer the chicken to a baking sheet; bake until done. If you have a rack to fit on baking sheet use it so the chicken gets crisp, not soggy.

10. To make salad, toss the mint, cucumbers, dill, onion, garlic and salt in a small bowl.
11. Finely grate the zest from the lemon into the salad and squeeze in the lemon’s juice.
12. Stir the yogurt into the salad.
