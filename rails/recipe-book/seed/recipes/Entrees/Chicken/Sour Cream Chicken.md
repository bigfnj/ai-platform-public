# Sour Cream Chicken

## Ingredients
- 4-6 boneless chicken breasts
- 1 can beef consume
- 8 oz container sour cream
- ½ stick butter
- 4 TBSP oil
- ½ cup Romano cheese
- Salt
- Pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Pat dry the chicken, salt and pepper on both sides.
3. Melt the butter and oil together.
4. Sauté chicken on both sides until golden brown.
5. Transfer chicken to a pyrex dish.
6. Add ¾ can of the consume and bake for 15 minutes.
7. Remove from oven and add the sour cream and continue to bake until chicken is done.
8. Remove from oven.
9. Sprinkle with the Romano cheese, run under broiler until the cheese is melted or golden brown, about 2-3 minutes.
