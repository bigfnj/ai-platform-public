# Broccoli Chicken Casserole

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts chopped
- 2 packages frozen broccoli
- 1 cup short grain rice cooked
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 1 cup grated cheese
- 1 teaspoon curry powder (or more)
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 375.
2. Sprinkle chicken with black pepper and sauté until white. (can use cooked chicken or turkey)
3. Mix cooked chicken with broccoli, cooked rice, cream of chicken soup, mayonnaise and curry.
4. Top with cheese and bake for 30 minutes.
