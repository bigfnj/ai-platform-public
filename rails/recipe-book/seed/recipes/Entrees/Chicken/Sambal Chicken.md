# Sambal Chicken

## Ingredients
- 8 skewers (soaked in water for at least an hour)
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ½ cup rice vinegar
- ⅓ cup hot chili paste
- ¼ cup fish sauce
- ¼ cup Sriracha
- 2 tsp. ginger
- 1 lb chicken

## Instructions
1. In bowl whisk ingredients together.
2. Add chicken to coat.
3. Transfer marinade to small pot and bring to boil.
4. Reduce heat to simmer and simmer until reduced by half.
5. Grill or bake chicken and baste often with reduced marinade.
