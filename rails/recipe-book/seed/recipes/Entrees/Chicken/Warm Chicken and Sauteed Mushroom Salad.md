# Warm Chicken and Sautéed Mushroom Salad

*Serves 4*

## Ingredients
- 5 oz mixed greens (use a combo of lettuce, arugula, savoy cabbage, and spinach)
- 2 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1/2 lb. mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tsp garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced
- 4 oz goat cheese, crumbled
- 1/4 cup toasted pine nuts

## Instructions
1. Put greens and chicken in a large serving bowl; set aside.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
3. Add mushrooms and garlic and cook until browned and fragrant about 5 minutes (longer if mushrooms give off a lot of water).
4. Add remaining 2 tbsp oil, the vinegar, and mustard, stirring to combine.
5. Cook until mixture starts to boil.
6. Pour over greens, tossing to coat.
7. Top with onion, goat cheese, and pine nuts.
