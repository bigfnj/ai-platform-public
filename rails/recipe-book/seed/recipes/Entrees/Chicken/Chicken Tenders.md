# Chicken Tenders

## Ingredients
- Panko Crumbs
- Egg Wash
- Onion Powder
- Garlic Powder
- Parmesan Cheese
- Smoked Paprika
- Italian Seasoning
- Seasoned Salt
- Pinch of Salt
- Butter

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Mix all ingredients in a bag.
3. Cover chicken with seasoning and drizzle with butter.
4. Cook for 7 minutes and flip.
5. Once done, drizzle with salt.
