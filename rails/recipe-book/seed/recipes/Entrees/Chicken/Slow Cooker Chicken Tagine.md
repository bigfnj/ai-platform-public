# Slow Cooker Chicken Tagine

*Serves 4*

## Ingredients
- 400g chicken thighs (skinless and boneless)
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 5cm piece fresh ginger, grated
- ½ tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp Ras El Hanout
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- Pinch of saffron
- 400ml chicken stock, hot
- Pepper
- 3 Belazu Preserved Lemons, cut in half
- Handful of fresh mint and fresh flat leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp Belazu Rose Harissa Paste
- Handful small green and black olives
- Belazu Barley Couscous with a chopped preserved lemon and extra rose harissa, to serve

## Instructions
1. Place all the ingredients except the herbs and harissa paste in the slow cooker. Cook on low for 8 hours or on high for 4 hours.
2. 1 hour before serving, add most of the fresh herbs, a tbsp of Belazu Rose Harissa Paste and the olives.
3. Serve with Belazu Barley Couscous – I added a chopped preserved lemon to the couscous whilst it was steaming and stirred through a tbsp of rose harissa.
4. Sprinkle the remaining fresh herbs over and serve with a generous dollop of rose harissa on top!
