# Moroccan Lamb or Beef with Olives and Preserved Lemon

*Serves: 4*

*Cooking Time: 60 min*

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lbs beef or lamb, cut into 3 inch pieces
- 1 large onion, finely chopped or grated
- 2 cloves of garlic, chopped very fine or pressed
- 1/4 C. olive oil
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/2 tsp ginger
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp saffron threads, crumbled
- 1/4 C. chopped parsley and/or cilantro
- 2 1/2 to 3 cups water
- 1 handful red or green olives, pitted
- 1 preserved lemon, quartered and seeds removed

## Instructions
1. In a large pot, mix the meat with the onions, garlic, oil, butter, salt, pepper, ginger, and turmeric. Heat the meat over medium-high heat, and stir to brown all sides.
2. Add the water, saffron, parsley and cilantro. Cover the pot, and bring the liquids to a fast simmer.
3. Simmer, covered over medium heat for 1 1/2 to 2 hours, or until the meat is quite tender. Stir occasionally and add a little water if necessary to prevent the meat from burning. When the meat is cooked, add the olives and lemon, and reduce the liquids until they are mostly oils or have the appearance of a thick sauce.
4. Serve with a side of oven fries, and some crusty bread to scoop up the thick sauce.
