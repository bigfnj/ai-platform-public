# Indian Curry Shrimp

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- ½ onion, chopped
- ¾ pound raw shrimp
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tablespoons Calcutta Heat seasoning
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons lime juice

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in skillet over medium heat.
2. Sauté onion until tender.
3. Add shrimp, garlic, seasoning, milk and juice, stir well.
4. Simmer, covered for 10 minutes or until shrimp is opaque and sauce is thickened.
5. Serve over rice.
