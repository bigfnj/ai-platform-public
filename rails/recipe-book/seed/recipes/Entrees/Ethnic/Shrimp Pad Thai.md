# Shrimp Pad Thai

## Ingredients
### For Shrimp:
- 3 Tbsp. tamarind pulp
- 1 cup boiling water
- ¼ c. orange juice
- 3 Tbsp. rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp. honey
- ¼ c. Thai basil, or regular basil with some mint
- Salt & pepper
- ½ c. canola oil
- 1 pound raw shrimp, shelled and deveined

### For Pad Thai:
- 12 oz. dried flat rice noodles, or Pad Thai noodles
- 3 Tbsp. tamarind pulp
- 1 cup boiling water
- ½ c. soy sauce
- ¼ c. brown sugar
- 2 Tbsp. hot sauce (Sriracha sauce)
- 1 c. peanut oil
- 4 shallots, sliced
- 1 pkg. firm tofu, cut into cubes
- 4 eggs
- Salt & pepper
- 1 Tbsp. water
- 1 bunch scallions, cut up
- 4 garlic cloves finely chopped
- 2 cups bean sprouts
- Thai basil
- Cilantro leaves
- ½ c. roasted peanuts

## Instructions
### For Shrimp:
1. Place tamarind in boiling water until soft.
2. Press pulp out into a bowl and discard seeds.
3. Combine orange juice, tamarind, vinegar, honey, basil and salt and pepper, blend until smooth.
4. Strain mixture.
5. Add 6 Tbsp. oil and blend until emulsified.
6. Heat remaining oil in pan.
7. Season shrimp with salt and pepper add to pan.
8. Stir in tamarind glaze, turn the shrimp over continue to cook until shrimp is cooked through and glazed.

### For Pad Thai:
1. Heat noodles in boiling water until tender, drain.
2. Cover with damp paper towel to keep warm
3. Soak tamarind in boiling water until soft.
4. Press pulp out into the water save the water.
5. Add soy sauce, brown sugar, hot sauce to tamarind water stir until sugar has dissolved.
6. Set aside.
7. Heat oil in skillet, sauté shallots until golden brown, remove from skillet to paper towel .
8. Fry tofu until golden brown on all sides, transfer onto paper towel.
9. Lightly beat eggs with salt, pepper and tbsp. water.
10. Heat 2 Tbsp. of the shallot oil in skillet add the eggs and cook stirring gently until cooked through.
11. Break into chunks and transfer to bowl.
12. Heat shallot oil, add scallions, garlic for about 1 minute.
13. Add noodles to pan and stir fry, add all ingredients to heat through including the sauce.
