# Bombshell Bags

## Ingredients
- 1 box rice-a-roni Spanish Rice
- 1 can (14.5 oz) stewed tomatoes
- 2 bell peppers (red & yellow)
- 1 onion
- 2 jalapenos
- 1 lb spicy sausage (jalapeno sausage)
- garlic powder
- paprika
- salt
- pepper
- red pepper flakes
- cayenne pepper
- olive oil
- butter

## Instructions
1. Dice up the bell peppers, onion & jalapenos (remove seeds if desired). Get a large pan and coat with olive oil.
2. Saute the diced vegetables together, add a little salt/pepper & red pepper flakes. Coat the vegetables with a generous portion of paprika and then stir in.
3. Once the onions begin to caramelize, add the sausage. Do one more round of salt and pepper (not much) and stir in the sausage. A second time, coat the entire pan of veggies & sausage with a generous coat of paprika.
4. Stir frequently (breaking down the sausage) until the sausage is completely cooked. A final coat of paprika, and two or three taps of cayenne pepper. Stir until uniformly distributed. Everything should have a slight red hue to it.
5. Set aside and cover, allowing the steam to keep everything moist.
6. Now begin making the rice - follow the directions on the box but basically... brown the rice with a quarter stick of butter on low heat. Once brown add 2 cups of water, seasoning packet and the can of stewed tomatoes, turn up the heat and bring to a boil. Once boiling turn heat to a simmer and put the lid on. Rice should take 15-20 minutes to complete.
7. When the rice is completed, combine with the veggies & sausage.
8. Coat a cupcake pan to prepare for awesome Bombshell Bags
9. Pre-heat oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
10. Take a serving spoon and place 1 spoonful into the middle of a 6 inch round dough. Fold the four corners up and twist, rip off the excess once the "Bag" is sealed and save excess for more dough!
11. Place the "Bag" into an open cupcake slot in your cupcake pan.
12. Once you have a dozen and your cupcake pan is full, take a small bowl and put in 2 egg whites. Use a brush and coat the top of your "Bags" with egg white. This will give it a nice golden brown color when cooked and helps keep it moist.
13. Take a toothpick and poke about 5 or so holes in the top of the "Bag". If you don't they may spring a leak and nobody likes that.
14. Turn oven down to 350 degrees Fahrenheit and place inside. Let cook 15-20 minutes or until golden brown on top, slight crisping around the top portion of the bag is to be expected.
15. Pull out and let cool 10-15 minutes and serve.
