# Mexican Chicken Casserole

## Ingredients
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 can cheddar cheese soup
- 1 (10 ounce) can Mexican tomatoes
- 1 chicken, shredded (about 4 cups)
- 1 package flour or corn tortillas
- 2 cups shredded Cheddar and Pepper Jack cheese
- 1 can diced chilies
- 1 package taco seasoning
- 1 can El-Pato sauce

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine the soups, chicken, chilies, tomatoes, El-Pato sauce, and taco seasoning.
3. Spray a casserole dish with Pam.
4. Layer the chicken mixture and tortillas, beginning with the tortillas, and ending with tortillas.
5. Sprinkle cheese between layers and on top.
6. Bake for 30 minutes.
