# Ribs

## Ingredients
- dry rub
- bbq sauce

## Instructions
1. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30 minutes bone side up.
2. Drop to 225°F (110°C) for 1½ hours bone side up.
3. Pull out and open up and baste with BBQ sauce, put back in oven for 30 minutes.
4. Pull out and open up and baste other side with BBQ sauce, put back in oven for 30 minutes.
5. Pull out and finish on grill.

## Shopping List
- dry rub
- bbq sauce
