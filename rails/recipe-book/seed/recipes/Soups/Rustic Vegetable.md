# Rustic Vegetable & Polenta Soup

## Ingredients
- 3 Tbsp. olive oil
- 2 med. Carrots, diced
- 1 med. Onion, chopped
- Salt & pepper
- 2 Tbsp. chopped fresh flat leaf parsley
- 2 Tbsp. chopped fresh thyme
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 5 plum tomatoes, diced
- 4 med. Zucchini, diced into ½ inch pieces
- 4 cups low sodium chicken broth
- 1/3 cup instant polenta, such as Gia Russa
- 1 Tbsp. butter, at room temperature

## Instructions
1. In a heavy saucepan, heat the oil over med.-high heat.
2. Add the carrots, onions, 3/4 tbsp. salt and 1 tsp. pepper. Cook stirring frequently, until the onion begins to brown.
3. Add the parsley, thyme, garlic, tomatoes and zucchini, and cook for 3 minutes.
4. Add the chicken broth and bring to a boil.
5. Slowly stir in the polenta and cook until the soup thickens and the vegetables are tender, about 8 minutes.
6. Stir in the butter and season with salt and pepper.
