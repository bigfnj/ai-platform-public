# Pea and Basil Soup

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. olive oil
- 1 Tbsp. butter
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves , diced
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. cayenne pepper
- ½ Tbsp. crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 ½ pounds frozen peas
- ¾ cups chopped basil leaves
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups milk
- Mozzarella cheese (soft) to top off and diced roasted red bell pepper, from jar

## Instructions
1. In a pot over medium heat add the oil, butter, onions, garlic salt and pepper.
2. Cook until onions are soft, add the peas and basil, until peas are thawed.
3. Blend the mixture in blender with some broth.
4. Return pureed mixture to the pot.
5. Stir in any remaining broth and the milk.
6. Heat through and serve with cheese and roasted pepper.
