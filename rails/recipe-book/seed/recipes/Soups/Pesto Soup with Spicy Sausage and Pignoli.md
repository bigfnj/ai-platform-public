# Pesto Soup with Spicy Sausage and Pignoli

## Ingredients
- 6 links spicy sausage
- 1 onion, chopped
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups beef broth
- 2 large Maine potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 1 tablespoon fresh basil, chopped
- ½ cup pesto
- 2 (15 oz) cans cannellini beans, drained
- 8 cups baby spinach
- ½ cup heavy cream
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground pepper
- 8 oz Parmesan cheese

## Instructions
1. Remove casing from sausage.
2. Brown the sausage.
3. Add onion and garlic and cook until translucent.
4. Add broth, potato, basil.
5. Bring to boil, reduce heat and cook until potato is soft.
6. Add pesto, beans and spinach.
7. Cook until spinach is wilted.
8. Add cream, salt, sugar and pepper and stir until ingredients are evenly mixed.
9. Top with parmesan cheese.
