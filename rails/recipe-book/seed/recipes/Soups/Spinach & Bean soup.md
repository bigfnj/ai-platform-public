# Soup with Spinach & Beans

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup olive oil, plus some for drizzling on bread
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 carrot, chopped
- 4 oz pancetta, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, 1 minced, 1 whole
- 1 tsp salt
- ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 pound frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
- 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 1 (15 oz) can cannelloni beans, drained
- 1 tbsp herbs de Provence
- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1 bay leaf
- Parmesan cheese rind for soup, & grated for serving
- Ciabatta rolls, halved or a loaf, sliced

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in pot over medium heat.
2. Add onions, carrot, pancetta, minced garlic, salt, and pepper. Cook until onion is golden brown and pancetta is crisp.
3. Add tomato paste and stir until dissolved.
4. Add tomatoes, stirring to scrape the bottom of pan with a wooden spoon to release all the brown bits.
5. Add spinach, beans, herbs, stock, bay leaf, and Parmesan rind.
6. Bring soup to a boil, reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes.
7. Preheat oven to 350°F, drizzle bread with oil. Toast until golden brown.
8. Remove from oven and rub the top with the whole garlic clove.
9. Place toast in soup bowl and ladle the soup over the toast.
10. Sprinkle with Parmesan and serve.
