# Hearty Meatball Stew

## Ingredients
- 1 pound ground meat
- 1 pound spicy Italian sausage
- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon chopped flat-leaf parsley
- salt, to taste
- pepper, to taste
- bread crumbs
- all-purpose flour
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1 large red bell pepper, cut into strips
- ½ pound white mushrooms, sliced
- 1 large russet potato, sliced
- ½ pound green beans, cut up
- 1 can diced tomatoes, drained
- red pepper flakes
- 1 ½ cups chicken broth

## Instructions
1. For meatballs: Mix together ground meat, sausage, bread crumbs, egg, parsley, salt and pepper.
2. Make the meatballs and place them on baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
3. Sprinkle the meatballs with flour to coat.
4. Heat a large pot over medium heat; add a little olive oil.
5. Add meatballs and cook until just browned on all sides.
6. Transfer the meatballs to a plate.
7. For Stew: Heat a little more oil in the same pot.
8. Add the onions, peppers, salt & pepper.
9. Cook until soft.
10. Add the mushrooms and cook until soft.
11. Add the potatoes, beans, tomatoes, red pepper flakes, broth and meatballs.
12. Bring to a boil.
13. Reduce heat to a simmer.
14. Cover and cook until potatoes are tender and the meatballs are cooked through.
