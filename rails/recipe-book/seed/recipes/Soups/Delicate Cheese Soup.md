# Delicate Cheese Soup

## Ingredients
- 1 large onion, peeled and sliced
- 2 cups water
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon freshly ground white pepper
- 2 cups half & half, warmed
- ½ cup grated gruyere cheese
- Poppy seed croutons
- 2 tablespoons chopped chives

## Instructions
1. Cook the onion in 2 cups of water for 15 minutes or until tender.
2. Drain, save the liquid and discard onion.
3. Melt the butter in a soup pot.
4. Blend in flour, salt and pepper.
5. Blend cream and onion liquid gradually.
6. Cook, stirring constantly over low heat, until thickened and smooth.
7. Add cheese and stir until melted.
8. Serve at once in warm cream-soup bowls.
9. Garnish with poppy seed croutons and chopped chives.
