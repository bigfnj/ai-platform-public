# Butternut Squash Soup

## Ingredients
- 4 bags of butternut squash (from Trader Joes)
- 20 cloves garlic
- 6 Tbsp. olive oil
- ¼ cup water
- 2 leeks, green and white parts, finely chopped
- 5 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- Salt
- White pepper
- 4 Tbsp. chopped chives

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Combine squash, garlic, olive oil, salt and pepper. Place in roasting pan or large cookie sheet. Add water and roast until golden brown (about 45-50 min.) remove from oven and let cool slightly.
3. In a skillet heat a little olive oil. Add the leeks and sauté until golden brown.
4. In a blender (or hand held blender) combine in batches the squash, garlic and leek with a little broth. Blend until smooth.
5. Add remaining broth bring to a simmer over medium heat. Season to taste with salt and white pepper.
6. Serve with chopped chives.
