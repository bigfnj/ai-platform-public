# Matzoh Balls

## Ingredients
- 2 large eggs, beaten lightly
- 3 Tbsp. seltzer
- 2 Tbsp. rendered chicken fat (Nyfat), melted
- ¼ tsp. Kosher salt
- ¼ tsp. black pepper
- ½ cup Matzoh Meal

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl mix the eggs, seltzer, chicken fat, salt and pepper.
2. Gradually stir in the matzoh meal until everything is well combined.
3. Chill, covered, in the refrigerator at least 4 hours and up to 8 hours.
