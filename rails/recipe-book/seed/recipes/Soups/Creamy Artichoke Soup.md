# Creamy Artichoke Soup

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. extra virgin olive oil
- 2 leeks, white part only, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 small potato, chopped
- 1 (8 ounce) package frozen artichoke hearts, thawed
- 2 cups chicken broth
- ½ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. ground pepper
- 2 Tbsp. mascarpone cheese
- Chopped chives for garnish

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in pot over medium heat.
2. Add the leeks and the garlic and stir.
3. Add the potatoes and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring often.
4. Add the artichokes, broth, salt and pepper and cook until the vegetables are tender, about 20 minutes.
5. Using a handheld immersion blender or in a blender blend until smooth.
6. Add the cheese and continue to blend until all is combined.
7. Serve with chives.
