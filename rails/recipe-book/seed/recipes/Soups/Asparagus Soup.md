# Asparagus Soup

## Ingredients
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 2-2.5 cans asparagus
- 2 cups milk
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/2 tsp cornstarch
- 1 chicken bouillon cube

## Instructions
1. Sauté ½ onion.
2. Add 2-2 ½ cans canned asparagus.
3. Add 2 cups milk.
4. Add ½ tsp salt and pepper.
5. Add ½ tsp cornstarch.
6. Bring to a boil.
7. Add cube of chicken stock/mix.
8. Cool then blend.
