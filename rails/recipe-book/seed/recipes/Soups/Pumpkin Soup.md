# Pumpkin Soup

## Ingredients
- 2 cups water
- 1 medium potato, peeled and chopped
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 small leek, chopped (about 1 - 1½ cups)
- 1 tablespoon dry white wine
- ½ teaspoon dry mustard
- ½ teaspoon saffron
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon white pepper
- 1 cup canned pumpkin
- ½ carrot, chopped
- 1¼ cans creamed corn
- 2½ cups milk

## Instructions
1. Boil potato in 2 cups water until tender.
2. Drain and save the broth.
3. Melt butter in a soup pot over medium heat.
4. Add the leek, wine, mustard, saffron, salt, and pepper.
5. Sauté for 5 minutes.
6. Add the potato, broth from potato, pumpkin, carrots, half the corn, the 2 cups water, stir and bring to a boil.
7. Reduce heat, simmer covered for 15 minutes.
8. In the blender, blend half the soup with the milk until thick and silky smooth.
9. Return blended soup to pot and stir, and add remaining corn.
