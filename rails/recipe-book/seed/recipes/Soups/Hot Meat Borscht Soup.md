# Hot Meat Borscht Soup

## Ingredients
- 4 cans whole beets
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 quarts water
- 2 cans beef broth
- 2 pounds short ribs or breast flank beef bones
- 2 ½ teaspoons salt
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- 1 cup chopped cabbage
- 4 tablespoons brown sugar
- ½ cup lemon juice
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 2 cans diced tomatoes
- 2 potatoes, chopped

## Instructions
1. Combine beets, onions, garlic, water and meat, broth, tomatoes, and potatoes in a large saucepan.
2. Bring to a boil.
3. Cover over medium heat for 1 ½ hours.
4. Add the salt, pepper, and cabbage and stir.
5. Cook uncovered for 15 minutes.
6. Add the lemon juice and sugar.
7. Cook for 15 minutes.
8. Correct taste, if necessary add more lemon juice or sugar.
9. Beat the eggs in a bowl, add some soup to the eggs to incorporate the eggs with the hot soup.
10. Gradually add the eggs to the soup.
