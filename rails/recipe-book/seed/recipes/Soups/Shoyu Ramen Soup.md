# Shoyu Ramen Soup

## Ingredients
- 9 oz organic ramen noodles
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 6 tbsp soy sauce
- pinch of salt
- 1 onion, chopped
- bunch of basil
- bok choy

## Instructions
1. Heat the broth to a boil.
2. Add soy sauce and salt until heated.
3. Cook noodles until soft.
4. Serve noodles, top with broth and trimmings.
