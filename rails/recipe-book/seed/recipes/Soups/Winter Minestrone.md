# Winter Minestrone

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. olive oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 large carrots , peeled & chopped
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- Pkg. of diced pancetta
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 pound Swiss chard, stems trimmed, leaves coarsely chopped
- 1 large russet potato, peeled and cubed
- Salt & pepper
- 1 (14 ½ ounce ) can roasted diced tomatoes in juice
- 2 fresh rosemary sprigs
- 1 (15-ounce ) can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (14 ounce) cans beef , chicken or vegetable broth
- Parmesan ring, cut up into small pieces
- ¼ cup dry parsley

## Instructions
1. In a pot or Dutch oven, heat the oil over medium heat.
2. Add the onions, carrots, celery, pancetta, and garlic. Cook, stirring frequently, until the onions are soft.
3. Add the Swiss chard and potato. Season with salt and pepper and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Stir in the tomatoes and rosemary sprigs. Bring the mixture to a boil.
5. Reduce the heat and simmer until the chard is wilted and the tomatoes are soft.
6. In a food processor or blender, combine ½ of the beans with ½ cup of broth. Blend until almost smooth.
7. Add the pureed bean mixture, and remaining broth, and Parmesan rind to the vegetable mixture.
8. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until the potato pieces are tender.
9. Stir in the remaining beans and parsley.
10. Simmer until the beans are heated through and the soup is thick.
11. Discard the rosemary stems (the leaves will have fallen off) and season with salt and pepper, to taste.
