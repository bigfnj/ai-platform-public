# Udon Noodles with Mushrooms

## Ingredients
- 10 cups vegetable broth
- 3 cups water
- 1 1/2 cups chopped cilantro
- Shitake mushrooms, chopped
- Fresh ginger , sliced thin
- 1 tbsp tamari sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- Napa cabbage, sliced
- snow peas
- carrots cut into cubes
- bok choy chopped
- Japanese style udon noodles
- Green onions chopped
- Tofu, cut into cubes
- Salt & pepper

## Instructions
1. In large pot bring broth and water to a simmer.
2. Add cilantro, mushrooms and ginger and cover and simmer for 30 minutes.
3. Strain broth, discard cilantro and ginger.
4. Reserve mushrooms with broth.
5. Add tamari sauce and rice vinegar.
6. Return broth and mushrooms to pot.
7. Bring to boil cook carrots for about 3 minutes,
8. add rest of vegetable and cook for a few minutes until tender.
9. Add tofu, cook until heated.
10. Add noodles until done about 3 minutes.
11. Season with salt & pepper.
