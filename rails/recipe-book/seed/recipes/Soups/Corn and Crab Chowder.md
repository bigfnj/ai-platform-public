# Corn and Crab Chowder

## Ingredients
- 1 Tbsp. vegetable oil or extra virgin olive oil
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- 2 russet potatoes, peeled and diced
- 2 ribs celery, chopped
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- 1 small red bell pepper, diced
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and pepper
- 1 Tbsp. Old Bay seasoning blend
- 2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- 3 Tbsp. flour
- 1 quart milk
- 3 cups corn kernels, fresh or frozen
- 8 ounces cooked lump crab meat
- 4 small bread rounds, hollowed

## Toppings:
- Oyster crackers
- Hot cayenne pepper sauce
- Sliced scallions

## Instructions
1. Heat a deep pot over moderate heat.
2. Add oil and butter.
3. Add the vegetables to the pot.
4. Add bay leaf and season vegetables with salt, pepper, and Old Bay seasoning.
5. Sauté for about 5 minutes, then sprinkle in flour.
6. Cook flour for 2 minutes, stirring constantly.
7. Stir in broth and combine.
8. Stir in milk and combine.
9. Bring soup to a bubble, add corn and crab meat, simmer soup for 5 minutes.
10. Adjust seasonings.
11. Remove bay leaf.
12. Ladle soup into bread bowls, top with toppings.
