# Vegetarian Chili Verde

## Ingredients
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 1 cup chopped onion
- One 8 ounce Yukon potato, peeled and cut into small cubes
- One 8 ounce yam , peeled and cut into small cubes
- 4 large cloves garlic, peeled and chopped
- 4 large tomatillos, husked, rinsed, cored and chopped
- 2 large poblano chilies, stemmed, seeded, 1 diced, 1 cut into strips
- 2-3 serrano chilies
- 2 Tbsp. dried oregano
- 1 Tbsp. flour
- 1 Tbsp. cumin
- Salt and pepper
- One 28 ounce can hominy with juices
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- One can diced green chilies

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a pot.
2. Add the onions, potatoes, yams, garlic, tomatillos, poblano and serrano chilis.
3. Cover and sweat until the onions are tender, stirring often to prevent browning, about 8 minutes.
4. Mix in the oregano, flour, cumin, 1 tsp. salt and ½ tsp. pepper.
5. Add the hominy with the juices and broth and bring to a simmer.
6. Place the green chilies in a processor.
7. Blend until smooth.
8. Scrape the chili sauce into the pot.
9. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes.
10. Uncover and simmer until the potatoes and yams are tender and chili is reduced to desired consistency. Stirring often, 20 to 25 minutes longer.
11. Season with salt and pepper if needed.
