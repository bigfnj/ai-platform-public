# Matzo Ball Soup

## Ingredients
- 1 whole chicken
- 2 containers (48 oz each) chicken broth
- 4 tbsp chicken bouillon
- 4 large carrots, cut into ¼ inch dice
- 1 large turnip, cut into ¼ inch dice
- 2 parsnips, cut into ¼ inch dice
- 1 leek, chopped
- 1 onion, cut into ¼ inch dice
- 2 zucchinis, sliced
- ½ cup fresh dill

## Instructions
1. Place chicken in large stock pot and fill with water.
2. Bring to boil, then simmer and cook until chicken is cooked.
3. Remove chicken and shred the dark meat.
4. Return to pot and add rest of the ingredients.
5. Simmer until vegetables are tender.
