# Celery Soup

## Ingredients
- ¼ cup (½ stick) butter
- 1 cup chopped celery
- ½ cup chopped shallots
- 2 lbs. celery root, chopped
- 1 large russet potato, peeled and cut into cubes
- 5 cups chicken broth
- 1 ½ tsp. thyme
- ¼ cup whipping cream

## Instructions
1. Melt butter in pot.
2. Add celery, cover and cook until tender.
3. Add shallots, sauté for about 3 minutes.
4. Add celery root, potato, broth and thyme.
5. Bring to a boil.
6. Reduce heat, cover and simmer until vegetables are tender, about 45 minutes.
7. Let cool slightly.
8. Puree vegetables in a blender until smooth.
9. Stir in cream and bring to a simmer.
10. Season to taste with salt and pepper if needed.
