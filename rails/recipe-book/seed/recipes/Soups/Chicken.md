# Chicken, Artichoke and Cannellini Bean Stew

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. olive oil
- 4 ounces diced pancetta
- Diced carrots
- 2 celery stocks, thinly chopped
- 1 small onion diced
- 4 cloves garlic chopped
- 1 tsp. salt & pepper
- 2 (14 ounces) cans chicken stock
- ½ cup packed chopped fresh basil leaves
- 3 Tbsp. tomato paste
- 2 tsp. dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 skinless chicken breasts
- 12 ounces frozen artichoke hearts, thawed and chopped into 1 inch pieces
- 1 (15 ounce) can cannellini beans, rinsed and drained

## Instructions
1. In a heavy pot, heat the oil over med heat, add the pancetta and cook stirring frequently, until brown and crispy.
2. Remove to paper towel to drain.
3. Add the carrots, celery, onion garlic, 1 tsp salt and pepper and cook until onion is translucent.
4. Stir in the chicken stock, basil, tomato paste, thyme and bay leaf.
5. Add the chicken and press down to submerge.
6. Bring the liquid to a simmer.
7. Reduce the heat to med-low and simmer, uncovered, turning the chicken over and stirring occasionally, about 20 minutes.
8. Add the artichokes and the beans and simmer until the chicken is cooked through and the liquid has reduced slightly. About 10-15 minutes
9. Remove the chicken and cool before cutting it up into bite size pieces.
10. Return chicken and pancetta to pan and simmer until warmed through.
11. Remove bay leaf.
