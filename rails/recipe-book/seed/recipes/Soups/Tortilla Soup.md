# Tortilla Soup

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp salad oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 can (4 oz) chopped green chilies
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 can beef broth
- 1 can chicken broth
- 1 ½ cups water
- 1 ½ cups tomato juice
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 Tbsp coriander (optional)
- 1 tsp salt
- ⅛ tsp pepper
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 Tbsp bottled steak sauce
- crushed tortilla chips
- shredded Cheddar cheese

## Instructions
1. In large pan heat oil.
2. Add onions, chilies, garlic, and sauté until soft.
3. Add tomatoes, beef and chicken broths, water, tomato juice, cumin, chili powder, salt and pepper, Worcestershire sauce, and steak sauce.
4. Bring soup to a boil, reduce heat, cover and simmer for about 45 min.
5. Add tortilla chips and cheese, cook for about 15 more min.
