# Slow Cooker Massaman Curry

## Ingredients
- approx 800g-1kg beef cheek
- 2 cans coconut milk
- 1 cup chicken stock
- handful cherry tomatoes
- 2 carrots
- 1 brown onion
- 1 potato
- 4 tbsp massaman curry paste (I always use Valcom brand)
- minced garlic
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- salt and pepper
- 1 tsp sugar
- few drops of fish sauce
- handful crushed peanuts

## Instructions
1. Cut the beef cheeks into bite size chunks.
2. Coat the beef chunks in flour, salt and pepper.
3. Fry beef in fry pan and brown the edges, then add to slow cooker.
4. Halve the cherry tomatoes, cut the onion, potato and carrots into thick chunks.
5. In the same fry pan, fry all the vegetables together for a few minutes.
6. Season veggies with salt and pepper and add some 2 tsp minced garlic.
7. Add veggies to slow cooker after 1-2 minutes.
8. Open the coconut milk cans (do not shake them) and scrape out the thick milk at the top. Add this thick milk to the fry pan.
9. Combine curry paste with the thick milk bits. Fry for a few minutes until reduced and a richer colour.
10. Add the rest of the coconut milk and stir until all combined.
11. Sprinkle curry with fish sauce and sugar (optional).
12. Add curry sauce to slow cooker.
13. Add cup of stock to slow cooker.
14. Cook on low for 7 hours.
15. Sprinkle crushed peanuts on curry.
16. Serve with rice.

## Shopping List
- beef cheek (approx 800g-1kg)
- coconut milk (2 cans)
- chicken stock (1 cup)
- cherry tomatoes (handful)
