# Garlic-Miso Chicken Soup

*Ready in under an hour.*

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 5 small carrots, peeled and thinly sliced (about 2 cups)
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped (about 1½ cups)
- 6 medium cloves garlic, finely chopped (about 2 tablespoons)
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 9 cups reduced-sodium chicken broth
- 4 cups shredded cooked white and dark meat chicken
- 12 ounces baby spinach (about 12 cups)
- 3 tablespoons white miso
- ¼ cup thinly sliced scallion

## Instructions
1. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add sliced carrots and chopped onion; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 6 to 8 minutes. Stir in chopped garlic and ¼ teaspoon salt; cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add 9 cups broth and bring to a boil over high heat.
2. Reduce heat to medium. Add 4 cups chicken; cook, stirring occasionally, for 2 minutes. Add 12 ounces spinach in batches; cook, stirring constantly, until wilted and vibrant green, 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Remove from heat. Transfer ¼ cup hot broth to a small heatproof bowl; stir in 3 tablespoons miso until dissolved. Return the miso mixture to the soup and stir to combine. Divide among 6 bowls. Top with ¼ cup sliced scallion.
