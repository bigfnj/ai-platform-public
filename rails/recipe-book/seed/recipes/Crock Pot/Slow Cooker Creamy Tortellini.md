# Slow Cooker Creamy Tortellini, Spinach and Chicken Soup

## Ingredients
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp dried basil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 3 cups packed spinach
- 2 (14.5 oz) cans petite tomatoes
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 4 cups frozen cheese tortellini
- 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup heavy cream or 1 cup half and half
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese

## Instructions
1. Saute the onions and garlic in oil and dried basil.
2. Sprinkle with flour and mix in tomato paste after onions have softened.
3. Transfer all contents to slow cooker.
4. Add chicken thighs, tomatoes (with juice), chicken broth, salt, and pepper.
5. Cook on low for 6-8 hours.
6. Remove the chicken from the crockpot and shred it with two forks.
7. Stir in spinach, tortellini, heavy cream or half and half, and Parmesan cheese.
8. Add the shredded chicken back into the slow cooker and stir to combine.
9. Cook on high for an additional 30 minutes.
