# Taco Soup

## Ingredients
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 2 cups diced onions
- 2 (15 ½ ounce) cans pinto beans
- 1 (15 ½ ounce) can kidney beans
- 1 (15 ½ ounce) can whole kernel corn, drained
- 1 (14 ½ ounce) can Mexican style stewed tomatoes
- 1 (14 ½ ounce) can diced tomatoes
- 1 (14 ½ ounce) can tomatoes with chilies
- 2 (4 ½ ounce) cans diced green chilies
- 1 (4.6 ounce) can black olives, drained
- 1 (1 ounce) package ranch salad dressing mix
- 1 (1 ¼ ounce) package taco seasoning mix
- Corn chips for serving
- Sour cream for garnish
- Grated cheese for garnish
- Chopped onions for garnish
- 2 to 3 cans of El Tapo sauce

## Instructions
1. Brown the beef and onions in a large skillet with salt and pepper, drain excess fat.
2. Transfer beef and onions to a slow cooker or stockpot.
3. Add the beans , corn, tomatoes, green chilies, black olives, taco seasoning and ranch dressing mix.
4. Cook in slow cooker on low for 6 to 8 hours or simmer over low heat for about 1 hour on the stove.
5. Serve with toppings.
