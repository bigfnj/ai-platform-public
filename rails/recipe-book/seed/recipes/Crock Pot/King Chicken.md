# King Ranch Chicken

*Slow cooker menus week vi*

## Ingredients
- 4 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, chopped
- 10.5 oz can cream of chicken soup
- 10.5 oz can cream of mushroom soup
- 10 oz can Ro-tel tomatoes
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 12 fajita size corn or flour tortillas
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions
1. Mix together first 7 ingredients.
2. Tear tortillas into 1-inch strips.
3. Place ⅓ tortilla pieces into a greased slow cooker.
4. Add ⅓ chicken mixture and ⅓ cup cheese.
5. Repeat layers twice.
6. Cover, and cook on low for 3½ hours.
7. Uncover, and continue to cook on low another 30 minutes.
