# Barbecue Stuffed Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 1½-2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 18 oz bottle barbecue sauce
- ½ cup Italian salad dressing
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- 2 tsps Worcestershire sauce
- 4-6 baking potatoes
- optional toppings: cheese, sour cream, and chives

## Instructions
1. Place chicken breasts into bottom of slow cooker.
2. Mix together barbecue sauce, Italian dressing, brown sugar and Worcestershire sauce. Pour on top of chicken.
3. Cover, and cook on low 5-6 hours or on high 3-4 hours.
4. Meanwhile, bake potatoes in oven or cook in slow cooker.
5. Shred chicken, and serve over potatoes; add desired toppings.
