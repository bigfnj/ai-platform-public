# Santa Fe Chicken

## Ingredients
- 1½ lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 15 oz can black beans, rinsed and drained
- 14 oz can chicken broth
- 10 oz can Ro-tel tomatoes
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 2 Tbsps dried cilantro
- 3 green onions, chopped
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, to taste)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- tortillas, rice or tortilla chips (optional)
- optional toppings: sour cream, shredded Mexican blend cheese and salsa

## Instructions
1. Mix together cilantro, green onions, cayenne pepper, cumin, garlic powder, and onion powder.
2. Add chicken on top of bean mixture.
3. Cover and cook on low 8 hours or high 3-4 hours in slow cooker.
4. Remove chicken from slow cooker, shred meat, return to slow cooker, and stir.
5. Serve over tortillas, rice or tortilla chips, and add your favorite taco toppings.
