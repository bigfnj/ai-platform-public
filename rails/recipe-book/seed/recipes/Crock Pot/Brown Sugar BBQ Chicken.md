# Brown Sugar BBQ Chicken

*Servings: 10*

## Ingredients
- 2.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast or thighs
- 1 cup barbecue sauce
- 1/4 cup dark brown sugar
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Instructions
1. Add all ingredients to a slow cooker. Cook on low for 4-6 hours or on high for 3-4 hours.
2. Shred chicken using two forks. Top with extra barbecue sauce if desired.
