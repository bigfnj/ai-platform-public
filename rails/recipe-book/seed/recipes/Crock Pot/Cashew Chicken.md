# Cashew Chicken

## Ingredients
- 2½ lbs bone-in chicken thighs with skin
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper
- ½ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
- 1 Tbsp honey
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh minced ginger
- 1 cup whole cashews
- 4 green onions, sliced
- cooked brown rice (optional)

## Instructions
1. Sprinkle chicken with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil over medium-high heat in a large skillet; add chicken, and brown about 2 minutes per side.
3. Remove chicken from skillet, and place into slow cooker.
4. In a small bowl, mix together soy sauce, garlic, ginger, apple cider vinegar, and honey; pour over chicken.
5. Cover, and cook on low for 3-4 hours.
6. Add in cashews; cover, and cook an additional 15 minutes on low.
7. Top with sliced green onions, and serve over rice.
