# Mongolian Beef

## Ingredients
- 2 lb flank steak, cut into strips
- ¼ cup cornstarch
- 1 Tbsp canola oil
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 large green onions, chopped
- ¾ cup soy sauce
- ¾ cup water
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- brown rice (optional)

## Instructions
1. Coat flank steak strips with cornstarch, and let rest.
2. Turn slow cooker on high, and add oil, ginger, garlic and stir to combine.
3. Stir in soy sauce, water and sugar, and combine; add in coated steak.
4. Cover, and cook on low 4-5 hours or high 2-3 hours until tender.
5. Serve over brown rice, and top with extra green onions, if desired.
