# Slow Cooker Bread

## Ingredients
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup lukewarm water
- 1 packet instant yeast
- 3 tbsp salt
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp garlic powder

## Instructions
1. Mix the water, sugar, and yeast and let rest for a second before adding all of the dry ingredients to mix.
2. Knead for about five minutes, no need to proof.
3. Roll into a ball and place into a slow cooker lined with parchment paper. Cook on high for around 2 hours.
4. Pull bread out after internal temp of about 190 degrees Fahrenheit.
5. Place into the oven to broil for about three minutes to brown the top.
