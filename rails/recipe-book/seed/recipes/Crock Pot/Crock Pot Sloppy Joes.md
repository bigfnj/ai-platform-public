# Easy Crock Pot Sloppy Joes

*Serves: approx. 6-8 burgers*

## Ingredients
- 2 pounds ground beef (or turkey)
- 2 cups chopped onion
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons prepared yellow mustard
- 3/4 cup ketchup
- 2 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- hamburger buns
- cheese, for serving

## Instructions
1. Brown ground beef or turkey in a saucepan.
2. Add the browned meat and remaining ingredients to slow cooker and cook on low for 4 - 6 hours.
3. Serve on buns with a sprinkling of cheese and enjoy!
