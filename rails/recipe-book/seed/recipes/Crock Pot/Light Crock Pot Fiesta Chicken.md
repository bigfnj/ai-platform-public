# Light Crock Pot Fiesta Chicken

## Ingredients
- 1 lb uncooked chicken, cubed
- 1 cup uncooked jambalaya rice
- 1 can (10 oz) enchilada sauce
- 1 can (4 oz) chopped green chilies
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 avocado
- 1 cup pico de gallo
- 1/2 cup queso fresco, crumbled
- OPTIONAL - 1 cup non-fat plain Greek yogurt or light sour cream

## Instructions
1. Spray your Crock Pot with non-stick spray and add the chicken, rice, enchilada sauce, green chilies, chicken broth, onion, cumin and salt. Stir to combine and cook on low for 2 hours.
