# Guinness Pulled Pork Sandwiches

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp coarse salt
- ¼ tsp ground black pepper
- 2.5 pounds boneless pork butt (may be labeled shoulder)
- 12 oz Guinness
- 3 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 small onion, chopped
- Hamburger buns

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. In a small bowl, combine brown sugar, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper. Rub it all over your pork butt. If your pork is wrapped in twine, cut and remove the twine.
2. Place pork, fat side up, in a Dutch oven and place on the stove. Add Guinness, garlic, and onions. Bring to a boil.
3. Cover, turn off the heat, transfer to the oven, and cook for 3-4 hours or until the pork is fork tender, basting a few times. Remove from the heat and let cool slightly. Shred pork with two forks (or hands if cool enough to handle) and stir to incorporate with braising liquid. Serve on hamburger buns.
