# Chicken & Dumplings

## Ingredients
- 1-2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 (10.5 oz) cans cream of chicken soup
- 14 oz can chicken broth
- ¼ cup water
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 2 (10 oz) cans store brand biscuits, cut in quarters

## Instructions
1. Place chicken breasts and remaining ingredients except biscuits into slow cooker.
2. Cover, and cook on low 6-7 hours.
3. 1 hour before dinner time, add quartered biscuits to slow cooker (I use kitchen scissors to cut the biscuits).
4. Gently stir in biscuits so that they are covered.
5. Cover, and cook 1 more hour on low, and serve.
