# Chicken Parmesan

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts - cut into 6 pieces
- 24 oz jar marinara sauce
- 14 oz can Italian style diced tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp Italian seasoning
- 1½ cups grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 Tbsps butter
- 1½ cups panko bread crumbs
- ½ tsp garlic salt
- ½ cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- pasta (optional)

## Instructions
1. Place chicken into the bottom of slow cooker.
2. Stir together marinara, tomatoes, garlic and Italian seasoning,
3. pour over chicken.
4. Cover, and cook on low 5-6 hours or until chicken is tender.
5. Sprinkle Parmesan over top of chicken, cover and cook on high an additional 30 minutes to melt cheese.
6. In a medium skillet, melt butter, add bread crumbs and garlic salt.
7. Cook 4 minutes or until golden brown, stirring often.
8. Before serving, sprinkle bread crumb mixture and a sprinkle of shredded mozzarella cheese over chicken. Serve with pasta, if desired.
