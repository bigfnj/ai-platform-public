# Slow-Cooker Creamy Tomato Basil Soup with Grilled Cheese Croutons

*Prep Total: 25 min 3 hr 10 min*

## Ingredients
- 2 cans (28 oz each) Muir Glen™ organic fire roasted crushed tomatoes, undrained
- 1½ cups vegetable broth
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon pepper
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- ⅓ cup fresh basil, finely chopped
- 4 slices Italian or homestyle white bread
- 2 tablespoons butter, softened
- 4 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
- 4 oz Cheddar cheese, thinly sliced

## Instructions
1. Spray 4-quart slow cooker with cooking spray.
2. In slow cooker, mix tomatoes, broth, onion, garlic, sugar, salt and pepper.
3. Cover; cook on High heat setting 3 hours or Low heat setting 6 hours.
4. Add whipping cream.
5. Cover; cook about 15 minutes or until hot.
6. Stir in basil.
7. Meanwhile, spread one side of each bread slice with butter.
8. Sprinkle 1 tablespoon Parmesan cheese on top of buttered side of each slice.
9. Press gently to adhere cheese to bread.
10. Heat 12-inch skillet over medium heat.
11. Place 2 slices bread, buttered side down, in skillet and top each with half of the Cheddar cheese.
12. Place remaining 2 slices of bread, buttered side up, on top of cheese.
13. Cook 5 to 7 minutes, turning once, until golden brown on both sides and cheese is melted.
14. Cool 5 minutes; cut each into 1-inch cubes.
15. Serve with soup.
