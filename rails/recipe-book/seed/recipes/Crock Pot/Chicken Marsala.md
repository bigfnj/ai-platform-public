# Chicken Marsala

## Ingredients
- 1½ lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1 cup flour
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- 3 Tbsps olive oil
- 8 oz pkg sliced fresh mushrooms
- ⅔ cup chicken broth
- 4 slices pre-cooked bacon
- ¼ cup fresh parsley
- pasta (optional)

## Instructions
1. Mix together flour, salt and pepper in a shallow bowl. Coat chicken with flour mixture, and shake off any excess flour.
2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat; brown chicken on both sides, and transfer to slow cooker until they start to brown;
3. Add mushrooms to skillet, and cook stir in chicken broth.
4. Pour mushroom mixture over chicken into slow cooker; cover, and cook on low 4 hours
5. Heat bacon according to package directions to make crispy; crumble.
6. Top chicken with crumbled bacon and parsley. Serve over pasta, if desired.
