# Italian Beef Sliders

## Ingredients
- 2-3 lb beef shoulder roast
- 16 oz jar pepperoncini peppers
- 1 oz pkg Au Jus gravy mix
- 4-6 hoagie rolls

## Instructions
1. Trim fat from roast.
2. Place roast into slow cooker; cover with undrained pepperoncini peppers.
3. Cover, and cook on low for 7-8 hours.
4. Remove peppers; shred beef, and mix in Au Jus gravy mix.
5. Cover, and cook another 30 minutes on low.
6. Serve on hoagie rolls.
