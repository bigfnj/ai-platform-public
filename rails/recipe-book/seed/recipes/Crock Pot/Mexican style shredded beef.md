# Mexican Style Shredded Beef

## Ingredients
- 4 lb beef shoulder or chuck roast
- 1 Tbsp ground cumin
- 1 Tbsp ground coriander
- 1 Tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp ground red pepper
- 1 cup salsa

## Instructions
1. Combine cumin, coriander, chili powder, salt and red pepper in a small bowl; rub all over roast.
2. Add ½ cup salsa to bottom of slow cooker; add roast and top with remaining salsa.
3. Cover, and cook on low 8-10 hours or until meat is tender.
4. Remove meat; trim and discard fat.
5. Shred roast, and add back to juices in cooker.
6. Serve alone, as tacos or even over baked potatoes!
