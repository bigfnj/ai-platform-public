# Brunswick Stew

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 15 oz can creamed corn
- 15 oz can whole kernel corn
- 15 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup chili sauce
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- ¾ cup barbecue sauce
- 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp dry mustard
- 2 medium russet potatoes, peeled and cubed

## Instructions
1. Place chicken into bottom of slow cooker, add remaining ingredients; stir.
2. Cover ,and cook on low 6-8 hours.
3. Remove chicken, and shred; return to slow cooker, and stir to combine.
4. Serve with extra barbecue sauce, if desired.
