# Potato Soup

## Ingredients
- 4 slices bacon
- 1½ cups chopped yellow onion
- 5 cups peeled and cubed russet potatoes (about five potatoes)
- 1 stalk celery, chopped
- 32 oz carton chicken broth
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- ½ cup flour
- 1½ cups half and half or milk
- 8 oz pkg shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions
1. In a large skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until browned and crispy.
2. Remove from skillet; drain and crumble. Store in refrigerator until dinner.
3. In bacon drippings, cook onion over medium heat 4-5 minutes, stirring frequently until tender.
4. Spray slow cooker with cooking spray. In slow cooker, mix onions, potatoes, celery, broth, salt and pepper. Cover and cook on low 6-7 hours.
5. In a small bowl, mix flour and half and half (or milk) with wire whisk until well blended; stir into soup.
6. Increase heat on slow cooker to high. Cover; cook about 30 minutes longer or until thickened.
7. Stir in cheese until melted and smooth. (If you want fewer lumps in your soup, mash the potatoes a bit with a potato masher.)
8. Serve crumbled bacon on top of Soup.
