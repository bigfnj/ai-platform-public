# Black Bean Stuffed Peppers

## Ingredients
- 1 onion, chopped
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper
- ¼ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp chili powder
- 30 oz black beans, canned, rinsed and drained
- 6 bell peppers – seeded and cored
- ½ cup grated cheese
- ½ cup tomato salsa
- ¼ tsp dried oregano
- Sour cream

## Instructions
1. Saute onions and season with cayenne pepper, oregano, cumin and chili powder.
2. In bowl, mash ½ of the black beans with sautéed onions.
3. Mix in remaining beans.
4. Stuff the bell peppers and put cheese on top.
5. Place in Crock Pot and pour salsa over it.
6. Cook on low for 6-8 hrs and then serve with sour cream.
