# Crock Pot Cherry and Peach Dessert

## Ingredients
- 1 (26 oz.) cherry pie filling
- 2 (15 oz) cans diced peaches in syrup
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 1 box yellow cake mix
- 1 stick butter, cut into small pieces
- 1 packet maple and brown sugar instant oatmeal
- ¼ cup sliced almonds
- butter flavored cooking spray

## Instructions
1. Spray a 5 quart slow cooker with butter flavored cooking spray.
2. Mix together pie filling ,fruit and vanilla extract. Pour into slow cooker.
3. Sprinkle cake mix over the fruit mixture.
4. Dot with butter.
5. Pour oatmeal packet and almonds over cake mixture.
6. Place 8 paper towels over slow cooker bowl and place lid on top.
7. Cook on low setting for 4-6 hours. Do not lift lid to check cake for the first 3 hours.
8. Serve with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.
