# Guinness Beef Stew

*Serves 4*

## Ingredients
- 1 pound braising beef, cut into cubes
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, peeled and cut into coins
- 1 cup Guinness
- 1 teaspoon brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 325°F. Make sure the Dutch oven you will use is oven safe.
2. In a large plastic bag or bowl, toss together the beef, flour, and salt.
3. In a large Dutch oven on the stove, heat 1 tablespoon oil. Add the beef (in batches if necessary) and cook until brown on both sides. Remove from the pot and transfer to a bowl.
4. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil to the pot. Add the onions and carrots. Cook for 5 minutes until softened. Return the beef to the pot then add the Guinness, sugar, thyme, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil.
5. Once boiling, cover and cook in the oven for 2 hours or until the meat is soft. Discard the bay leaf before serving.
