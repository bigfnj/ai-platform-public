# Cheeseburger Soup

## Ingredients
- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 stalk celery, finely chopped
- 2 Tbsps flour
- 3 cups beef broth
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 8 oz pkg shredded low-fat cheddar cheese
- ½ tsp paprika
- baked tortilla chips

## Instructions
1. In a large non-stick skillet sprayed with cooking spray, add onion, garlic and celery; sauté until vegetables are tender, about 5 minutes.
2. Spray slow cooker with cooking spray, and pour in vegetables.
3. Add ground beef to skillet, and cook until browned and crumbly, drain; add to slow cooker.
4. In a small bowl, combine flour and 2 cup broth; stir until smooth.
5. Add flour mixture along with the remaining 2 cups beef broth to skillet with any of the browned bits left from the beef. Bring to a simmer, stirring well. Transfer broth mixture to slow cooker.
6. Add evaporated milk, cheese, paprika, ½ teaspoon salt and ½ teaspoon pepper to the slow cooker. Cover and cook on low 2 hours.
7. To serve, put a few chips into the bottom of your bowl, add soup and a few more crumbled chips.
