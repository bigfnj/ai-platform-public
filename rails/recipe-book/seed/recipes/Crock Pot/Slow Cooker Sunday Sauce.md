# Slow Cooker Sunday Sauce

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp bacon grease (or oil of choice)
- 3 carrots, peeled
- 2 onions
- 4 stalks celery
- 6 cloves garlic, finely sliced
- 4 lbs minced beef (85%)
- 5 tbsp tomato paste
- 1.5 cups crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup red wine
- 3 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 4 beef stock cubes (or 4 tsp bullion powder)
- 4 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp balsamic glaze or vinegar
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp fresh ground pepper
- 4 bay leaves

## Instructions
1. Blitz carrot, celery and onions in food processor or chop really finely.
2. Saute in oil until soft and slightly browned; add to slow cooker.
3. Brown beef in batches and add to slow cooker (feel free to drain fat however fat is flavor).
4. Add wine to pan and deglaze, add to slow cooker.
5. Add remaining ingredients to the slow cooker and mix well, cook on low for 12 hours.
6. Whisk to break up the meat or leave if you like it chunkier; check for seasoning.
