# Crock-Pot Moroccan Chicken

*Serves 8.*

## Ingredients
- 1 large onion cut into thin wedges
- 1.5 kg chicken breasts skin removed
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp African spice mix
- 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
- 400 g chicken broth
- 400 g diced tomatoes drained
- 1/2 cup slivered almonds grated
- 1 cup pitted dates quartered

## Instructions
1. Layer onion and chicken in slow cooker.
2. Mix flour, garlic powder, salt, spice mix and turmeric. Sprinkle over chicken.
3. Add broth, tomatoes, dates and almonds to slow cooker. Cover.
4. Cook 4 hours on HIGH or 8 hours on LOW.
5. Serve with hot cooked couscous, if desired.
