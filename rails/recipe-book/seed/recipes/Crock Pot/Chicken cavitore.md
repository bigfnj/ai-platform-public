# Chicken Cacciatore

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs chicken tenderloins
- 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 28 oz can crushed tomatoes
- 6 oz can tomato paste
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1½ tsps Italian seasoning
- salt and pepper to taste
- 16 oz pkg favorite pasta, cooked
- grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

## Instructions
1. Place chicken into slow cooker.
2. Add sliced onions, green and red peppers on top of chicken.
3. In a small bowl, mix together crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, garlic, Italian seasoning, salt and pepper. Pour tomato mixture over chicken and veggies.
4. Cover, and cook on low 7 hours or on high 4-5 hours.
5. Serve over pasta, and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese, if desired.
