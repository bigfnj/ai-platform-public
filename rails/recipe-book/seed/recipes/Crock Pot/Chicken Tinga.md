# Chicken Tinga

## Ingredients
- 2-3 chicken leg quarters - That's 4-6 pieces of dark meat. You can use breast white meat, about 2-3 half breasts.
- 7.5 ounce can of chipotle peppers in adobo sauce - I used the whole can, but you can remove seeds from chiles and use half of the sauce, for less spicy heat. Okay to substitute with enchilada or red chile sauce from a 14.5 ounce can (it's easy to find, and with no spicy heat.)
- 14.5 ounce can of tomatoes - diced, tomato sauce or even whole tomatoes. Just break up tomatoes as they soften from stewing.
- 1/2 onion - chopped
- 1 teaspoon garlic - chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon of dried oregano - or, one teaspoon of fresh oregano.
- 1/4 teaspoon of ground cumin - optional
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. For dark meat with skin, saute in a large pan, skin side down, about 5-10 minutes over a medium/high heat. You want to brown the skin and get some charred bits as extra flavor.
2. Okay to remove skin for a lighter version. Use the same procedure for breast meat. No need to brown both sides of the chicken, or cook it all the way - you will simmer it later, until well done. (Of course, keep semi-raw poultry on a different plate from other ingredients to prevent cross-contamination.) And, if you are in a hurry, you can skip this browning chicken part.
3. While chicken browns, chop half an onion. When one side of chicken is brown, remove it and add the chopped onion. Stir onion and scrape loose the brown bits on the bottom of pan. Saute onion about 5 minutes until soft.
4. Add garlic and saute another minute. Now add spices and herbs.
5. Sprinkle in ground cumin, dried oregano, and salt and pepper to taste.
6. Pour in a can of chipotle peppers. For a less spicy version, add half of the liquid and peppers.
7. (To reduce spicy heat, substitute chipotle peppers in adobe sauce with a small 14.5 ounce can of enchilada or red chili sauce.) You can also only use tomato sauce with a tablespoon of dried chili.
8. Add a can of diced tomatoes or plain tomato sauce. Mix all the ingredients.
9. Finally add the browned chicken pieces. Bring up liquid to a low simmer. Cover the pot and cook about one hour, until chicken is cooked all the way through.
10. Remove chicken pieces from sauce and allow to cool for a few minutes. Slice into thickest part of chicken to make sure it's cooked all the way through. (This is a good time to break up tomatoes, if you used large pieces.)
11. Remove the skin and meat from the bone. You can discard the skin and bones, it was just for flavor and a little chicken fat. Shred chicken pieces and mix the meat into sauce.
12. While meat heats up get all the taco ingredients ready. Heat up some corn tortillas. I like to top my tacos with fresh chopped onion and cilantro. Other toppings include - tomato, lettuce, avocado, cheese, and a squeeze of lime.

- 2-3 chicken leg quarters or 2-3 half breasts of white meat
