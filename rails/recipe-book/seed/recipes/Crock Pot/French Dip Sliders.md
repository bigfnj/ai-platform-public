# French Dip Sliders

## Ingredients
- 3 lb boneless shoulder or chuck roast
- 10.5 oz can condensed French onion soup
- 10.5 oz can condensed beef consomme
- 10.5 oz can condensed beef broth
- 1 beef bouillon cube
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 6 oz pkg provolone cheese slices
- 1 pkg mini French or Italian rolls

## Instructions
1. Place roast into slow cooker.
2. Combine the soup, consomme, broth, bouillon and garlic; pour over roast.
3. Cover and cook on low 7-8 hours or until meat is tender.
4. Remove meat and shred. Place desired amount of meat and cheese on each roll to serve.
5. Skim fat from remaining broth; serve juices on the side for dipping.
