# Slow Cooker Chicken Fajitas

*Prep Time: 5 minutes, Cook Time: 3 hours*

## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon white sugar
- 1 teaspoon dried onion flakes (or onion powder)
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 yellow onion, cut into ½ inch wedges
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- chopped cilantro, as a garnish

## Instructions
1. Mix the cornstarch, chili powder, salt, paprika, white sugar, dried onion flakes, cayenne pepper and ground cumin in a small bowl.
2. Add the chicken, onions, garlic, bell peppers and salsa to your slow cooker.
3. Add the seasonings over the top of the mixture.
4. Cook on high for 3 hours or on low for 6 hours. If you cook longer it will also taste fine, but cutting may be more difficult because the chicken will break down easier.
5. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the top of your chicken (do not add before, it won't have the same flavor after cooking for so long).
6. Garnish with cilantro (optional).
