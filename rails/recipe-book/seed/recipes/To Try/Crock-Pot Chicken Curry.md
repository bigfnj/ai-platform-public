# Chicken Curry

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- ½ tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1.5 tsp garam masala
- 2.5 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 pound chicken thighs - bite sized
- 2 yellow / red bell peppers diced
- 4 cloves garlic minced
- 2 inch ginger minced
- 1 yellow onion minced
- 1 can chick peas, drained
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 2 scoop tomato paste
- 1 13oz bag frozen peas
- 3.5 cup rice
- Lemon Juice
- Cilantro

## Instructions
1. Add spices and other ingredients to crock pot
2. Cook on low 6 hours
3. Microwave green peas and add them at the end
4. Serve over rice with some lemon juice and cilantro to top (if you have it)
