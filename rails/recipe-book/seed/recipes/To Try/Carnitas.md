# El Jefe's Carnitas

## Ingredients
- 2 lb pork (any cut except belly)
- 2 medium oranges
- 3 large limes
- 2 fresh jalapenos
- 2 Mexican Cokes
- cumin
- salt
- black pepper
- seasoned salt
- sour cream
- cheese of your choice
- cilantro

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Marinate pork for a minimum of 12 hours (chimichurri recommended).
3. After marinating, cut the pork into manageable pieces.
4. Peel the oranges and 2 limes, dice the jalapenos, then add them all to a bowl.
5. Braise pork on high heat, seasoned with cumin, salt, black pepper, and seasoned salt.
6. After pork is mostly cooked, add in the limes, oranges, jalapenos, and 1½ to 2 Cokes.
7. Bring the heat down and simmer for 30-45 minutes, or until the meat is cooked.
8. Shred the meat, put it and some of the liquid in a baking pan, and bake in the oven until the edges of the pork are slightly crispy.
9. Serve on tortillas with sour cream, cheese of your choice, lime juice, and cilantro.
