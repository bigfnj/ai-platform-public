# Creamy Chicken Poblano Pepper Soup

*4 servings.*

## Ingredients
- 3 poblano peppers
- 3 chicken breasts (bone in and skin on)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt (divided)
- ¼ teaspoon coarse black pepper
- 2 tablespoons canola oil
- ¼ cup butter
- ¼ cup flour
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 16 ounces roasted corn (frozen)
- 2 cans (15 ounce each) black beans, rinsed and drained
- 2 red bell peppers, diced finely
- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 cup shredded pepper jack cheese
- 1 lime (juiced)
- 2 tablespoons ground cumin
- ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper
- ½ cup half and half or heavy cream
- 1 jalapeño, thinly sliced

## Instructions
1. Roast the poblano peppers until charred, steam covered to loosen the skins, then peel, seed, and dice.
2. Season the chicken with half the salt and the black pepper and roast at 400°F until cooked through (~35-40 min); shred, discarding skin and bones.
3. In a large pot, heat the canola oil with the butter, then whisk in the flour and cook 1-2 minutes to make a roux.
4. Whisk in the chicken broth; bring to a simmer, stirring, until slightly thickened.
5. Add the roasted poblanos, red bell peppers, jalapeño, roasted corn, black beans, cumin, cayenne, and remaining salt; simmer 15-20 minutes.
6. Stir in the shredded chicken, cheddar, pepper jack, and half and half; cook on low until the cheese melts and the soup is creamy (do not boil).
7. Finish with the lime juice, adjust salt and pepper, and serve.
