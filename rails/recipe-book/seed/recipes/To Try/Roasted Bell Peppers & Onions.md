# Roasted Bell Peppers & Onions

## Ingredients
- 1 pan
- vegetables
- olive oil
- vinegar
- salt
- pepper

## Instructions
1. pre-heat oven to 350
2. oil vegetables and bottom of pan
3. bake for 45 minutes
4. pull out, and hand peel into strips
5. take leftover juice and put in jar, add olive oil (equivalent to what you put in jar), couple squirts of vinegar, salt & pepper..... shake for a topping sauce

## Shopping List
- pan
- vegetables
- olive oil
- vinegar
