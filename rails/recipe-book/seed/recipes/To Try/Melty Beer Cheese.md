# Melty Beer Cheese

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup beer
- 1/2 tsp sodium citrate
- 8 oz cheese

## Instructions
1. Simmer – dissolve citrate.
2. Stir until dissolved, simmer until desired gooey.
