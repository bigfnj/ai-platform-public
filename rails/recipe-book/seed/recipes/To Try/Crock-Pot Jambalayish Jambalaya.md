# Jambalayish Jambalaya

## Ingredients
- 1 (14.5-ounce) can diced tomatoes
- 1 (14.5-ounce) can beef broth
- 1 (8-ounce) can tomato paste
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 teaspoons dried basil
- 1 1/2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon Tony Chachere's Creole Seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon Tabasco sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 medium red onion, chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, seeded and chopped
- 2 celery ribs, minced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 pound Andouille sausage or use kielbasa, cut into slices
- 2 chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces

Add later:
- 1/2 pound shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 1/2 cup brown rice
- 1 can chicken broth

## Instructions
1. Combine the first group of ingredients in a slow cooker and stir to combine.
2. Cover the crock pot and cook on LOW for 4.5 hours.
3. Get two cups of liquid in the crock pot through a strainer. Supplement with chicken broth to get enough liquid for 1.5 cup rice. Boil liquid, add rice, cook rice.
4. Stir in shrimp while rice is cooking.
5. Stir in rice. Cover the crock pot and leave on warm if it’s still al dente.
