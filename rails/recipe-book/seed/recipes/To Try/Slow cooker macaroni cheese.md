# Slow cooker macaroni cheese

*Makes: 4 servings*

## Ingredients
- 1 l (1 ¾ pint) whole milk, plus extra if needed
- ½ tsp english mustard powder
- 250 g (9oz) mature Cheddar, grated
- 50 g (2oz) parmesan
- 100 g (3 ½oz) cream cheese
- 400 g (14oz) macaroni
- 6 bacon rashers

## Instructions
1. Put the milk, all the cheeses, mustard powder, a generous pinch of salt and freshly ground black pepper into a slow cooker.
2. Stir well, then add the macaroni and mix again.
3. Cook on low for 1 ½- 2 ½ hours until the pasta is cooked and the sauce is creamy.
4. Stir in a splash of milk if the mixture is a little stiff.
5. Cook bacon, then add before serving (you can use ready-cooked bacon if you prefer).
