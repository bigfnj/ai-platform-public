# Crockpot Grilled Fiery Habanero Apricot BBQ Ribs.
*Prep time: 10 minutes. Cook time: 8 hours. Total time: 8 hours 10 minutes.*

## Ingredients
- 3 pounds baby back ribs (around 1 rack)
- 2 tablespoons honey or brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 2 teaspoons chipotle chili powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon salt + pepper
- 1 ounce beer (just use your favorite)
- 1¼ cups ketchup (preferably organic)
- 1 cup dark brown sugar
- ¼ cup molasses
- ¼ cup pineapple juice or apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 2½ teaspoons ground mustard
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- ¼-½ teaspoon cayenne pepper (use less if you don't like the heat)
- 1½ teaspoons kosher salt + 1 teaspoon pepper
- 8 ounces fresh or frozen raspberries
- 4-5 apricots (diced)
- 4-6 habanero chilies (seeds removed)

## Instructions
1. Grab the ribs and place them on a cutting board.
2. Take a butter knife, wedge it just underneath the membrane to loosen and pull the membrane up and off the bone.
3. Then use your hands to pull the rest of the membrane off.
4. In a small bowl, combine the brown sugar, smoked paprika, chipotle chili powder, garlic powder, cinnamon, salt, and pepper.
5. Rub the mixture all over the ribs.
6. Place the ribs in the bowl of a crockpot and pour the beer into the crockpot.
7. Cover and cook overnight or 6-8 hours on low.
8. While the ribs are cooking, make the BBQ sauce.
9. Combine all the ingredients for the BBQ sauce in a medium-size sauce pot.
10. Bring the mixture to a boil and cook until the apricots are very soft, about 10-15 minutes.
11. Once the fruit is soft, remove from the heat and allow to cool slightly, then puree the sauce in a blender until completely smooth.
12. Pour into a glass jar. Store any leftovers in the fridge.
13. Once the ribs are finished cooking and you are ready to grill them, preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
14. Remove the ribs from the crockpot and brush them with the BBQ sauce.
15. Be careful when handling; the ribs are really going to want to fall apart.
16. Place the ribs on the grill and grill for 5-8 minutes or until light grill marks appear.
17. Carefully remove the ribs from the grill and onto a serving plate.
18. Brush with more BBQ sauce and serve warm.
