# Asian Style Crockpot Chicken Drumsticks

*5 servings*

## Ingredients
- 2 1/2 lbs chicken drumsticks
- salt and pepper
- 1/4 tsp. crushed red pepper flakes (optional)

### Sauce
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/8 cup rice wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp. tomato paste
- 3 garlic cloves minced
- 1 1/2 tsp. freshly chopped ginger

## Instructions
1. Arrange chicken in 6-quart slow cooker. Season with salt, pepper, and pepper flakes.
2. Combine sauce ingredients and pour over chicken. Cover and cook on low heat for 4-5 hours, or until chicken is cooked through (160-165F.)
3. For crispy skin, broil your chicken until golden! You can also do this before placing into the slow cooker but I find the final broiling to yield great results!
4. Garnish with green onion and sesame seeds if desired. Serve over rice.
5. Note: My husband tends to remove all the meat after cooking and throw it into a big batch of rice!
