# Southern Slow Cooked Collard Greens

*Prep Time: 15m, Cook Time: 5h 30m, Total Time: 5h 45m*

## Ingredients
- 16 cups water (4 quarts)
- 2 pounds fresh collard greens
- 1 ½ pounds pork neck bones
- ½ tablespoon garlic powder
- ½ tablespoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 2 tablespoons salt
- 8 ounces pork jowl

## Instructions
1. Add the water to a large pot.
2. Add the pork neck and bring to a simmer over medium heat.
3. Let simmer for 30 minutes.
4. Rinse each leaf from the batch of collard greens.
5. Carefully, use a sharp knife and slice the large middle vein from each leaf.
6. Stack a few leaves on top of each other and roll them up.
7. Starting on the end, make 1 inch slices down the roll to slice the collard greens into strips.
8. After the pork has simmered for 30 minutes, add the collard greens to the water.
9. Add in the garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, and salt.
10. Stir.
11. Cover and lower the temperature to low and let the collard greens simmer for 3 hours.
12. After the 3 hour mark, it's time to add the pork jowl.
13. Heat a medium skillet over medium heat.
14. Remove the rind from the pork jowl and add it to the pot of collard greens.
15. Slice the remaining pork jowl into cubes and add them to a skillet.
16. Cook the cubes until some of the fat has rendered and the cubes are somewhat crispy, about 10 minutes.
17. Add the drippings and cubes of pork jowl to the collard greens.
18. Cover and let simmer for 2 more hours.
