# Taco Bell Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 1 pound unpeeled russet potatoes, diced ½-inch
- ⅓ cup Live Más Dust (recipe follows)
- neutral cooking oil
- canned nacho cheese sauce
- refried beans
- hot sauce
- bottled ranch dressing
- cooked ground beef or crispy tofu crumbles

### Live Más Dust:
- 1 heaping teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 heaping teaspoon granulated garlic
- 1 heaping teaspoon dehydrated onion flakes or onion powder
- 1 heaping teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 heaping teaspoon cayenne pepper or red chili powder
- 1 heaping teaspoon powdered bouillon (chicken) or nutritional yeast
- 1 heaping teaspoon white sugar
- 2 heaping tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 2 heaping teaspoons adobo seasoning

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 475°F with a rack in the upper third.
2. Cut potatoes into ½-inch dice and toss them with about a third of the Live Más Dust.
3. Heat a large, oven-safe skillet over medium heat, then add enough neutral cooking oil to coat the bottom.
4. Add the seasoned potatoes to the hot oil in a single layer and cook, undisturbed, for 3-5 minutes.
5. Turn them over with a spatula and transfer to the oven.
6. Roast for 10-15 minutes, flipping the potatoes once or twice, until they’re golden brown and crispy.
