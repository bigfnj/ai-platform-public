# Copycat Spaghetti Squash Casserole

*Serves 4-6 as a main dish.*

## Ingredients
- 1 (3 ½ to 4-pound) spaghetti squash
- grapeseed oil
- 5 ounces caramelized onions
- black pepper
- kosher or sea salt
- 1 (25-ounce jar) pasta sauce
- 4-6 tablespoons chopped herb mix of fresh parsley, oregano and thyme
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 8 ounces fresh mozzarella pearls, drained

## Instructions
1. First roast squash: Heat oven to 375°F. Microwave whole squash 2 minutes to soften skin a bit. Cut squash in half lengthwise and scrape out seeds and strings and discard. Rub cut insides of squash halves with grapeseed oil, place cut side down on a cookie sheet or roasting pan, add ¼ cup water to pan and roast in oven for about 45-50 minutes, until squash is tender all the way through.
2. Remove squash from oven and set aside to cool. Once squash is cooled, scoop out flesh with a large fork and place it in a bowl. As you remove the flesh, it will begin to separate into long strings, like spaghetti (hence the name!).
3. Assemble casserole: Mist a 13- by 9-inch baking dish with nonstick spray. Turn oven down to 350°F.
4. First layer: Using your hands, break half the squash into long threads and place in prepared dish. Top with half the caramelized onions, flinging them over top with your fingers. Grind a few turns of black pepper and a pinch of salt over top. Ladle on 1½ cups of the sauce, sprinkle with half the fresh herb mix, and sprinkle on half the Parmesan.
5. Second layer: Using your hands, break the rest of the squash into long threads and place in dish. Top with the rest of the caramelized onions, a few grinds of black pepper and a pinch of salt, the rest of the sauce, the rest of the fresh herb mix, the rest of the Parmesan, and all of the little mozzarella balls, spacing them relatively evenly on top.
6. Bake, uncovered, for about 30 minutes, until casserole is bubbly and cheese is melted. Remove from oven and serve.

## Shopping List
- 1 (3 ½ to 4-pound) spaghetti squash
- grapeseed oil
- 5 ounces onions
- black pepper
- kosher or sea salt
- 1 (25-ounce jar) pasta sauce
- fresh parsley
- fresh oregano
- fresh thyme
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 8 ounces fresh mozzarella pearls
