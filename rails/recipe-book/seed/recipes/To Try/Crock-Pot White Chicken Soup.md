# Crock-Pot White Chicken Soup

## Ingredients
- 3 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 can chicken stock (try without for thicker)
- 6 (4-ounce) cans diced green chiles
- 3 (15.5 ounce) cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (15.5 ounce) can garbanzo beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 medium sweet onion, diced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons coriander
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- ½ teaspoon cayenne
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano

Later:
- 2 cans corn
- 2 teaspoons salt
- black pepper
- lemon juice

## Instructions
1. Add the first set of ingredients to a slow cooker.
2. Cover and cook on low heat for 7-8 hours.
3. Remove chicken from the slow cooker and shred, using two forks. Optionally take out ⅓ of beans and mash / blend before adding back.
4. Stir in chicken and corn; season with salt and pepper. Put on lemon juice before serving.

## Shopping List
