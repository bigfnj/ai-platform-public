# Slow Cooker Chicken Pie Soup

*Yield: serves 6-8 prep time: 15 minutes cook time: 4 hours and 30 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb boneless chicken breast
- ¾ cup onion, chopped
- ½ cup celery, chopped
- ¾ cup carrots, chopped
- 7 oz can peas, drained (roughly ⅔ cup)
- 7 oz can corn, drained (roughly ⅔ cup)
- 3½ cups russet potatoes, peeled and chopped into 1-inch pieces
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 3½ cups chicken broth
- 1½ cups heavy cream
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour

## Instructions
1. Trim chicken breast of any fat.
2. Chop onion, celery and carrots into small pieces.
3. Peel and chop potatoes into 1-inch pieces.
4. Drain peas and corn.
5. Mince garlic.
6. To the bowl of a 5-6 quart slow cooker add prepared chicken and vegetables, along with salt, pepper, thyme, bay leaf and chicken broth.
7. Cover and allow to cook on high for 4 hours.
8. Remove and discard bay leaf.
9. Remove chicken breast, shred and return to slow cooker.
10. In a medium bowl whisk together flour and heavy cream until smooth.
11. Pour into slow cooker, stirring to combine.
12. Replace cover and continue cooking for an additional 30-45 minutes.
13. Serve warm.
