# Chicken Pot Pie Surprise

*Makes 6 servings*

## Ingredients
- 4 large chicken breasts, skinless/boneless (cut into small bite-sized pieces)
- ¼ cup Italian dressing
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup (any type)
- ½ bag mixed frozen vegetables
- 1 can chicken broth
- 1 box Stove Top Stuffing - Chicken

## Instructions
1. Cut chicken into small pieces.
2. Add Italian dressing, both cans of cream soups and ½ bag of Frozen Vegetables and toss together.
3. Mix Stove top Stuffing with can of Chicken Broth and let sit until broth is absorbed.
4. Spread Stuffing over chicken mixture.
5. Bake covered for 45 minutes and uncovered for 15 minutes.
