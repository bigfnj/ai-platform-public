# Creamy Tomato & Basil Risotto

*30 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 2 large cloves garlic
- ¼ oz fresh basil, leaves and stems separated
- 1 packet vegetable broth concentrate
- 5 oz sushi rice
- ¼ cup tomato paste
- 3 oz mascarpone cheese
- 3 oz baby spinach
- kosher salt
- ground pepper
- olive oil
- white wine vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)

## Instructions
1. Peel and finely chop garlic.
2. Pick basil leaves from stems, reserving stems for step 3, and tear any large leaves into ½-inch pieces.
3. In a liquid measuring cup, stir to combine vegetable broth concentrate, 3½ cups water , and ¾ teaspoon salt .
4. Heat 1½ tablespoons oil in a medium skillet over medium. Add rice, chopped garlic, and tomato paste. Cook, stirring, until rice is toasted and garlic is fragrant, about 2 minutes.
5. To skillet with rice, add basil stems, ½ cup of the broth, and ½ tablespoon vinegar . Cook, stirring, until nearly absorbed, 1–2 minutes.
6. Continue adding remaining broth, ½ cup at a time, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking (keep at a strong simmer, adjusting heat if necessary), until all of the broth is nearly absorbed, 20–25 minutes total. Rice will be al dente and suspended in a thick sauce.
7. Discard basil stems from risotto. Stir in mascarpone, spinach , and half of the basil leaves; cook, stirring, until spinach is wilted, 1–2 minutes. Remove from heat; season to taste with salt and pepper.
8. Serve creamy tomato and basil risotto garnished with remaining basil leaves.

## Shopping List
- garlic (2 large cloves)
- fresh basil (¼ oz)
- vegetable broth concentrate (1 packet)
- sushi rice (5 oz)
- tomato paste (¼ cup)
- mascarpone cheese (3 oz)
- baby spinach (3 oz)
- kosher salt
- ground pepper
- olive oil
- white wine vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)
