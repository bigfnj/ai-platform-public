# Two Cheese Pizza Gnocchi

*Is it pizza? Is it pasta? Don't question. Just grab a fork and dig into this cheesy skillet of saucy gnocchi swirled with fresh spinach.*

## Ingredients
- 3 oz baby spinach
- 1 tsp pizza spice blend
- 1 cup tomato sauce (from one can)
- 17 oz pkg gnocchi
- 8 oz mozzarella cheese, shredded
- ¾ oz Parmesan cheese, grated
- 1 large clove garlic, chopped
- kosher salt and ground pepper
- olive oil
- sugar

## Instructions
1. Preheat broiler with top rack 6 inches from heat source.
2. Peel and finely chop 1 teaspoon garlic. Grate Parmesan on the small holes of a box grater. Shred mozzarella on the large holes of box grater.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium ovenproof skillet over medium-high. Break apart any gnocchi that are stuck together, then add to skillet in one layer, overlapping slightly. Cover and cook, without stirring, until tender, well browned, and crisp underneath, 4–5 minutes. Transfer to a bowl and cover to keep warm. Wipe out skillet.
4. Heat chopped garlic and 1 tablespoon oil in same skillet over medium-high; cook, stirring, until fragrant, 1 minute. Add 1 cup of the tomato sauce, ½ cup water , 1 teaspoon of the pizza seasoning, and ½ teaspoon sugar; bring to a boil. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until reduced to 1 cup, about 5 minutes.
5. Stir spinach into skillet with sauce; cover and cook until spinach is wilted, 1–2 minutes. Add gnocchi and half of the Parmesan to skillet, stirring to coat; season to taste with salt and pepper.
6. Top pizza gnocchi with mozzarella and remaining Parmesan. Broil on top oven rack until cheese is melted and browned in spots, 2–3 minutes (watch closely). Enjoy!
