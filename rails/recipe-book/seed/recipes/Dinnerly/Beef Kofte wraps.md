# Beef Kofte Pita Wraps

*At the end of a long day, all you want to do is wrap it up. So try this one—with beef kofte and roasted tomato vinaigrette!*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 plum tomatoes
- 4 pita breads
- 1 large egg
- 3 tsp kosher salt, divided
- ½ tsp ground pepper
- ⅓ cup olive oil, plus more for brushing and drizzling
- 2¼ tsp berbere spice blend
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 romaine heart
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar

## Instructions
1. Preheat broiler with a rack in the center. Lightly oil a rimmed baking sheet.
2. Peel and finely grate 2 teaspoons garlic. Finely chop 1 pita. Quarter tomatoes into wedges.
3. In a medium bowl, use a fork to mash together 1 large egg and chopped pita, forming a paste. Add beef, chopped garlic, 2¼ teaspoons berbere spice, and 2 teaspoons salt; stir gently to combine. Shape beef mixture into 12 football-shaped ovals and place directly on prepared baking sheet.
4. Add tomatoes to prepared baking sheet with kofte. Drizzle tomatoes with oil, then season with salt and pepper. Broil on center oven rack until kofte are golden brown, crisp, and cooked through, and tomatoes are lightly browned and softened, about 10 minutes (watch closely).
5. While kofte cook, halve romaine lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise, discarding ends.
6. In a large bowl, whisk together 2 tablespoons vinegar and ⅓ cup oil. Add cooked tomatoes, then coarsely mash using a potato masher or fork; season to taste with salt and pepper.
7. Brush 4 pitas lightly with oil (save remaining for own use). Broil directly on center oven rack, until lightly browned and crisp on the edges, 1–3 minutes (watch closely), flipping once.
8. Top pitas with lettuce, then spoon about ⅓ of the dressing over top. Top with beef kofte and drizzle with remaining roasted tomato dressing.
