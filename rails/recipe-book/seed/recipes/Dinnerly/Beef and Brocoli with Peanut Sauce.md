# Beef & Broccoli Noodles with Teriyaki-Peanut Sauce

*30 minutes, 4 servings*

## Ingredients
- garlic (use 4 large cloves)
- 1 lb broccoli
- 2 (1½ oz) pkts peanut butter
- 4 oz teriyaki sauce
- 2 pkts chili sauce
- 1 lb spaghetti
- 2 (10 oz) pkgs ground beef
- kosher salt & ground pepper
- sugar
- neutral oil, such as vegetable

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Peel and finely chop 4 teaspoons garlic. Trim ends from broccoli, then cut into ½-inch florets. In a medium bowl, whisk to combine chopped garlic, peanut butter, teriyaki sauce, chili sauce, ¼ cup water, and 2 teaspoons sugar.
3. Add pasta to boiling water and cook until al dente, about 9 minutes. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain pasta, and return to pot.
4. Meanwhile, heat 2 teaspoons oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add beef and cook, until browned, breaking up into smaller pieces, about 5 minutes. Stir in broccoli, a pinch of salt , and ½ cup water. Cover, reduce heat to medium, and cook until broccoli is crisp-tender, 5-7 minutes.
5. Add beef and broccoli along with ¾ cup of the pasta water to pot with pasta. Cook over medium heat, tossing, until liquid is absorbed, about 2 minutes.
6. Remove pot from heat, add peanut sauce, and toss to coat. Add 1 tablespoon pasta water, as needed, if sauce is too thick. Season to taste with salt and pepper.

## Shopping List
- garlic (4 large cloves)
- broccoli (1 lb)
