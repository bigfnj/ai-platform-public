# Mexican Mac and Cheese

*30 minutes, 2 servings*

## Ingredients
- 2½ tsp taco seasoning
- 5 oz corn
- ½ lb pasta
- 4 oz roasted red peppers
- 1 large garlic clove
- 3 (¾ oz) pieces sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 scallion
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- kosher salt
- ground pepper

## Instructions
1. Bring a large saucepan of salted water to a boil.
2. Preheat broiler with top rack 6 inches from heat source.
3. Trim and discard ends from scallions, then thinly slice, keeping dark greens separate.
4. Finely chop all of the cheddar cheese.
5. Peel and finely chop garlic clove.
6. Cut peppers crosswise into thin slices.
7. Add pasta to boiling water and cook, stirring occasionally, until al dente, 7–9 minutes.
8. Drain pasta and set aside.
9. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat.
10. Add scallion whites and chopped garlic; cook, stirring, until fragrant, 30 seconds.
11. Add corn and cook, stirring, until golden and tender, about 2 minutes.
12. Transfer to a plate and wipe out skillet.
13. Melt 1 tablespoon butter in same skillet over medium heat.
14. Sprinkle 1 tablespoon flour and taco seasoning into skillet.
15. Cook, stirring, until smooth, about 1 minute.
16. Add milk, whisking constantly, until sauce is smooth, 2–3 minutes.
17. Remove from heat, then add cheese, whisking until completely melted; season to taste with salt and pepper.
18. Stir pasta, corn , and roasted peppers into cheese sauce.
19. Broil on top oven rack until golden and lightly browned in spots, 1–3 minutes (watch closely).
20. Serve Mexican mac & cheese with remaining scallions sprinkled over top.
