# Za'atar Chicken with Lemony Tabouleh

*30 minutes*

## Ingredients
- 1 lb plum tomatoes
- 2 cucumbers (7 oz each)
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (½ lb each)
- 7 tsp za'atar spice blend
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 lemon
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- ½ cup water
- ½ lb quick cooking bulgur
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- ½ tsp ground pepper

## Instructions
1. Cook bulgur: Peel and finely chop 3 cloves garlic. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in medium saucepan over medium heat. Add 1½ teaspoons garlic and cook until softened, 1 minute. Add bulgur and cook until toasted, 2 minutes. Add 1½ cups water and 1 teaspoon salt; bring to a boil. Cover and simmer over low heat until tender and water absorbs, 10–12 minutes. Cover to keep warm off heat.
2. Prep ingredients: While bulgur cooks, finely grate ¾ teaspoon lemon zest and squeeze 3 tablespoons lemon juice into a large bowl; cut any remaining lemon into wedges. Cut tomatoes and cucumbers into ¼-inch pieces (peel cucumbers if desired). Pat chicken dry.
3. Make dressing: Add the remaining chopped garlic into the bowl with lemon zest and juice. Whisk in ½ cup oil; season to taste with salt and pepper. Transfer 2 tablespoons of the dressing to a small bowl and set aside for serving.
4. Season & cook chicken: In a small bowl, combine 7 teaspoons za'atar spice blend, 2 tablespoons flour, and a pinch each salt and pepper; sprinkle all over chicken, pressing gently to help adhere seasoning. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a medium skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken until golden brown on both sides and cooked through, 3–5 minutes total. Transfer to plates.
5. Finish & serve: Stir bulgur, tomatoes, and cucumbers into large bowl with dressing; season to taste with salt and pepper. Serve za'atar chicken with lemony tabouleh alongside. Drizzle with reserved dressing and squeeze lemon wedges over top, if desired. Enjoy!
6. Raid the condiment rack: Try stirring a tablespoon of nutty tahini or a dash of spicy hot sauce into the dressing in step 3!
