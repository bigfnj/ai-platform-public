## Coverage legend — bloom-form buckets
Used by the analysis playbook to narrow an unknown flower fast.
- **MASS** — round, voluminous, many-petaled (rose, carnation, peony)
- **FORM** — distinctive architectural shape (orchid, calla, lily, protea)
- **DAISY** — ray petals around a central disc (gerbera, sunflower, aster)
- **LINE** — tall vertical spikes (snapdragon, delphinium, gladiolus)
- **SPRAY/FILLER** — small blooms in clusters/clouds (baby's breath, wax flower)
- **FOLIAGE** — greenery (eucalyptus, ruscus, fern)
