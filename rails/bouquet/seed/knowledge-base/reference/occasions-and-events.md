# Occasions & Events

What flowers and colors belong to which holidays, celebrations, and rites.
Used to infer the *purpose* of a bouquet from its contents, and vice versa.

## Romantic & relationship
| Occasion | Typical flowers | Palette |
|---|---|---|
| **Valentine's Day** | Red roses (the icon), tulips, ranunculus, orchids | Red, deep pink |
| **Anniversary** | Roses (esp. red), plus the year-specific flower (see below) | Varies |
| **"I'm sorry" / apology** | Purple hyacinth, white tulips, roses | Soft, humble |
| **New romance / first date** | Tulips, mixed pastels, single rose | Light, cheerful |

## Family & life milestones
| Occasion | Typical flowers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mother's Day** | Carnations (pink/red for living, white to honor deceased — the tradition's origin), peonies, roses, tulips, lilies | Carnation is the historic Mother's Day flower (Anna Jarvis) |
| **New baby** | Sweet peas, daisies, soft roses | Pink/blue/pastel by convention |
| **Birthday** | The recipient's birth-month flower (see below), or bright mixed | Cheerful |
| **Graduation / congratulations** | Sunflowers, gerberas, bright mixed bouquets | Bold, celebratory |
| **Get well** | Sunflowers, gerberas, daisies | Bright, low-scent; note many hospitals restrict flowers in ICU/immunocompromised units, and avoid heavy fragrance/pollen |

## Weddings
- **Classic bridal:** garden roses, peonies, ranunculus, hydrangea, calla
  lilies, orchids, lisianthus, anemones, with baby's breath and eucalyptus.
- **Palettes:** white/blush (traditional), or the couple's chosen scheme.
- **Symbolic picks:** ivy (fidelity), myrtle (love/marriage — a British royal
  tradition), stephanotis (marital happiness).

## Sympathy & funerals
- **Flowers:** white lilies (esp. Oriental & the peace lily plant), white
  roses, chrysanthemums (funerary across much of Europe & Asia), gladioli
  (tall standing sprays), carnations, orchids.
- **Forms:** standing sprays, wreaths, casket sprays, and potted peace lilies.
- **Palette:** white and soft pastels convey peace; deep red conveys deep
  love/loss.
- **Cultural cautions:** chrysanthemums are strictly for the dead in much of
  continental Europe and East Asia — cheerful in the US, funereal elsewhere.
  In some faiths flowers are discouraged (traditional Jewish and Islamic
  funerals often prefer donations); check before sending.

## Seasonal & religious holidays
| Holiday | Flowers | Palette |
|---|---|---|
| **Christmas** | Poinsettia, amaryllis, holly, roses, paperwhites | Red, white, green |
| **Easter / spring** | Easter lily, tulips, daffodils, hyacinths | White, pastel |
| **Thanksgiving / autumn** | Sunflowers, chrysanthemums, dahlias | Warm amber, rust, gold |
| **Diwali** | Marigolds (garlands), roses | Orange, red, gold |
| **Day of the Dead (Día de los Muertos)** | Marigolds (cempasúchil) | Orange, gold |
| **Hanukkah** | Blue & white arrangements | Blue, white |
| **Nowruz (Persian New Year)** | Hyacinth (sonbol), tulips | Spring pastels |
| **Lunar New Year** | Plum blossom, orchids, peonies, kumquat | Red, gold, pink |
