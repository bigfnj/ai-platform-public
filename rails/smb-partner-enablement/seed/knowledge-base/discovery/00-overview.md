# Discovery — scope of this collection

> Source: PLACEHOLDER — discovery guides, qualification frameworks, persona material
> As of: PLACEHOLDER · Owner: PLACEHOLDER

## What belongs in this collection

The questions a partner asks to qualify and shape an SMB opportunity, organised by persona
and by play. For each question, record the signal a good answer gives — that is what makes
the assistant useful live on a call rather than just a question generator.

## Why this collection carries the voice experience

Discovery is the content a partner needs hands-free, walking into a meeting. It is the
collection most likely to be queried by voice on a phone, so keep answers speakable: short
sentences, no tables, no nested bullets. If a section only makes sense as a grid on screen,
it belongs in a different collection.
