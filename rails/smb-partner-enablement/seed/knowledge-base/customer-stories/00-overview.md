## What belongs in this collection

Proof points a partner can say out loud on a call: what the customer's problem was, what was
deployed, and what changed. Segment and industry matter more than logo — an SMB buyer wants
to hear about a business their own size.

## Only publishable references

Everything in this collection can be repeated verbatim to a customer, because that is exactly
what will happen. Anything under NDA, unapproved, or internal-only does not belong here — the
assistant cannot distinguish a reference story from a confidential one, so the boundary has
to be enforced at authoring time.
