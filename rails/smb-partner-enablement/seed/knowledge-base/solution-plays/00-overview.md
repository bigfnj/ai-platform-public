## What belongs in this collection

One file per play a partner actually sells into SMB. Each play should carry: the customer
problem in the customer's language, the workloads involved, the buyer persona, the typical
deal shape, and what "good" looks like at close.

## Structure that retrieves well

Lead each play with the problem, not the product. A partner asks "the customer is worried
about staff sharing passwords" — retrieval lands on the play only if that phrasing appears
in the prose. Product names are the answer, not the question.
