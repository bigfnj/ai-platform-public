# Benefits packages: what a partner receives and how it gets activated

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/benefits/benefits-at-a-glance · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/benefits/manage-your-partner-network-benefits · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/benefits/benefits-faq-new · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/mpn-overview · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/introduction-to-pcs
> As of: 2026-08 (benefits guide dated 2026-02-18; FY26 refresh effective 2026-02-13) · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What kinds of benefits does a partner membership actually deliver?

Benefits arrive in seven documented categories, and they are all surfaced in the Benefits workspace in Partner Center. **Azure credits** are monthly or yearly credits usable in the Azure portal. **Cloud services** are licence-based subscriptions requiring a product key for activation, spanning Modern Work, Business Applications and Windows online subscriptions, redeemed in the Microsoft 365 admin portal. **Software benefits** are on-premises products such as SQL Server. **Developer tools** cover Visual Studio and GitHub. **Technical support and consultation** is delivered by raising incidents. **Technical presales and deployment** provides one-to-one consultations to plan, build and publish. **Marketing benefits** are the gateway to go-to-market resources and Microsoft Marketplace. A separate **Logo Builder** tool generates partner badges and certified letters.

## What does the entry-level package contain?

Partner Launch Benefits is a small, deliberately starter-sized package priced at $350 USD. Per Microsoft's benefits summary aligned to the 18 February 2026 benefits guide, it includes **$700 USD per year of Azure bulk credits (production)**, 5 users of Microsoft 365 Business Premium and 5 of Teams Enterprise, 1 Visual Studio Enterprise IDE user, 5 users each of Entra ID P2, Defender Suite and Defender for Endpoint P2, 4 users each of Dynamics 365 Business Central Premium, Dynamics 365 Sales Enterprise and Power BI Premium, single-user entitlements for Power Apps Premium, Power Automate Premium, Project Plan 5 and Visio Plan 2, and a 5-user Dynamics 365 Business Central partner sandbox. On the go-to-market side it includes Partner Marketing Center Pro and a Geo Expansion Readiness Assessment. It carries **no** support or advisory hours.

## What do the Partner Success packages add over the entry package?

The two Partner Success packages are where support and advisory entitlements appear, and the licence pools widen substantially. **Partner Success Core Benefits** ($925 USD) provides **$2,400 USD per year** of Azure bulk credits, 15-user pools across Microsoft 365 Business Premium, Teams Enterprise, Entra ID P2, Defender Suite, Dynamics 365 Business Central Premium and Sales Enterprise, 8 Visual Studio Enterprise IDE users, Windows Server and System Center licences, plus **5 hours of technical presales and deployment advisory** and **2 partner cloud support incidents**. **Partner Success Expanded Benefits** ($4,125 USD) scales to **$5,000 USD per year** of Azure credits, 35-user pools, 10 Microsoft 365 Copilot users, Copilot Studio capacity, Viva/Entra/Intune Suites, a much wider Dynamics 365 sandbox set, and **10 advisory hours plus 5 partner cloud support incidents** with unlimited technical sales-prep assistance.

## What extra support do I get from a Solutions Partner designation?

The support and advisory step-up at designation level is the single largest jump in the benefits ladder, and it is common to all designations regardless of solution area. Microsoft lists, as included with **all** Solutions Partner designations: **50 hours** of technical presales and deployment advisory hours, **unlimited** assistance with technical sales prep and deal enablement, **50 partner cloud support incidents**, and **20 Microsoft Product Support (on-premises) incidents**. The go-to-market set — Partner Concierge (50), Partner Marketing Center Pro, and the Geo Expansion Readiness Assessment — matches the Partner Success packages. Product benefits then vary by which designation pathway was earned.

## Do the three Azure designation benefits stack if I earn all three?

Only partially, and this is a frequent source of disappointment. Microsoft grants a shared "core" set **once** across the three Azure-related designations rather than three times: "Solutions Partner product benefits for Data and AI, Digital and App Innovation, and Infrastructure include a shared 'core' set that is granted once across those three Azure designations." Microsoft's benefits FAQ spells out the incremental portion for each — for Data & AI, Digital & App Innovation and Infrastructure individually, the extra incremental benefits are 100 users of Microsoft Entra ID P2 and 100 users of Defender for Endpoint P2, again granted only once across the three. Each Azure pathway does list $10,000 USD per year of Azure bulk credits and $5,000 USD per year of GitHub Copilot Enterprise via Azure credits in its own table.

## How do I activate or redeem my benefits?

Benefits are never automatic — purchasing does not deliver them, redeeming does. Microsoft's warning is direct: "Membership renewal doesn't automatically redeem benefits. You must manually redeem the new set of benefits awarded upon renewal." Redemption happens in the Benefits workspace in Partner Center, and requires the **Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program partner admin** role, though Microsoft notes some marketing benefits are available to other roles. Cloud service benefits are redeemed by fetching a 5x5 licence key from the Cloud services section and activating it in the Microsoft 365 admin portal. Where a partner enrols in a new solution area or specialization mid-membership, a **Claim benefits** call to action appears in the Benefits workspace — it only appears when there is something new to claim.

## Why haven't my benefits appeared after I paid?

There is a documented payment-processing lag that depends on whether it is your first purchase. Microsoft explains two credit card modes: on a **first purchase** the First Charge policy applies, the card is charged immediately and benefits are provisioned right away. **From the second purchase onward**, Post Pay mode applies with daily invoicing — a consolidated invoice is generated the next day and sent to the bank for confirmation, and "your benefits are provisioned automatically four days after your purchase date." If payment fails, a banner appears in the Benefits or Membership workspace prompting the partner to clear the dues, after which benefits are provisioned. A partner who paid three days ago and sees nothing is almost certainly in this window rather than facing an error.

## When do my benefits expire?

Three separate dates govern benefit lifetime and they do not coincide. The **membership end date** is 365 days from the purchase date. The **benefits end date** is the membership end date plus the 30-day renewal window, during which benefits do not expire. The **benefits activate-by date** is the deadline for redeeming what was granted, and Microsoft sets it at **13 months from the membership start date** — "benefits expire if not redeemed within this timeframe." Once redeemed, each benefit type then runs its own clock: Azure credits, developer tools, support and advisory benefits, and marketing benefits all expire **12 months from the benefits unlocked date**, while cloud services expire **12 months from the date the keys were redeemed** in the Microsoft admin portal.

## Can I keep the software licences if I don't renew?

No — the licences are use rights tied to active membership, not perpetual grants, and this is one of the highest-consequence facts in the whole program. Microsoft's answer is unambiguous: the on-premises software product licences included with a Solutions Partner designation, legacy gold or silver benefits, or the Microsoft Action Pack "aren't perpetual. You're entitled, and have the right, to use the licenses only during your membership. You need to obtain license rights via Commercial Licensing or uninstall any on-premises software obtained via the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program if you choose not to renew your membership offer." Microsoft separately notes that product benefit licence terms differ from commercially acquired licences, that they do not include Software Assurance, and that other restrictions apply.

## What are the practical gotchas with benefit tokens and keys?

Several redemption rules are irreversible, so they are worth knowing before the first click. Tokens cannot be split — "a 25-license token can't be split into five different five-license tokens." Replacement tokens for activation on the wrong tenant are issued **one time only, within 30 days** of the original activation, via Support. Microsoft 365 and Teams benefits come in **EEA/Switzerland** and **non-EEA** versions, and "once you retrieve keys for a specific version on Partner Center, you can't request keys for a different version" without a support ticket. Azure bulk credits must create a **new** subscription and cannot be redeemed against an existing one, and user assignment for bulk credits cannot be changed until the next renewal. Some keys are Multiple Activation Keys with a finite number of activations, and extra activations are not granted.

## Currency warning

The benefit contents, quantities and credit amounts in this file come from Microsoft's benefits summary aligned to the **18 February 2026** Benefits Guide, reflecting an FY26 refresh that took effect **13 February 2026** — the second catalogue change in roughly a year, following the 22 January 2025 refresh. Microsoft states that removed benefits "won't be offered after February 13, 2026" and that newly added benefits are only partially retrofitted to existing purchases. Quantities differ between EEA and non-EEA versions of several products. Never quote a licence count, credit amount or support incident number without re-reading the current Benefits Guide at aka.ms/solutionspartner.benefits and the partner's own Benefits workspace.
