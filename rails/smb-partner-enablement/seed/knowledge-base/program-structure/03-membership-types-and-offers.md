# Membership types and offers — what you can buy and what each grants

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/mpn-overview · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/intro-to-cloud-partner-program-membership · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/isv-success · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/mpn-benefits-map-competency-learning-option-enroll · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/silver-gold-membership-competency · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/csp-overview
> As of: 2026-08 (prices per Microsoft Learn, article dated 2025-01) · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## Is there a fee to join the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program?

The program is structured as free enrolment plus paid offers on top, though the "free" part warrants care in how it is stated. Microsoft's enrolment documentation walks through creating a work account, submitting the enrolment request and passing verification without describing any fee for enrolment itself, and every fee documented on Microsoft Learn attaches to a specific benefit package or designation rather than to membership. Microsoft's public partner marketing describes enrolling at no cost. **Verification limitation:** the partner.microsoft.com marketing pages returned HTTP 403 during this research, so the precise "no cost to join" wording was not confirmed against a live Microsoft-hosted page. The defensible statement to a partner is that enrolment carries no documented fee and that all documented costs are for optional offers.

## What benefit packages can a services partner buy?

Microsoft offers a laddered set of three packages for partners delivering services, sized to where the business is in its journey. **Partner Launch Benefits** is the entry package, described as helping a partner "discover, use, and test Microsoft products" with Azure credits and licences for Microsoft 365 Business Premium, Dynamics 365 Business Central and Sales Enterprise, and Visual Studio. **Partner Success Core Benefits** is aimed at building a cloud and AI practice, adding technical presales and deployment hours, support incidents, and a wider licence pool. **Partner Success Expanded Benefits** is the largest package, which Microsoft recommends specifically "for partners who require a significant number of product benefits and support, especially partners who migrate from legacy Silver and Gold benefits."

## What are the published prices for the MAICPP offers?

Microsoft publishes list prices in USD on its membership overview page, and these are the figures partners plan against. Partner Launch Benefits is listed at **$350 USD**; Partner Success Core Benefits at **$925 USD**; Partner Success Expanded Benefits at **$4,125 USD**; the Solutions Partner designation offer at **$4,875 USD**; the Training Services Partner Program at **$4,875 USD**; and ISV Success is free for the first year and **$1,550 USD** from the second year onward. Each is quoted as "or equivalent in local currency." Microsoft notes that from December 2023 exchange rates began updating dynamically rather than being static, so local-currency prices move and can differ significantly from a previously quoted figure.

## What do ISVs and software companies get instead?

Software developers have their own track, because the value they need is build-and-publish support rather than services enablement. **ISV Success** is a 12-month program for B2B software companies building on the Microsoft Cloud and intending to publish to Microsoft Marketplace; it bundles Azure credits, a Microsoft 365 E5 developer subscription, Visual Studio and GitHub Enterprise entitlements, Dynamics 365 partner sandboxes, and one-to-one technical consultations. **Marketplace Rewards** is offered at no cost once a partner has transactable apps published, providing marketing, sales and technical support for their offers. **Solutions Partner with certified software** is the designation route for ISVs, earned by validating a solution's capability rather than by a capability score.

## What is required to be a Training Services partner?

Training delivery is governed by participation requirements rather than by a points score, with an optional designation layered on top. Microsoft requires Training Services partners to enrol via the Training Services Partner Onboarding Form (onboarding typically takes one to two weeks), to use official Microsoft Courseware from Microsoft Learn, to deliver all courses through Microsoft Certified Trainers, to give every student a post-training achievement code, and to run a post-training quality survey through the Metrics that Matter platform. Meeting those requirements is enough to deliver official Microsoft training. Meeting additional solution-area KPI benchmarks is what earns the Training Services Solutions Partner branding, Marketplace promotion and software benefits — Microsoft notes those KPI requirements are subject to change annually.

## Is the Cloud Solution Provider program a membership tier?

No — CSP is a transactional authorisation that sits alongside membership, not a rung on a membership ladder. Microsoft describes two CSP sales models. In the **indirect model**, a partner becomes an indirect reseller buying through an authorised distributor; the stated requirements are an active MAICPP membership with a Partner Location Account ID for the selling location, the ability to sign legal agreements, and a partnership with an authorised distributor. In the **direct-bill model**, the partner buys from Microsoft and bills customers directly; Microsoft's stated requirements include at least the prior 12 months as an authorised indirect reseller, at least USD one million in CSP trailing-twelve-month transactional revenue at the Partner Global Account level, a passing capabilities assessment, at least one managed service or IP service, at least one Solutions Partner designation, completion of Partner Center security score requirements, and purchase of an Advanced or Premier Support for Partners plan.

## Which legacy offers were retired, and when?

Four legacy offers were withdrawn from sale simultaneously on **22 January 2025**: legacy Silver (listed at $1,670 USD), legacy Gold ($4,730 USD), Microsoft Action Pack Subscription or MAPS ($475 USD), and Microsoft Learning Action Pack or LAPS ($475 USD). Microsoft's rule for all four is identical — partners with active purchases retain access to the benefits until the end date, but renewal is not possible. The last date to renew legacy Silver or Gold was 21 January 2025, and only if the anniversary date fell on or before that date; where a renewal window was already open on 22 January 2025, Microsoft honoured it for 30 days from the date it opened. Microsoft's recommended replacements are Partner Success Core or Expanded Benefits for MAPS and LAPS holders, Partner Success Expanded Benefits for legacy Silver, and a Solutions Partner designation for legacy Gold.

## Can I upgrade from one package to another, or buy the same offer twice?

Neither, and this catches partners out at purchase time. Microsoft states that "Partner Center doesn't support upgrades, including prorated pricing for new offers" — so a partner holding Partner Launch Benefits cannot upgrade to Partner Success Core Benefits. Each offer can be purchased **only once**, though a partner can buy different offers separately to accumulate more benefits, since each is individually purchasable one time. Microsoft is equally clear that a package cannot be swapped mid-term: "The benefits package selected at the anniversary date is valid for 12 months and can't be changed." The practical consequence is that the package choice made at purchase is locked for the year, so it should be sized against the coming year's needs rather than today's.

## Currency warning

Every price, threshold and offer name in this file is subject to change and several have already changed within a fiscal year — the legacy offer retirements landed on 22 January 2025 and the benefits catalogue was refreshed again effective 13 February 2026. The USD list prices here come from a Microsoft Learn article dated January 2025 and last updated June 2026; local-currency amounts float with dynamic exchange rates and may differ materially. The CSP direct-bill revenue threshold and capability requirements are likewise revised periodically. Re-verify all figures in Partner Center's Membership offers page and the current benefits guide before quoting them to a partner.
