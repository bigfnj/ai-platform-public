# Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program — what it is and why a partner joins

## What is the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (MAICPP)?

The Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program, abbreviated MAICPP, is Microsoft's single umbrella commercial partner program. Microsoft's documentation describes it as the vehicle through which Microsoft "provides benefits, offerings, and incentives for partners," and describes joining it as becoming "part of a global community that connects you to the relationships, insights, tools, resources, and programs" a partner needs to serve customers and grow. It is not a product and not a certification — it is the membership relationship that sits underneath everything else a company does commercially with Microsoft: buying benefit packages, earning designations, transacting through Cloud Solution Provider, publishing to Microsoft Marketplace, and claiming incentives.

## What do I actually get out of joining the partner program?

Membership gives a company access to a pool of resources it would otherwise have to buy at commercial rates. Microsoft's membership overview lists the benefit categories explicitly: Azure credits, cloud services, product licenses, technical consultation, developer tools, and marketing benefits such as customer-facing badges generated from Logo Builder. Microsoft frames the packages as delivering "package-based product and support portfolios at significant discount versus standalone access." Beyond the licences themselves, membership is the gate to co-sell with Microsoft sellers, to receive referrals and leads, and to enrol in incentive programs — none of which are available to a company that has not enrolled.

## What is Partner Center and how does it relate to the partner program?

Partner Center is the web console where the partner program is actually administered; the program is the entitlement, Partner Center is the place you exercise it. Microsoft describes Partner Center as the unified place to "manage your Microsoft account and users, engage with customers, build relationships with other partners, enrol in incentive programs, manage customer subscriptions, bill and get paid, and contact support." It is organised into workspaces — Membership, Benefits, Referrals, Incentives, Insights, Customers, Billing, Marketplace offers, Earnings, Security, Account settings, Help and support — and which workspaces a given person sees depends both on which programs the company has enrolled in and on which roles that person holds.

## Who is the partner program for — is it only for resellers?

The program deliberately covers several distinct business models, and Microsoft splits its offer catalogue along those lines rather than assuming every partner resells. Microsoft's membership documentation groups offers into three partner profiles: **services partners**, who deliver consulting, implementation and managed services; **ISV partners**, meaning independent software vendors who develop software and publish it to Microsoft Marketplace; and **Training Services partners**, who deliver Microsoft training. A single company can hold offers from more than one of these tracks. Reselling specifically is a separate authorisation — the Cloud Solution Provider program — layered on top of partner program membership rather than being the definition of it.

## What does it mean when a company calls itself a "Solutions Partner"?

Microsoft attaches a specific and deliberately narrow meaning to the term, and publishes a disclaimer about it. In Microsoft's words, *Solutions Partner* "refers to a company that is a member of the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program and can offer software, services, and solutions to customers," and Microsoft states that references to Solutions Partner and any associated designation "shouldn't be interpreted as an offer, endorsement, guarantee, proof of effectiveness or functionality, commitment, or any other type of representation or warranty on the part of Microsoft." Microsoft is explicit that decisions about partner selection rest solely with the customer's business. Treat a Solutions Partner badge as evidence of measured attainment against Microsoft's criteria, not as a Microsoft warranty.

## Is being in the partner program the same as being a Cloud Solution Provider?

No — these are two different things and a company can hold one without the other. Partner program membership is the base relationship. The Cloud Solution Provider (CSP) program is a separate transactional authorisation that lets a partner sell Microsoft subscriptions to customers, either buying from a distributor as an indirect reseller or buying from Microsoft directly as a direct-bill partner. Microsoft's CSP requirements list "an active membership in the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (MAICPP) and Partner Location Account (PLA) ID for the location you want to sell in" as a prerequisite for both CSP models, which makes program membership a precondition for CSP rather than a synonym for it.

## Currency warning

Program names, offer catalogues, benefit contents and prices in this collection reset on Microsoft's fiscal year and have in practice changed mid-year as well — Microsoft refreshed the benefits catalogue on 22 January 2025 and again effective 13 February 2026. Everything here reflects Microsoft Learn documentation as retrieved on 14 August 2026. Before quoting a figure, a threshold or a program name to a partner, re-verify it against Partner Center and the current Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program Benefits Guide, because acting on a stale number costs the partner money.
