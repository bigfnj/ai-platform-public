# SMB Copilot SKUs and published prices

> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What are the durable Microsoft 365 Copilot SKUs for SMB?

From **1 July 2026** Microsoft transitioned its promotional Copilot bundles into two durable
SKUs, both sold at 1–300 seats on an annual subscription with annual billing:

- **Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot — USD 23.50 per user per month**
- **Microsoft 365 Business Premium with Copilot — USD 32.00 per user per month**

Microsoft's guidance is explicit about leading with these: partners are encouraged to "lead with
Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot or Business Premium with Copilot as integrated
offerings for productivity, AI, and security." The commercial logic is that a durable SKU is
"an always-on offer that can be standardized across accounts and carried confidently into
renewal conversations," rather than a promotion a partner has to re-justify each cycle.

## Which Copilot promotions are still running?

Two, both published as running through **31 December 2026** and both at 1–300 seats. A **25%
promotional offer** on Microsoft 365 Business Basic plus Microsoft 365 Copilot Business lands at
**USD 21.00 per user per month**. A **15% promotional offer** on Microsoft 365 Copilot Business
alone lands at **USD 18.00 per user per month**.

Note the shape of the ladder: Basic-plus-Copilot at 21, Copilot Business alone at 18, Standard
with Copilot at 23.50, Premium with Copilot at 32. The gap between Basic-plus-Copilot and
Standard-with-Copilot is small, which makes the upgrade conversation easy — and the step to
Premium buys the security and compliance layer that Microsoft positions as the prerequisite for
responsible AI adoption.

## What does the Copilot in 30 trial actually cost?

Nothing. Microsoft describes it as "a 25-seat, 30-day Microsoft 365 Copilot Business trial at
**$0** through CSP New Commerce," available to CSP partners serving customers with fewer than 300
employees, transacted through a **$0 SKU in CSP** from 1 August 2026.

One operational detail that matters: **a 7-day cancellation window applies**, consistent with the
standard New Commerce policy. The trial is a real subscription, not a sandbox.

Microsoft's targeting advice is to "prioritize customers already using Microsoft 365 Business
Standard or Microsoft 365 Business Premium," and to treat renewal discussions as the natural
opening for a structured AI evaluation.

## What do the Business Premium security add-ons cost?

Microsoft publishes three add-on prices for Microsoft 365 Business Premium, which it describes
as "our productivity and security solution designed for SMBs (1–300 users)":

- **Microsoft Defender Suite for Business Premium — USD 10 per user per month**
- **Microsoft Purview Suite for Business Premium — USD 10 per user per month**
- **Both suites together — USD 15 per user per month**

Microsoft states the bundle delivers "up to 68% savings compared to buying the products
separately." Separately, a **50% discount on the Purview Suite for Business Premium** runs from
1 December 2025 through 31 December 2026 for customers who buy Business Premium together with
Microsoft Copilot Business or Microsoft 365 Copilot.

## How big is the SMB AI opportunity, in Microsoft's own numbers?

Microsoft describes the SMB segment as "a Microsoft-estimated **$625 billion** market opportunity
in FY27," footnoted precisely as "Microsoft analysis based on IDC, *Worldwide ICT Spending Guide:
Enterprise and SMB by Industry*, IDC_P33207_0725, July 2025. Figures represent Microsoft's FY27
total addressable market estimates."

Attribute it that way — it is a Microsoft *estimate built on IDC data*, not an IDC figure and not
a reported result. Microsoft also states that "Copilot-related partner investment
increas[ed] 130% year over year" in FY27, sourced to an internal MCAPS Start for Partners
session, which is a partner-facing claim rather than an audited disclosure.

## Currency warning

**This page is the most perishable in the corpus.** Every figure here is a list price or a
promotional rate with an end date, and several are already extensions of earlier deadlines.
Prices vary by market and currency, partner cost is not list price, and promotional eligibility
has conditions this summary does not reproduce. **Never quote a price to a customer from this
page.** Confirm the current price list and promotion terms in Partner Center first — the price
list is republished monthly and has been republished mid-month at least once in 2026.
