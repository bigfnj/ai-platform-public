# Current SMB offers and promotions — what a partner can actually lead with

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-august
> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## Copilot in 30 — the partner-led SMB Copilot trial

Copilot in 30 is a partner-led Microsoft 365 Copilot Business trial, generally available through
CSP New Commerce and running through **31 December 2026**. It is built specifically for
organisations with **fewer than 300 employees**, which makes it the single most relevant Copilot
motion for an SMB-focused partner today.

The mechanics are a **25-user, 30-day trial** combined with guidance to help the customer
identify priority users, explore role-relevant use cases, and evaluate value before committing to
a broader deployment. Partners transact it using **product ID CFQ7TTC0MM8R, SKU ID 006Z**. It is
available to CSP authorised partners holding global admin or billing admin roles.

Microsoft also introduced a customer evaluation tool alongside it: the customer selects their
industry and receives tailored prompts and scenarios, so the trial becomes a role-relevant
experience rather than an empty licence.

## Why lead a Copilot conversation with security

Microsoft is explicitly positioning security as the prerequisite for AI readiness in SMB, not as
a separate sale. The argument is that AI adoption widens the blast radius of poor governance, so
Microsoft 365 Business Premium, Defender and Purview become the foundation a Copilot deployment
sits on. For a partner this is commercially convenient: it turns a single Copilot conversation
into a security practice conversation with recurring services attached.

The concrete lever is **50% off the Microsoft Purview Suite for Business Premium**, available to
customers licensing Business Premium together with Microsoft 365 Copilot Business or Microsoft
365 Copilot, running through **31 December 2026**.

## Which Microsoft 365 promotions are live right now?

Two Microsoft 365 E3 offers run through **31 December 2026**: 10% off on a three-year term for
new-to-offer customers, and 20% off on a one-year term as a targeted offer. These support both
new customer acquisition and upgrading existing Office 365 customers.

Separately, there is a **10% discount on Microsoft 365 E3 and 15% on Microsoft 365 E5** for
new-to-E5 customers on one- and three-year CSP terms, plus 10% off the Defender and Purview
suites — running through **30 September 2026**. Offer stacking is not allowed.

**Microsoft 365 E7 promotions retire on 1 October 2026.** Eligible purchases remain available
through 30 September 2026, so any E7 opportunity sitting in a pipeline should be closed before
that date. Subscriptions bought before retirement continue on their contracted terms.

## New CSP security add-ons for E3 customers

For customers who want stronger security but are not ready to commit to E5 or the full suites,
Microsoft added CSP add-ons with pricing consistent across Enterprise Agreement and CSP:
**Microsoft Entra ID P2**, **Defender for Endpoint P2** as an add-on for Microsoft 365 E3, and
**Defender for Office 365 P2**. The consistent pricing matters commercially — it removes the
licensing-model arbitrage conversation and lets a partner attach advanced security without
restructuring the customer's agreement.

## The shift from promotions to growth margin

Microsoft is signalling a deliberate move away from promotional transactions toward **growth
margin**, which rewards sustained customer growth and deployment success over time rather than
one-off discounted deals. The retirement of the E7 promotions is framed explicitly as part of
this shift.

The practical implication for a partner is that a business model built on chasing promotional
pricing has a shrinking runway. The durable position is deployment depth and usage growth —
which, not coincidentally, is also what the partner capability score's Customer Success metrics
measure.

## Currency warning

Every offer on this page carries an end date, and several were already extensions of earlier
deadlines. Promotional terms, eligibility and product IDs change monthly. **Confirm current
eligibility, terms and end dates in Partner Center before quoting any price to a customer** —
this page is a snapshot of August 2026, not a live price list.
