## What belongs in this collection

One heading per objection, phrased the way a customer actually says it. The body leads with
the response and then the evidence behind it.

## Heading style matters here more than anywhere else

Chunks carry their nearest heading as the citation title, and this collection is queried
almost entirely by matching the customer's own words. A heading of
`## Objection: "We already pay for Google Workspace"` retrieves on that phrasing; a heading
of `## Competitive — productivity suites` does not.

## Keep the response honest

If the honest answer is that the customer is right for their situation, write that. A
partner who gets caught repeating a weak rebuttal loses the room, and the assistant has no
way to tell a strong argument from a wishful one — it only repeats what is here.
