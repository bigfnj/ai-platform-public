# Solutions Partner for Infrastructure (Azure) — what it covers and how it is scored

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-azure · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/partner-capability-score
> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What does the Infrastructure (Azure) designation cover?

Infrastructure (Azure) is the migration-and-run designation among the three Azure solution areas. It covers moving and operating server, network, desktop and workload estates on Azure — datacentre migration, Windows Server and hybrid estates, networking, Azure Virtual Desktop, VMware on Azure and SAP on Azure. Like the other Azure areas it is scored from Azure consumed revenue and Azure certifications with no Microsoft 365 component, and under the August 2026 badge rollup it contributes to the **Solutions Partner for Cloud & AI Platforms** badge.

## Which customer problems does Infrastructure (Azure) map to?

This is the designation to lead with when the customer has physical or virtual estate they want to stop owning: a datacentre lease expiring, ageing Windows Server or VMware hosts, an end-user compute estate that needs to move to Azure Virtual Desktop or Windows 365, a network that needs re-architecting for cloud, or an SAP landscape looking for a home. The required certification set signals the scope directly — Azure administration and solution architecture as the base, then Azure Network Engineer, Windows Server Hybrid Administrator, Azure Virtual Desktop Specialty and Azure for SAP Workloads Specialty.

## How does the Net customer adds metric score in Infrastructure (Azure)?

Performance is worth up to **30 points**, with each net new customer contributing **10 points**, so three net adds reaches the ceiling. The calculation is `Net customers added = (Eligible Customers last month) – (Eligible Customers same month last year)`. An eligible customer on the **Enterprise** track is a unique tenant contributing at least **USD 1,000** of Azure consumed revenue in one of the last two months; on the **SMB** track the threshold is **USD 500** in at least one of the last two months. All Azure Service Level 2 services are eligible, all offers except Support, Internal, Benefit Program and Trial count, and eligible association types are CSP Tiers 1 and 2, DPOR and PAL. These Performance rules are common to all three Azure solution areas.

## How does the Intermediate certifications metric score in Infrastructure (Azure)?

Intermediate certifications is worth up to **20 points**, from up to **five individuals at 4 points each**, structured as a two-step gate. Step 1 requires **Azure Administrator Associate** — two people on the **Enterprise** track, earning no points in themselves, or one person on the **SMB** track, earning a flat 4 points capped regardless of how many complete it. Step 2 awards up to 4 points per individual holding at least one of **Azure Network Engineer Associate** or **Windows Server Hybrid Administrator Associate**. Step 1 is a hard gate: without it, step 2 scores zero. A person can count in both steps but only once per step.

## How does the Advanced certifications metric score in Infrastructure (Azure)?

Advanced certifications is also worth up to **20 points** from up to **five individuals at 4 points each**. Step 1 requires **Azure Solutions Architect Expert** — two people on the Enterprise track earning no points, or one person on the SMB track earning a flat capped 4 points. Step 2 awards up to 4 points per individual holding at least one of **Azure Virtual Desktop Specialty** or **Azure for SAP Workloads Specialty**. The step-2 list here is notably narrow: only two specialty certifications qualify, which means an Infrastructure partner with no AVD or SAP practice has a structural problem in this metric even if their Azure migration business is healthy.

## How do Usage growth and Deployments score in Infrastructure (Azure)?

Both Customer success metrics are scored identically across the three Azure areas and identically for Enterprise and SMB. **Usage growth** is worth up to **20 points**, with each one percent of trailing-year Azure consumed revenue growth contributing one point, subject to a floor of at least **USD 1,000** total qualifying ACR before the metric scores at all. **Deployments** is worth up to **10 points**, with each eligible deployment contributing **two points** to a maximum of five. The Deployments exclusion bites hardest here: eligible deployments are unique Azure Service Level 2 services contributing more than zero ACR over 12 months, **excluding Virtual Machines and Virtual Machines Licenses**. A partner whose Azure business is entirely lift-and-shift VMs will score zero on Deployments and therefore cannot qualify, regardless of total points.

## What does a qualifying Infrastructure (Azure) score look like in practice?

The five metrics sum to exactly 100: Performance 30, Intermediate certifications 20, Advanced certifications 20, Usage growth 20, Deployments 10. Because 70 is the bar and every metric must be non-zero, the two binding constraints for most infrastructure practices are the Azure Solutions Architect Expert gate on advanced certifications and the VM exclusion on Deployments. The practical implication is that an Infrastructure partner needs to be selling around the VM — backup, networking, Azure Files, monitoring, identity, AVD — both to earn Deployments points and to build the certification profile the skilling metrics require.

## Currency warning

Point weights, ACR thresholds and certification lists here come from the Microsoft Learn Solutions Partner for Data & AI, Infrastructure, and Digital & App Innovation page carrying a page date of 30 June 2026 and last updated 3 August 2026. Certification names and retirement dates change frequently and Microsoft explicitly warns that workload eligibility is subject to change. Microsoft's worked examples on this page contain at least one copy-paste error (an Infrastructure advanced-certification example refers to Azure Administrator Associate where the rule requires Azure Solutions Architect Expert), so trust the stated rules over the examples and verify against your Partner Center metric cards before quoting numbers to a partner.
