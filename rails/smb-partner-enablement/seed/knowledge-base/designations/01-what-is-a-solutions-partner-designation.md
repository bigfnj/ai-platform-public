# What a Microsoft Solutions Partner designation is and why a partner would pursue one

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/introduction-to-pcs · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-faq · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/partner-capability-score
> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What is a Microsoft Solutions Partner designation?

A Solutions Partner designation is a customer-facing status inside the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program that says your organisation has demonstrated broad capability in one of Microsoft's commercial solution areas. Microsoft's own definition is that "Solutions Partner refers to a company that is a member of the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program and might offer software, services, or solutions to customers. This designation identifies partners' broad capabilities and experience in high-demand solution areas." It is not a self-declared claim and it is not bought outright: eligibility is calculated automatically from telemetry Microsoft already holds in Partner Center, and only once you qualify can you pay to enrol.

## What are the six Solutions Partner designation pathways?

There are six designation pathways, each aligned to a Microsoft solution area: Data & AI (Azure), Digital & App Innovation (Azure), Infrastructure (Azure), Business Applications, Modern Work, and Security. Each pathway is scored and earned separately, has its own metric thresholds, and has its own set of incremental product benefits. A partner can hold one, several, or all six. In Partner Center you continue to see and work with these six individual designations — they are the unit of qualification, scoring and benefit entitlement.

## What are the three Solutions Partner designation badges?

As of the August 2026 refresh of the Microsoft documentation, the six designation pathways roll up into three customer-facing badges. Data & AI (Azure), Digital & App Innovation (Azure) and Infrastructure (Azure) all roll into **Solutions Partner for Cloud & AI Platforms**. Modern Work and Business Applications roll into **Solutions Partner for AI Business Solutions**. Security maps to **Solutions Partner for Security**. Microsoft is explicit that qualifying for any pathway that maps to a badge earns you that badge, and that the way you qualify, enrol, earn benefits and are scored is unchanged — the badge is purely an external representation that rolls up the designations you earned.

## Why would a partner pursue a Solutions Partner designation?

Microsoft's stated answer is differentiation and credibility. In the Solutions Partner FAQ, Microsoft writes that "customers want to work with partners who have the right skills and capabilities to meet their needs, along with demonstrated ability to deliver customer success. The Solutions Partner designations clearly differentiate you from your competition." Beyond the marketing claim, three concrete things follow attainment: a customer-facing badge you may use under the Microsoft brand guidelines, a materially larger benefits bundle than any non-designated membership offer, and eligibility to co-sell services with Microsoft sellers. The designation is also a hard prerequisite for every specialization.

## Do I have to pay for a Solutions Partner designation, and how much?

Yes, there is an annual fee, but Microsoft does not publish a single fixed price in its Learn documentation. The FAQ states that "for partners who attain a Solutions Partner designation, the required annual fee is based on the benefits selected and is due at your existing anniversary date." Two practical points matter more than the number: you cannot pay your way in — you must first reach Qualified status through your partner capability score — and you only pay once. Microsoft confirms that "once you attain a Solutions Partner designation, subsequent Solutions Partner designations can be attained, after requirements are met, with no extra fee."

## What is the difference between Qualified status and Enrolled status?

These are two distinct states in Partner Center and partners routinely confuse them. **In progress** means you have earned some points but are still below 70 total, or you have zero points in at least one individual metric. **Qualified** means you meet the qualification criteria and are now able to purchase the designation — you are not yet a Solutions Partner. **Enrolled** means you have made the payment and hold the designation. Critically, once you are Enrolled you stay enrolled until your anniversary date regardless of whether your score rises or falls in the meantime.

## How long does a Solutions Partner designation last before I have to renew?

Enrolment runs to an anniversary date one year after purchase, with a further 30-day renewal window on top. Microsoft describes the "Valid-till" date as "13 months from the date of the Solutions Partner designation purchase, including a one-month renewal window." Their worked example: a designation purchased on 15 January 2025 has an anniversary date of 15 January 2026, with the renewal window running 16 January 2026 to 15 February 2026. To keep benefits flowing without interruption, Microsoft advises renewing within those 30 days; if you miss the window you have to process a new purchase rather than a renewal.

## If I earn a second designation mid-year, when does it expire?

It inherits the anniversary date of your first designation rather than getting its own twelve-month clock. Microsoft's published example is unambiguous: if Modern Work is attained in November 2024, Business Applications in January 2025 and Security in February 2025, all three designations share the same anniversary date of November 2025. The benefits, however, are not delayed — Microsoft confirms that if you earn a subsequent designation between anniversary dates you can access the additional benefits immediately without waiting for the next anniversary.

## What happens if my score drops below 70 after I have already earned the designation?

Nothing happens immediately. Microsoft checks your eligibility only at your anniversary date, not continuously. The FAQ states: "If your organization meets requirements for the Solutions Partner designation, you immediately receive the corresponding customer-facing badge. On your anniversary date, not beforehand, we check if you still meet the requirements." Furthermore, the renewal test is generous — you only need to have qualified on at least one day within the qualification eligibility window (your anniversary month plus the previous five months, plus the renewal window), not on the anniversary date itself.

## Who in my organisation can see and manage the designation?

Designation status, scoring detail and enrolment are gated behind a specific Partner Center role. Every Microsoft Learn page on this topic opens with the same line: the appropriate role is **Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program partner admin**. If a colleague cannot see the Membership workspace or the Solutions Partner dashboard, the cause is almost always a missing role assignment rather than a data problem. Badges and benefits are attained at the partner global account (PGA) level and may then be distributed at the PGA's discretion to associated partner location accounts (PLAs).

## Currency warning

Program mechanics in this file were verified against Microsoft Learn pages carrying update dates between May 2026 and August 2026. The three-badge rollup is a recent change (the introduction page carries an August 2026 date) and older partner material — including some Microsoft FAQ content still live at the time of retrieval — refers instead to six individual badges plus a "Solutions Partner for Microsoft Cloud" badge for partners holding all six. Treat any fee figure, badge name, anniversary rule or benefit entitlement quoted in secondary material as unverified until checked against Partner Center. The annual fee itself is not published as a number on Microsoft Learn and should never be quoted from memory.
