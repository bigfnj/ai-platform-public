# How Solutions Partner designations gate benefits and co-sell — what you cannot do without one

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/benefits/benefits-at-a-glance · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/referrals/co-sell-requirements · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/referrals/services-co-sell · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/introduction-to-pcs · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/specializations
> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What can a partner not do at all without a Solutions Partner designation?

Four things are hard-gated. **You cannot earn any specialization** — Microsoft states that "to enroll into a Specialization, you need to already be enrolled into the aligned Solutions Partner designation pathway." **You cannot make a professional-services offer co-sell-ready** — services partners must "have at least one solution partner designation in Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program" for a Professional service solution to reach co-sell-ready status. **You cannot receive inbound services co-sell opportunities from Microsoft sellers**, which are routed only to partners who hold designations, specializations or Azure Expert MSP. And **you cannot display a Solutions Partner badge**, which is the credential customers and tender processes increasingly ask for by name.

## What is the practical difference in benefits between a designation and the entry-level offers?

The gap is largest in support and advisory entitlement, which is usually what a small partner feels first. Partner Success **Core** Benefits include **5 hours** of technical presales and deployment advisory time and **2** partner cloud support incidents. Partner Success **Expanded** Benefits include **10 hours** and **5** incidents. Benefits included with **all Solutions Partner designations** include **50 hours** of advisory time, **unlimited** assistance with technical sales prep and deal enablement, **50** partner cloud support incidents, and **20** Microsoft on-premises product support incidents. A partner who regularly burns support incidents or leans on presales architecture help is buying a materially different product once designated.

## What product benefits does a Solutions Partner designation unlock that lower tiers do not?

The Azure credit line alone illustrates the step change. Partner Launch Benefits include **USD 700** per year of Azure bulk credits; Partner Success Core **USD 2,400**; Partner Success Expanded **USD 5,000**. Each of the three Azure designations (Data & AI, Digital & App Innovation, Infrastructure) carries **USD 10,000** per year, as does **Security**; Business Applications and Modern Work each carry **USD 4,000**. Designation-level packages also add products the entry tiers do not include at all — for example Microsoft 365 E5 at 200 users under Modern Work, GitHub Copilot Enterprise at USD 5,000 per year via Azure credits under each Azure designation, Security Copilot at USD 5,000 per year under Security, and Microsoft 365 Copilot at 20 users under Modern Work and Business Applications.

## Are designation product benefits additive if I hold several designations?

Mostly yes, with one important exception. Microsoft states that "benefits that depend on attained Solutions Partner designation(s) are additive to common designation benefits (exceptions apply)." The exception is the three Azure designations: "Solutions Partner product benefits for Data and AI, Digital and App Innovation, and Infrastructure include a shared 'core' set that is granted once across those three Azure designations." So earning all three Azure designations gives you the incremental Azure-specific items three times but the shared core licence set only once. Since subsequent designations carry no additional fee, holding several designations is generally the highest-return move available to a designated partner.

## How do specializations add benefits on top of a designation?

Specialization benefits are incremental to designation benefits and are provisioned only if you have already purchased Solutions Partner benefits. Microsoft explains that "if you already have the relevant Solutions Partner designation and opt to select the Solutions Partner benefits (and pay the fee), you receive incremental specialization benefits after you earn the specialization. They're available through your next Solutions Partner designation anniversary date." They are subject to per-category caps: **five** for Azure specializations and expert offerings, **three** each for Business Applications, Modern Work and Security. As examples of what is on offer, an Azure specialization adds USD 12,000 per year of Azure bulk credits plus GitHub Copilot and GitHub Enterprise credits, and a Security specialization adds USD 9,000 per year of Azure credits plus USD 5,000 of Security Copilot credits.

## What does a Solutions Partner designation unlock for services co-sell?

It is the entry ticket. Microsoft's services co-sell overview states that "services co-sell enables such skilled partners who attain Microsoft AI Cloud Partner program designations to unlock better outcomes by working with Microsoft sellers in a co-selling motion," and — importantly for smaller partners — that "qualified Microsoft partners who have either Solutions partner designations, Specializations, or Azure Expert Managed Services Provider (MSP), can now co-sell with Microsoft sellers **without having to publish a Microsoft Marketplace Consulting services offer**." That removes what used to be a significant barrier: you no longer need to build and publish a marketplace offer just to co-sell services.

## How do Microsoft sellers actually find a designated partner to co-sell with?

Through the **business profile** published in the Referrals workspace in Partner Center. Microsoft states that this profile "showcases their capabilities and designation status to Microsoft Sellers appropriately in our internal tools as well on Microsoft Marketplace listings," and that Microsoft sellers "evaluate these partners for co-selling based on the partner's business profile that is published in Partner center." Inbound opportunities arrive on the **Inbound** tab of the Co-sell opportunities page. Creating and updating the profile requires the business profile admin role. A designation with no published business profile is a badge nobody at Microsoft can act on.

## What do I have to specify when I create a services co-sell opportunity?

You define the deal in the Referrals workspace and request help from the Microsoft sales team, specifying your **partner role** in the deal — the published options are presales envisioning, solution design, proof of concept, business strategy, deployment services, adoption and change management, transaction, and managed services. You must also indicate the **solution area** and **solution play**, so the opportunity routes to the right Microsoft seller and is described in language that seller recognises. Attaching a consulting services offer is now optional rather than required. Opportunities that form part of the Microsoft Commerce Incentive programme are tracked as co-sell opportunities.

## What are the FY26 solution areas and solution plays used in co-sell?

Co-sell routing uses Microsoft's own go-to-market taxonomy rather than the designation names, and the three solution areas are **AI Business Solutions**, **Cloud and AI Platforms**, and **Security** (plus Microsoft Unified). Plays under **AI Business Solutions** include Copilot and Agents at Work, Secure AI Productivity, Sales Transformation with AI, Service Transformation with AI, ERP Transformation with AI, Innovate with Low Code AI and Agents, Converged Communications, Scale with Cloud and AI Endpoints, Data Security, AI Ready with Surface Copilot PCs, and Scale Business Operations with AI. Plays under **Cloud and AI Platforms** include Innovate with Azure AI Apps and Agents, Unify Your Data Platform, Migrate and Modernize Your Estate, Modern SecOps with Unified Platform, and Protect Cloud AI Platform and Apps. **Security** plays include Protect Cloud AI Platform and Apps, Data Security, Modern SecOps with Unified Platform, and Secure AI Productivity.

## What does an ISV need beyond a designation for co-sell?

The requirements diverge for software rather than services. To reach **co-sell-ready** status any partner needs a PartnerID and active Microsoft Marketplace account, a complete business profile, a live published offer, a sales contact for each co-sell-eligible geography, and the required listing and collateral (a one-pager and a pitch deck) on the Co-sell > Solutions page. Business Applications ISVs additionally need **ISV Success** enrolment. To reach **Azure IP co-sell eligible** status there are four further requirements on top: at least **USD 100,000** of Azure Consumed Revenue or Marketplace Billed Sales over the trailing 12 months at organisation level (Azure credits do not count), Microsoft technical validation that the solution is primarily platformed on Azure, a reference architecture diagram, and — for new offers since 11 July 2023 — transactability on Microsoft Marketplace.

## Does holding a designation guarantee incentives?

No, and this is worth stating plainly because partners conflate the two. Microsoft's designation documentation describes benefits (licences, credits, support, go-to-market resources) and co-sell eligibility; it does not describe incentive rates, which are governed separately by the Microsoft Commerce Incentives programme and its own published guides. The only incentive linkage stated in the designation documentation is historical: partners with active legacy Silver/Gold status as of 21 January 2025 remained eligible for applicable MCI incentives through 30 September 2025. Do not infer an incentive rate from a designation.

## What is the honest answer to "is the designation worth the fee?"

The defensible answer is to compare what is gated rather than to quote a payback figure. On the benefits side, a designation moves you from 5 or 10 advisory hours and 2 to 5 support incidents to 50 hours and 50 incidents, and from USD 2,400–5,000 of Azure credits to USD 4,000–10,000 depending on the designation — before counting the licence entitlements. On the access side, it is the only route to specializations, to co-sell-ready professional services offers, and to inbound co-sell opportunities from Microsoft sellers. Against that, the annual fee is not published as a number on Microsoft Learn and depends on the benefits selected, so any ROI arithmetic has to start with the partner's actual quoted fee in Partner Center.

## Currency warning

Benefit quantities in this file come from the Microsoft Learn "Benefits by program and offer" page, which states it is aligned to the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program Benefits Guide dated 18 February 2026. Benefit packages are refreshed at least annually and quantities move — Microsoft added more than 25 new product licences to benefit offerings in January 2025 alone. Co-sell requirements come from a page carrying a July 2024 page date last updated May 2026, and the FY26 solution play list will change at the FY boundary. The annual designation fee is not published on Microsoft Learn and must be read from Partner Center rather than quoted from any secondary source. Always confirm current entitlements in the live benefits guide at aka.ms/SolutionsPartner.benefits.
