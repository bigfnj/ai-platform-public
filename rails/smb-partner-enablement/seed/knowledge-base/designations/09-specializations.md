# Specializations — what they are, how they differ from designations, and the full list

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/specializations · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/specializations-apply · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/introduction-to-pcs · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-faq
> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What is a Microsoft specialization?

A specialization — formerly called an *advanced* specialization — is a second-tier credential that evidences deep technical expertise in one narrow, high-demand scenario, as opposed to the broad capability a Solutions Partner designation evidences. Microsoft's framing is that "specializations display your deep technical expertise and experience in areas with high customer demand. After you attain a Solutions partner designation, earning a specialization further differentiates your organization from your competitors and helps customers identify your capabilities to meet their specific needs." Each specialization is aligned to one solution area and carries its own badge and its own incremental product benefits.

## What is the difference between a Solutions Partner designation and a specialization?

The designation comes first and proves breadth; the specialization comes second and proves depth. Microsoft puts it this way: "the Solutions Partner designation becomes the first opportunity for you to demonstrate your breadth of skills, knowledge, and set yourself apart from the competition. Once you attain a Solutions Partner designation, you can further differentiate your deep technical expertise and experience with specializations." Three mechanical differences follow. A designation is earned from an automatically computed partner capability score; a specialization is earned by meeting a published, scenario-specific checklist. A designation costs an annual fee; a specialization does not. And most specializations require a human validation step — an audit or customer references — that no designation requires.

## Which Solutions Partner designation do I need before I can earn a specialization?

Every specialization has a prerequisite designation, and Microsoft is unambiguous that "to enroll into a Specialization, you need to already be enrolled into the aligned Solutions Partner designation pathway." Some specializations accept any one of several designations. The published mapping is: **AI and Machine Learning on Microsoft Azure** and **App Modernization on Microsoft Azure** need Data & AI *or* Digital & App Innovation; **Analytics on Microsoft Azure** needs Data & AI; **Build and Modernize AI Apps with Microsoft Azure** needs Data & AI *or* Business Applications; **DevOps with GitHub on Microsoft Azure** needs Digital & App Innovation; **Hybrid Cloud Infrastructure with Azure Stack HCI** needs Infrastructure *or* Data & AI *or* Digital & App Innovation; **Infra and Database Migration to Microsoft Azure** needs Data & AI *or* Infrastructure; and **Microsoft Azure VMware Solution**, **Microsoft Azure Virtual Desktop**, **Networking Services in Microsoft Azure** and **SAP on Microsoft Azure** all need Infrastructure. Where more than one designation is listed, you only need one of them.

## Which designation do the Modern Work, Security and Business Applications specializations require?

**Modern Work** specializations — Calling for Microsoft Teams, Customer/Custom Solutions for Microsoft Teams, Meetings and Meeting Rooms for Microsoft Teams, Modernize Endpoints, and the retired Adoption and Change Management — require the Modern Work designation. **Secure AI Productivity** (formerly Teamwork Deployment) is the exception: it requires an active designation in **both Modern Work and Security**. **Security** specializations — Cloud Security, Identity and Access Management, Data Security (formerly Information Protection and Governance) and Threat Protection — require the Security designation. **Business Applications** specializations — Finance, Sales, Service, Small and Midsize Business Management and Supply Chain — require the Business Applications designation, while **Agentic Business Solutions** accepts Business Applications *or* Digital & App Innovation *or* Modern Work. The **Microsoft 365 Copilot** specialization accepts Modern Work *or* Business Applications *or* Security.

## What is the full list of Azure specializations?

As published in August 2026, the Azure specializations are: **AI Platform on Microsoft Azure** (formerly AI and Machine Learning on Microsoft Azure); **Analytics on Microsoft Azure** (a merger of the former Analytics on Microsoft Azure, Data Warehouse Migration to Microsoft Azure and Business Intelligence); **AI Apps on Microsoft Azure** (formerly Build AI Apps with Microsoft Azure); **Agentic DevOps with Microsoft Azure and GitHub** (formerly Accelerate Developer Productivity with Microsoft Azure); **App Modernization on Microsoft Azure** (a merger of Kubernetes on Microsoft Azure and Migrate Enterprise Applications to Microsoft Azure); **Hybrid Cloud Infrastructure with Microsoft Azure Stack HCI**; **Infra and Database Migration to Microsoft Azure**; **Microsoft Azure Virtual Desktop**; **Microsoft Azure VMware Solution**; **Networking Services in Microsoft Azure**; and **SAP on Microsoft Azure**. Partners already enrolled in a merged predecessor are automatically enrolled in the successor, carrying forward the later anniversary date if they held both.

## What is the full list of Business Applications, Modern Work and Security specializations?

**Business Applications**: Agentic Business Solutions (a merger of Microsoft Low Code Application Development and Intelligent Automation), Finance, Sales, Service, Small and Midsize Business Management, and Supply Chain. **Modern Work**: Adoption and Change Management (retired), Calling for Microsoft Teams, Custom Solutions for Microsoft Teams, Meetings and Meeting Rooms for Microsoft Teams, Modernize Endpoints, Secure AI Productivity (formerly Teamwork Deployment), and Microsoft 365 Copilot. **Security**: Cloud Security, Identity and Access Management, Data Security (formerly Information Protection and Governance), Threat Protection, and Microsoft 365 Copilot. Note that Microsoft 365 Copilot is listed under both Modern Work and Security.

## Which specializations require a third-party audit?

Most Azure and all Security specializations require an audit conducted by a third-party auditor. The published audit list is: Analytics on Microsoft Azure, AI Apps on Microsoft Azure, App Modernization on Microsoft Azure, Infra and Database Migration to Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Azure Virtual Desktop, AI Platform on Microsoft Azure, Hybrid Cloud Infrastructure with Microsoft Azure Stack HCI, Microsoft Azure VMware Solution, Agentic DevOps with Microsoft Azure and GitHub, Networking Services in Microsoft Azure, SAP on Microsoft Azure, Cloud Security, Identity and Access Management, Data Security, Threat Protection, and Microsoft 365 Copilot. You cannot schedule the audit until you already meet every other prerequisite — the **Schedule audit** button in Partner Center stays unavailable until then. **If you fail the audit you can re-initiate it after seven days.**

## Which specializations require customer references instead of an audit?

Customer references are required for specific Modern Work specializations: Calling for Microsoft Teams, Custom Solutions for Microsoft Teams, Meetings and Meeting Rooms for Microsoft Teams, Modernize Endpoints, Secure AI Productivity, and the now-retired Adoption and Change Management (whose reference submissions are closed). Microsoft's rule of thumb is that "customer references are required for specific Modern Work and Security specializations. Most Azure specializations require an audit instead of customer references," and the Partner Center specialization detail page tells you which applies.

## What makes a customer reference valid, and how many do I need?

You must submit **three customer references** per specialization, each representing a separate customer engagement. To be valid, a reference must be from a **named, real customer**; be based on an engagement from the **last 24 months**; align clearly to the specific specialization; include **customer contact information** so Microsoft can validate it; describe the partner solution, the Microsoft technologies used and the business outcomes; use the **official Microsoft customer reference PowerPoint template** (one slide per reference, available on the Customer references tab of that specialization in Partner Center); and be **submitted in English**. Microsoft states plainly that "workshop-only or training-only engagements don't qualify." References are typically reviewed within one to two weeks and Microsoft may contact the customer directly. **If your references are rejected you must wait six months before resubmitting** — a far harsher penalty than the seven-day audit retry.

## Which specializations require neither an audit nor customer references?

The Business Applications specializations are the exception: Agentic Business Solutions, Small and Midsize Business Management, Sales, Service, Finance and Supply Chain need neither. Microsoft states that for Business Applications specializations, "after you meet the qualification requirements, you're automatically enrolled for this specialization." That makes them structurally the cheapest specializations to obtain in terms of process overhead — though each still carries performance, skilling and (in most cases) a Microsoft Marketplace consulting-offer requirement.

## Do I have to pay separately for a specialization?

No. Microsoft states: "you don't need to purchase or buy specializations separately, if you're enrolled in the Solution partner designation program." A specialization is included in what your Solutions Partner designation fee already buys. What a specialization does add is **incremental product benefits**, granted on top of your designation benefits and valid through your next Solutions Partner designation anniversary date — but only up to a per-category cap.

## How many specializations can I earn benefits for?

Benefits are capped per solution-area category, even though your enrolment is not. The published maximums are **five** for Azure (Data & AI, Digital & App Innovation, Infrastructure — including Azure Expert MSP), **three** for Business Applications, **three** for Modern Work and **three** for Security. Microsoft's worked example: under Security, a partner enrolled in Cloud Security, Identity and Access Management and Data Security receives three product benefits; enrolling in Threat Protection as a fourth yields no additional benefits because the Security cap is three. You can still hold and badge the fourth specialization — you simply do not get incremental product benefits for it.

## How does specialization renewal work, and when does the audit come back?

Enrolment lasts one year to a "Valid till" date, and the renewal window **opens 60 days before your anniversary date and closes 30 days after it**, giving a 90-day period in which you must meet the published qualification requirements. Qualification requirements — Solutions Partner designation, performance, knowledge, and Microsoft Marketplace offer publishing where applicable — are revalidated **annually**. Audits and customer references are revalidated **every other year**. Concretely: at the first, third and fifth anniversaries (odd years) the specialization renews automatically once you meet the qualification requirements. At the second, fourth and sixth anniversaries (even years) you must also pass the audit or resubmit customer references. Microsoft sends renewal reminders to the partner admin at 120, 90, 60 and 30 days before the anniversary, on the day, and 30 days after.

## What happens to my specialization if I lose the underlying Solutions Partner designation?

You do not lose it immediately. Microsoft's answer to this exact question is that "your enrollment in the specialization stays intact for one year, until the specialization 'Valid till' date. During this period, even if you don't meet all the qualification requirements — for example, Eligibility (Solutions Partner designation), Performance, Skilling, and Microsoft Marketplace requirements — you'll still be enrolled." The consequence lands at renewal: to re-enrol when the renewal window opens you must meet all qualification requirements again, including holding the aligned designation. The same forgiveness applies to any other qualification requirement you drop below mid-year.

## What happened to the Adoption and Change Management specialization?

It is retired. Microsoft no longer accepts new enrolments or renewals, and customer reference submissions are closed. Partners currently enrolled remain active until their anniversary date, after which the specialization lapses and is not renewed. Microsoft states that "adoption and change management capabilities are now evaluated within product-aligned specializations, such as the Copilot specialization," that there is **no replacement specialization**, and that partners are **not automatically enrolled into another specialization**. Any SMB partner material that lists Adoption and Change Management as an attainable credential is out of date.

## Currency warning

The specialization list, prerequisites, audit/reference split, renewal cadence and benefit caps here come from Microsoft Learn pages carrying page dates of 31 July 2026 and last updated 3 August 2026, plus the Solutions Partner introduction page dated 5 August 2026. This list changes often — several specializations were renamed or merged during 2025–2026, and the two Microsoft pages consulted differ slightly on naming (the designation-mapping table still uses older names such as "AI and Machine Learning on Microsoft Azure," "Build and Modernize AI Apps," "DevOps with GitHub on Microsoft Azure" and "Customer Solutions for Microsoft Teams," while the specializations overview page uses the current names). Per-specialization performance and skilling thresholds are not reproduced in this file; check the Apply for specializations page for the current numbers before quoting any.
