# Frontier Partner — the top-tier AI badge, and its retirement

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-august · https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/partnernews/stand-out-with-designations-specializations-and-badges-introduced-in-recent-mont/4504790
> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What is the Frontier Partner badge?

Frontier Partner is Microsoft's top-tier recognition for partners taking an AI-first, human-led
approach — combining AI agents with human expertise. It sits above the Solutions Partner
designations rather than replacing them, and it is deliberately hard to reach: Microsoft
examines demonstrated capability across Cloud & AI Platforms, AI Business Solutions and
Security in equal measure, so a partner strong in only one area cannot earn it.

Partners who meet the requirements find the badge in Logo Builder (`aka.ms/logobuilder`),
which is accessible to MAICPP admins.

## What does Frontier Partner require?

Publicly reported requirements are three Solutions Partner designations plus three
specializations. The designations are one in AI Business Solutions (or Business Applications),
one in Security, and one Cloud & AI Platform-aligned designation. The specializations are
Microsoft Copilot, Data Security, and one of Build AI Apps, AI Platform on Microsoft Azure, or
Accelerate Developer Productivity.

Treat the exact list as directional. Microsoft's own announcement page describes the badge and
its retirement without restating the full criteria, and the detailed requirement lists come
from partner announcements and third-party summaries that vary slightly in wording — some say
"Modern Work or Business Applications" where others say "AI Business Solutions". Confirm
against Partner Center before advising anyone to pursue it.

## Is Frontier Partner realistic for an SMB-focused partner?

Almost never, and it is worth being direct about that. Three designations plus three
specializations — including specializations that carry independent audits and substantial
skilling requirements — represents a scale of investment that a small reseller will not reach.
Partners who publicly announced the badge are large systems integrators and global services
firms. An SMB partner's effort is far better spent attaining one designation on the SMB track
and, if differentiation is needed, a single well-chosen specialization.

## When does the Frontier Partner badge retire?

**The badge retires at the end of June 2027**, as announced at MCAPS Start for Partners in July
2026. Partners holding it can continue using it through fiscal year 2027 provided they continue
to meet the requirements, and partners can still newly achieve it during FY27. After June 2027
no partner may use the badge, regardless of when it was attained.

Microsoft is transitioning the concept to a **Frontier Partner specialization**, which expands
on the capabilities the badge represented and is expected in Partner Center during FY27. A
partner weighing the investment now should be pursuing the specialization path rather than a
badge with a known end date.

## Currency warning

This entry describes a benefit with a published retirement date of June 2027 and a successor
specialization that was not yet available in Partner Center as of August 2026. Both the
requirement list and the specialization's criteria are subject to change. Re-verify against the
Partner Center announcements for the current month before relying on any of it in a partner
conversation.
