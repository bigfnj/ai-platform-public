# Which designations are realistically attainable for an SMB-focused partner

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-modern-work · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-security · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-business · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-azure · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/partner-capability-score · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/specializations-apply · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-faq

## Is the Solutions Partner programme designed for small partners at all?

Yes, deliberately so — Microsoft built parallel SMB attainment paths precisely because a partner serving many small customers cannot be measured on the same thresholds as one serving a handful of large ones. Multiple qualification tracks exist in Data & AI, Digital & App Innovation, Infrastructure, Modern Work and Business Applications, and the Security solution-area page documents its own Enterprise and SMB paths as well. The FAQ says it directly: "for services partners who focus on small and medium business, there's a path within the Solutions Partner for Modern Work." The SMB path is not a lesser badge — it produces the same designation, the same benefits and the same badge as the Enterprise path.

## Which designation should a small reseller pursue first?

**Modern Work**, in almost every case. It scores the workloads an SMB reseller already sells and deploys — Exchange Online, Teams, Microsoft 365 Apps, Intune, Entra ID P1, SharePoint, Teams Phone and Copilot — its SMB track accepts **CSP Tier 1 and Tier 2 association**, so your own transacting customers count, and its skilling requirement is the lightest in the programme. On the SMB path you need only **two people with an intermediate certification and one with an advanced certification** to max out the full 25 skilling points, and Microsoft 365 Fundamentals counts at the intermediate level. That is a genuinely small ask for a small team.

## Which designation is the second-best target for an SMB partner?

**Security**, more often than partners expect. Its skilling metric is worth **40 of the 100 points** — the heaviest single metric anywhere in the programme — and on the SMB track those points come cheaply: one person holding Azure Security Engineer Associate or Cloud and AI Security Engineer Associate earns 4 points, one person holding Microsoft Security Operations Analyst earns another 4, and every individual holding one of the step-3 certifications adds **8 points each**. Four or five certified engineers, counted across steps, can carry 40 points. The SMB track also awards **4 points per net new customer** to a cap of five customers, versus 2 points and ten customers on the Enterprise track, and its Microsoft 365 tenant floor is only **5 paid licences**.

## Is Business Applications realistic for a small Business Central or Power Platform reseller?

Yes, with one significant caveat. The SMB classification (four or fewer unique workloads contributing more than USD 5,000 revenue per customer tenant) makes the skilling requirement very reachable — **five certified people for the full 20 intermediate points and two for the full 15 advanced points**, versus 20 and 7 on the Enterprise track. Net customer adds needs only five workload adds at USD 250 or more per month each, and deployments needs five workloads at 5,000+ monthly consumption value, which for Business Central is five monthly active users. The caveat is **Usage growth**, the heaviest metric at 30 points: you must have a **baseline consumption value of 25,000 twelve months ago** to score anything, and then grow it. A flat installed base scores zero on that metric and zero anywhere disqualifies you regardless of your total.

## Which designations are hardest for a small reseller, and why?

The three Azure designations — Data & AI, Digital & App Innovation and Infrastructure — are the hardest, for two structural reasons rather than one. First, the **skilling gates require senior credentials**: you cannot score any intermediate points in any Azure area without **Azure Administrator Associate**, and you cannot score any advanced points in Digital & App Innovation or Infrastructure without **Azure Solutions Architect Expert**. A partner without an architect scores a structural zero and cannot qualify at any total. Second, the **Deployments metric excludes Virtual Machines and Virtual Machines Licenses**, so a partner whose Azure business is lift-and-shift VM hosting scores zero on that metric too. The consumption thresholds themselves are not the obstacle — the SMB track only asks for USD 500 of ACR per customer per month and USD 1,000 total to activate usage growth.

## What is the easiest Azure designation for an SMB partner to attempt?

**Infrastructure or Digital & App Innovation**, ahead of Data & AI. Both need only **five certified individuals at 4 points each** to max each skilling metric (20 points intermediate, 20 advanced), and on the SMB track the gating step itself is satisfied by **one person** and is worth a flat 4 points. Data & AI concentrates 40 points in a single intermediate metric requiring up to 10 certified individuals, and its gate needs both Azure Administrator Associate and Azure Solutions Architect Expert before any step-3 point lands. Either way, the honest advice to a small reseller is to get the Azure Solutions Architect Expert credential in the building first, because every Azure path depends on it.

## What is the smallest realistic team that can reach a designation?

There is no published minimum headcount, but the certification requirements imply one. On the SMB tracks: **Modern Work** needs as few as three certified people (two intermediate, one advanced) for full skilling, and fewer if one person holds both levels. **Business Applications** needs about seven (five intermediate, two advanced), again fewer with overlap. **Security** needs roughly four to six counted across three steps. The **Azure** areas need one Azure Administrator Associate and one Azure Solutions Architect Expert as gates, plus enough step-2 certified individuals to accumulate points. Certifications are counted per person, not per exam — each individual counts once per metric no matter how many certifications they hold — so depth on one person does not substitute for breadth across people.

## Which specialization is designed for SMB partners?

**Small and Midsize Business Management**, under Business Applications. Its requirements are entirely SMB-shaped: an active Business Applications designation; **at least 15 new Business Central deployments with five or more monthly active users** in the trailing twelve months; monthly active user growth of at least **15%** in the trailing twelve months from a baseline of at least **50** monthly active users across the whole customer base twelve months ago; **five or more individuals** holding Dynamics 365 Business Central Functional Consultant Associate; and at least one Business Central-focused consulting offer published on Microsoft Marketplace. Critically, it requires **no audit and no customer references** — like all Business Applications specializations, enrolment is automatic once you meet the requirements.

## Which specializations should an SMB partner avoid as a first target?

The ones with large absolute performance thresholds, which do not scale down for small partners the way designation tracks do. Specialization requirements have **no SMB variant**. For context: Calling for Microsoft Teams needs **1,000** Teams Phone enabled users of growth; Meetings and Meeting Rooms and Modernize Endpoints each need **5,000** MAU growth; Identity and Access Management needs **2,000** Entra ID Premium MAU growth; Data Security needs **2,500** Purview MAU growth; Cloud Security needs **USD 200,000** of ACR; Threat Protection needs 1,000 MAU growth or USD 100,000 of Sentinel ACR. Microsoft 365 Copilot is comparatively approachable at **1,000 Copilot MAU growth and five net new customers**, but requires an audit and ten certified individuals across two skill groups.

## Should an SMB partner chase a designation or a specialization first?

The designation, always — and not merely because it is the prerequisite. Microsoft answers this explicitly: asked whether working toward a specialization boosts a capability score under 50 points, the FAQ says "No. Specializations and expert programs continue to be a way to further differentiate your organization's deep technical expertise." Specialization work does not move the capability score. Given that specializations require no additional fee once you hold a designation, but their performance thresholds are absolute and large, the rational sequence for a small partner is: attain Modern Work, then add Security or Business Applications at no extra fee, then evaluate Small and Midsize Business Management or Microsoft 365 Copilot as the first specialization.

## What is the single highest-leverage habit for an SMB partner's capability score?

**Filing CPOR claims for the workloads you deploy, rather than relying on CSP association alone.** In Modern Work on the SMB path, 500 users of usage growth via CPOR earns the full 30 points, while it takes 2,000 users via CSP to earn the same 30 — a four-to-one difference for identical customer activity. The same asymmetry appears in Deployments, where each qualifying CPOR deployment is worth 5 points against 2.5 for a CSP deployment. Microsoft awards points on whichever association type scores highest, so the claim costs you nothing and can quadruple the score. One constraint matters: "you can only receive points for usage growth from the time you claim or associate with a customer," so claims must be filed early — there is no retrospective credit.

## What else quietly costs an SMB partner points?

Three things recur. **Churn is netted, not ignored** — net customer adds subtracts lost customers from new ones over the trailing twelve months, so losing two customers to win three nets one. **Certifications expire** — Partner Center raises a recommendation when a valid-until date approaches, and an expired certification drops your skilling score directly, which matters disproportionately in Security where skilling is 40 points. **Unlinked learners earn nothing** — skilling credits count only certifications earned by learners linked to your organisation in Partner Center, so an engineer who certified under a personal account contributes zero until the association is fixed.

## How long does it take an SMB partner to go from starting to qualifying?

Microsoft does not publish a timeline, but the data mechanics set a floor. Performance and Customer success metrics are trailing-twelve-month comparisons, so a customer added today does not fully register until a year has passed. Newly associated customers take up to two update cycles — roughly three to four weeks — to appear at all, and Performance and Customer success data refreshes only around the 20th of each month. Certifications appear faster, within about ten days of completion. The practical implication is that skilling is the only lever that moves a score within weeks; performance and customer success levers take a year to mature, which argues for starting the certification programme first and the customer-association hygiene immediately after.

## Currency warning

Track thresholds, point weights, certification counts and specialization performance requirements in this file were verified against Microsoft Learn solution-area pages carrying page dates between April 2025 and June 2026, all last updated between 31 July and 3 August 2026, plus the Apply for specializations page dated 31 July 2026. Thresholds change at least annually and certification names and retirement dates change more often than that — several certifications named in the underlying requirements retire during 2026. The team-size estimates in this file are arithmetic derived from published per-person point values, not figures Microsoft publishes; treat them as planning guidance and confirm the current requirement in Partner Center before committing a partner to a certification plan.
