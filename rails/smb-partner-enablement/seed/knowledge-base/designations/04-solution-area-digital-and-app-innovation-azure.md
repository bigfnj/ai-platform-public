# Solutions Partner for Digital & App Innovation (Azure) — what it covers and how it is scored

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-azure · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/partner-capability-score
> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What does the Digital & App Innovation (Azure) designation cover?

Digital & App Innovation (Azure) is the developer and application-platform designation among the three Azure solution areas. It covers building, modernising and running custom applications on Azure — application development, DevOps and GitHub practice, containers and Kubernetes, and increasingly AI application and agent development. Like the other Azure areas it is scored purely from Azure consumed revenue and Azure/GitHub certifications, it carries no Microsoft 365 component, and under the August 2026 badge rollup it contributes to the **Solutions Partner for Cloud & AI Platforms** badge.

## Which customer problems does Digital & App Innovation (Azure) map to?

Reach for this designation when the customer conversation is about software the customer owns rather than software they license: a legacy line-of-business application that needs modernising, a monolith that needs containerising, a development team with no CI/CD discipline, a green-field product build, or an AI-powered application or agent that has to be built rather than configured. The certification set is the clearest signal of scope — Azure development, Power Platform development, Azure AI cloud development, agentic AI development, Azure DevOps engineering, and the GitHub Actions, Administration, Advanced Security and Copilot credentials.

## How does the Net customer adds metric score in Digital & App Innovation (Azure)?

Performance is worth a maximum of **30 points** and each net new customer contributes **10 points**, so three net adds reaches the cap. The calculation is `Net customers added = (Eligible Customers last month) – (Eligible Customers same month last year)`. Eligibility differs by track: on the **Enterprise** track an eligible customer is a unique tenant contributing at least **USD 1,000** of Azure consumed revenue in one of the last two months; on the **SMB** track the bar is **USD 500** in at least one of the last two months. All Azure Service Level 2 services are eligible workloads, all offers except Support, Internal, Benefit Program and Trial count, and eligible association types are CSP Tiers 1 and 2, Digital Partner of Record (DPOR) and Partner Admin Link (PAL). These Performance rules are shared across all three Azure solution areas.

## How does the Intermediate certifications metric score in Digital & App Innovation (Azure)?

Intermediate certifications is worth a maximum of **20 points**, earned from up to **five individuals at 4 points each**, and runs as a two-step gate. Step 1 requires **Azure Administrator Associate**. On the **Enterprise** track at least two people must hold it and step 1 itself earns no points; on the **SMB** track one person suffices and step 1 earns a flat 4 points, capped at 4 no matter how many people complete it. Step 2 is where the points come from: each individual holding at least one of **Azure Developer Associate**, **Power Platform Developer Associate**, **Azure AI Cloud Developer Associate**, or **GitHub Certified: Agentic AI Developer** adds up to 4 points. Step 1 is a mandatory gate — without it step 2 scores zero. Azure Developer Associate carries a retirement date of 31 July 2026, remaining valid for one year if completed before then.

## How does the Advanced certifications metric score in Digital & App Innovation (Azure)?

Advanced certifications is also worth a maximum of **20 points** from up to **five individuals at 4 points each**, and mirrors the same two-step structure. Step 1 requires **Azure Solutions Architect Expert** — two people on the Enterprise track earning no points, or one person on the SMB track earning a flat capped 4 points. Step 2 awards up to 4 points per individual holding at least one of **Azure DevOps Engineer Expert**, **Power Platform Solution Architect Expert**, **GitHub Actions**, **GitHub Administration**, **GitHub Advanced Security**, or **GitHub Copilot**. As with the intermediate metric, step 1 is a hard gate and a person can count toward both steps but only once per step. The presence of four GitHub credentials here is the practical signal that Microsoft expects a modern DevOps practice, not just Azure familiarity.

## How do Usage growth and Deployments score in Digital & App Innovation (Azure)?

Both Customer success metrics are scored identically across all three Azure solution areas and identically for Enterprise and SMB. **Usage growth** is worth up to **20 points**, with each one percent of trailing-year Azure consumed revenue growth contributing one point; your total qualifying ACR must be at least **USD 1,000** for the metric to score at all. **Deployments** is worth up to **10 points**, with each eligible deployment contributing **two points** to a maximum of five. An eligible deployment is a unique Azure service (Service Level 2) contributing more than zero ACR over the last 12 months across your customer base — with **Virtual Machines and Virtual Machines Licenses explicitly excluded**, so breadth of platform services, not VM count, is what scores.

## What does a qualifying Digital & App Innovation (Azure) score look like in practice?

The five metrics sum to exactly 100: Performance 30, Intermediate certifications 20, Advanced certifications 20, Usage growth 20, Deployments 10. Every metric must be greater than zero and the total must reach 70. The structural difficulty for a small partner is that both skilling metrics gate on senior credentials — you cannot score any intermediate points without an Azure Administrator Associate and you cannot score any advanced points without an Azure Solutions Architect Expert. On the SMB track those two gates are satisfied by one person each and are themselves worth 8 points combined, which makes the area reachable, but a partner with no architect on staff scores a structural zero in advanced certifications and cannot qualify at any total.

## Currency warning

Point weights, ACR thresholds and certification lists here come from the Microsoft Learn Solutions Partner for Data & AI, Infrastructure, and Digital & App Innovation page carrying a page date of 30 June 2026 and last updated 3 August 2026. Certification names and retirement dates change frequently — Azure Developer Associate retires 31 July 2026 — and Microsoft warns that workload eligibility is subject to change. Microsoft's own worked examples in the Enterprise intermediate section of this area contain an inconsistency (one example lists a "step 3" that the rules do not define), so trust the stated rules over the examples and confirm against your Partner Center metric cards before quoting numbers.
