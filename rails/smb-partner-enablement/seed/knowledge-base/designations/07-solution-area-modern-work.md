# Solutions Partner for Modern Work — what it covers and how it is scored

> Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-modern-work · https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/partner-capability-score
> As of: 2026-08 · Retrieved: 2026-08-14

## What does the Modern Work designation cover?

Microsoft's definition is that "a Solutions Partner for Modern Work demonstrates a broad capability to help customers boost their productivity and make the shift to hybrid work using Microsoft 365." In practice it covers the whole Microsoft 365 productivity and collaboration estate: Exchange Online, SharePoint, Teams and the Teams telephony and meeting-room workloads, Microsoft 365 Apps, Intune, Entra ID P1, the Viva suite, Windows 365 and Microsoft 365 Copilot. Under the August 2026 badge rollup it contributes, alongside Business Applications, to the **Solutions Partner for AI Business Solutions** badge.

## Which customer problems does Modern Work map to?

This is the designation that maps most directly to the everyday SMB conversation: an ageing on-premises Exchange or file server, a business standardising on Teams, a customer who bought Microsoft 365 licences and never deployed half of them, a hybrid-work policy nobody has tooling for, a phone system due for replacement, an unmanaged device estate, or a customer asking about Copilot. Because the scored workload list is dominated by things a small reseller already sells and deploys, Modern Work is usually the first designation an SMB-focused partner should pursue.

## How do the Enterprise and SMB attainment paths work in Modern Work?

Modern Work runs two distinct paths with different criteria and — importantly — you do not choose one. Microsoft "evaluates partners on both paths and selects the highest score from either path," and it does so "at the solution area level, rather than at the individual metric level." So Partner Center computes a complete Enterprise score and a complete SMB score for you and qualifies you on whichever total is higher; you cannot cherry-pick the best metric from each. The difference between the paths is tenant size: an **Enterprise**-track eligible tenant has at least one workload with **more than 300 paid licences**, while an **SMB**-track eligible tenant has at least one workload with **11 to 300 paid licences**. The same tenant can participate in both tracks, and a tenant promoted to Enterprise remains eligible for SMB if it was classified SMB in the previous year.

## How does the Net customer adds metric score in Modern Work?

Performance is worth up to **20 points** on both tracks, but the arithmetic differs. On the **Enterprise** track each net new customer contributes **4 points**, reaching the 20-point cap at **five customers**. On the **SMB** track each net new customer contributes **2 points**, reaching the cap at **ten customers**. The calculation is `Net customer adds = [Total qualifying current customer tenants] - [Total qualifying customers tenants 12 months ago]`. Association types also differ: the Enterprise track accepts **CPOR and DPOR**, while the SMB track accepts **CPOR and CSP (Tier 1 and Tier 2)** — which is the mechanism by which a CSP reseller's own transacting customers count.

## Which workloads count toward Modern Work scoring?

The eligible workload list is common to Net customer adds, Usage growth and Deployments, and covers Exchange Online, Intune, M365 Copilot, Microsoft 365 Apps, Microsoft Entra ID P1, SharePoint Online, Teams, Teams Apps, Teams for Frontline Workers, Teams Meetings, Teams Phone, Teams Phone calling, Teams Phone Enabled Users, Teams Rooms Monthly Active Devices, Teams Premium (on the usage and deployment metrics), Viva Connections, Viva Engage, Viva Goals, Viva Insights, Viva Learning, Viva Topics, and Windows 365. Note that Copilot appears on this list — Copilot deployment work counts toward Modern Work scoring, not only toward the separate Microsoft 365 Copilot specialization.

## How does the Skilling category score in Modern Work?

Skilling is worth **25 points in total**, split as **Intermediate certifications: 10 points** and **Advanced certifications: 15 points**. The number of certified people needed to max out is the sharpest difference between tracks. On the **Enterprise** path you need **four certified people for intermediate and two for advanced**. On the **SMB** path you need only **two certified people for intermediate and one for advanced**. Each person is counted once regardless of how many certifications they hold, and the same person may hold both an intermediate and an advanced certification — but to satisfy the "non-zero in every metric" rule you must have at least one qualifying person with an intermediate certification and at least one (the same or different) with an advanced certification.

## Which certifications count for Modern Work skilling?

The **intermediate** list is Microsoft 365 Fundamentals, Microsoft 365 Certified: Teams Administrator Associate, Microsoft Certified: Identity and Access Administrator Associate, Microsoft 365 Certified: Collaboration Communications Systems Engineer Associate, Microsoft 365 Certified: Endpoint Administrator Associate (previously Modern Desktop Administrator Associate), and Microsoft 365 Certified: Copilot and Agent Administration Fundamentals. The **advanced** list has only two entries: Microsoft 365 Certified: Enterprise Administrator Expert, and the Teams Meetings and Meeting Rooms Technical Assessment delivered through Partner University. The inclusion of Microsoft 365 Fundamentals at the intermediate level is significant for small partners — it is a genuinely entry-level credential.

## How does the Usage growth metric score in Modern Work?

Usage growth is the heaviest metric in Modern Work at **30 points**, and it measures net growth in eligible active users: `Usage growth = [Eligible Active users last month] - [Eligible Active users same month last year]`. Points are awarded proportionally against a threshold that depends on your association type, and Microsoft scores each association option separately then takes the higher result. On the **Enterprise** path the thresholds for maximum points are **1,000 usage growth via CPOR** or **4,000 via DPOR**. On the **SMB** path they are **500 monthly active users via CPOR** or **2,000 MAU via CSP**. You can only earn points for one association type per metric — Microsoft awards points based on whichever earns the most.

## Why does CPOR score so much better than CSP in Modern Work usage growth?

Because the thresholds differ by a factor of four while the maximum points do not. On the SMB path, 500 users of growth via CPOR earns the full 30 points, whereas it takes 2,000 users of growth via CSP to earn the same 30. Microsoft's own worked example makes the consequence explicit: a partner with 1,000 users of CSP growth and 1,000 of CPOR growth scores 15 points on the CSP calculation and would score 60 on the CPOR calculation, so the net score is the capped 30. The practical lesson for a CSP reseller is that filing CPOR claims for the workloads you actually deploy — rather than relying on the CSP association alone — can be worth many multiples in score for the same customer activity. One important caveat: "you can only receive points for usage growth from the time you claim or associate with a customer," so there is no retrospective credit.

## How does the Deployments metric score in Modern Work?

Deployments is worth up to **25 points** and counts net new customer tenants that have crossed a real adoption bar. An eligible deployment is a unique customer tenant where **40% of paid licences are active** (monthly active usage) on an eligible workload, and the calculation is `Deployments = (Eligible deployments last month) - (Eligible deployments same month last year)`. Microsoft excludes deployments claimed or associated within the last 12 months that were already above 40% at the time of claim — you get credit for driving adoption, not for inheriting it. Thresholds again vary by association type: on both tracks **five net new CPOR deployments** earns the maximum, versus **ten net new DPOR deployments** (Enterprise) or **ten net new CSP deployments** (SMB). Each qualifying CPOR deployment is worth 5 points; each DPOR or CSP deployment is worth 2.5.

## What does a qualifying Modern Work score look like in practice?

The five metrics sum to exactly 100: Performance 20, Intermediate certifications 10, Advanced certifications 15, Usage growth 30, Deployments 25. For a small partner on the SMB path, a credible route to 70 is full skilling (25 points from three certified people, or fewer if one person holds both levels), ten net customer adds (20 points), five CPOR deployments at 40% active licences (25 points) — that is 70 already — plus whatever usage growth follows naturally, which is required to be non-zero. The binding constraints are usually the 40% active-licence bar on deployments and getting CPOR claims filed rather than relying on CSP association.

## Currency warning

All weights, thresholds, workload lists and certification lists here come from the Microsoft Learn Solutions Partner for Modern Work page carrying a page date of 4 February 2026 and last updated 31 July 2026. Microsoft notes that eligible workload lists change and should be checked frequently, and certification names change (Endpoint Administrator Associate was renamed from Modern Desktop Administrator Associate; Enterprise Administrator Expert appears elsewhere in Microsoft's own documentation as Microsoft 365 Certified: Administrator Expert). The source page also contains an arithmetic slip in one scoring example (a CPOR growth figure quoted as 1,500 in a scenario stated as 1,000). Re-verify against Partner Center before quoting any threshold.
