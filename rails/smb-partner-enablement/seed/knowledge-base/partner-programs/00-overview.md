## What belongs in this collection

The mechanics a partner has to navigate rather than sell: Partner Center workflows,
incentive structures, solutions partner designations and their measurement, co-sell
eligibility, and the deal-registration path.

## Why this collection needs a date on every file

Program mechanics reset on Microsoft's fiscal year. An incentive rate or designation
threshold that was true last year and is quoted confidently this year is worse than no
answer at all, because a partner will act on it. Every file here carries an "As of" line,
and anything past its fiscal year should be revised rather than left to age quietly.
