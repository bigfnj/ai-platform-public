# SMB-relevant funding, benefits and accelerator programs

## Currency warning

**This is the area of the knowledge base where public documentation is thinnest and where getting a number wrong costs the most.** Microsoft does not publish the monetary value of partner benefits, the size of Azure credit allocations, incentive rates or SMB-specific fund amounts on Microsoft Learn — these live in the MAICPP benefits guide, program guides and rate cards behind partner sign-in, and they are reset every fiscal year. This file therefore states **no benefit values, credit amounts or funding figures** except where Microsoft publishes them openly, and those are attributed. Anything a partner needs to quote commercially must be re-checked in Partner Center or the current benefits guide. Where this file says a figure is not publicly available, treat that as a statement about the public record rather than a claim that no such figure exists.

## What funding-adjacent programs actually exist for an SMB partner?

Four distinct things get loosely called "funding," and it helps to separate them because they behave differently. **Incentives** are earnings calculated against Microsoft Commerce Incentives engagements and paid to your bank. **Co-op funds** are the accrued portion of those earnings, released only against qualifying activity claims with proof of execution. **Partner benefits** are entitlements — software licences, cloud service credits, technical support and presales assistance — that come with a Solutions Partner designation or MAICPP membership rather than being earned per transaction. **Promotions** are discounts applied at the point of sale to the partner price, which improve deal economics without any claim process. A realistic SMB funding plan uses all four, and confuses none of them for the others.

## What are Solutions Partner designations and why do they matter commercially?

The Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program offers **three Solutions Partner designation badges** and **six designation pathways**. The pathways are Data & AI (Azure), Digital & App Innovation (Azure) and Infrastructure (Azure) — all rolling up to the **Solutions Partner for Cloud & AI Platforms** badge; Security, rolling up to **Solutions Partner for Security**; and Business Applications and Modern Work, rolling up to **Solutions Partner for AI Business Solutions**. Qualifying for any pathway that maps to a badge earns you that badge, while your individual pathway names, capability scores and progress stay visible in Partner Center. For a CSP partner this is not cosmetic: holding at least one Solutions Partner designation is a stated requirement for CSP direct-bill authorization and for its annual renewal, and designations unlock the benefits bundle. Silver and gold memberships no longer exist and partners should stop referring to them.

## How does a partner qualify for a Solutions Partner designation?

Eligibility is based on the **partner capability score** achieved by your organization — a composite score quantifying performance across three categories: **Performance, Skilling and Customer success**. The score is calculated from information already captured in Partner Center, so it reflects what you have actually transacted, certified and delivered rather than a submitted application. Partners holding the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program partner admin role can track progress toward any designation from the Solutions Partner dashboard in Partner Center. **The point thresholds required per category and per pathway are not published on the pages reviewed here** — check the Solutions Partner dashboard and the partner capability score documentation for the current numbers rather than relying on a remembered threshold.

## What happens after qualifying — is a designation free?

No, and the renewal timing has a trap in it. Once you qualify in one or more pathways your status changes to **qualified**, and you then proceed with **payment** to enroll in the Solutions Partner designation offer. After purchase, you can renew after one year; the renewal window opens on your anniversary date, one year after your last purchase, and you can renew any time after that. But to keep your benefits flowing — Microsoft names **Azure credits, cloud services and software** — you must **renew within 30 days of your anniversary date** during the open renewal period. Miss that 30-day window and you can't renew; you have to process a new purchase after the renewal period, with the benefit gap that implies.

## What benefits come with a Solutions Partner designation?

The designation offer provides a benefits bundle aligned to the six pathways: the three Azure pathways share core benefits and each has incremental benefits on top. Microsoft names the benefit categories in the renewal context as **Azure credits, cloud services and software**. **The specific quantities and values are published in the MAICPP benefits guide (`aka.ms/Solutionspartner.Benefits`), not on the public documentation pages reviewed here** — do not quote a credit amount or licence count from memory. Enrolling in a **specialization** adds further benefits on top, subject to a maximum cap by pathway: up to **5** specializations earn benefits under Azure (Data & AI, Digital & App Innovation, Infrastructure), and up to **3** each under Business Applications, Modern Work and Security. Specialization benefits align to your Solutions Partner designation enrollment anniversary date.

## Which specializations are relevant to an SMB-focused partner?

Specializations require the aligned Solutions Partner designation as a prerequisite, so plan the designation first. Under **Modern Work** the specializations are Calling for Microsoft Teams, Customer Solutions for Microsoft Teams, Meetings and Meeting Rooms for Microsoft Teams, Secure AI Productivity (formerly Teamwork Deployment), and Modernize Endpoints; Adoption and Change Management is retired. Under **Security** they are Cloud Security, Identity and Access Management, Data Security (formerly Information Protection and Governance), and Threat Protection. Under **Business Applications**, one is named explicitly for this segment: **Small and Midsize Business Management**, alongside Agentic Business Solutions, Finance, Sales, Service and Supply Chain. Note that Secure AI Productivity requires *both* Modern Work and Security designations, and Agentic Business Solutions can be reached via Business Applications, Digital & App Innovation or Modern Work.

## Can co-op funds be used to fund a partner's own skilling and certification?

Yes, and this is the most under-used SMB funding route. Microsoft explicitly states that co-op funds can be invested in activities that facilitate readiness, develop specializations, or attain Microsoft certifications. The published eligible expenses under **Microsoft exams and tuition** include Microsoft exam fees for certifications and competencies, Microsoft tuition fees including on-demand training expenses, third-party ISV solution training where the solution demonstrably uses or resides on Microsoft applications and platforms, and Microsoft Certified Trainer renewal fees. Proof required is a third-party invoice showing the course or exam code and the completion date, with an attendee list and per-person cost as the metric. Given that skilling feeds the partner capability score, which gates Solutions Partner designations, which gate CSP direct-bill eligibility and the benefits bundle — co-op-funded certification is a compounding investment rather than a cost.

## What technical and presales help is available at no cost?

Microsoft's **Technical Presales and Deployment (TPD)** team is named in the CSP overview as a resource partners can call on. In the CSP context specifically, TPD will advise on the most appropriate CSP model and partner tenant architecture, answer authorization requirement questions and risk scenarios, and guide the design of secure customer tenancies including best-practice access and administration models. For a small partner without a dedicated cloud architect this is meaningful unpaid help. Separately, direct-bill partners are required to hold a paid premium support plan — either **Advanced Support for Partners (ASfP)** or **Premier Support for Partners (PSfP)** — which is a cost, not a benefit, and one Microsoft tells you not to purchase until after you have passed credit approval in the direct-bill application process.

## How do promotions function as deal funding for an SMB partner?

Promotions discount the **partner price**, not the estimated retail price, which means the discount lands as margin or as a price concession you can choose to pass through. Nearly all new commerce promotions are auto-applied when the transaction meets the constraints, so there is no claim process and no proof of execution — the economics simply improve at checkout. Promotions carry constraints on seat count ranges, term, billing cycle, first-purchase status and redemption limits, and where two promotions overlap on a SKU the deeper discount applies automatically. Find current promotions by downloading the promotions list from the **Pricing workspace > Benefits** in Partner Center, filtered by market and segment, by calling the `getPromotions` API, or in the **Global Promo Readiness Guide** (partner sign-in required). Always confirm on the Partner Center review cart screen that the promotion applied before submitting the order.

## Where does margin actually come from in CSP, and can a partner quote it?

Not from a published rate. Microsoft's public pricing documentation states that "partner margins, the difference between the list price and the estimated retail prices, can vary from segment to segment," that "typically, education and nonprofit tend to have lower or no margins for CSP partners," and directs partners to "refer to the license-based price list for exact values." That is the whole of the public position — the actual difference between list price (what the partner is billed) and estimated retail price (Microsoft's suggested retail price, also called MSRP) has to be read out of the price list for the specific SKU, market and segment. **Never quote a CSP margin percentage from memory.** For an indirect reseller there is a further layer: only partners with the capability to transact can download price lists, so indirect resellers must obtain pricing from their distributor, whose own margin sits between the Microsoft price list and the reseller's cost.

## What SMB-specific funding could not be verified from public documentation?

Several things a partner may hear referenced are **not verifiable from the Microsoft Learn documentation reviewed for this file**, and should be checked in Partner Center or with a Microsoft or distributor contact rather than asserted. These include: the existence and terms of any named SMB-only accelerator or growth fund; per-fiscal-year SMB campaign funds and market-development funds offered through distributors; the value of Azure credits or software licences attached to a given Solutions Partner designation; any incentive rate, multiplier or tier structure under Microsoft Commerce Incentives; the actual rebate-versus-co-op percentage split for a CSP program; and the partner earned credit rate for Azure. Distributor-run programs in particular are commercial arrangements between the distributor and the reseller, not Microsoft programs, and their terms vary by distributor. Treat all of these as "verify before quoting."

## What is the single most reliable order of operations for an SMB partner building a funding plan?

Sequence it so each step unlocks the next. First, complete **incentives enrollment** with a validated bank and tax profile per location, because nothing pays without it. Second, check the **MCI Engagements page** for the engagements your locations are actually eligible for, and read each Summary page for its rates and proof-of-execution requirements. Third, build a **co-op plan** before the earning period closes, since co-op is use-it-or-lose-it on a six-month usage window and needs a marketing calendar behind it. Fourth, use co-op to fund **certification and skilling**, which raises the partner capability score. Fifth, use that score to qualify for a **Solutions Partner designation**, which is a hard requirement for CSP direct-bill authorization and unlocks the benefits bundle. Sixth, layer **promotions** on individual deals as they arise. The mistake to avoid is starting at step six.
